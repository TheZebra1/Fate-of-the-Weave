# Welcome to **Fate of the Weave**

> _“In the Year of the Pox Plague, 1502 DR, the Weave shudders… and so too does the fate of Faerûn.”_

Welcome, adventurer! You’ve found your way to the knowledge repository of **Fate of the Weave**, a high-stakes campaign set in the Forgotten Realms. This wiki is your guide to surviving—and thriving—in a world on the brink of unraveling.

Whether you're a newcomer to the Realms or a seasoned hero of many taverns, here you'll find everything you need to engage with the campaign: lore, mechanics, custom content, and house rules. Use the links below to get started and stay informed.
