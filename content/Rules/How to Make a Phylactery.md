# **Ritual of Eternal Undeath: The Creation of a Phylactery**
## **Required Sacrifices**
- **One individual twice your age**
- **One individual half your age**

## **Steps of the Rite**
1. **Blood Harvest**
   - Slay both sacrifices in a place of power (a desecrated altar, ancient battlefield, or cursed glade).
   - Collect their blood in a consecrated obsidian chalice.

2. **Potion of Transcendence**
   - Combine the blood with:
     - Grave ash from your ancestors
     - The venom of a serpent that has never bitten
     - A drop of your own blood, willingly given
   - Boil the mixture over balefire until it turns deep black.
   - Let it cool beneath a funerary shroud.

3. **Lunar Consumption**
   - Under the light of a **full moon**, stand within a protective circle inscribed with your true name in infernal.
   - Drink the potion in total silence. Let no sound escape your lips.

4. **Embrace Death**
   - Take your own life before the moon sets.
   - Your soul will be violently torn from your body.

5. **Binding the Soul**
   - The phylactery (prepared beforehand with unholy sigils and precious soul metals) will trap your fleeing essence.
   - You will remain dormant, in spectral limbo.

6. **Rebirth**
   - After three nights, your body shall reform near the phylactery.
   - You are now lich—eternally undead, sustained by the phylactery.

> *"Life is fleeting. Undeath is eternal. But the price must always be paid."*
