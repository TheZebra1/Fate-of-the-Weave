[[Travel Calculator]]
[[Travel Considerations]]
### By Ground 
>Ground mail is cheap, but egregiously slow as a messenger can only travel *24 miles* a day.
>- *Cost: 2 copper/mile*

### By Sea 
> **Sea mail** is the same price as **Ground Mail**, however ships can travel around the clock and therefore travel *72* miles a day.
>- *Cost: 2 copper/mile*
 ### By Air 
>Air mail is double the cost of ground mail, but extremely fast with a high quality pigeon able to travel up to *600 miles* a day.
>- *Cost: 4 copper/mile*
