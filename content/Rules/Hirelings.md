# Staff & Hirelings
This page provides standard wages, hiring costs, and equipment requirements for staff employed by a business or stronghold.

Most employees are assumed to be **Commoners (CR 0)** unless otherwise noted. Guards and soldiers are typically **1st-level Fighters**, while certain specialists may possess class levels or tool proficiencies appropriate to their profession.
# Hiring & Wages
Employees expect to be paid at the end of each month. Failure to pay wages may result in workers leaving, refusing service, or seeking employment elsewhere.
## Standard Wages

| Labor Type      |    Daily Wage | Monthly Wage | Lifestyle   |
| --------------- | ------------: | -----------: | ----------- |
| Unskilled Labor |          2 sp |         6 gp | Poor        |
| Skilled Labor   |          2 gp |        60 gp | Comfortable |
| Messenger       | 2 CP per Mile |              |             |

> **Regional Pricing:** These wages assume a typical fantasy kingdom.
> - Poor settlements may pay as little as **1 sp/day** for unskilled labor.
> - Wealthy cities may pay **5 sp/day** or more.
> - Particularly dangerous work may command double or triple normal wages.

### Higher Challenge Ratings
For creatures above **CR 3**, determine the daily wage using:

> **Daily Wage = CR² gp**

Examples:

| CR | Daily Wage |
|---:|-----------:|
| 4 | 16 gp |
| 5 | 25 gp |
| 6 | 36 gp |
| 8 | 64 gp |
| 10 | 100 gp |
## Hazard Pay
Mercenaries rarely agree to dangerous work for standard wages.
>The DM may increase costs based on the assignment:

| Assignment | Typical Multiplier |
|------------|------------------:|
| Routine guard duty | ×1 |
| Military campaign | ×1.5 |
| Monster hunting | ×2 |
| Dungeon expedition | ×2–3 |
| Nearly suicidal mission | ×4 or more |

Many experienced mercenaries also require a **minimum contract length**.

For example, a Knight may cost **270 gp/month**, but refuse contracts shorter than **10 days**, requiring payment for the entire period up front.

> **Note:** Permanent stronghold guards are considerably cheaper than short-term mercenaries. A full-time guard employed by a business or stronghold earns only **8 gp per month**, while hiring an equivalent guard for a few days of temporary work is substantially more expensive.
