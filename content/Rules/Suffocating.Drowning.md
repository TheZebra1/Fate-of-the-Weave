# ### Suffocating (2024 Modified)
While suffocating, a creature gains **levels of exhaustion at the end of each of its turns**, based on its Constitution modifier:

- **CON modifier 0 or +1:** Gain **6** levels per turn  
- **CON modifier +2:** Gain **3** levels per turn  
- **CON modifier +3:** Gain **2** levels per turn  
- **CON modifier +4:** Gain **1** level per turn  
- **CON modifier +5:** Gain **1** level per turn; however, the creature **ignores all effects of exhaustion** until it reaches level 6

When a creature reaches **6 levels of exhaustion** from suffocating, it falls **unconscious** instead of dying. It remains unconscious until it can breathe again and has fewer than 6 levels of exhaustion.
- A creature that begins to breathe again loses all exaustion gained from **Suffocation**

### Definitions 
**Holding Breath**: 
- Voluntary choice, requires no action.
	- a creature can hold it's breath for a number of ***minutes*** equal to ***1 + Constitution Modifier***.
- Sustaining damage requires a *Concentration saving throw*. On a failure, begin **choking/suffocating.**
