# Long-Term Madness
*Source: SRD / Basic Rules* 

`dice: [](long-term-madness.md#^long-term-madness)`

**Long-Term Madness**

| dice: d100 | Effects (lasts 1d10 × 10 hours)                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01-05      | The character falls [unconscious](conditions.md#unconscious). No amount of jostling or damage can wake the character.                                                                                                                     |
| 06-10      | The character loses the ability to speak.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 11-15      | Whenever the character takes damage, he or she must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be affected as though he or she failed a saving throw against the [confusion](confusion.md) spell. The confusion effect lasts for 1 minute. |
| 16-25      | The character suffers from partial amnesia. The character knows who he or she is and retains racial traits and class features but doesn't recognize other people or remember anything that happened before the madness took effect.       |
| 26-35      | The character experiences uncontrollable tremors or tics, which impose disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws that involve Strength or Dexterity.                                                                |
| 36-45      | The character is [blinded](conditions.md#blinded) (25%) or [deafened](conditions.md#deafened) (75%) Roll a d4 to decide.                                                                                                                  |
| 46-55      | The character becomes attached to a "lucky charm," such as a person or an object, and has disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws while more than 30 feet from it.                                                |
| 56-60      | The character experiences a powerful delusion. Choose a ***potion*** --> [[Herbs-Potion Crafting#Potion Costs]]. The character imagines that he or she is under its effects.                                                                                                               |
| 61-70      | The character regards something (usually the source of madness) with intense revulsion, as if affected by the antipathy effect of the [antipathy/sympathy](antipathy-sympathy.md) spell.                                                  |
| 71-80      | The character suffers extreme paranoia. The character has disadvantage on Wisdom and Charisma checks.                                                                                                                                     |
| 81-85      | The character experiences vivid hallucinations and has disadvantage on ability checks.                                                                                                                                                    |
| 86-90      | The character feels compelled to repeat a specific activity over and over, such as washing hands, touching things, praying, or counting coins.                                                                                            |
| 91-100     | The character's madness is down-graded to short term. Roll on the [short term madness](short-term-madness) table.                                                                                                                         |
^long-term-madness
