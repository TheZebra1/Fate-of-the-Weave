# Infection of the Curse

### Initial Infection
- Once a creature is infected with lycanthropy, one week must pass before they can transform into their lycanthrope form. 
- During this time, the curse has not yet anchored itself to the creature’s body, mind, and soul, and can be removed by the *remove curse* spell. 
- After one week, the curse becomes intrinsic to the creature, and only a *wish* spell, a magical ritual, or a specific item can remove it.

### Born Lycanthropes
- Born lycanthropes — individuals with at least one lycanthrope parent — are affected by the curse since their inception.
- The curse remains dormant until puberty, preventing any transformation until it awakens.
- Once awakened, they follow the same rules as infected lycanthropes.
- However, a born lycanthrope’s curse cannot be removed with the *remove curse* spell.

# Transformation Rules

### Curse DC
The Curse DC represents your struggle to control your mind during a transformation. It changes based on your interaction with lycanthropy:

- **Starting Curse DC**: 10
- **Changes to Curse DC**:
  - **+2**: Willingly transform into your lycanthrope form.
  - **+1**: Unwillingly transform into your lycanthrope form.
  - **-2**: Successfully resist an unwilling transformation.
  - **+1**: Fail to transform back into your normal form.
  - **-1**: Successfully transform back into your normal form.
  - **+1**: Lose control of your lycanthrope form when HP < 1/3 of max HP.
  - **-1**: Successfully control your lycanthrope form when HP < 1/3 of max HP.
  - **-1**: Each week without willingly transforming.
- **Limits**: Curse DC cannot go below 8 or above 20 unless otherwise specified.

### Maximum Curse DC
- If your Curse DC reaches 20, you are permanently stuck in lycanthrope form. Only *greater restoration* or death can return you to normal form.

# Lycanthrope Form

### Features
- **Transformation**: As a bonus action, you can willingly shift into your lycanthrope form for up to 1 hour.
- **Appearance**: A hybrid of your normal form and the animal associated with your lycanthropy.
- **Attributes**:
  - Creature type: Humanoid (shapechanger).
  - Walking speed: +5 feet.
  - Strength or Dexterity: +2 (chosen at infection).
  - Temporary HP: Equal to twice your proficiency bonus.
  - Unarmed strikes:
    - Claws: 1d6 slashing damage.
    - Bite: 1d6 piercing damage.
    - Use Strength or Dexterity for attack and damage rolls (chosen at infection).
  - Full control is maintained unless HP < 1/3 of max HP.

### Losing Control
- When HP < 1/3 of max HP:
  - Make a Constitution or Wisdom saving throw (your choice) against your Curse DC.
  - **Success**: Maintain control and reduce Curse DC by 1.
  - **Failure**: Lose control and increase Curse DC by 1.

- **Effects of Losing Control**:
  - Lose concentration on spells.
  - GM decides:
    - Attack the nearest creature (friendly or hostile) using only ***bite*** attacks, or
    - Run away from the nearest threat.

- **Regaining Control**:
  - Make a saving throw against your Curse DC at the end of each turn.

### Additional Rules
- Transform back to normal form:
  - Use a bonus action (requires control).
  - Cannot move or take actions until after your next turn.
- Automatic reversion:
  - Unconscious (unless Curse DC = 20).
  - Death.

- **Full Moon**:
  - Make a saving throw against your Curse DC to resist unwilling transformation.
  - All saving throws against Curse DC are at disadvantage during the full moon.

- **Transformation Limit**:
  - Can only transform once per long rest.

# Infecting Lycanthropy

- A lycanthrope can infect another creature with lycanthropy through a bite attack.
- Target must make a Wisdom or Constitution saving throw (their choice) against the lycanthrope’s Curse DC.
  - **Failure**: The creature is infected and becomes a lycanthrope after 1 week.
  - **Willing Failure**: The creature accepts the curse.

- **Restrictions**:
  - Only humanoids can carry the curse.
  - Natural shapechangers (e.g., doppelgangers) are immune.

# Lycanthropy Feats

### Lycanthrope Fledgling
**Prerequisites**: 4th Level, Lycanthrope  
**Benefits**:
- Increase Strength, Dexterity, Wisdom, or Constitution by 1 (max 20).
- Walking speed +5 feet in lycanthrope form.
- Willingly transforming increases Curse DC by only 1.
- When you lose control, only attack friendly creatures if no hostile creatures are visible.

---

### Lycanthrope Adept
**Prerequisites**: Fledgling Lycanthrope feat, Lycanthrope  
**Benefits**:
- Increase Strength, Dexterity, Wisdom, or Constitution by 1 (max 20).
- Unarmed strikes deal 1d8 damage and are magical.
- Saving throws against Curse DC to maintain control are at advantage.
- If no hostile creatures are visible, flee instead of attacking friendly creatures.
- No disadvantage on saving throws against Curse DC during a full moon.

---

### Lycanthrope Master
**Prerequisites**: Adept Lycanthrope feat, Lycanthrope  
**Benefits**:
- Increase Strength, Dexterity, Wisdom, or Constitution by 1 (max 20).
- Curse DC cannot exceed 16.
- Proficient with Wisdom or Constitution saving throws against Curse DC.
- Cannot unwillingly transform into lycanthrope form.
- Can only infect others while in control of lycanthrope form.
- Transform twice per long rest.
