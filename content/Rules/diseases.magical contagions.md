### Magical Contagions
Alchemists, potion brewers, and areas of wild magic are credited with creating the first magical contagions. An outbreak of such a contagion can form the basis of an adventure as characters search for a cure and try to stop the contagion's spread.

#### Rest and Recuperation
If a creature infected with a magical contagion spends 3 days recuperating—engaging in no activities that would interrupt a Long Rest—the creature makes a DC 15 Constitution saving throw at the end of the recuperation period. On a successful save, the creature has Advantage on saving throws to fight off the magical contagion for the next 24 hours.

### Demon Fever
*Fiendish Contagion*

Demon Fever is a supernatural disease inflicted upon humanoids by wounds from demonic creatures or exposure to corrupted environments. The fever ravages the body, weakening its victims over time. 

A creature suffers the following effects at dawn each day starting 1d4 days after infection:
The creature must make a DC 13 Constitution Saving throw or suffer the following effects:
- **Burning Fever**: Gain 1 level of Exhaustion which is not reduced by a long rest & lasts until the contagion ends.
- **Constitution Drain**:  Take **7 (2d6)** Necrotic damage. This reduction lasts until the contagion is cured. 
- **Delirium**: While infected, the creature has disadvantage on **Intelligence** and Wisdom **checks** & saves due to fevered hallucinations.

**Fighting the Contagion**: 
- A [[lesser-restoration-xphb|Lesser Restoration]], [[greater-restoration-xphb|Greater Restoration]], [[heal-xphb|Heal]] or a spell of similar nature as deemed appropriate by the DM resolves illness.
- After succeeding on three of the creatures saves, at dawn, the contagion ends, and the creature is immune to Demon Fever for 1 year.

**Spreading the Contagion**: Any creature that consumes food or water contaminated with the contagion or takes damage from an infected creature's unarmed or natural attack (bite, claws, etc) must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or also become infected with Demon Fever. A creature that succeeds on this save is immune to infection from that particular source for 24 hours.

#### Indoctrination
##### Vision 1: "The Blooming Silence"
*Trigger: First night after infection*

You stand in a cavern of bioluminescent moss. There is no sound—no echo, no breath, not even your heartbeat. You watch as a cluster of mushrooms blooms in slow, rhythmic pulses, like lungs breathing. A single spore floats toward you and lands on your skin. It doesn't hurt. It feels... *welcoming*. A voice like a whisper in damp soil hums:

**"Stillness is peace. Let go of the noise."**

You wake up with the memory already fading, but you can still feel the phantom touch of that spore.

##### Vision 2: "The Garden That Listens"
*Trigger: After the party visits or passes through any natural cavern or fungal growth*

You're standing in a lush fungal grove. The caps of giant mushrooms open like flowers, turning to face you. As you step forward, you hear laughter—your companions' laughter—but muffled, distant. You see their bodies lying on the ground, peaceful, half-consumed by mold. One of them opens their eyes. They smile. 

**"Don't fear the change. We hear you. We understand."**

Vines creep up your legs, gently. 
- What do you do as the vines snake their way up? Do you resist? You're not sure you *want* to.
	- WIS save if tries to fight vines. 
##### Vision 3: "The Spore Communion"
*Trigger: Failed Wisdom save or moment of emotional vulnerability*

You float, weightless, inside a pulsing mass of hyphae and root. Countless voices murmur in unison—not separate, but one. Thoughts not your own pass through your mind: plans, designs, the need to spread, to make things whole. You see the Underdark—not as it is, but *as it should be*: covered in soft, warm mycelium. No war. No fear. Just *oneness*.

Then, something steps into your vision: a woman-shaped mass of rot and bloom, crowned in toadstools and veiled in filaments.

**"You belong. Soon, all will belong. All will have peace."**

You jolt awake. You're sweating. Your mouth tastes like earth and old leaves.

##### Vision 4: "The Wedding Hymn"
*Trigger: Approaching Neverlight Grove or reaching high corruption threshold*

A grand chamber of flesh and fungus. An aisle of spongy ground leads you to an altar carved from bone and lichen. Figures bow in rows, faceless, swaying to a rhythm only they can hear. At the altar stands **Her**—[[Zuggtmoy]], towering and elegant in a gown of living moss and twitching vines. She offers you her hand.

- Take it?
- Reject it (WIS save)
Your own arm lifts—not by choice. You are crying and smiling. She speaks directly into your soul:

**"Be my vessel. Let us wed the world in bloom."**

Failure here is indoctrination and you work for  [[Zuggtmoy]]. Disobeying her will cause 1d12 psychic damage every direct action against or every minute of working against the wishes of [[Zuggtmoy]].

### Mad Monkey Fever
Mad monkey fever is a **disease** spread by the **mad monkeys** of [[Chult]], as well as exposure to contaminated jungle environments. 
#### **Transmission**
- Contact with infected **mad monkeys** or their remains.
- Exposure to contaminated areas within the jungle.
- **DC 13 Constitution Check**
#### **Symptoms**
- High fever
- Uncontrollable tremors
- Hallucinations leading to erratic behavior
#### **Effects**
- **Incubation Period:** 1d6 hours after exposure.
- **Initial Symptoms:** Disadvantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma checks.
- **Progression:** At the end of each long rest, the infected must make a **DC 13 Constitution saving throw**.
  - **Failure:** Gain one level of **[[madness]]** (roll on the Short-Term Madness Table, *Dungeon Master’s Guide*, p. 258).
>- ***ROLL***
`dice: [](short-term-madness.md#^short-term-madness)`

  - **Three Failures:** The [[madness]] becomes **Long-Term** (*DMG*, p. 260) and persists even after the disease is cured.
>***ROLL***
`dice: [](long-term-madness.md#^long-term-madness)`

  - **Success:** The disease is cured after **three consecutive successful saves**.
#### **Cure**
- A creature afflicted with mad monkey fever can be cured by:
  - **Lesser Restoration** or more powerful magic.
  - **Herbal remedies** found in Chult (requires DC 15 Medicine check).
  - Natural recovery after three successful Constitution saves.
