# Business Profits

Since D&D is about adventuring, getting "free gold" from a business does not always work well in a game system where players look for treasure and loot. It is also added work for the DM to manage and track. However, some DMs may wish to add a little more realism. While a party is adventuring, it is possible for a business to thrive and become a lucrative enterprise. Alternatively, a business can be raided by bandits, fall prey to arson, be infested by rats, or simply have very few customers.

Every business cycle (determined by the DM: month, week, quarter, year, etc.), roll on the following table.

To use this table, the DM should know:

- **Maintenance Cost** (typically staff salaries) 
	- [Price of Staff](https://www.thievesguild.cc/stronghold/staff)/[[Hirelings]]
- **Business Value** (usually the purchase price of the business; see Stronghold structures for examples)
	- [Price of Buisness](https://www.thievesguild.cc/stronghold/index.php)/[[Stronghold Structures]]

Profits and losses are based on the business's total value.

> **Note:** Profit/loss based on business value uses a percentage of the business's value. A luxurious tavern worth **50,000 gp** will naturally generate much larger profits—and losses—than a run-down tavern worth **6,000 gp**. DMs should use caution with percentage-based results.

> **Business Value Rule:** Whenever a business gains or loses gold equal to a listed percentage of its value, the business's value also increases or decreases by that same percentage.  
>
> For example, if a business worth **10,000 gp** gains **2% of value + 3d10×5 gp**, it earns **200 gp + 3d10×5 gp**, and its value increases by **2%**, becoming **10,200 gp** before any other adjustments.

> **Adventure Hook:** Any loss result can become an adventure.  
> Examples:
>
> - Cult symbols appear on the building → Hunt down the cultists.
> - A suspicious fire damages the business → Investigate possible arson.
> - A theft occurs → Track down the thieves.
> - The business is seized → Convince the ruler or uncover corruption.

## Modifiers to d100
>Apply all applicable modifiers before rolling. Unless otherwise noted, each modifier may only be applied once per business cycle.

| Condition | Modifier |

| Experienced Manager or Clerk (12 gp/month) | +1 |
