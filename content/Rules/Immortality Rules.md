# **Immortality Wish Consequences Toolkit**  
_A guide for DMs to adjudicate players wishing for immortality using the Wish spell (or similar magic)._

## **Core Philosophy**
- All wishes should be technically granted — but in a way that teaches caution.
- The clearer and wiser the wish phrasing, the better the possible outcome.
- Low rolls = ironic horror or punishment.
- High rolls = truer realization of the wish, though not without weight or narrative consequences.
- Imagination and intuitive fairness are key.

## **General Immortality Rules**
Upon using *[[wish-xphb|Wish]]* for immortality:
- Player suffers standard *Wish* stress penalties (per the 2024 rules).
>- *Player must immediately roll on the appropriate **Immortality Table** based on phrasing.*
- DM describes the consequence in a vivid, narratively satisfying way.

# **Immortality Tables**
## **Table 1: "I want to live forever." (d6)**

| d6 | Outcome |
|:--|:--|
| 1 | **Petrified:** You become an eternal stone statue, fully conscious, unable to move or communicate. |
| 2 | **Tree Binding:** Your body merges with a great ancient tree; as long as the tree lives, so do you — rooted, immobile, and aware. |
| 3 | **Undead Hunger:** You rise as an undying ghoul who must devour living flesh monthly or decay into insanity. |
| 4 | **Aging Without End:** You continue aging indefinitely, becoming more frail and decrepit but never dying of old age. |
| 5 | **Timeless Isolation:** You are immortal, but time for you dilates — a year passes for you for every day others experience. [*] |
| 6 | **True Longevity:** You no longer age and cannot die from natural causes, though you are still vulnerable to violence and magic. [*] |

## **Table 2: "I wish never to die." (d6)**

| d6 | Outcome |
|:--|:--|
| 1 | **Living Corpse:** Your body cannot die but still rots. You become a shambling, rotting, suffering thing. [*] |
| 2 | **Eternal Dismemberment:** You can survive any injury — even beheading or disintegration — but cannot heal naturally without powerful magic. [*] |
| 3 | **Tethered to Pain:** When reduced to 0 HP, you remain conscious and suffer, unable to stabilize or pass out. |
| 4 | **Spirit Anchor:** Your soul binds to your remains; if your body is destroyed, you linger as a ghost, unable to move on. |
| 5 | **Conditional Deathlessness:** You are immortal unless slain by a secret weakness, unknown even to you. |
| 6 | **Functional Immortality:** You survive any mortal harm; even complete destruction reforms you after 1d10 days. [*] |

# **Cosmic Enforcement Rule**

> **Those who defy death shall not go unnoticed.**

If a character's wish violates the natural laws of life and death (as marked [*]), the multiverse responds:
- Roll 1d4:
  - On a roll of 1, an **Inevitable Marut** becomes aware and begins to hunt the character.
  - Even without an immediate hunt, Mechanus records their existence. Further cosmic consequences may occur at the DM’s discretion.

> _"The gears of Mechanus turn. The violation is logged. Correction shall be enacted."_

# **DM Tips**
- Reward creative, careful phrasing — but still respect the spirit of the monkey’s paw.
- Always make consequences feel narratively earned and mythically powerful.
- Lean into the mythic horror: immortality is rarely a blessing.
- Use the Inevitables not just as punishment — but as mythic, awe-inspiring forces of cosmic justice.
