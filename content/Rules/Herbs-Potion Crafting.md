### **Gathering Ingredients**
>***Gathering this way is not as thorough as [[Gathering Reagents.Crafting|Slow Gathering]], and so you gain none of those materials, but this only takes 1-2 hours.***
- Make a ***DC Survival or Nature***, using the **"Foraging"** column of the [[Travel Considerations#Terrain Foraging & Navigation DC's|Terrain Foraging Table]] to establish a **DC**.
	- Success yields **1 herb** and **1 suspension** ingredient by rolling on the corresponding tables below.
	- Regional effects may apply
### **Crafting a Potion**
- **In 8 hours of work**, a character with an **[[herbalism-kit]] or [[alchemists-supplies]]** can combine a ***herb***,  ***suspension***, and **25gp** to craft a potion.
	- *Create as many potions as you have **supplies** & **adequate brewing** stations for.*
- If the ingredients do not correspond to a real potion, a **[[Herbs-Potion Crafting#Wild Potion Outcome System|Wild Potion]]** is created.

# Consuming the Same Potion Twice/Alchemical Toxicity
- **Healing Potions**: No toxicity effect.
- **All Other Potions**: If the same potion is consumed twice within 24 hours, a **Constitution saving throw** is required.

#### Toxicity Saving Throws:
- **Second Dose**: **DC 12** **=** *Disadvantage on ability checks for 1 hour.*   
- **Third Dose**: **DC 15** **=** *Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws for 1 hour*.
- **Fourth Dose**: **DC 20** **=** *4 Levels of [[conditions#Exhaustion|Exhaustion]]*
	- **Critical Failure (nat. 1) on 4th dose** **=** ***Severe effect (DM's discretion, e.g., temporary debuff or permanent damage).***

# Wild Potion Outcome System
When a player mixes herbs and suspensions that do not result in a potion it creates a "***Wild Potion***"
### **How the System Works**

1. **Roll a d20**: The player rolls a **d20** to determine the general type of effect (negative, magical, or positive):
   - Low rolls (1-7): Major Negative
   - Mid rolls (8-14): Minor Negative
   - High rolls (15-19): Minor Magical (fun but harmless)
   - **Nat 20**: Unexpected Boon (a positive surprise!)

2. **Roll a d10**: Based on the result of the d20, roll a **d10** to determine the specific effect from the corresponding table.
