# 📖 The Tempest Vanguard: The Journey So Far
The adventuring party — **The Tempest Vanguard** — began chasing the strange appearance of **zombie-laced beer** in *Trostenwald*, unknowingly stepping into a web of ancient arcane threats, political intrigue, criminal underworld wars, and echoes of long-lost civilizations.

Their journey took them from *Trostenwald* to a gnoll cult summoning a manticore in *Felderwin*, through deep investigations and forbidden ruins beneath *Zadash*, and now into the swamp town of *Berlebun*, where they are fighting a faction war against the brutal *Iron Shepherds* while acting as proxy agents for the criminal guild known as the *Myriad*.

At the heart of their quest lie mysteries and connections to:
- *Aeor* — the lost floating city and Eldric’s place of origin  
- Tybalt’s mysterious amulet  
- The buried pasts of *Gideon Jura* and *Tavin*  
- The dark ambitions of the *Cerberus Assembly*  
- The rising corruption within the *Dwendalian Empire*  

## 🧙‍♂️ Party Roster: *The Tempest Vanguard*
- **Tybalt** – 🐉 *Kobold Druid*  
  Dino-obsessed shapeshifter with a strange amulet that pulses with ancient energy. Loose cannon.  
  - Often summons and shapeshifts into raptors, plesiosaurs, or allosaurs.  
  - Bad with money. Ripped off by a drunk and a shady alley vendor.  
  - Has an ancient amulet like Eldric.  
    - Amulet glowed when hunting wolves. Tybalt saw a rabbit in the road and passed a WIS save.

- **Tavin** – 🎭 *Halfling Bard/Rogue/Fighter*  
  Charming, elusive, and deeply connected to the *Myriad*. Skilled in espionage, gambling, and debt navigation.  
  - Cleared his debts in a game of Knucklebones with *The Gentleman*.  
  - Observed *Darien Dondarien* reminiscing over armor — possible history?

- **Eldric** – ⚡ *Cleric of Kord / Storm Sorcerer*  
  Survivor of the fall of Aeor. Carries a divine spark of storm and legacy.  
  - Opened a hidden chamber beneath Zadash using his amulet.  
  - Tensions rising with agents of the *Cerberus Assembly*.

- **Gideon Jura** – 🏹 *Human Ranger (Criminal Past)*  
  Brooding hunter with a dark family history. Haunted by visions and fate.  
  - Rescued his long-lost brother, sister-in-law (Talora), and niece in Felderwin.  
  - Had a fortune read: *Past: Mind Flayer / Present: Strength / Future: Wellspring*  
  - Saw a man — likely his father — in a vision.

## 🧵 Ongoing Threads & Plotlines
### The Cerberus Assembly’s Shadow
- Behind the distribution of zombie-laced beer, first uncovered in *Trostenwald*.  
- Used necrotic agents and *doppelgangers* — likely tied to Aeorian research.  
- *Voss* and *June* were early suspects, now thought to be minor assets.  
- The party is now marked by the Assembly after clashing with *Thatus Ilveth*, a Volstrucker agent.  
- One Assembly mage attempted to immolate the party in Zadash — they escaped.  
- *Kara*’s parents may have been taken to *Rexxentrum* by Assembly agents.  
- Chamber beneath *Zadash* (opened via Eldric's amulet) may hold deeper connections to their plans.

### Iron Shepherd Conflict
- Party hired by the *Myriad* to install *Darien Dondarien* as Berlebun’s puppet mayor.  
- The town is under oppressive martial law by the *Iron Shepherds*, led by a yet-unnamed (but Spanish-accented) commander.  
- *Ophelia*, former Myriad leader, plans a coup and offered the party any loot found in her captured manor.  
- Party has just infiltrated the manor grounds via secret tunnel.

## 🗺️ World & Setting
### 📍 Key Locations
- **Trostenwald** – Site of the first zombie beer incident.  
- **Felderwin** – Location of the gnoll cult and manticore summoning.  
- **Zadash** – City atop ancient elven ruins. Cerberus Assembly experiments conducted here.  
- **Berlebun** – Swamp-bound town locked in gang war. Myriad vs. Iron Shepherds.  
- **Aeor** – Lost flying city tied to ancient magic and current mysteries.  
- **Rexxentrum** – Cerberus Assembly stronghold. Possible location of abducted citizens.

### ⚔️ Factions
- **Myriad** – Criminal syndicate using the party to influence Berlebun.  
- **Iron Shepherds** – Violent rival gang imposing martial law.  
- **Cerberus Assembly** – Arcane elite experimenting with necromancy, possession, and Aeorian ruins.  
- **Volstruckers** – Assembly enforcers and spies.  
- **Kryn Dynasty** / **Kriks** – Dark elf kingdom stirring tension with the Empire.  
- **Cobalt Soul** – Monastic scholars tracking forbidden knowledge.
