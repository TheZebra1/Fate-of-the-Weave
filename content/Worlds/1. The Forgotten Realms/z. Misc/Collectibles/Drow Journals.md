# Take 2
### Journal 1: The Blade  
**Author:** Sullenul Duskryn *(Elite Guard)*  
**Date:** Uktar 3, 1502 DR  

He is called nothing. Not even by the Matron. But we speak of him—the green-eyed storm with dual blades and hair blacker than the Weeping Caves. I’ve never seen him, but the accounts ripple through the caravan. Fast as faerzress lightning, silent as priestess' judgment. A metal cord is said to run down his back like a forged web. Some say he was made in pain, not born in it. Others whisper that he is the [[Avatar]] of [[Vhaeraun]], but to insinuate he would manifest as a human?? It is heresy is it not?

### Journal 2: The Pale Spider  
**Author:** Drisyluld Duskryn  
**Date:** Uktar 4, 1502 DR  

The white-haired one remains far above, pulling threads like a patient weaver. He has the ear of the Matron Mothers—not through respect, but advantage. A surface noble, or so they say, with a voice smooth as oil on obsidian and schemes thicker than web. His plan, not ours: construct these modular temples, call forth guardians, tear the veil, bathe Toril in shadow. But Lolth's patience thins. The Matron speaks often now of “when he is no longer useful.” Let the surface puppet string his little show. In the end, his bones will line the crypt he opened.

### Journal 3: The Arachnir’s Hunger  
**Author:** Telervid Duskryn  
**Date:** Uktar 6, 1502 DR  

The arachnir... it thinks. it remembers. It *must* feed on the essence of a living being before we can interact with it. It's food is the spark of life itself. Kobold slaves expire too quickly. The kenku last longer. We drag them along like the refuse they are but they are dying too fast and the arachnir hungers ever more.

### Journal 4: The Ambush at Dolblunde  
**Author:** Sablennil Duskryn *(Elite Guard)*  
**Date:** Uktar 7, 1502 DR  

They came cloaked in midnight—Waterdhavian steel-bearers in quiet formation. Thought they had us cornered near the ruins of Dolblunde. Don't they know we breathe darkness? My squad watched them toddle about for two days. Liana took out their vision first. They fell before they even knew the source of their blindness. One screamed for Mystra’s light before Sullenul silenced him. We left their bodies for the spiders. 

There will be more, but by the time Waterdeep dispatchs a large enough force to stop our work we will already be ascending through the secret tunnel to their very gates!!
### Journal 5: A New Dawn  
**Author:** Nelidul Duskryn *(Acolyte)*  
**Date:** Uktar 9, 1502 DR  

I’ve seen the sky only once during my [[Blooding]] at [[Trollbridge]], it was so wide and vast and all of it was stolen from us... But soon, when the veil breaks and the surface is folded in shadow, it will belong to us once again. A dusk eternal, born of shadowfell breath and shaped by our will. No sun to burn our eyes, no temples built for lesser gods. Just silence. And control. I dream of standing atop a Waterdeep temple and watching it all begin. Lolth’s threads reach far, but at last they will have no end!

### Journal 6: Fractures in Faith  
**Author:** Jysralriv Duskryn  
**Date:** Uktar 10, 1502 DR  

The priestesses forbid it, but I remember the old verses. Before I was cloaked, I studied the paths of Eilistraee. Vhaeraun. Even Kiaransalee. Not all drow kneel the same. We serve Lolth with everything down to the tooth and nails... but does she see what we are doing in her name? This plan... it does not feel like worship. Lolth's victory could never come from a human? So how it that we dote on the plans of one now? I do not speak this aloud. But my hand hesitates when it reaches for my blade.

# Take 1
### **Jysralriv’s Journal**  
**Day 1**  
Praise be to Lolth for sending us that **paladin** from **House Xorlarrin** and for setting us on this path to ascension! Her blessed darkness is overwhelming; Nothing can prevent our success so long as she walks among us!  

**Day 25**  
Captain **Narrisan** drives the slaves past their breaking point. We lose more slaves to exhaustion than to Underdark predators.  

**Day 30**  
Reports are coming in from **Skullport** that there's been an attack on **Waterdeep** of some kind. Apparently, it was repelled, but some of its **magical defenses** were **damaged**. Lolth smiles upon us with this timing! The Captain is driving everyone harder than ever now so that we do not miss this window of opportunity.  

**Day 38**  
The slaves aren't going to make it. Some stronger slaves tried to escape last night, and we cut them down like the animals they are, but only a handful remain. The expedition is nearly at its end. Narrisin will **HAVE** to *rest* the slaves for at least a full night before the assault begins, or their bodies **WILL** give out on the journey.  

### **Nelidul’s Journal**  
**Day 1**  
We set out today under the direction of **Velryn Xorlarrin**. Jysralriv muttered prayers the whole way—always does. I don't mind, though. As long as he keeps his eyes sharp. Our orders are simple enough: *scout, report, do not engage*. But where's the fun in that? I plan to engage a *little*. 

**Day 12**  
**Velryn**’s weapon is unlike any I've seen. Some **enchanted axe** from the **Dwarven** forges of **Gauntlgrym**, they say. I watched her strike down an Umber Hulk today. The monster's eyes filled with **terror** after one strike and cowered before as she dismantled it. Whatever magic runs through that blade doesn't just cut flesh—it cuts **courage**. Jysralriv thinks it’s a blessing from Lolth herself. Maybe it is.

**Day 18**  
Supplies arrived today for the reinforcements. **Healing potions**, **crossbows**, and an armory carriage packed with **mundane blades** and **drow-forged steel**. There’s also some **cursed circlet** among the gear—supposedly it can **transform** one of us into a ***drider***... I've got enough legs thank you very much, someone else can use that thing.

**Day 22**  
Another slave collapsed today. No water left in his skin, no strength left in his bones. We ration water like gold here, yet there’s never enough. Strange, isn’t it? An operation this big, this critical—yet no cleric to conjure water. Somehow Velryn and Narrisin are equipped with the finest weapons but we but we can’t afford more water. Jysralriv says faith keeps him going. For the rest of us, thirst is a more honest god.  

### **Drisyluld’s Journal**  
**Day 3**  
The shipment arrived in Dolblunde just as planned, but I still can’t bring myself to trust these surface-worlders. Garrick Voss, Melvaunt Inc.’s representative, was there to greet us. He’s a cold, calculating human, ***well-dressed and efficient***. It’s unnerving to see that kind of ruthlessness from someone outside the Underdark. Perhaps we have more in common with the surface than I care to admit.

**Day 6**  
I’ll give credit where it’s due. The temple pieces are immaculate. Every joint, every line—engineered for seamless assembly. It’s maddening how much we’ve had to rely on these outsiders to get this far. ***Without Melvaunt, this temple would take months to construct***. Instead, we’ll have it raised within hours of arrival.

**Day 12**  
One of the ogres slipped today. For a moment, it looked like the entire piece of stone it carried would crush it, but it straightened under the weight. Velryn and Narissisin don’t speak of it openly, but it’s clear—those beasts are pushed far ***beyond what’s natural***. I believe Telervid's right about the **orb**. Without it... well, I doubt we’d be going anywhere fast. 

### **Telervid’s Journal**  
**Day 2**  
The ogres press on, their backs bent under impossible weight. It’s a wonder they don’t collapse, though I suspect that’s more ***magic*** than strength. I’ve seen the way Velryn hovers near that cursed orb—whispering incantations, adjusting its position. Whatever power they’re tapping into, it’s the only thing keeping those creatures moving. I’ve never liked relying on forces I don’t fully understand. But I would never speak against Lolth, ***Elliya Lolthu***.

**Day 9**  
I spotted that old man while out on patrol again, he's ***still*** following us. I brought it up to **Narrisan** ***again***. She gave the same stupid answer "*Scout and report, do not engage anything*." I just wanna kill something, an old man would do nicely... Narissan wouldn't have to know.

**Day 15**  
There’s been talk among the slaves. Quiet murmurs, but I’ve noticed the way they glance at each other when they think we’re not watching... ***they're looking for openings***. Another escape attempt is brewing; fear keeps them in line for now, but their desperation is growing. 

### **Sullenul’s Journal**  
**Day 5**  
Another escape attempt today. One of the kobolds made it nearly to the edge of the camp before we caught him. The others are whispering about some kind of ***safe haven*** nearby—nonsense, of course. The Underdark would be no kinder to these dragon rats than we are. Still, the rumors are enough to make them restless. We can’t afford more delays. The priests made an example of the handler responsible.

### **Sablennil’s Journal**  
**Day 1**  
It’s easy to forget, amidst the strain of this expedition, that this is only the beginning. The first temple of ***nine***. **House Duskryn**’s ambitions stretch far beyond **Waterdeep**. ***Three*** temples will be enough to claim dominance.

Of course **House Baenre** will want to see all nine constructed. ***HER*** vision is grand. A **year** from now, the surface world will bow before us.

**Day 8**  
The scale of this operation is staggering. The labor, the materials, the sheer logistics of it all. **Melvaunt** has proven indispensable. Their ability to deliver such precisely crafted pieces is... well incredible. Still, I can’t help but think of the cost. If we need them for ***every*** temple, how long before they realize their position of power? Have they already? Outsiders may serve us now, but loyalty goes only as far as the status quo.

**Day 20**  
The strain is visible everywhere now. The kobolds are dropping faster than we can replace them, the ogres grow more irritable, and some of us are starting to fray at the edges. **Velryn** and **Narissin**, of course, ignore it all.
