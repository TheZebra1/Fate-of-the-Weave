**Signatories:** [[Tyr]] (Lawful Good Supreme), [[Asmodeus]] (Lawful Evil Supreme)

## Purpose
[Source Material](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/arceiron-diarioz/a/the-pact-primeval-article)
- Prevent further divine war or overreach following the Dawn War.
- Enshrine **mortal autonomy** and protection from unchecked divine influence.
- Formalize the role of **Baator** in processing corrupted mortal souls.

## Locations 
There exist ****three** copies of the **Pact Primeval**

One is kept in [[Baator|The Nine Hells]], hidden and secured on [[Asmodeus]]'s realm of [[Nessus]]. The pact is encased in a ruby that is 10 feet high and 6 feet wide. It weighs 20 tons.

There is also one copy in [[Mechanus]] and one in [[Mount Celestia]]. These pacts are impervious to destruction and if removed from the lawful planes, ***chaos would be strengthened***.

## Key Clauses
### Article I – Divine Boundaries
- Deities shall not intervene directly in mortal realms unless in accordance with the pact’s terms.
- Manifestation of avatars, miracles, or planar incursions requires lawful consensus or pantheon sanction requiring a sufficient *"mortal trigger"*.
- Devils cannot enter the heavenly realms unbidden by a God of those realms.
- God's & angels shall not be permitted in [[Baator]] unbidden by [[Asmodeus]].

### Article III – Soul Adjudication
- Baator assigned authority to contain and reform mortal souls deemed irredeemable.
	- ***Fine Print: The soul itself may condemn itself if signed consent is provided***
- Infernal jurisdiction granted to Asmodeus, Arch-Governor of [[Nessus]], by celestial decree.

### Article VII – Divine Armistice Clause
> *“When mortal will stands imperiled by divine suffocation, let the gods withdraw for seven cycles, that balance be restored unburdened by celestial hand.”*

- Triggers a **seven-cycle divine ceasefire** across all pantheons and planes.
- During this time, no divine action or manifestation may occur in the mortal realm.
- **Activation requires**:
  - Majority celestial consensus, **or**
  - **Authorization by both [[Tyr|Tyr]] and [[Asmodeus]]**, the pact’s supreme Lawful Good and Evil signatories.

## Historical Context
- Created at the end of the [[Dawn War]] as a check against divine escalation and to preserve mortal destiny.
- [[Tyr]] commissioned [[Erathis]] to draft a divine-legal framework to limit interference and ensure balance.
	- She worked very closely with [[Asmodeus]] in this regard.
- **Tyr** and **Asmodeus** were granted dual custodial authority to maintain neutrality.
- Asmodeus was allowed to retain Baator due to his neutrality during the war and his mastery of order.
