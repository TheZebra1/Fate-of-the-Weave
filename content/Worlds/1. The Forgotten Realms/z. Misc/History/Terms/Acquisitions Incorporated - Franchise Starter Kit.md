# Acquisitions Incorporated - Franchise Starter Kit
**Welcome, Franchise Operators!**  
Congratulations on the successful onboarding and activation of your official [[Acquisitions Incorporated]] branch. The following tools, assets, and accessories have been provided on a provisional basis under standard-use guidelines.

## Included Items:
**1. Inked Quill & 100 contracts**
- Quill magically records all contracts signed at your location.
- Each entry is soul-linked to the signatory. Tampering with the contracts voids them and your location and your profits for the completed job will be forfeited to the [[Head Office]].

**2. Branded Franchise Uniforms for Staff Permanent Staff (Size-adjusting)**
- Matching dark green-and-black uniforms with **AI sigil**.
- Heat-resistant. 

**3. “Percolator of Productivity” (Espresso Machine)** *(Available for purchase after first completed mission)* **100gp**
- Grants +1 temporary hit point per cup.
- Second cup in a day causes mild planar indigestion *(Disadvantage on CHA checks & saves*)*.
- Third cup opens a minor rift to [[Dis]] that follows you around causes any ***liquid*** you touch for the ***rest of the day to [[burning-xphb|Burn]] you***. *Drink responsibly.*

**4. Sending Stone to [[Head Office]] (1 use per day)**
- Contact “Head Office” directly with queries, complaints, or confessions.
- Replies may be delayed due to bandwidth congestion.

**5. “AI Head Office Liason” (Auditor, named: Tip Dneif)**
- Appears weekly to review performance metrics.
- Counts contracts completed, offer tips and helpful resources, assess public image of location, shut down unsuccessful location.
