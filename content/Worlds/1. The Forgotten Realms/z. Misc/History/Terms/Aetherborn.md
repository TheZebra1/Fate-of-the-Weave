# Aetherborn (Overview)
>Nemesis of [[Karsite#Bloodline|Karsite]] people.
>- [[Aether.Karsite Shadow Wars]]

The Aetherborn were humans transformed by [[Mystra]] into arcane conduits following the fall of [[Karsus]], created to restore balance to the [[Weave]] and eliminate the disruptive [[Karsite#Bloodline|Karsite]] bloodlines. Gifted with innate magical precision and divine purpose, they operated as nomadic arcane enforcers—deploying wards, traps, and precision spells to hunt rogue magic-eaters across [[Faerûn]]. Though revered by some and feared by many, their zeal and unchecked power eventually led to internal strife and their downfall. After the catastrophic [[Aether.Karsite Shadow Wars#The Sundering of Shaethra (ca. 157 DR)|Sundering of Shaethra]], the Aetherborn vanished into legend, their teachings and glyphwork buried beneath ruined sanctuaries and shattered mythals.

## The Aetherborn: Zealots of the Living Metal
The **Aetherborn** were **religious zealots**; the first to discover [[Aetherium]] and worship it as a divine gift. They believed Aetherium was the **essence of [[Mystra]]** made material, a sacred metal that could elevate mortals beyond their flesh, granting them **immortality through unity with the material**. 

One tribe of Aetherborn forsook their purpose of hunting [[Karsite#Bloodline|Karsite]] after discovering a massive [[Aetherium]] deposit.

Isolated in a **hidden, sequestered land**, they built their civilization around this belief, **guarding Aetherium from the outside world** while conducting radical experiments to fuse themselves with it. They saw the merging of flesh and metal as **spiritual ascension**, sacrificing countless lives in pursuit of a perfected form. Their obsessive devotion, however, became their undoing—whether through internal collapse, divine punishment, or the inevitable horrors their experiments unleashed, the Aetherborn fell, and their once-mighty cult was lost to history.  

In the centuries that followed, **the few survivors abandoned their old ways**, scattering across the world to live among common folk, their bloodlines diluted and their beliefs forgotten.
