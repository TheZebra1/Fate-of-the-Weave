# A Chronological Overview
The **Dawn War** was an ancient and monumental conflict between the **gods** and the **primordials**, occurring in the **Age Before Ages**. This war shaped the very fabric of the multiverse and had lasting impacts on numerous worlds, including **Abeir-Toril**, **Nerath**, **Oerth**, and **Athas**.

## Timeline of Events
### **Age Before Ages** (Circa -35,000 DR to -30,000 DR)
- **Primordial Creation**:  
  The **primordials**, powerful entities from the **[[Elemental Planes]]**, coveted the newly formed worlds within **Realmspace**. Their desire to control and reshape these worlds led to inevitable conflict with the gods.

- **Defense of Realmspace**:  
  **Selûne** and **Shar**, twin goddesses representing light and darkness, took up the mantle to defend the nascent worlds of Realmspace against the encroaching primordials. This marked the beginning of the **Dawn War**. 

- **Creation of Additional Deities**:  
  As the war intensified, other gods were either created from the residual energies of the conflict or summoned from other dimensions to aid in the battle against the primordials.

- **Betrayal and Turning Points**:
  - **Ubtao's Defection**:  
    The primordial **Ubtao**, known as "the Deceiver," turned against his kin and allied with the gods. His assistance was pivotal in imprisoning many of his former allies, shifting the war's momentum in favor of the deities.

  - **Battle of Pesh**:  
    On the world of **Oerth**, **Bahamut's** allies, including the **Wind Dukes of Aaqa** and an exarch of **Moradin**, crafted the **Rod of Law**. They used this powerful artifact to defeat **Miska the Wolf-Spider** at the **Battle of Pesh**, causing the demonic armies to shatter and forcing the **Queen of Chaos** to retreat into the Abyss.

- **Demise of Erek-Hus**:  
  The primordial **Erek-Hus**, known as the **King of Terror**, engaged in a fateful battle with the dragon god **Asgorath** (also known as **Io**). Erek-Hus slew Asgorath by cleaving him in two with his **adamantine axe**. However, from Asgorath's sundered form arose **Bahamut** and **Tiamat**, who united to vanquish Erek-Hus before he could celebrate his victory.

### **The [[Pact Primeval]] – Divine Regulation** (Circa -30,000 DR)
As the gods neared victory, concerns arose about preventing future conflicts of such magnitude. The **Dawn War** had nearly unraveled the planes, and unchecked divine intervention posed a significant risk.

- **Drafting of the Pact**:  
  **Erathis**, goddess of civilization and law, proposed the **Pact Primeval**, a cosmic agreement aimed at regulating divine actions and maintaining balance.

- **Terms of the Pact**:  
  - **Establishment of Divine Roles**:  
    The pact delineated the responsibilities and domains of deities, ensuring that no single entity could exert unchecked influence over the multiverse.

  - **Creation of the Nine Hells**:  
    As part of the agreement, the plane of **Baator** was formalized as the **Nine Hells**, a realm designated for the punishment of souls and the embodiment of lawful evil.

  - **Appointment of Asmodeus**:  
    The archdevil **Asmodeus** was appointed as the ruler of the Nine Hells, tasked with upholding the laws of the pact and ensuring that the souls of the damned were justly punished.

  - **Article VII – Divine Armistice Clause**:  
    A critical component of the pact, this clause allowed for a **seven-cycle armistice** during which divine beings were prohibited from interfering in mortal affairs, emphasizing the importance of mortal autonomy.

### **Post-War Aftermath** (Following -30,000 DR)
- **Impact on Athas**:  
  In the world of **Athas**, the gods were either destroyed or driven away by the primordials during the conflict. Some surviving primordials became the new masters of this world, leading to a unique and isolated development distinct from other realms.

- **The Primal Ban**:  
  Disturbed by the immense destruction wrought upon the Material Plane, nature spirits enacted the **"primal ban."** This powerful magic prohibited outsiders from entering the Material Plane unless summoned by its inhabitants, aiming to preserve the world's integrity.

- **Restoration of Realmspace**:  
  Following the cessation of hostilities, the sun was recreated, and the oceans thawed, marking the end of the Dawn War and the beginning of the **Days of Thunder** on **Abeir-Toril**.

## Legacy

The **Dawn War**'s repercussions were profound and far-reaching. It led to the establishment of the **Dawn War pantheon**, a coalition of deities who, despite their differences, united to prevent such cosmic threats in the future. The war also set the stage for subsequent conflicts, such as the **Blood War** between devils and demons, as the multiverse continued to grapple with the balance between law and chaos, creation and destruction.
