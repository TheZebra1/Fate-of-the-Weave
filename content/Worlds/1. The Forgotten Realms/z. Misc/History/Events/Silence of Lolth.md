# A Chronological Overview
The **Silence of Lolth**, also known as the **Wrath of Lolth**, was a pivotal event in drow history from **Eleasias 28, 1372 DR**, to **Ches 28, 1373 DR**. During this period, **[[Lolth]]** ceased all communication with her followers, cutting off divine magic and closing the **[[Demonweb Pits]]** to planar travel, leading to widespread chaos.

## Timeline of Events  
### **Eleasias 28, 1372 DR** – *Commencement of the Silence*  
- **[[Lolth]]** **withdraws her presence**, cutting off spells and planar travel to the **[[Demonweb Pits]]**.  

### **Marpenoth 20, 1372 DR** – *Slave Revolt in [[Menzoberranzan]]*  
- Disillusioned drow males, driders, and slaves stage an uprising, but it is **suppressed** by **[[Pharaun Mizzrym]]** and **[[Ryld Argith]]**.  

### **Uktar 15, 1372 DR** – *Siege of [[Menzoberranzan]]*  
- The city is besieged by **duergar** from [[Gracklstugh]] and the **Scoured Legion** under **[[Kaanyr Vhok]]**, taking advantage of the priestesses’ weakened state.  

### **Ches 28, 1373 DR** – *Conclusion of the Silence*  
- **[[Lolth]]** **resumes her connection**, restoring divine power to her priestesses and enabling them to repel the siege.  

## **Purpose of the Silence**  

- **Divine Ascension**:  
  - **[[Lolth]]** sought to **become a greater deity**, reshaping her domain to reflect her heightened status.  
  - The **[[Demonweb Pits]]** were **removed from the Abyss** and established as an **independent plane** under her direct control.  

- **Selection of the [[Yor’thae]] (Chosen of Lolth)**:  
  - Three drow priestesses—**[[Quenthel Baenre]]**, **[[Danifae Yauntyrr]]**, and **[[Halisstra Melarn]]**—were chosen to compete for the title.  
  - **[[Danifae Yauntyrr]]** was ultimately selected and transformed into **Lolth’s divine emissary**.  

- **Purging Weakness from Drow Society**:  
  - Without her power, **drow society fell into turmoil**, exposing the weak and rewarding the strong.  
  - Many **heretics** sought other gods, such as **[[Vhaeraun]]**, **[[Kiaransalee]]**, or **[[Selvetarm]]**, but were **eradicated** once Lolth returned.  

## **Lasting Effects**  
- **Shift in Drow Power Structures**:  
  - **Matriarchal dominance weakened** temporarily, as male factions took advantage of the priestesses’ vulnerability.  
  - **Increased factionalism**, leading to further schisms in drow society.  

- **Destruction of [[Ched Nasad]]**:  
  - The city **collapsed** due to internal conflicts and external manipulation during the Silence.  

- **Final Fate of the [[Demonweb Pits]]**:  
  - **[[Lolth]]** successfully **removed her domain from Abyssal influence** and ruled as a greater deity from her own plane.  
  - However, following the **[[Second Sundering]]**, she **returned the Demonweb Pits to the Abyss**, securing her power in the reshaped multiverse.  

## **Summary of Accomplishments**  
- **Elevated to greater deity status**  
- **Erased unworthy or weak followers**  
- **Reaffirmed dominance over the drow**  
- **Temporarily gained an independent plane** before returning to the Abyss  
- **Enforced a stronger, more devoted priesthood**  

## Legacy  

The **Silence of Lolth** reshaped **[[drow]] civilization** permanently. Though she ultimately **returned the [[Demonweb Pits]] to the Abyss**, her **ascended power remained intact**, and her influence over the **[[drow]]** was stronger than ever.
