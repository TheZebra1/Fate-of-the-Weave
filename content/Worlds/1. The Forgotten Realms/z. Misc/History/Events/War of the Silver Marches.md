### Date
1484–1485 DR

### Belligerents
- **[[Kingdom of Many-Arrows]]**: Orcs led by warlord Hartusk.
- **Drow of [[Menzoberranzan]] and Q'Xorlarrin**: Drow factions under Matron Mother [[House Baenre#1. Quenthal Baenre.png Quenthel Baenre|Quenthel Baenre]].
- **Allied Forces**: Various frost giant tribes and two white dragons, Arauthator and Aurbangras.

**Opposing:**
- **[[Silver Marches]]' Alliance**: Coalition of dwarven strongholds (Citadel Adbar, Citadel Felbarr, Mithral Hall), the city of Silverymoon, and allied forces led by Bruenor Battlehammer and Drizzt Do'Urden.

### Key Events
- **The Darkening**: Initiated by drow wizard Tsabrak Xorlarrin under [[Lolth]]'s orders, this spell shrouded the Silver Marches in perpetual darkness, providing strategic advantage to the invading forces.
- **Destruction of Nesmé**: The town was obliterated by an orc horde led by Tiago Baenre riding the white dragon Arauthator.
- **Fall of Sundabar**: Conquered by orc forces under warlord Hartusk and renamed Hartusk Keep.
- **Sieges**: Major dwarven citadels and Silverymoon were besieged by the invading armies.
- **Battle of the Cold Vale**: Bromm, one of the twin kings of Citadel Adbar, was deceived and slain by Hartusk with the aid of half-drow Doum'wielle Armgo.
- **Retaking of the River Surbrin Bridge**: In spring 1485 DR, Bruenor united the dwarven forces to reclaim this strategic point from Many-Arrows' control.
- **Aerial Battle**: Drizzt Do'Urden and Afafrenfere, mounted on copper dragons Ilnezhara and Tazmikella, confronted Tiago Baenre and Tos'un Armgo on white dragons, resulting in the deaths of Tos'un and Aurbangras, and Arauthator's retreat.
- **Conquest of Dark Arrow Keep**: In summer 1485 DR, Bruenor led a successful assault, restoring the keep to its rightful ruler, Lorgru, and defeating Hartusk's remaining forces.
- **Dispelling the Darkening**: Archmage [[House Baenre#2. Gromph Baenre.png Gromph Baenre|Gromph Baenre]], opposing Matron Mother Quenthel Baenre, secretly countered the Darkening, disguising the act as divine intervention by Mielikki through Drizzt.

### Outcome
A pyrrhic victory for the Silver Marches. While the invading forces were repelled and key territories reclaimed, the region suffered significant destruction, including the annihilation of [[Nesmé]] and severe damage to [[Sundabar]]. The war left lasting scars on the Silver Marches, with widespread loss of life and infrastructure.
