## Overview
The Wailing Death was a virulent and mysterious magical plague that devastated [[Neverwinter]] in 1372 DR. This affliction resisted magical healing, arcane remedies, and divine intervention, causing mass panic and societal collapse.
- Worshipped by the [[Silent Choir]].
- Created [[Wail-born]].

## History
- **Origin Year:** 1372 DR (Year of Wild Magic)
- **Initial Outbreak:** [[Beggar’s Nest]], spreading rapidly throughout [[Neverwinter]]
- **Disease Characteristics:**
  - Victims began coughing blood and experiencing seizures.
  - Ended with victims wailing in pain, leading to the name "Wailing Death".
  - Immunity was virtually nonexistent; standard healing spells proved ineffective.
- **Citywide Quarantine:** Enforced to prevent spread to nearby regions.

## Purpose and Plot
The Wailing Death was not a natural occurrence—it was a magical plague engineered for a darker purpose:

- **Primary Orchestrators:** [[Cult of the Eye]]
- **Leader of the Cult:** [[Desther Indelayne]]
  - Masqueraded as a cleric of [[Helm]] to infiltrate [[Castle Never]] and the city’s leadership.
- **True Goal:** 
  - Weaken [[Neverwinter]] to facilitate the resurrection of [[Morag]], Queen of the [[Old Ones]].
  - Destabilize political and divine order in the city to pave the way for ancient powers.
- **Divine Influence:**
  - The cult’s efforts were aided by dark extraplanar entities and corrupt divine manipulation.

## Major Events
### Phase 1: Outbreak
- Disease begins spreading in the [[Beggar’s Nest]].
- Panic grips the city as healing fails.
- [[Aribeth de Tylmarande]] calls for outside help, leading to the arrival of the Hero.

### Phase 2: Investigation & Reagents
- The Hero of Neverwinter is tasked with retrieving rare reagents from each district to craft a cure.
- Key districts include:
  - [[Peninsula District]]
  - [[Blacklake District]]
  - [[Docks]]
  - [[Beggar’s Nest]]

### Phase 3: Cult Revealed
- [[Desther Indelayne]] is exposed as the cause of the plague and arrested.
- The cult’s true plan—awakening [[Morag]]—is uncovered.

### Phase 4: Final Confrontation
- The Hero retrieves ancient artifacts known as the [[Words of Power]].
- A climactic battle unfolds in the [[Source Stone]].
- [[Morag]] is defeated, halting the rise of the [[Old Ones]].

## Notable Figures
- **[[Desther Indelayne]]** – Corrupt cleric, plague architect.
- **[[Aribeth de Tylmarande]]** – Paladin of [[Tyr]], later turns antagonist due to grief and corruption.
- **The Hero of Neverwinter** – Unnamed player character who investigates the plague and defeats the cult.
- **[[Morag]]** – Ancient Queen of the [[Old Ones]], the ultimate force behind the cult's goals.
- **[[Fenthick Moss]]** – Aribeth’s lover and fellow paladin, executed in a political miscarriage of justice.

## Legacy
- The plague left lasting scars on [[Neverwinter]]:
  - Entire districts lost or in ruins.
  - Civil trust in leadership and the divine shattered.
  - Aribeth’s fall from grace and later redemption (or death, depending on events).
- Despite its magical origins, the Wailing Death became a cautionary tale of hubris, secrecy, and divine silence.
- Left behind [[Wail-born]].
