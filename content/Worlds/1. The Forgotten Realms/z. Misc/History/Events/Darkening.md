***Can be countered by [[Selûne’s Eye]]***
### Date  
1484 DR (Year of the Awakened Sleepers)  

### Description  
The Darkening was a powerful magical effect cast by drow wizards under Matron Mother **[[House Baenre#1. Quenthal Baenre.png Quenthel Baenre|Quenthel Baenre]]**’s orders, with **Tsabrak Xorlarrin** leading its execution. This spell created an unnatural, roiling mass of dark clouds that completely obscured the sun over the **Silver Marches**, shrouding the region in perpetual twilight. It removed daylight advantages from surface-dwellers while giving the drow and their allies a strategic edge.  

### Purpose  
The Darkening was a key component of the **[[War of the Silver Marches]]**, allowing the **drow, orcs of Many-Arrows, frost giants, and white dragons** to launch a devastating campaign against the alliance of surface nations. The lack of sunlight created confusion and fear among the defenders and aided the invaders in their conquest.  

### Consequences  
The darkness sowed panic among the people of the **Silver Marches**, disrupting trade, daily life, and defensive coordination. The war saw the destruction of **[[Nesmé]]**, the sacking of **[[Sundabar]]**, and heavy casualties throughout the region. The warlords of Many-Arrows, led by **Hartusk**, wreaked havoc upon the north while the drow continued their own ambitions in the chaos.  

### Resolution  
The Darkening was ultimately **counterspelled by Archmage [[House Baenre#2. Gromph Baenre.png Gromph Baenre|Gromph Baenre]], who secretly opposed Matron Mother Quenthel Baenre and sought to undermine Lolth’s influence. To conceal his rebellion, **Gromph manipulated events to make it appear that the ranger [[Drizzt Do'Urden]], with the power of [[Mielikki]], had dispelled the darkness.** With the sun restored, the tide of war turned against the invaders, leading to the end of the **War of the Silver Marches**.
