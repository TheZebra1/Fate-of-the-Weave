# (c. –12,000 DR to –9,000 DR)

The **Crown Wars** were a series of brutal conflicts between ancient elven nations, spanning over 3,000 years. While history often paints the **[[Ilythiiri]] dark elves** as villains due to their fall into the Underdark, it was **[[Aryvandaar]] (sun elves)** that initiated these wars through conquest and imperialism. The Ilythiiri turned to **[[Lolth]] and dark magic out of desperation**, not innate evil.

## Timeline of the Crown Wars
### **1st Crown War (c. –12,000 DR to –11,700 DR)**
- **Instigator:** Aryvandaar (sun elves).
- **Cause:** Aryvandaar seeks to dominate [[Miyeritar]] (a peaceful nation of green and dark elves).
- **Major Events:**
  - Aryvandaar invades Miyeritar to expand its empire.
  - Despite resistance, Miyeritar is eventually conquered.
- **Outcome:** Aryvandaar’s aggressive expansion disrupts the balance of power.

### **2nd Crown War (c. –11,500 DR to –11,300 DR)**
- **Instigator:** Aryvandaar.
- **Cause:** Aryvandaar pushes further, attempting to absorb Shantel Othreier.
- **Major Events:**
  - A brutal war unfolds as [[Shantel Othreier]] fights back.
  - Widespread destruction weakens many elven nations.
- **Outcome:** Aryvandaar tightens its control, but unrest spreads.

### **3rd Crown War (c. –11,200 DR to –10,900 DR) — The Ilythiiri’s Desperate War**
- **Instigator:** Aryvandaar’s relentless expansion.
- **Cause:** The dark elves of Ilythiir, witnessing the destruction of their kin, retaliate against Aryvandaar and its allies.
- **Major Events:**
  - Ilythiir wages war on Orishaar, Thearnytaar, and Eillur—nations allied with Aryvandaar.
  - The conflict escalates into scorched-earth tactics and the use of powerful magic.
  - Facing overwhelming opposition, some Ilythiiri turn to **Lolth and darker magics** for survival.
  - The **Seldarine (elven gods)**, horrified by the bloodshed, **curse the Ilythiiri**, transforming them into the **drow**.
- **Outcome:** The **drow** are driven underground, forever exiled.

> **Key Takeaway:** The Ilythiiri did not start the war—**they retaliated against Aryvandaar’s conquests**. Their fall to [[Lolth]] was a last resort, not an inherent evil.

### **4th Crown War (c. –10,900 DR to –10,500 DR)**
- **Instigator:** Aryvandaar, in its final bid for control.
- **Cause:** With the drow exiled, Aryvandaar attempts to consolidate power over all elven lands.
- **Major Events:**
  - The green elven nation of Miyeritar is **annihilated** by a magical cataclysm, turning it into the **High Moor**.
  - The resistance finally overwhelms Aryvandaar.
- **Outcome:** Aryvandaar is **overthrown**, marking the fall of its empire.

### **5th Crown War (c. –10,500 DR to –9,000 DR)**
- **Instigator:** The last remnants of warlords trying to hold onto power.
- **Cause:** Struggles over the shattered remains of elven civilization.
- **Major Events:**
  - The last battles of the Crown Wars unfold.
  - Many elves flee Faerûn, seeking refuge elsewhere.
- **Outcome:** The wars finally end. **Evermeet is founded** as a sanctuary for elves.

## Aftermath
- **The drow are permanently exiled to the [[Underdark]]**, where they forge new civilizations under **Lolth’s influence**.
- The **Aryvandaaran empire is erased from history**, but its actions left the elven people fractured.
- Evermeet becomes the last great refuge of the elves.

## The Truth Behind the War
Many later elven historians, especially those from [[Evermeet]], paint the **Ilythiiri** as the great villains of the Crown Wars. However, a discerning scholar will see that **Aryvandaar was the true aggressor**. The Ilythiiri fought to protect their people, but their methods—while brutal—were born from desperation, not inherent malice.

The **real lesson of the Crown Wars** is the **dangers of elven arrogance and imperialism**. What began as **one nation’s hunger for power** led to the **greatest tragedy in elven history**.
