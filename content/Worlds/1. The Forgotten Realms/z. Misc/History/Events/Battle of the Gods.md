**−1071 DR** during the **[[Orcgate Wars]]**. This cataclysmic confrontation involved the deities of the **[[Mulhorand|Mulhorandi]]** and **[[Unther|Untheric]]** pantheons clashing against the invading **Orcish pantheon**.

## Background

The origins of the conflict trace back to **−1081 DR**, when the theurgist adept **Thayd**(***Founder of [[Thay]]***), the last surviving apprentice of the [[Imaskar|Imaskari]] wizards, led a rebellion against the empires of Mulhorand and Unther. Before his defeat and execution, Thayd opened a portal to a brutal world dominated by empires of fanatically religious orcs. This portal remained dormant for several years until the orcs discovered it and began pouring through, leading to the **Orcgate Wars**.

## The Battle

In response to the orcish incursion, the deities of Mulhorand and Unther manifested avatars to combat the threat directly. The ensuing battle, later termed the **Battle of the Gods**, took place in the region that would become Thay.

During this confrontation, several deities met their demise:

- **Re**, the leader of the Mulhorandi pantheon, was slain by **Gruumsh**, the chief deity of the orcs.

- **Inanna** was killed by **Bahgtru**.

- **Girru** fell to **Luthic**.

- **Nergal** was defeated by **Ilneval**.

- **Nanna-Sin** was slain by **Shargaas**.

- **Ki** and **Utu** were also killed during the battle.

A significant duel occurred between **[[Tiamat]]** and **[[Bahamut|Marduk]]** (the Untheric aspect of Bahamut), resulting in both deities [[Avatar]]'s killing each other.

## Aftermath

Despite their initial victories, the orcish deities and their forces were eventually defeated by the surviving members of the Mulhorandi and Untheric pantheons in **−1069 DR**, leading to the conclusion of the Orcgate Wars.

The death of Re led to his power being transferred to his son, **Horus**, who then became known as **Horus-Re**, assuming leadership of the Mulhorandi pantheon.

The Battle of the Gods had lasting impacts on the religious and political landscapes of [[Mulhorand]] and [[Unther]], reshaping their pantheons and influencing their cultures for centuries to come.
