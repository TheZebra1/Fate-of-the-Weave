# **The Burden of [[Annam]]**  
## **Overview**  
Annam All-Father, progenitor of giantkind, shaped the **[[Ordning]]** to instill order and greatness among his children. His children founded the **empire of [[Ostoria]]** and flourished; giants stood as masters of **Toril**. However [[Othea]]'s infidelity led to the fall of Ostoria with it being blanketed in ice.

Though the fall of Ostoria weakened Giants, Annam wanted to help them rebuild by sending his son [[Hartkiller]].

It was the **rejection of Hartkiller**, that sealed the both Annam and all giants fate. Hartkiller, a mortal-born son of Annam, was meant to be the **prophesied ruler** who would restore giantkind, yet his own kin denied him. **Disillusioned and angered, Annam withdrew**, ceasing all divine guidance and leaving his children to govern themselves.  Giants fell into ruin and dwindled in numbers.

Yet, a god cannot simply **turn his back on his domain**. **[[Ao]], Overgod of the Realms, intervened.** A god **may not forsake his duty**. As punishment, Annam was **bound into the [[Spine of the World]]**, his divine essence woven into the fabric of reality itself. Now, he slumbers beneath stone and ice, his power serving a singular purpose: **to stabilize the [[Material Plane]], [[Feywild]], and [[Shadowfell]].**  

But before his final silence, he left behind a last hope—a way to remember, a way to restore, a way to reclaim what was lost.

## **Annam’s Withdrawal & Ao’s Judgment**  
- **Ostoria’s fall** in the war against dragons marked the decline of giantkind.  
- Seeing his children grow **divided and unworthy**, Annam **abandoned** them.  
- Ao decreed that **a god may not forsake his creation without consequence.**  
- Annam’s **avatar was fused into the Spine of the World**, becoming a **pillar of planar stability.**  

Though **some giants remain**, their numbers have dwindled to obscurity, scattered and aimless without divine guidance. With every **giant lost**, Annam’s presence weakens further, reducing his influence to nothing more than an echo in forgotten myths.

## **The Last Play: Annam’s Desperation**  
The **Mark of Annam** is all that remains of his divine tether. If the **last bearer falls**, Annam will not die, nor be exiled—he will simply **cease to matter**, his final remnants extinguished.  

Faced with **eternal silence**, he will have no choice but to act. In desperation, Annam will **manifest his full avatar**, bellowing a final call to all remaining **giants and giantkin**, demanding their faith to rekindle his godhood.  

A last, ***reckless*** attempt to reclaim what was lost.
