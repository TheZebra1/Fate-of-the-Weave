# **The Heart of [[Ostoria]]**  
In the **frozen ruins of Ostoria’s capital: [[Voninheim]]**, buried under miles of ice, lies **Annam’s Hope**—a ritual site created before his punishment. It was meant for Hartkiller, a **throne of storm and stone**, a place where a true heir could rise and reshape the future of giantkind.  

## **[[Hartkiller]]'s Crown**  
- A **stormforged artifact**, crafted by Annam himself.  
- Not a physical crown, but a **manifestation of divine authority**.  
- Only a **giantkin bearing the Mark of Annam** can awaken it.  
## **Awakening the Crown**
*Your vision dims. The cold stone beneath your feet gives way to memory, not your own, but older than the mountains. A voice—deep, sorrowful, and distant—echoes in your mind:*

> *“They could not see him. And I could not make them.”*

You are shown glimpses—blinding flashes of a world unraveling:
- The [[Throne Wars]] are reaching their end as the Dragon's consume more and more of [[Toril]].
- **Ostoria, fractured**, its mighty cities crumbling under the weight of war and loss.
- Giants **splintered into tribes**, ruled more by tradition and fear than purpose.
- The **Ordning, once divine law**, now a faded ideal—ignored, misused, or misunderstood.

And in the midst of it all: **Hartkiller**.

The mortal-born son of Annam. A storm giant raised among both mortals and titans.  
He was not a conqueror, nor a tyrant, but a visionary—one who believed the old ways could be reborn not by dominion, but by unity.

> *“He asked for trust. They answered with silence.”*

The high council was called—the last time giants of all kinds stood under one roof.  
A few saw hope in Hartkiller. Most saw only weakness, inexperience, and a boy vying for power.

> *“Their fear chained their judgment. And in fear, they struck.”*
> *“And when his blood was spilled, I turned away.”*

## **The Ritual: Ascension of the Heir**  
- **Step 1:** The marked heir must **stand upon the Throne of the First Storm**.  
- **Step 2:** A trial of **ancestral judgment begins**—echoes of Ostoria’s lost rulers test the heir’s worthiness.  
- **Step 3:** If the heir prevails, the **Crown of the First Storm manifests**, and Annam **acknowledges them as his successor**.  

## **Effects of the Ritual (Success)**
- **Annam’s Silence Ends** → His voice reaches his children once more.  
- **The Ice Blight Recedes** → The frozen North is **restored**, allowing giants to thrive again.  
- **The Ordning Can Be Reforged** → The heir gains authority to **unite** or **reshape** giantkind.  

## **Consequences of Failure**
- If **an unworthy heir** attempts the ritual, the throne **rejects them**, unleashing a **storm of divine fury**.  
- If **the last Mark of Annam is lost before the ritual is completed**, the throne becomes **dormant forever**, and **Annam’s last hope is extinguished**.

# **Ascension of the Heir – The Ritual Trials**
The **Ascension of the Heir** ritual comprises three distinct trials designed to test the aspirant's might, insight, and empathy. Success in these trials signifies worthiness to lead giantkind and restore Ostoria.

## **Trial of Might**
**Objective:** Demonstrate unparalleled strength and combat prowess.
**Challenge:** Engage in battle with a spectral Storm Giant, an echo of Ostoria's ancient guardians.
**Environment:** The battle occurs in a vast chamber reminiscent of a stormy sea, with spectral waves and lightning illuminating the arena.

## **Trial of Insight**
**Objective:** Exhibit wisdom and problem-solving acumen.

**Challenge:** Solve a puzzle that reflects the aspirant's understanding of giant lore and the values of leadership.

**Puzzle:** The aspirant encounters a massive stone door inscribed with ancient runes and a riddle:

*"I am taken from a mine, and shut up in a wooden case, from which I am never released, and yet I am used by almost every person. What am I?"*

**Solution:** "Pencil lead" or "Graphite."

**Mechanics:**
- The aspirant must interpret the riddle and provide the correct answer.
- A correct answer causes the door to open, revealing the path forward.
- An incorrect answer triggers a trap or summons guardians to challenge the aspirant.

*Note: This puzzle can be adjusted to align with the campaign's themes.*
