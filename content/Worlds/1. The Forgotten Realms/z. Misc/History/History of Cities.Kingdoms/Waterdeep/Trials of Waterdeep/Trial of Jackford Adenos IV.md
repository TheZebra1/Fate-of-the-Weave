## Trial of [[Jackford Adenos IV]]
### Magistrates Speech
*City of Splendors, Year of the Unseen Warning*  

The chamber is heavy with silence, broken only by the flickering of torches. The masked Lords of Waterdeep sit in judgment, their expressions unseen. A single candle, short and already burning, stands at the center of the room—its wax pooling like time melting away. Before them stands the accused, bound, awaiting his fate.  

**Magistrate Arven Tarm (adjusts his robes, clears throat, and leans forward, tone sharp and measured):**  

*"Jackford Adennos IV, you stand before the Lord's Court accused of grievous crimes against the people and laws of Waterdeep, and frankly, against all reasonable standards of conduct."*  

*(Pauses. Lets the weight of the statement settle.)*  

*"We have before us a list of offenses so egregious that it pains my heart that you were ever allowed to wear the mantle of Paladin for this city of splendors."*  

*(Raises a hand and begins listing the charges, ticking them off one by one.)*  

*"First, **two counts of unlawful entry & trespassing**. First, the home of Safira Watts, and later, the forge where you were captured where no doubt you were attempting to rearm yourself for further skullduggery."*  

*"Next, **possession of a highly dangerous and illegal artifact**. A yellow stone, our wizards have examined this stone and are calling it a "[[Soul Stone]]". If their reports are accurate, this stone can quite literally suck the soul out of a man.... or a woman!!"*  

*(Lowers voice slightly, gaze hardening.)*  

*"Then we come to the most serious of your crimes—**murder in the first degree**. You assailed  Safira Watts in the streets, and struck her down in cold blood. The evidence? Your own Battleaxe, covered in her dried blood, and the blades congruent with her fatal injuries."*  

*(A pause. A slow shake of the head, voice edged with disgust.)*  

*"Was that enough?.. No. After ending her life, you chose to **desecrate her corpse**—mutilating the body to **prevent identification**. Her fingers, removed. Her face, burned beyond recognition. And her teeth punched into the recesses of her brain and then removed. In the end, it was only through the discovery of her ransacked home that the Watch was able to piece together the identity of Safira Watts."*  

*(Steps forward slightly, tone firm, unwavering.)*  

*"Which brings us to **your final murder**. In a twisted effort to **cover your trail**, you **murdered your own servant**, Isolde, a servant girl of House Adenos, a simple woman of the working class... the type of person that as a paladin of Waterdeep you swore to protect."*  

*(Sighs, rubbing his temples in feigned exhaustion.)*  

*(Leans back slightly, letting silence settle before delivering the final line.)*  

*"To summarize: You broke into places you shouldn’t have. You were possessed a soul sucking stone. You killed not **one**, but **two** people, and then **defiled** one of their corpses. You killed, you stole, you lied, you ran and least shockingly of all... you got **caught**."*  

*(Tilts head slightly, tone shifting to dry amusement.)*  

*"Mr. Adenos, If you were attempting a **speedrun to an execution**, then you have done a spectacular job."*  

*(Gestures to the silver candle, the wax creeping lower.)*  

*"But Waterdeep is a city of laws. And unlike you, we do not break them on a whim.*

*You have the time it takes for this candle to burn to its end to **justify your actions, explain yourself, or—if you so choose—beg**. Once the flame dies, so too does any final hope for leniency.... you may begin"*  

*(Steps back, folding hands behind back, gaze unmoving.)*  

*(The Lords remain silent. The candle flickers. Jackford’s fate inches closer.)*  

### Jackford's Speech
You call it **murder**. I call it **justice**.

In the count of **Safira**, the woman I killed was no **healer**, no **head of R&D**, no **innocent victim**—she was an **architect of suffering**, an **engineer of torment** disguised as the head of **research and development**. Deep within that hospital, she did not **mend disease**; she **fostered it**. Her creations did not **heal**—they **enslaved souls**. 

I did not **take a life**. I **ended a threat** to **Waterdeep**, and only then did I acquire the **soul stone**, which was being used to hold the souls of **innocent patients**.

---

In the count of **murder in the first degree** against **Isold**—she has served as my **retainer** for my entire life, and her **life’s work** came to fruition when she **gave her life** to my **axe** as a **selfless sacrifice** to gain the trust of an **enemy**. She died **doing what she loved**, and if she were here today, she would tell you:

*"If the glove does not fit, you must acquit."*

---

On the count of **destruction of property**, I admit—I may have **rearranged some cobblestones**, but it was not for **myself**. It was for the **greater good** of **defending Waterdeep** from this **temptress of evil**. 

---

And **theft**? Yes, I **took**—but not for **greed**, not for **gain**. I **stole** for **survival**, for **hope**, from those deemed **unworthy of being heard**. That **axe** was a **symbol of justice** in itself. I took not what could not have been returned in the form of **freeing the people of Waterdeep from imminent doom**. 

---

You sit here, **cloaked in law**, speaking of **order**, yet you **blind yourself** to the **rot beneath your feet**. **Waterdeep** is not just its **gilded streets** and **noble halls**—it is the **shadows** where the **forgotten suffer**. 

I did what was **necessary**. What **you would not**.

So pass your **sentence**, if you must. Call me a **murderer**. Call me a **thief**. But know this: the **true crime** would have been **doing nothing**.
