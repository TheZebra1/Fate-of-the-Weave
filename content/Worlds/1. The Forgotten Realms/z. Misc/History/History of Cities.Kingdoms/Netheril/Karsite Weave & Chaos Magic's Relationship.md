The Karsite Weave is a partially realized magical force originally created by [[Karsus]], the [[Netheril|Netherese]] archwizard. Unlike [[Mystra]]’s Weave, which imposes structure and control, the [[Karsite Weave]] represents **pure, unrestrained magic**, inspired by Karsus’ research into both **[[Feywild]] magic** and the **Phaerimm’s unique, destructive magic**.

## Origins of the Karsite Weave

Karsus sought to create a new Weave that could rival or replace Mystra's, providing magic-users with unrestricted access to primal magic. His studies of the Feywild led him to discover that Fey magic was a **close echo of “Pure Magic,”** the original, divine magic wielded by gods and ancient primal forces. This type of magic, as seen in the Feywild, seemed to exist "almost" outside the structure of Mystra’s Weave, offering boundless creative potential, though without the stability seen in traditional arcane magic.

In addition to the Feywild, Karsus was influenced by the **Phaerimm**, an ancient race whose own magic was capable of **cannibalizing Mystra’s Weave** directly. He observed that their approach to magic absorbed raw pure energy in its unfiltered form, allowing for incredible power at a great cost. Fascinated by both forms, Karsus combined these concepts to create what he called the **Karsite Weave**, a new type of magic closer to the **Pure Magic** of the gods.

## Chaotic Magic as an Echo of the Karsite Weave

While incomplete, the Karsite Weave allows users to channel magic in ways that **blend chaotic creation and raw absorption**, resulting in highly volatile but powerful magic. This unpredictable force is often mistaken for chaos magic or fey influence. Since it lacks structure and clashes with Mystra’s Weave, it frequently produces erratic and unstable effects. Those using this weave often experience “***wild magic***” as Mystra’s Weave and the Karsite Weave resist one another, manifesting in random or powerful outcomes.

### Why the Karsite Weave Remains Unstable

Karsus never completed his work on the Karsite Weave, but his unfinished research was preserved in a powerful **orb** before the fall of Netheril. **[[Gale Dekarios]]** rediscovered this orb in the 15th century DR, reawakening the dormant Karsite Weave and setting the stage for its incomplete energies to begin influencing magic once more. This instability is due to the weave's unfinished form, resulting in **violent interactions** with Mystra’s Weave, manifesting as chaotic surges and unpredictable outcomes when magic users unknowingly tap into it.

## Implications for Magic and Reality

If completed, the Karsite Weave would rival or supplant Mystra’s offering mortals access to **unrestrained divine-like power**, reshaping the nature of magic and reality itself. 

### Perspectives 
- Ultimate Magical Freedom
- An existential threat that could destabilize Mystra’s carefully woven fabric of reality?

### Summary

The Karsite Weave, inspired by both Feywild magic and Phaerimm absorption, represents **an unfiltered creative and destructive force**, designed to access “***Pure Magic***” closer to divine origins. Although powerful, it remains dangerously unstable and inherently at odds with Mystra’s Weave, posing a challenge for those who seek to harness its potential or protect against its influence.
