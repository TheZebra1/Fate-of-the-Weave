*A classified Melvaunt Inc. initiative focused on arcane-human integration*  

## **Overview**  
Project Lidbrani was a **top-secret research initiative** conducted by **[[Melvaunt Incorporated]].**, designed to **push the limits of arcane augmentation in living subjects**. The project aimed to develop **a direct neural interface between humans and magitek weaponry**, allowing for **seamless integration between the body and arcane-enhanced technology**.  

Though officially **conceived as a breakthrough in military prosthetics**, its true purpose was far more sinister—**creating high-speed, precision assassins** capable of eliminating **any threat desired.**  

## **The Origins of the Name**  
The project took its name from **[[Lidbrani Prohorov|Lidbrani]]**, a brilliant artificer who **pioneered the first arcane prosthetics** while working for Melvaunt Inc. His work in **merging magical constructs with living tissue** laid the foundation for the **company’s more extreme experiments**.  
- While Lidbrani **envisioned his work as a means of restoring lost limbs**, Melvaunt Inc. **saw greater potential**—turning living beings into **weapons.**  
- Without his knowledge, his research was **repurposed and expanded upon**, leading to the **creation of the first arcane spinal implants** designed to directly interface with weaponized technology.  

## **The Research & Experimentation**  
The project sought to **bond [[Aetherium]]-infused implants** directly to the **nervous system**, allowing for instantaneous reflexive control over advanced magitek systems like the **[[Omni-Directional Mobility (ODM) Gear]]**.  

### **Testing Phases & Failures**
1. **Phase I: Initial Subjects**  
   - Conducted on **low-class workers from the [[Galena Mountains|Galena Mountain]] mines**, forcibly conscripted or bribed into participation.  
   - **Fatalities**: Nearly 100%. Subjects **suffered neural rejection, organ failure, or arcane burnout.**  

2. **Phase II: Military Operatives**  
   - Believing **malnourishment** was the issue, Melvaunt Inc. **began testing on elite soldiers** with peak physical conditioning.  
   - **Fatalities**: 100%. Even the strongest subjects **could not withstand the strain of full synchronization.**  

3. **Phase III: The Anomaly**  
   - **[[Elias Thorne]] became the first and only survivor.**  
   - His body uniquely **synchronized with the implants**, forming a **perfect neural bond** without rejection.  
   - Though Melvaunt Inc. **never understood why**, they saw him as **proof of concept** and began planning for **mass deployment**—until he escaped.  

## **Project Lidbrani’s Downfall & Cover-Up**  
The project was **deemed a failure** after Elias’ escape, and **all records were buried** to avoid public or governmental scrutiny. However, **the knowledge was never lost**—Melvaunt Inc. still possesses **all data from the experiments** and is merely **waiting for another viable subject.**  

- **The [[Shadow Syndicate]]**, having funded much of the research, still believes **Project Lidbrani can succeed**, and they have **Elias' brother as their backup plan.**  
- If **Melvaunt Inc. uncovers the truth of Elias’ Aetherborn heritage**, they may **resume testing, targeting anyone with similar bloodlines** in the hopes of **restarting the project.**  

## **Current Status**  
- **Officially Terminated** – Public records indicate the project was an **unsuccessful venture**.  
- **Covertly Suspended** – The research **still exists**, awaiting a **new test subject.**
