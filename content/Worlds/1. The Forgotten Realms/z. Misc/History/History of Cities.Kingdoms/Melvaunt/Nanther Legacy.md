## 1. **The Fall of the Nanther Family (Exile and 1388 DR)**
- **The Nanther Exile (1345 DR)**:  
  - The Nanther family was banished from [[Melvaunt]] after **Lyran Nanther**’s failed claim to [[Shadowdale]] and proven ties to the [[Zhentarim]].  
  - Stripped of their wealth and influence, the family scattered across the [[Moonsea]], attempting to rebuild through mercantile ventures and alliances.  
  - Their absence and tarnished reputation made them vulnerable to exploitation and manipulation.

#### **Naeryx Targets the Nanthers (1388 DR)**:  
- Naeryx Krumple, a Shardar-Kai owner of the [[Witchlight Carnival]] visits many locations during his time running the carnival and comes across some intel that helps him realize the oppurtunity for wealth and power in Melvaunt through this exiled noble family.
  - The scattered Nanthers are systematically eliminated by **Naeryx’s Shadar-kai assassins** under the guise of unrelated tragedies.  
  - He spares one female Nanther, **Sara**, who was estranged from her family due to rebellion or scandal.  
  - Naeryx manipulates Sara into believing her family abandoned her and presents himself as her savior.

- **Marriage Alliance**:  
  - Naeryx marries Sara, gaining legitimacy through the Nanther name.  
  - Together, they fabricate a story of family tragedy, blaming the Zhentarim for the Nanthers' demise, and return to Melvaunt as the sole survivors of the noble house.

## 2. **Return to Melvaunt**
- **Restoring the Nanther Name**:  
  - Naeryx and Sara re-establish the Nanther family’s place in Melvaunt’s aristocracy, presenting themselves as symbols of resilience.  
  - Behind the scenes, Naeryx’s Shadar-kai assassins eliminate opposition, ensuring their rise to power.

- **Rise to Power**:  
  - Naeryx (as [[Warren Nanther]]) targets the rival **Leiyraghon** family, historically the holders of the **Lord Chancellor** title.  
  - Through blackmail, sabotage, and assassination, Naeryx orchestrates the downfall of the Leiyraghon family.  
  - He seizes the position of Lord Chancellor, relegating the Leiyraghons to the role of Lord Envoy.

- **Shadow Operations**:  
  - While running Melvaunt, Naeryx also expands his corporate empire and maintains control over the [[Witchlight Carnival]] with his partner Mr. Light.

## 3. **[[Sara Nanther]]**
- **Role**: Loyal and ambitious wife of Naeryx.  
  - Deeply respects Naeryx for "**rescuing**" her and giving her **purpose**.  
  - Actively supports his political ambitions, hosting social gatherings and alliances.  
  - Raises their son, **[[Killian Nanther]]**, often on her own due to Naeryx’s constant political and business moves.

## 4. **The Legacy of Killian Nanther**
- **Early Life**:  
  - Killian Nanther is born shortly after Naeryx and Sara’s return to Melvaunt.  
  - Trained by Naeryx’s Shadar-kai assassins, he becomes a master of subterfuge and assassination.  
  - Naeryx splits his time between running the Carnival & his schemes in Melvaunt leaving Killian neglected by his Father much of the time.
	  - Killian always harbored resentment towards the Carnival and it's Feywild connection believing the Feywild's inhabitants were robbing him of time with his Father.

- **Shadow Syndicate**:  
  - As an adult, Killian founds the **[[Shadow Syndicate]]**, a criminal organization with reach from the Moonsea to [[Luskan]] and even the [[Shadowfell]].  
  - The Syndicate dominates smuggling, assassinations, and espionage, **surpassing** even his **father**’s shadow network in **power** and influence.

## 5. **Key Themes and Potential Hooks**
- **Themes**:  
  - Killian becomes hardened, lethal and harbors a resentment for the [[Feywild]].
