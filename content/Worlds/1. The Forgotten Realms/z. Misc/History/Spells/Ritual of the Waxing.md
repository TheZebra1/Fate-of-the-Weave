# Quest Brief: The Ritual of the Waxing

> **Campaign Arc:** The Return of [[Selûnarra]]  
> **Quest Level Range:** 10–15  
> **Theme:** Divine Ritual, Lost Magic, Lunar Mysticism

## **Summary**
A long-lost ritual tied to the goddess [[Selûne]] may be the only hope of summoning the mythic moon-enclave **[[Selûnarra]]** back to the [[Material Plane]]. The ritual is incomplete and forgotten—but clues remain scattered in sacred texts, hidden ruins, and divine artifacts. Powerful forces, including the shadowy agents of **[[Shar]]**, seek to stop this at all costs.

## **Quest Phases**
### **Phase 1: The Trail of Moonlight**
- **Start Location:** *[[Selûne’s Smile]]*, [[Waterdeep]]
- **Goals:**
  - Speak with **[[Luna]]**, avatar of Selûne.
  - Visit the **House of the Moon** to consult High Moonmistress.
  - Retrieve the **Lost Verses of the [[Selûne's Song.wav|Selûne's Song]]** from the **Ruins of [[Myth Rhynn]]** in the [[Graycloak Hills]].
- **Challenges:**
  - Lunar wards, golems, and puzzles.
  - Competing cultists of Shar seeking the same knowledge.

### **Phase 2: The Lunar Keystone**
- **Goal:** Recover and reactivate **[[Selûne’s Eye]]**, an ancient planar artifact.
- **Steps:**
  - Retrieve the artifact from a dusty shelf in Selûne’s Smile.
  - Reawaken it under moonlight at a sacred moonwell:
    - Bathe in moonlight during a full moon.
    - Cast *Moonbeam* and invoke Selûne in High Celestial.
    - Succeed on a DC 18 Religion check or commune directly with the goddess.

### **Phase 3: Journey to [[Elah’zad]]**
- **Location:** [[Hills of Scent]], [[Anauroch Desert]]
- **Objective:** Reach and unseal the hidden temple of Elah’zad.
- **Obstacles:**
  - Shadow ambushes by Thultanthar scouts.
  - Undead “Lunar Wraiths” of failed ritualists.
  - Open the Moon-Door using Selûne’s Eye and a sung incantation in Deep Celestial.

### **Phase 4: The Ritual of the Waxing**
- **Must be performed during:** *Waxing Gibbous Moon*
- **Participants:**
  - A Selûnite cleric (lvl 10+)
  - A spellcaster with *[[plane-shift-xphb|Plane Shift]]* or *[[gate-xphb|Gate]]*
  - A “Moontouched” individual (born under a rare celestial omen or blessed by Selûne)
  - [[Selûne’s Eye]]

- **Ritual Procedure:**
  1. Place Selûne’s Eye on the altar of Elah’zad.
  2. Sing the *complete Song of Selûne* in harmony.
  3. Cast *Moonbeam*, *Greater Restoration*, and *Plane Shift* at the ritual climax.
  4. Succeed on a DC 20 Arcana or Religion check to stabilize Selûnarra’s return.

- **Failure Risk:** Without success, Selûnarra returns to the divine plane within 1 hour.

## **Potential Outcomes**
- **Success:**
  - Selûnarra returns to Faerûn, shining with silver spires and floating above the Anauroch or tracking the moon’s orbit.
  - The Moonmaiden grants a divine boon or vision.
- **Consequence:**
  - The return reignites the ancient conflict between **Selûne** and **Shar**.
  - Shar’s cult may retaliate in force to undo the ritual.

## **Adventure Hooks**
- A prophetic dream of silver towers falling into shadow.
- A Moontouched PC begins glowing under moonlight and hearing hymns in their sleep.
- A corrupted Selûnite relic begins leaking black radiance and must be purified.

**DM Notes:** This quest arc blends mystery, divine influence, and arcane precision. Use moon phases in your campaign calendar and introduce celestial omens and lunar tide effects for flavor.
