## **Significance of the Prophecy**  
The prophecy is revered by both the devout followers of [[Khepri]] and [[Zehir]]:  
- **Khepri’s Followers** view the Scion as their god’s chosen savior, destined to bring rebirth and extinguish Zehir’s corruption.  
- **Zehir’s Cultists** believe the Scion will tip the scales in their god’s favor, spreading destruction and chaos across the mortal and divine planes.  

The *Lost Addendum*, suppressed by Zehir’s cultists and nearly forgotten even by Khepri’s followers, suggests that the Scion’s choice may not be his alone. The mention of the *"girl of shifting sight"* implies a mortal ally whose destiny is entwined with [[Zahur]]’s—one who can reveal the path to salvation if she can reach him in time.

## **The Girl of Shifting Sight**  
**Mulhorandi Name for the Girl:** *"An-Re Setut"*  
(*Translation: Girl of Shifting Sights*)  

The girl [[Khepthara]], transported from Earth by Thoth and Khepri, is a wild card in this divine conflict. Her prophetic dreams, still raw and uncontrolled, offer glimpses of the potential futures tied to Zahur. Her presence may solidify Zahur’s alignment with Khepri, for she carries the wisdom and foresight to show him what lies ahead should he fall to Zehir’s temptations. This creates a moral and emotional anchor for Zahur, adding another layer to his journey and giving the players a tangible quest to ensure the meeting takes place.  

Both factions will seek to control or eliminate the girl:  
- **Khepri’s Devout** will seek to guide her safely to Zahur.  
- **Zehir’s Cultists** will stop at nothing to silence her, believing her existence jeopardizes their god’s ultimate victory.  

This introduces an immediate quest for the players: protect the girl, uncover her identity, and ensure she meets Zahur in time to change his destiny.
