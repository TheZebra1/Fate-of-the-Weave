# Chrysalis Codex
## Description

The **Chrysalis Codex** is an **ancient Gith artifact** that holds the secret to reversing the **ceremorphosis** process. It has the power to free **Gith'Zerath** and his mind flayers from their Illithid transformation, restoring them to their **original Gith forms**. This process, however, requires **great psionic power** and often a **sacrifice**, typically linked to the power of those involved in the ritual.

The Codex’s existence is a **well-kept secret** among the **Githyanki**, and its location is hidden in a **fortress in the Astral Plane**. It is said that unlocking the **Codex** may not only **reverse ceremorphosis**, some believe it has a **secondary effect**—one that might bring about the return of the [[Illithid Empire]].

## Known Properties
- **Restoration of Gith Forms**: The Codex can reverse the transformation of mind flayers back into their original Gith forms, though this requires great power to perform.
- **Secondary Effect**: Some suspect the Codex could also **restore Ilsensine** to its original Elder Brain state, though this is purely speculative and highly dangerous.

## Current Knowledge
- **Activation**: Requires a **psionic ritual**, steeped in ancient Gith lore, often involving the **sacrifice of psionic energy** and knowledge of the **Githyanki**.
- **Location**: Hidden in the fortress of the **Githyanki**, deep within the **Astral Plane**.

## Important Considerations
- The Codex’s **unstable nature** may have unpredictable effects if used improperly.
- **Githyanki** seek the Codex to restore their people, but there is a strong risk that **Ilsensine** could also be reborn as a result.
