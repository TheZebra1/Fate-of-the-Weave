*Moil is a crumbling metropolis adrift in the Shadowfell, a bastion of entropy ruled by the primordial He That Endures. Shifting constantly between decay and rebirth, the city exports chaos to [[Faerûn]] and draws in those obsessed with loss, forgotten futures, or power beyond time.*

## Architecture & Civil Planning
- City layout folds in on itself, with half-collapsed towers standing beside half-born structures.
- Time-worn roads spiral endlessly, intersecting portals to half-formed timelines.
- Sewers breathe entropy mist. Magic suffuses the city’s core.

### Notable Buildings
- **[[Clockless Tower]]** – Black obelisk leaking pale motes of frozen time.
- **[[The Never-Ending Market]]** – Bazaar where everything ages unnaturally fast.
- **[[Mirrored Alleys]]** – Lanes reflecting unrealized life choices.

## Clothing & Decoration
- Cloaks of threadbare shadow, entropy-sigil tattoos, and face-covering veils.
- Decoration favors fractal decay: patterns eroding with time.

## Culture & Art & Music
- Musical motifs mimic broken chords and unfinished songs.
- Statues crumble by design—representing impermanence and transformation.
- Artists prize "momentary masterpieces"—works designed to erode.

## Customs
- Visitors must offer a “memory of loss” to enter central districts.
- Celebrations begin with creation and end in intentional ruin.

## Entertainment
- Shadow plays depicting alternate lives.
- Entropy duels—ritual combats where weapons break and forms shift mid-strike.

## Festivities
- **Festival of the Waiting**: When the city pauses, every being stands still for one full shadow-day.
- **Night of the Forgotten Path**: Residents relive a choice never made.

## Food & Drink
- Time-wilted fruit, fermented thought-wines, and echo-bread (changes flavor with emotion).
- Food imported from Faerûn quickly spoils—entropy accelerates its decay.

## Law & Military
- The Waiting Wardens enforce minimal order; they exist to preserve entropy’s balance, not prevent it.
- Crime is judged based on whether it disrupts decay or accelerates it unnaturally.

## Literature
- Tomes record events that have never happened.
- Books unravel as they are read—knowledge as ephemeral as the city itself.

## Religion & Superstition
- Shrines to He That Endures pulse with unstable magic.
- Temples to Shar and the Raven Queen exist, but are watched closely by entropy cultists.
