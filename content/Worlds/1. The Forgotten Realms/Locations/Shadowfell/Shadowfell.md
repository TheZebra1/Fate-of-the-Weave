## Overview
The **Shadowfell**, also known as the Plane of Shadow, is a parallel plane to the [[Material Plane]], embodying darkness and decay. It serves as a shadowy reflection of the world, where light is dimmed, emotions are dulled, and the landscape is a twisted mirror of the natural world. 
- Being isolated in the Shadowfell causes **time** to **rapidly pass**, not because time is changing but, because the negative energies invade the **mind** of creatures causing long spells of **lethargy, mourning, or melancholy** that feel like moments but may stretch on for **hours, days, or weeks**.

## Locations 

- **[[Gloomwrought]]** – The City of Midnight, a shifting, intrigue-filled shadow-port.  Most famous of Shadowfell cities, learned people on the Material Plane have at least heard of this place.
- **[[Moil]]** – The City That Waits, a shadow fell area shrouded in mystery and decay that few know much about.  
- [[Letherna]] - Domain of the [[Raven Queen]] and Shadar-kai.
- **[[Shadowdark]]** – The Shadowfell's version of the Underdark, even more twisted sinister.  
- **[[Evernight]]** – The Corpse Market city, filled with undead and flesh-trade echoing the Material Plane city of Neverwinter 
- **[[Rosok/Ravenkeep]]** – The dual capital of the Raven Queen's Shadar-kai & the Shadar-kai of ancient [[Netheril]]. This large city exists in The Raven Queens domain of **Letherna**, more specifically somewhere in the **Forlorn Forest**. The high society formed here have built [[Feywild|Fey]] and [[Material Plane|Material]] Havens where the elite can shelter from the Negative Energy of the Shadowfell  and remain productive and lucrative in their endeavors.  
- **[[Karsun]]** - ***Echo of Destroyed [[Thultanthar]] (City of Shade)*** – A fallen Netherese enclave inhabited by the last remnants of proud Netherese loyalists in the Shadowfell trying to piece back together shards of their decimated empire.

## Deities of the Shadowfell
Several deities and powerful entities are associated with the Shadowfell:

- **The [[Raven Queen]]**: A mysterious goddess who rules from the [[Fortress of Memories]]. She governs **death, fate, and winter**, and the **Shadar-kai.**

- **[[Shar]]**: The goddess of darkness and loss, Shar's influence permeates the Shadowfell. She embodies night, secrets, and forgetfulness, and her presence is felt throughout the plane.

- **[[Mask]]**: The god of shadows and thieves, Mask finds the Shadowfell a fitting domain for his clandestine activities. He is associated with intrigue, stealth, and the dark.

- **[[Null]]**: Known as the Reaver and the Guardian of the Lost, Null is the dragon god of death and undeath. He is worshiped by evil dragons and oversees the passage of dragon souls 

- [[Vecna]]

## Domains of Dread
Within the Shadowfell lies the **Demiplane of Dread**, a collection of isolated subdomains known as the **Domains of Dread**. Each domain is a prison tailored to a specific Darklord, a being cursed for their evil deeds. The enigmatic **Dark Powers** create and maintain these domains, trapping the Darklords within.

### Notable Domains
- **[[Barovia]]**: Ruled by **[[Strahd von Zarovich]]**, Barovia is a land shrouded in perpetual mist and darkness, plagued by undead and ruled by the vampire lord.

- **[[Kalakeri]]**: Inspired by Indian culture, Kalakeri is a domain of lush rainforests and political intrigue, where rival factions vie for control of the Sapphire Throne. 

- [[Darkon]]: A domain of ancient ruins and lingering magic, once ruled by the powerful lich **[[Azalin Rex]]**.

- [[The Carnival of Fear]]

## Influence on the Multiverse
The Shadowfell serves as a **conduit for souls**, a source of **shadow magic**, and a reflection of the Material Plane's darker aspects. Its deities and domains influence various facets of existence, from the cycle of life and death to the manifestation of fears and nightmares.
