## Overview
The Shadowdark is the Shadowfell's mirror of the Underdark—an expanse of oppressive gloom, where necrotic energies seep through every crack and despair is as common as air. Reflected tunnels twist through spectral stone, glowing with sickly fungi and shadowflame. Life here is cruel, calculated, and bound by ancient griefs.

## Geography
Shadowdark caverns mirror their Material counterparts, but the geography shifts with shadowquakes and planar tides. Stalactites grow like bone, lakes reflect dead faces, and entire sections vanish into despair-shrouded voids overnight. Shadow crossings occasionally connect with the Underdark, often spawning plagues, corruption, or shadow-horrors.

## Internal Relationships
Shadowdark alliances are tenuous at best. The Matron Mothers plot with and against the Mind Flayer Synod. The Duergar avoid all but calculated war-pacts, and Raven Queen cults harvest souls from the unwary, and hold tense skirmishes with other Shadowfell inhabitants but especially servants of Lolth.

## Outward Relationships
The Shadowdark connects to the Underdark through unstable shadow crossings. These are monitored, contested, or exploited for raids and corruption rituals. Surface world ties are rare, often mythic, but Raven Queen agents sometimes bargain with desperate necromancers above.

## Mechanics & Shadow Effects

- **Shadow Crossing Events**: 1d6 random planar effects per travel.

| d6 | Shadow Crossing Event                                                                                     |
|----|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1  | **Memory Bleed** – One party member loses access to a cherished memory. Disadvantage on WIS checks for 1 hour. |
| 2  | **Echoed Selves** – Shadow duplicates of the party appear and mimic them for 1d4 rounds before vanishing. |
| 3  | **Temporal Stagger** – Time dilates; the party is 1d6 hours early or late on the Material Plane timeline. |
| 4  | **Soul Glare** – A malevolent gaze watches through the crossing; each creature must pass a DC 15 CHA save or be frightened for 1 minute. |
| 5  | **Void Entanglement** – Shadow tendrils attempt to grasp travelers. STR or DEX save (DC 14) or be restrained for 1 round. |
| 6  | **Veil Thin** – A nearby Shadowdark creature slips through unnoticed and begins stalking the party.        |

- **Shadow Curse**: Extended exposure saps emotions, requires WIS save every long rest.
- **Magic Shifts**: +1 to necrotic/illusion/psychic spells. -1 to radiant/light-based spells.

## Summary
The Shadowdark is where memory decays, shadow binds, and power is seized through despair. It is the mirror, the echo, the hungry emptiness beneath the Underdark—and for those who seek dominion through darkness, it is home.
