## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Core Theme:** Joy, freedom, and celebration of life

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Verdant forests, towering mountains, eternal sunsets
- **Magic Effects:** Healing and enchantment spells enhanced
- **Hazards:** Emotional extremes (euphoria or rage), Wild feasts, Overwhelming beauty

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Eladrin, Celestials, Fey
- **Famous Figures:** Corellon Larethian, Zeus, other Olympian gods
- **Ruler:** No single ruler, but governed by various deities (e.g., Corellon for Arvandor, Zeus for Olympus)

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Characters may experience heightened emotions, Wisdom saves to resist becoming lost in revelry
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize beauty, passion, and boundless joy
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Join a divine festival to gain favor
  - Help a deity protect their sacred grove from corruption
