### Layers of Elysium
[[layers-of-elysium-xdmg|Layers of Elysium]]
## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Core Theme:** Pure bliss and serenity

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Peaceful rivers, eternal twilight, idyllic landscapes
- **Magic Effects:** Healing and restorative magic is amplified
- **Hazards:** None (but leaving the plane may require a Wisdom save)

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Guardinals, Celestials
- **Famous Figures:** Talisid and the Five Companions
- **Ruler:** None (governed by the principles of goodness)

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Characters may not want to leave due to overwhelming peace
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize tranquility, kindness, and contentment
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Aid a celestial seeking to cleanse a tainted region
  - Help a soul trapped in blissful oblivion return to reality
