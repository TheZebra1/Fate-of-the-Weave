## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Core Theme:** Pure chaos and constant change

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Shifting landscapes of raw elements, ever-changing reality
- **Magic Effects:** Wild magic reigns, Spells can produce unexpected effects
- **Hazards:** Unpredictable terrain, Elemental storms, Reality flux

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Slaadi, Githzerai, Chaos beasts
- **Famous Figures:** Ssendam, Ygorl (Slaadi Lords)
- **Ruler:** None (though Slaadi Lords hold influence)

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Wisdom checks needed to stabilize terrain, Chaos surges affect spells
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize unpredictability, change, and creativity
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Navigate through Limbo to reach a hidden citadel
  - Retrieve a powerful artifact lost in the chaos
