## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
- **Core Theme:** Despair, imprisonment, and eternal entrapment

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Red-tinged skies, desolate prisons, isolated orbs floating in void
- **Magic Effects:** Divination blocked, Escape and teleportation spells hindered
- **Hazards:** Strong emotional pull toward despair, Impossible-to-leave prisons, Poisonous swamps

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Demodands (Gehreleths), Night Hags, Corrupted spirits
- **Famous Figures:** Apomps the Three-Faced (leader of Demodands)
- **Ruler:** None (dominated by various demodand factions)

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Characters have disadvantage on saving throws to resist despair and imprisonment effects
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize hopelessness, isolation, and paranoia
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Free an ally wrongfully imprisoned in Carceri
  - Navigate through the layers to find an ancient secret hidden by a fallen god
