The Abyss is a chaotic, infinite expanse of twisted realities, each ruled by a different demon lord. It is a plane of madness, corruption, and entropy, where the strongest demons carve out their own domains amid an ever-shifting landscape of destruction. Below are some of its most infamous layers, organized by their numerical order:
[[layers-of-the-abyss-xdmg|Layers of the Abyss]]
## Notable Layers and Their Rulers

- **The [[Demonweb Pits]] (66th Layer) – [[Lolth]]**  
  A vast, tangled web of darkness and shifting pathways, where [[Lolth]], the Spider Queen, weaves her schemes. It is a twisted realm filled with monstrous arachnids and labyrinthine tunnels leading to other planes.

- **[[Gaping Maw]] (88th Layer) – [[Demogorgon]]**  
  A storm-wracked ocean filled with abyssal horrors and jagged rock spires. [[Demogorgon]] rules from his fortress, Abysm, deep beneath the waves.

- **[[Azzagrat]] (45th-47th Layers) – [[Graz’zt]]**  
  A luxurious but sinister realm of deception, filled with dark palaces, shadowy courts, and demonic intrigue. [[Graz’zt]]’s capital, Zelatar, serves as the heart of his influence.

- **[[Thanatos]] (113th Layer) – [[Orcus]]**  
  A frozen, corpse-littered wasteland dominated by necromantic magic. [[Orcus]] dwells in the fortress of Naratyr, overseeing legions of undead.

- **[[Hollow’s Heart]] (176th Layer) – [[Fraz-Urb’luu]]**  
  An illusionary wasteland where reality constantly shifts. Nothing is as it seems in this deceptive domain ruled by the Prince of Deception, [[Fraz-Urb’luu]].

- **[[Shedaklah]] (222nd Layer) – [[Zuggtmoy]] & [[Juiblex]]**  
  A realm of decay and corruption, half consumed by fungal overgrowth and half by oozing, churning sludge. [[Zuggtmoy]] and [[Juiblex]] are locked in an eternal struggle for dominance.

- **[[The Seeping Woods]] (422nd Layer) – [[Yeenoghu]]**  
  A rotting, blood-drenched hunting ground where the Demon Lord of Gnolls and his pack of ravenous hunters endlessly chase prey.

- **The [[Endless Maze]] (600th Layer) – [[Baphomet]]**  
  A massive, ever-changing labyrinth filled with deadly traps and bestial minions. [[Baphomet]]'s domain is a place of primal fury and savagery.

The Abyss is limitless, with countless other layers ruled by horrors yet undiscovered. Its chaotic nature ensures that no two realms are alike, and even the most powerful demon lords must fight endlessly to maintain control over their domains.
