## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Core Theme:** Purity, justice, and spiritual ascension

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Seven layers, each higher and more perfect, bathed in holy light
- **Magic Effects:** Healing and protection spells enhanced, Evil creatures suffer penalties
- **Hazards:** Divine presence (evil characters take radiant damage), Overwhelming sense of judgment

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Archons, Celestials, Lantern Archons
- **Famous Figures:** Bahamut, Moradin, Zaphkiel
- **Ruler:** No single ruler, governed by various Archons and deities (e.g., Bahamut)

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Evil characters feel discomfort (or even pain), Good-aligned characters may receive divine visions
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize righteousness, divine purpose, and redemption
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Ascend to the highest level to seek divine wisdom
  - Protect a sacred site from an infernal incursion
