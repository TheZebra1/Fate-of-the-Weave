## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Core Theme:** Perfect order, logic, and inevitability

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Infinite clockwork gears, perfect symmetry, absolute order
- **Magic Effects:** Divination spells enhanced, Chaos-based spells impeded
- **Hazards:** Reality is rigid (penalties to chaotic actions), Gears that crush anything in their path

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Modrons, Inevitables, Formians
- **Famous Figures:** Primus (the One and Prime), Various Inevitable Lords
- **Ruler:** Primus (oversees the Modrons)

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Lawful characters feel at ease, Chaotic characters face resistance
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize logic, structure, and inevitability
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Stop a rogue Modron that threatens the balance
  - Solve a complex puzzle to access hidden knowledge
