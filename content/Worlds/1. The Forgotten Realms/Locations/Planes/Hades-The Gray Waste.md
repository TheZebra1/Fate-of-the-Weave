## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
- **Core Theme:** Despair, apathy, and hopelessness

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Colorless wastelands, oppressive gloom, and eternal twilight
- **Magic Effects:** Necromancy enhanced, Healing impeded
- **Hazards:** Draining despair (Charisma saves or suffer exhaustion), Shadowy figures

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Night Hags, Yugoloths, Larvae
- **Famous Figures:** Hades, Hel, other underworld entities
- **Ruler:** None specific, but overseen by powerful underworld entities

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Prolonged stay causes despair (Charisma saves to resist)
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize bleakness, draining hope, and apathy
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Free a soul trapped in the Gray Waste
  - Find a way to escape the plane before despair overwhelms the party
