[[layers-of-gehenna-xdmg|Layers of Gehenna]]
## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Core Theme:** Ruthless ambition and suffering

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Steep volcanic slopes, perpetual darkness, rivers of lava
- **Magic Effects:** Fire spells are enhanced, Cold spells impeded
- **Hazards:** Constantly sloping terrain, Lava flows, Explosive geysers

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Yugoloths, Fire giants, Hell hounds
- **Famous Figures:** General of Gehenna, Various Yugoloth lords
- **Ruler:** None (ruled by powerful Yugoloth factions)

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Dexterity checks needed to avoid sliding, Fire damage increased
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize treachery, survival of the fittest, and suffering
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Retrieve a contract held by a Yugoloth lord
  - Survive a deadly game hosted by a local faction
