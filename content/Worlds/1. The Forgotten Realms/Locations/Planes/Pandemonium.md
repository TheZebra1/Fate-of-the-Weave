[[layers-of-pandemonium-xdmg|Layers of Pandemonium]]
## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Core Theme:** Madness, chaos, and endless howling winds

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Endless tunnels, deafening winds, total darkness
- **Magic Effects:** Madness-inducing effects, Psychic spells enhanced
- **Hazards:** Deafening noise (Constitution saves to avoid madness), Strong winds, Endless darkness

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Howlers, Derro, Mad cultists
- **Famous Figures:** Erythnul (God of Slaughter)
- **Ruler:** None (ruled by madness and chaos)

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Characters must make Wisdom saves to avoid insanity, Difficulty hearing and communicating
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize confusion, terror, and the loss of sanity
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Find a lost artifact in the depths of Pandemonium
  - Rescue a party member driven mad by the plane’s influence
