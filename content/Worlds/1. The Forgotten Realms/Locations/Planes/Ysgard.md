**A plane of glory and battle, where heroism is eternal and valor is celebrated.**
**Social Hubs:** [[Asgard]], [[Gates of the Moon]], [[Glorium]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[Asgard]]:** The divine realm of the Norse gods, with feasting halls and endless revelry.
- **[[Gates of the Moon]]:** Celestial domain of [[Selûne]], a tranquil counterpoint to the warrior realms.
- **[[Glorium]]:** Gate-town known for welcoming travelers and champions of all kinds.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Divided into realms, each ruled by its patron deity. Divine law and honor guide leadership.
- **Security:** Enforced by divine might, the [[Einherjar]], and mortal champions reborn in glory.
- **Underworld:** Nonexistent—Ysgard knows only struggle, revelry, and rebirth.
- **Altars:** Temples devoted to gods of battle, joy, and the moon are scattered across the plane.
- **Emotion:** **Exhilaration** – Every moment pulses with adrenaline, purpose, and celestial fire.
- **Resources:** Inspiration for mortals, strength for the devoted, and a proving ground for divine champions.

### [[Currency]]
[[Hero’s Tokens]] – Divine symbols of valor (Platinum)  
[[Battle Runes]] – Writs of martial success (Gold)  
[[Mead Vouchers]] – Tokens exchanged for divine feasts (Silver)  
[[Warrior’s Marks]] – Proof of participation in trials or battles (Copper)  
Barter System – Honor, legacy, and heroic deeds are often coin enough

### History:
- **Origins:** Ysgard is a major Outer Plane in the Great Wheel cosmology, tied to chaotic good and heroic neutral alignments.
- **Divine Presence:** Home to mythic deities such as [[Tyr]], [[Odin]], and others from both the Forgotten Realms and Norse pantheons.
- **Living Terrain:** Massive land masses float through space and are reborn after destruction, reflecting the cycle of heroism.
- **Cultural Legacy:** A destination for mortal warriors of glory, whose souls fight, fall, and rise again in eternal celebration.
