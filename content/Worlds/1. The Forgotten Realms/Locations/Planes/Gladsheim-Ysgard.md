## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Core Theme:** Heroism, valor, and eternal battle

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Floating earthbergs, eternal twilight, fields of battle
- **Magic Effects:** Enhanced healing, Resurrection spells work freely
- **Hazards:** Endless combat, Falling earthbergs, Shifting landscapes

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Einherjar, Valkyries, Giants
- **Famous Figures:** Odin, Thor, other Norse deities
- **Ruler:** Odin (for the Norse deities' realm)

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Heroes slain in battle revive at dawn, Wisdom saves to resist battle frenzy
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize bravery, camaraderie, and epic deeds
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Prove your worth in a grand contest of strength
  - Recover a legendary weapon lost in the eternal battles
