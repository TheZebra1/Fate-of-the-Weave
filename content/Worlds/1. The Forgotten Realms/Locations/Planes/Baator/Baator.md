[[layers-of-the-nine-hells-xdmg|Layers of the Nine Hells]]
## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Core Theme:** Tyranny, punishment, and infernal hierarchy
## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Nine layers, each more oppressive and dangerous than the last
- **Magic Effects:** Fire and charm spells enhanced, Healing impeded
- **Hazards:** Soul-draining contracts, Infernal traps, Fire pits, Quicksand
## Player Impact
### Alignment Effects Overview (per DMG)
- Spending prolonged time in a layer of Hell can subtly shift a character’s alignment toward **Lawful Evil** as they adopt the local values.
	- The stronger the sin of the layer aligns with a character’s desires, the faster they are influenced.
- Resisting these influences may require **DC 10** **Wisdom saving throws** at the DM’s discretion.
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize order, cruelty, and temptation
# The Nine Hells of Baator
## 1. [[Avernus]]
- **Ruler:** [[Zariel]], fallen angel turned archdevil.
- **Sin/Flavor:** War and bloodshed; the frontline of the Blood War.
- **Effects:** Mortals drawn into endless battles; those who linger risk growing increasingly aggressive and warlike.
## 2. [[Dis]]
- **Ruler:** [[Dispater]], paranoid and secretive archdevil.
- **Sin/Flavor:** Tyranny, paranoia, and espionage; city of iron.
- **Effects:** Those who stay too long become paranoid, suspicious, and obsessively secretive.
## 3. [[Minauros]]
- **Ruler:** [[Mammon]], the avaricious lord of greed.
- **Sin/Flavor:** Greed and corruption; an endless sinking mire.
- **Effects:** Prolonged exposure makes creatures increasingly greedy and unwilling to part with wealth.
## 4. [[Phlegethos]]
- **Ruler:** [[Belial]] and [[Fierna]], co-rulers of hedonism and pain.
- **Sin/Flavor:** Lust, indulgence, and suffering; burning rivers of fire.
- **Effects:** Prolonged exposure increases sadistic tendencies and reduces empathy.
## 5. [[Stygia]]
- **Ruler:** [[Levistus]], trapped in ice.
- **Sin/Flavor:** Betrayal and cold ambition; frozen seas.
- **Effects:** Creatures develop ruthless pragmatism and a willingness to betray for gain.
## 6. [[Malbolge]]
- **Ruler:** [[Glasya]], Asmodeus’s devious daughter.
- **Sin/Flavor:** Deception, cruelty, and corruption; ever-shifting, tumbling slopes.
- **Effects:** Those who stay too long become deceptive, manipulative, and power-hungry.
## 7. [[Maladomini]]
- **Ruler:** [[Baalzebul]], the fallen Lord of Lies.
- **Sin/Flavor:** Ruin, decay, and despair; endless desolation.
- **Effects:** Prolonged exposure leads to nihilism and self-destructive tendencies.
## 8. [[Cania]]
- **Ruler:** [[Mephistopheles]], master of forbidden knowledge.
- **Sin/Flavor:** Forbidden knowledge, obsession, and cold calculation; icy wasteland.
- **Effects:** Those who stay become obsessed with knowledge and power, disregarding morality.
## 9. [[Nessus]]
- **Ruler:** [[Asmodeus]], the supreme overlord of the Hells.
- **Sin/Flavor:** Ultimate tyranny and control; bottomless pit and infernal palace.
- **Effects:** Prolonged exposure instills utter obedience to authority and loss of free will.
