[[layers-of-acheron-xdmg|Layers of Acheron]]
## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral (with Evil tendencies)
- **Core Theme:** Eternal conflict and war

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Floating metal cubes, endless battlefield
- **Magic Effects:** Evocation spells are more potent
- **Hazards:** Colliding cubes, Sudden gravity shifts, Endless war zones

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Goblinoids, Orcs, Inevitables
- **Famous Figures:** Maglubiyet, Gruumsh
- **Ruler:** None (dominated by various warlords and factions)

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Enhanced damage from weapons, Constant battle sounds
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize militaristic discipline, Unyielding conflict
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Retrieve a powerful weapon from a fallen warlord
  - Broker a truce between warring factions
