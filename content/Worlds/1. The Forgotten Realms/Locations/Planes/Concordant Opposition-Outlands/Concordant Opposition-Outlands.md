## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Core Theme:** Balance and neutrality, connecting all outer planes

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Diverse landscapes, Spire at the center, 16 gate-towns
- **Magic Effects:** Magic becomes unpredictable closer to the Spire
- **Hazards:** Magic nullification near the Spire, Unpredictable terrain

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Rilmani, Outlandish beasts
- **Famous Figures:** The Lady of Pain (indirect influence)
- **Ruler:** None (though the Lady of Pain’s influence is felt)

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Planar travel is easier, Magic weakens near the Spire
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize neutrality, balance, and the absence of extremes
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Investigate a disturbance in one of the gate-towns
  - Recover an artifact hidden at the Spire’s base
