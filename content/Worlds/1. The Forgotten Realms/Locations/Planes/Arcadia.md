[[layers-of-arcadia-xdmg|Layers of Arcadia]]
## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good (with Neutral tendencies)
- **Core Theme:** Perfect harmony and order

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Lush landscapes, perpetual twilight, perfect weather
- **Magic Effects:** Healing spells are enhanced
- **Hazards:** None (though disobedience is harshly judged)

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Aasimar, Archons, Celestials
- **Famous Figures:** Erathaol the Seer, Barachiel
- **Ruler:** No single ruler, overseen by celestial hierarchies

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Lawful characters feel empowered, Chaotic characters face penalties
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize peace, order, and balance
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Seek guidance from an ancient celestial oracle
  - Resolve a rift threatening the plane's harmony
