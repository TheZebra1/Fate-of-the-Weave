## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good (with Neutral tendencies)
- **Core Theme:** Industrious harmony, balance between nature and civilization

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Two layers facing each other like a mirror (Dothion and Shurrock), idyllic farmlands, rugged mountains
- **Magic Effects:** Crafting and creation spells enhanced, Nature spells are potent
- **Hazards:** Environmental hazards in Shurrock (rockslides, storms), Overwhelming sense of duty in Dothion

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Gnomes, Celestials, Industrious animals
- **Famous Figures:** None specific, though Garl Glittergold is revered by many
- **Ruler:** None (each layer is self-governed by its inhabitants)

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Characters feel inspired to work and create, Chaotic characters may feel uneasy in the orderly environment
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize productivity, harmony, and the joy of craftsmanship
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Help resolve a dispute between farmers and miners
  - Craft a magical item using resources unique to Bytopia
