## Quick Summary
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Core Theme:** Nature's untamed beauty and harmony with beasts

## Environment
- **Key Traits:** Lush forests, endless daylight, moonlit nights
- **Magic Effects:** Animal summoning and druidic spells enhanced
- **Hazards:** None, but the natural order is strictly enforced

## Notable Inhabitants
- **Key Creatures:** Celestial animals, Intelligent beasts, Animal lords
- **Famous Figures:** Prince Talisid, The Animal Lords
- **Ruler:** None, but guided by powerful Animal Lords

## Player Impact
- **Special Rules:** Speak with animals freely, Nature-themed spells boosted
- **Roleplaying Tips:** Emphasize harmony, respect for nature, and freedom
- **Adventure Hooks:** 
  - Aid a dying forest spirit
  - Defend the plane from invaders who threaten its peace
