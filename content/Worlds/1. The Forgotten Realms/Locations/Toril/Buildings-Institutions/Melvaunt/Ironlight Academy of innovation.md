## Overview
Ironlight Academy of Innovation is a bustling forge of intellect, invention, and divine inspiration. Guided by the principles of [[Gond]] the Wonderbringer, the academy fosters the creation of magical machines, alchemical wonders, and practical enchantments to power [[Melvaunt]]’s economy and defenses. It serves as a bridge between academia and industry—where every invention has purpose and profit.

## Student Life
- **Internships:** Many students work part-time with Melvaunt’s forgemasters, mercenary guilds, or shipping companies.
- **Student Guilds:** The Cogs & Coils Society, Alchemist's Alehouse, and the Order of Practical Thaumaturgy.
- **Events:** Annual Invention Showcase, Steam Duel Championship, and the Lantern Parade of Gond.

## Notable Projects
- The **Ironlight Rail Prototype** (campus-to-docks tram)
- **Stormglass Batteries** powering local neighborhoods
- The controversial **Sentient Forgekeeper Golem** (decommissioned)
