# The Witchlight Carnival
## Overview
The **Witchlight Carnival** is a magical traveling fair that moves between planes, offering fantastical entertainment and a glimpse into the whimsical yet eerie [[Feywild]]. While it used to visit the [[Material Plane]] only once every 8 years, for the past 50 years, it has returned to set up outside of [[Melvaunt]] once every 3 months. The carnival stays for 2 weeks before continuing its interplanar journey.

![[Witchlight Carnival Alternate.png|500]]
*A colorful and magical carnival glowing in twilight, surrounded by rolling fog.*

## Notable NPCs
- **Mister Witch:** The co-leader of the carnival, reserved yet shrewd, who manages its day-to-day logistics.
- **Mister Light:** The flamboyant and energetic co-leader, the face of the carnival who ensures the guests’ enjoyment.

## Points of Interest
- **Bubble-Pop Teapot:** A whimsical tea ride that floats on bubbles.
- **Hall of Illusions:** A mysterious house of mirrors with a Feywild twist.
- **Snail Racing:** Guests can bet on enormous, colorful snails racing for glory.
- **Mystery Mine:** A thrilling and magical rollercoaster ride with Feywild enchantments.

## Mystical Nature
The carnival is more than entertainment; it serves as a subtle portal to the Feywild, making it a place of intrigue and danger for the unprepared. Deals with the Fey, the trading of memories, and unusual contracts are all part of its mystique.

## Background
For centuries, the Witchlight Carnival has provided wonder and escapism to those fortunate enough to attend, though its sudden increased appearances in Melvaunt have raised questions and sparked rumors. Some see it as an economic boon, while others worry about the unknown bargains struck under its magical lights.
