## **Location & Atmosphere**
**Club Transfusion** is a hidden, upscale venue known for its exclusivity, high-class entertainment, and mysterious entry process. There is no visible sign, only a **deep crimson door** with shifting carvings.

## **First Impression**
As the party approaches, they see a **massive orc bouncer** in a tailored **pinstriped suit** standing with arms crossed. He looks **imposing and professional**, but his expression is unreadable.

- **Perception (DC 12):** The carvings on the door subtly shift when not directly observed.
- **Investigation (DC 15):** The door radiates faint **enchantment magic**.

## **The Silent Bouncer**
The **orc bouncer** does not speak beyond a simple, polite, yet unsettling:
> **"Hello."**

- **He does not react** to bribery, intimidation, flattery, or persuasion.
- He **does not stop anyone** from approaching the door.
- **If attacked**, he defends himself efficiently but does not escalate.

## **The True Doorman: The Sentient Door**
Once the players step past the orc, they find the door is **enchanted and sentient**.
### **Personality & Behavior**
- Speaks in a **smooth, refined voice**.
- Introduces itself as **"The Doorman"**.
- States that **entry is not guaranteed**.
- Judges visitors and **sets challenges** based on their nature.

## **Entry Requirements: The Doorman’s Challenges**
Each character must **pass a test** to gain entry. The door assesses them magically and selects a challenge.
### **1. The Test of Secrets (Social / Deception / Insight)**
- The door asks:  
  > **"Tell me a secret you've never told anyone. If it is valuable, you may enter."**
- Players may:
  - **Tell a real secret** → Door grants entry.
  - **Lie convincingly (Deception DC 18)** → Entry granted.
  - **Give a boring secret** → Door asks for a better one.
### **2. The Test of Blood (Constitution / Endurance)**
- The door demands **a drop of their blood**.
- **Options:**
  - **Comply** → The door absorbs the blood and allows entry.
  - **Refuse** → Entry denied.
  - **Fake it (Sleight of Hand DC 16)** → The door chuckles but allows them in.
### **3. The Test of Class (Performance / Persuasion)**
- The door asks them to prove their **elegance, charm, or talent**.
- Requires:
  - **Performance (DC 14)** (dance, sing, etc.)
  - **Persuasion (DC 16)** (convince the door of their worth)
  - **Offering a luxury item** (a rare wine, artifact, or gemstone)
### **4. The Test of Shadows (Stealth / Subtlety)**
- The door challenges a rogue-like character:
  > **"Step through me without a sound."**
- **Stealth check (DC 18):** If successful, they are inside.
	- On a failure they suffer the [[banishment]] spell for 1 hour or *until they pay 100 gp to return at which point they can enter the club.*
### **5. The Test of Wisdom (Riddle)**
- The door poses a riddle based on the character’s background.
- Example:
  > **"I am not alive, yet I grow. I don’t have lungs, but I need air. What am I?"**
  - **Correct Answer:** *Fire.*
  - **Intelligence or Wisdom check (DC 14-18) if unsure.*
### **6. The Test of Coin (Bribery)**
- The door accepts a **bribe (minimum 100 GP per person).**
### **7. The Test of Reputation (Recognition)**
- If they have **a VIP or notable status**, the door **recognizes them** and grants entry.

## **Failure Consequences**
If a player **fails their test** or refuses:
- The door **laughs and denies them entry**.
- It may **magically eject them**.
- **A retry is allowed, but harder.**
- **Another party member can vouch for them.**
### **If the Door is Attacked**
- It **ripples like liquid**, forming **jagged teeth**.
- It **shouts loud enough to alert city guards**.
- The **orc bouncer finally reacts**, stepping forward to intervene.
## **Inside Club Transfusion**
Once inside, the party finds:
- **Lavish décor**: deep red velvet, golden chandeliers, magical lighting.
- **High-class patrons**: nobles, crime lords, supernatural entities.
- **The "Transfusion" Cocktail**: A **deep crimson drink with rumored magical properties**.
- **Private rooms**, gambling, and **magical performances**.
### **Notable Figures**
- The owner is an **enigmatic figure**—perhaps a **vampire, powerful mage, or fiend**.
- **Notable guests** may recognize the party and offer **side quests, deals, or intrigue**.
## **Summary**
1. **The Orc Bouncer** is **intimidating but passive**, only saying *"Hello."*
2. **The Enchanted Door is the True Doorman**, issuing **personalized challenges**.
3. **Failure** results in **denial, magical ejection, or alerts to security**.
4. **Inside, the club is an elegant hub of power and mystery.**
