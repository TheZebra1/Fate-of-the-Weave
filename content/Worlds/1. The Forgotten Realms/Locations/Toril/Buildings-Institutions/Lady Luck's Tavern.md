**A lively hub in Daggerford dedicated to Tymora, attracting those who embrace risk and adventure.**
**Social Hubs:** None (Tavern)

#### Notable Features:
- **Central Pillar:** A massive stone column in the taproom where patrons leave marks upon reaching the top.
- **Memorial Inscriptions:** Symbols of patrons who perished in quests are inscribed on the central pillar.

#### Key Summary
- **Geography:** Situated in central Daggerford, the tavern has relocated over time, originally in the Money Quarter and later near the Caravan Gate in the Caravan Quarter. 
- **Society:** The tavern serves as a gathering place for soldiers, adventurers, and risk-takers, fostering a culture of daring and storytelling.
- **Religion:** Dedicated to Tymora, the goddess of luck and good fortune, reflecting its patronage by those who take risks.
- **Emotion:** **Exhilaration** – The atmosphere is charged with tales of adventure and the spirit of daring exploits.
- **Resources:** While not a commercial hub, the tavern imports quality beverages and food supplies to cater to its clientele.

### Currency
Standard Faerûnian currency is accepted; barter is uncommon.

### History:
- **Founding:** Established in a former warehouse awarded to Darryl Orcslayer for his valor against orc raiders; later managed by his descendants, including Glenys Orcslayer. 
- **Cultural Significance:** The tavern has become a landmark in Daggerford, embodying the spirit of adventure and the favor of Tymora.
- **Relocations:** Moved from the Money Quarter to the Caravan Quarter over the years, adapting to the town's evolving landscape. 
- **Current Status:** Continues to thrive as a beloved establishment for adventurers and locals seeking camaraderie and tales of daring deeds.
