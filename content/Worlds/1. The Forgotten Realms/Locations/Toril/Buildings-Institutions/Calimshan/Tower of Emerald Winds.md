# Tower of Emerald Winds
## Overview
**Summary**:  
A floating tower of jade-hued glass and bronze filigree, the **Tower of Emerald Winds** hovers perpetually over the eastern edge of **[[Calimport]]**, just outside city jurisdiction. It serves as the **private residence and central command node** for Mr. [[Azmo Diaz]] and the Prime Office of [[Acquisitions Incorporated]]. Beautiful, immaculately kept, and heavily warded, few have entered and even fewer have seen the top.

## Description
- The tower appears **15 stories tall**, but fluctuates subtly depending on magical surveillance or proximity
- Constructed of **arcane-reinforced emerald crystal** that refracts light and disguises internal activity
- Connected to ground-level only by **select teleportation platforms** and **ritual-bound skyships** (like the *[[battle-balloon-ai|Hard Cloud]]*)

## Features
- **The Verdant Spiral**: A winding open-core staircase said to whisper to those who ascend it
- **The Reflecting Hall**: A chamber of infinite mirrors—used for communication, interrogation, and introspection
- **Contract Vault**: Lower-level vault rumored to contain original copies of every soulbound contract ever signed
- **Executive Garden**: An indoor arboretum containing extinct flora from multiple planes—always wind-blown
- **The Emerald Eye**: A scrying platform at the tower’s peak used to “survey global interests”

## History
**Founded:** Circa 1386 DR, shortly before the public launch of Acquisitions Incorporated  
**Construction Method:** Unknown. City mages claim there were no visible builders. It appeared one morning.

- Legally considered a “detached arcane enclave,” recognized by Calimport’s merchant elite but avoided by local clergy
- Has diplomatic immunity granted by a long-lost Calimshite treaty invoked by Mr. Diaz himself

## Secrets & Speculation
- The tower **never casts a shadow in the same place twice**
- No birds or insects will land on it
- Some believe the top floors do not exist *in this plane*
- It's said that every executive within has signed a contract in a language they no longer remember
- Old maps of the city from before its appearance show a faint silhouette in the same spot...

## Access & Defenses
- **Teleportation is restricted** to those marked by Mr. Diaz personally
- **Wards vs. divination, teleportation, undead, and godsight** are in constant rotation
- Intrusion triggers "Executive Response Protocol"—rumored to include a planar reset contingency

## Rumors & Legends
- A spiral staircase at the top leads directly to *Diaz’s true office*, where time is frozen during negotiation
- The winds that surround it are alive—listening, remembering, speaking in dreams
- Destroying the tower would unravel all current Acquisitions Incorporated contracts at once—but would also collapse local planar fabric
