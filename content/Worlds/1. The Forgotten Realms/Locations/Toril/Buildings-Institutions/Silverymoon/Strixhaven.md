## Campus Notes
- Students are divided among five Colleges, each with their own **philosophy of magic**.
- The entrance to the university is located in **Northbank** [[Silverymoon]], but is only accessible via **converging planar portals** which grants access to campus' location in the Demi-Plane of [[Arcavios]].
- Known for its **spell dueling**, **extraplanar scholarship**, and the **Biblioplex**—Strixhaven’s grand library.

## Background
- Strong ties to the [[Spellguard]]; called upon when the campus is in danger. 
- Accredited by the [[Accreditation Board for Magical Institutions]].
