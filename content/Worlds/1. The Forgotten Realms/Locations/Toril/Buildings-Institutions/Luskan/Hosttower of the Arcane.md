# **Hosttower of the Arcane**
![[Hosttower of the Arcane.png|500]]
[[Hosttower of the Arcane.png|Show]]
> *A twisted spire of petrified magic rising from Luskan's skyline—part fortress, part mystery, all danger.*

### 📍 *Location:* [[Luskan]], [[Sword Coast]]  
### 🏛 *Allegiance:* [[Arcane Brotherhood]]  
### 🌩️ *Reputation:* Fearsome nexus of arcane power and dark intrigue  

## 🧠 **Quick Lore**
Once built during the **Illusk Empire**, the Hosttower lay dormant for centuries before the **Arcane Brotherhood** revived it. Now it serves as a hub of magical research, political manipulation, and forbidden secrets. Whispers claim its roots reach other planes... and other times.

## 🧭 **Tower at a Glance**

| **Region**            | **Function**                                                                 |
|------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 🌀 **Central Spire**     | Residence of the Archmage; the tower’s beating heart of power.              |
| 🔬 **Lesser Spires**     | Laboratories, apprentices’ quarters, and personal research chambers.        |
| 📚 **Lower Levels**      | Libraries, common halls, meeting chambers, and arcane archives.             |
| 💀 **Catacombs**         | Sealed levels harboring failed experiments, ancient relics, and planar gates.|

## 🔮 **Key Features & Hazards**

- **Arcane Auras**: The tower distorts weather and time near it; wild magic surges are not uncommon.
- **Living Architecture**: Some rooms shift positions or change size based on the moon phase or magical events.
- **Wards & Defenses**: Intruders face sentient traps, shifting mazes, and planar guardians.
- **Interdimensional Rooms**: Libraries and vaults partly exist in other planes, accessible only by coded rituals.

## 🗺️ **Points of Intrigue**

- 🧾 **The Grand Library**  
  Holds self-updating grimoires, sentient scrolls, and sealed books that scream when opened.
  
- 🕯️ **The Summoning Chamber**  
  Heavily warded. Used for contacting planar entities—often with bloody consequences.

- 🔭 **Astral Observatory**  
  Allows scrying into other worlds; its lenses made of crystallized dreams.

- 🚪 **Forbidden Vaults**  
  Locked chambers buried in subspace, housing artifacts deemed too dangerous to destroy.

## 🧙 **Figures of Power**

- **Archmage Greeth** *(Historical)*  
  A necromancer of terrifying intellect; his death fractured the Brotherhood's leadership.

- **Current Leadership** *(DM's Choice)*  
  The Brotherhood is prone to infighting, coup attempts, and secret alliances.

- **Notables & Visitors**  
  Many ambitious wizards, treasure hunters, or cursed seekers arrive—and not all leave.

## 🎭 **Thematic Hooks for Players**

- Retrieve or seal a **forbidden artifact** before it falls into rival hands.  
- Uncover **internal plots** within the Brotherhood’s ranks.  
- Navigate its **ever-changing layout** during a timed escape.  
- Broker a dangerous **pact with an extraplanar entity** trapped in the tower.  

> *“Knowledge has a price, and within these halls, that price is often paid in blood, sanity, or both.”*  
> — Inscription at the tower’s gate
