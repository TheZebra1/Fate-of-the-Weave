## Overview
The **Academy of Shapers and Binders** stands as a monument to the Red Wizards’ mastery of arcane manipulation. Nestled within the burning slopes of Thaymount, this fortress-academy serves both as a place of education and indoctrination, where students are shaped as surely as the materials they transmute. Power, control, and evolution define the curriculum—and only the ambitious survive.

## Student Life
- **Cohorts:** Students are organized into Houses aligned with arcane specialties—each constantly vying for rank and favor.
- **Trials:** Advancement is earned through arcane duels, transmutative breakthroughs, or successful bindings—sometimes with mortal risk.
- **Privileges:** Higher-ranked students are granted access to the **Forbidden Vaults**, where ancient grimoires, spirits, and relics are stored.

## Forbidden Projects
- **The Akachi Vault:** A planar prison created in secret during the first Red Wizard revolt.
- **Essence Grafting Chambers:** Where soul essence is fused into constructs and students alike.
- **Chrono-Refraction Mirrors:** Allegedly allow glimpses of alternate timelines… or possible futures.
