**A storied realm of honor and tradition, Cormyr stands as a bastion of stability and martial excellence in Faerûn.**
**Social Hubs:** [[Suzail]], [[Marsember]], [[Arabel]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **Queen [[Raedra Obarskyr]]:** The reigning monarch, known for her wisdom and valor, especially during the defense against the Shadovar.
- **[[Knight Dragon's Cohort]]:** An elite unit formed under the guidance of the Knight Dragon, [[Vaerithar]], exemplifying the pinnacle of martial prowess.

#### Key Summary
- Army Size: 12,000 Purple Dragons + 500 war wizards all under the command of General [[Vaerithar]].
- **Government:** A constitutional monarchy led by Queen Raedra Obarskyr, supported by the Royal Court and various noble houses.
	- Monarch & Noble power limited by the [[Suzail Writ]].
- **Security:** Maintained by the renowned Purple Dragon Knights, the arcane War Wizards, and the newly established Knight Dragon's Cohort.
- **Underworld:** While generally stable, noble intrigues and external threats occasionally challenge the kingdom's peace.
- **Altars:** Numerous temples dedicated to major deities, reflecting the kingdom's diverse religious practices.
- **Emotion:** **Pride** – A nation steeped in tradition and honor, continually striving for excellence.
- **Resources:** Rich in natural resources, skilled artisans, and a strong military tradition.

### History:
- **1492 DR:** An adult purple-scaled Knight Dragon arrives in Suzail, offering its services to the crown. Recognizing the potential, Queen Raedra appoints it as the Grand War General of Cormyr.
- **1492–1502 DR:** Under the Knight Dragon's rigorous training and reforms, the Purple Dragon Knights undergo a transformation, becoming one of the most elite military forces in Faerûn.
- **1502 DR:** The Knight Dragon's Cohort is established, comprising the most skilled warriors trained directly under the dragon's tutelage.
