**A fragrant region of sandy hills, rich with frankincense trees and prowled by majestic lions.**
**Social Hubs:** [[Elah'zad]], [[Well of the Cloven Rock]]

#### Notable Locations:
- **[[Elah'zad]]:** A secluded oasis considered holy by the Bedine people.
- **[[Well of the Cloven Rock]]:** A large and well-known oasis, home to prides of lions.

#### Key Summary
- **Geography:** Located in the northern region of The Sword in Anauroch, just north of the Wall of Fallen Djinn mountain range. 
- **Society:** Inhabited by nomadic Bedine tribes who utilize the region's resources. 
- **Religion:** The Bedine worship various deities, with Elah'zad serving as a significant holy site. 
- **Emotion:** **Aromatic** – The pervasive scent of frankincense and the presence of majestic lions create a unique and memorable atmosphere.
- **Resources:** Abundant frankincense trees provide valuable resin; underground springs support oases vital for survival.

### Currency
Barter System – Predominantly used by nomadic tribes for goods and services

### History:
- **Cultural Significance:** The Hills of Scent have long been traversed by Bedine nomads, who revere the region's oases and utilize its natural resources. 
- **Natural Features:** Believed to house underground springs, contributing to the formation of vital oases like Elah'zad and the Well of the Cloven Rock. 
- **Wildlife:** The region's hills are commonly prowled by lions, adding to the area's mystique and danger.
