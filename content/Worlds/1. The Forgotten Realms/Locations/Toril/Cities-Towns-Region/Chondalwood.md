**Chondalwood** is a large forest located in East of the [[Plaguewrought Land]], It is also known as **Satyrwood**. The ruins of [[Elbulder]] is to the south of the forest

## Chondalwood

**Location**: South Faerûn, bordered by Chondath and Tethyr.

**Inhabitants**:
- **Native Creatures**: Satyrs, centaurs, treants, elves, and various fey creatures. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
- **Humblewood Creatures**: Birdfolk (e.g., corvids), humblefolk (e.g., raccoons, foxes).

**Theme**: A dense, mysterious forest with a strong isolationist culture, harboring ancient secrets and powerful natural magic.

**Struggles**: The Chondalwood has been the site of conflicts between its native inhabitants and encroaching human civilizations. The presence of groups like the Eldreth Veluuthra, who oppose human expansion, has led to a deep-seated mistrust of outsiders among the forest's denizens.
