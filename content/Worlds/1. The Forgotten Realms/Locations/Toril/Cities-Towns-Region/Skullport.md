**A festering sore beneath Waterdeep, Skullport thrives in darkness, where lawlessness and shadowy dealings are the norm.**
**Social Hubs:** [[Crookspar Parlor]], [[High Tide Alehouse]], [[Nightshade's Caress]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[Crookspar Parlor]]:** A two-story gambling den located in the Crown, known for high-stakes games and a neutral ground among factions.
- **[[High Tide Alehouse]]:** The largest alehouse in Skullport, offering exclusive brews and serving as a hub for information exchange.
- **[[Nightshade's Caress]]:** An apothecary run by the mummy Nightshade, dealing in rare and often illicit potions and herbs.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Governed by the Skulls of Skullport, undead remnants of Netherese mages who enforce order with capricious and often brutal judgments.
- **Security:** Maintained through a combination of the Skulls' oversight, gang enforcement, and magical defenses.
- **Underworld:** The entire city operates as an underworld haven, with various factions vying for power and control over illicit trades.
- **Altars:** While not prominent, some worship of deities like Eilistraee exists among certain inhabitants.
- **Emotion:** **Dread** – A pervasive sense of unease and danger permeates the city, where trust is scarce and betrayal is common.
- **Resources:** A hub for the black market, offering goods and services banned or frowned upon in surface societies.

### Currency

[[Duskgold]] – Tarnished gold coins accepted by most merchants (Gold)  
[[Sargauth Silver]] – Silver coins minted with the visage of a skull (Silver)

Barter System – Commonly used, with trades involving goods, services, or information

### History:
- **Origins:** Established on the ruins of a Netherese settlement, Skullport evolved into a haven for outcasts and criminals beneath Waterdeep.
- **Governance:** Initially overseen by the Skulls, the city later saw increased influence from gangs and criminal organizations, notably the Xanathar Thieves' Guild.
- **Notable Events:** Skullport has endured various calamities, including internal conflicts and external threats, yet continues to persist as a den of iniquity.
- **Current Status:** Remains a critical node in the network of the Underdark, facilitating trade and interaction between surface and subterranean realms.
