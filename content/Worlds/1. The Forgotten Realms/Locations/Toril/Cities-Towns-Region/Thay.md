### Thayan Anthem
>*Zháluvé'd strýks, vaír pówër drózh,*  
*In t'vyr's hârt, dárknëss dózh.*  
*Szass Tam.*  
#### Translation
>*In shadowed streets, our power rises,  
In the city's heart, darkness reigns.*
*Szass Tam*
