# Sehanine Moonbow
[[elven-sehanine-moonbow-scag|Sehanine Moonbow]]
## Quick Summary
**Tagline / Descriptor:** Elven goddess of the moon, dreams, and death, guiding elves through life's transitions.  
**Social Hubs:** [[Temple of Sehanine Moonbow]], [[Crescent Court]], [[Moonlit Glade]]  
**Affiliations:** [[Seldarine]], [[Elven Pantheon]], [[Followers of the Moonbow]]

## Personality & Traits
- **Temperament:** Serene and contemplative
- **Mannerisms:** Speaks in soft, melodic tones; often appears veiled in shimmering moonlight
- **Values:** Mystery, introspection, transcendence, the natural cycle of life and death
- **Fears:** Disruption of natural cycles, loss of elven heritage
- **Desires:** To guide elves toward spiritual enlightenment and a peaceful passing
- **Quirks:** Often communicates through dreams and visions; rarely appears in physical form

## Narrative Profile
### Origin & Background
- **Heritage:** Core member of the Seldarine, either as daughter or consort of Corellon Larethian
- **Upbringing:** Embodiment of the moon's mysteries, guiding elves in matters of life, death, and dreams
- **Training/Skills:** Divine abilities in illusion, divination, and guiding souls

### Major Life Events
- **Turning Point:** Played a crucial role in the creation of the elven race, with legends stating elves originated from a mixture of Corellon's blood and Sehanine's tears
- Became the [[Feywild]] aspect of [[Selûne]] for a time close the period of the [[Spellplague]].
- **Current Status:** Continues to guide elves through dreams, protect their souls, and oversee their journey beyond life
- **Hidden Truths:** As an aspect of Angharradh, she embodies the unity of the elven pantheon

## Abilities
| Power / Trait | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| Moonlit Veil | Cloaks areas in protective illusions, shielding from threats |
| Dreamwalking | Communicates and guides through dreams and visions |
| Lunar Blessing | Empowers followers with enhanced abilities under moonlight |
| Soul Guide | Escorts elven souls to their afterlife, ensuring peaceful passage |

## Relationships
### Allies
- **[[Corellon Larethian]]** – Consort and leader of the Seldarine; together, they nurture and protect the elven race
- **[[Angharradh]]** – Triune goddess formed alongside Aerdrie Faenya and Hanali Celanil; represents the unity and strength of the elven deities

### Enemies
- **[[Lolth]]** – Former member of the Seldarine turned demon queen; represents chaos and corruption opposed to Sehanine's ideals
- **[[Vhaeraun]]** – Drow deity of thievery and darkness; harbors enmity toward Sehanine for her role in elven salvation

### Key Connections
- **[[Eilistraee]]** – Adopted daughter; their relationship is strained due to differing paths but retains a distant respect

## Hooks & Secrets
- **Rumors:** Whispers tell of hidden temples where Sehanine's followers can glimpse visions of the future
- **Secrets:** Some believe Sehanine holds knowledge of the elven race's ultimate destiny, a truth yet unrevealed
- **Adventure Hook:** Players might seek Sehanine's guidance to uncover hidden truths in their dreams or to protect a sacred elven site under threat

## Quotes
> "_Embrace the night, for in its shadowed embrace, truths are unveiled._"

> "_Fear not the end, for it is but a doorway to new beginnings._"
