**A mist-shrouded region of rolling hills, rich in elven history and ancient tombs.**
**Social Hubs:** [[Evereska]], [[Greyhome]]

#### Notable Locations:
- **[[Greyhome]]:** An elven outpost serving as a training ground and settlement.
- **Ancient Tombs:** Burial sites from the Fallen Kingdom era, scattered throughout the hills.

#### Key Summary
- **Geography:** The Greycloak Hills are located north of [[Evereska]], marking the western boundary of the [[Anauroch Desert]]. The terrain consists of rolling hills adorned with grass, scrub, and occasional small tree groves.
- **Society:** Primarily inhabited and patrolled by elves from Evereska, with settlements like Greyhome serving as training grounds and residences.
- **Religion:** The elves maintain reverence for deities such as Corellon Larethian, Sehanine Moonbow, and Rillifane Rallathil, with shrines and temples present in settlements.
- **Emotion:** **Mystique** – The ever-present mists and ancient tombs evoke a sense of mystery and reverence.
- **Resources:** Known for the crafting of exquisite musical instruments and elven artifacts, though trade is minimal due to the secluded nature of the region.

### Currency
[[Elven Gold Leafs]] – High-value currency used among elven communities (Platinum)  
[[Silver Petals]] – Standard currency for everyday transactions (Gold)  
[[Copper Vines]] – Low-denomination coins for minor purchases (Copper)  
Barter System – Commonly employed, emphasizing goods and services over currency

### History:
- **Tomb Hills Era:** Originally known as the Tomb Hills, the area was dotted with burial sites from the Fallen Kingdom, encompassing both human and elven graves.
- **Elven Settlement:** Moon elves from Evereska settled the region, establishing outposts and shrouding the hills in magical mists for concealment and protection.
- **Annexation:** Officially annexed by Evereska in 1335 DR with the presentation of the Evereska Charter, solidifying elven control over the area.
- **Current Status:** The Greycloak Hills remain under the vigilant protection of Evereska's forces, serving as a buffer against potential threats from the Anauroch Desert and surrounding regions.
