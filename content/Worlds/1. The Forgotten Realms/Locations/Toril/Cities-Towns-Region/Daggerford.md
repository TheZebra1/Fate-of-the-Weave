**A small but ambitious town striving to rival larger cities like [[Waterdeep]].**
**Social Hubs:** [[Lady Luck's Tavern]], [[River Shining Tavern]], [[Happy Cow Tavern]], [[Derval's Smithy]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[Daggerford Castle]]:** The ducal castle overseeing the town.
- **[[Morninglow Tower]]:** Temple dedicated to [[Lathander]], prominent on the town's hill.
- **[[Table of the Sword]]:** Shrine dedicated to [[Tempus]], located near the barracks.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Ruled by the hereditary Duke of Daggerford, currently Duke [[Pwyll Greatshout Daggerford]].
- **Security:** Maintained by the [[Daggerford Militia]], with additional protection from the town's walls and strategic location.
- **Underworld:** Minimal; the town maintains a relatively peaceful environment with low criminal activity.
- **Altars:** Temples and shrines dedicated to [[Lathander]], [[Tymora]], and [[Tempus]] serve the spiritual needs of the populace.
- **Emotion:** **Aspiration** – The town harbors ambitions to grow and be recognized as a significant trade hub.
- **Resources:** Fertile lands supporting agriculture, proximity to the [[Delimbiyr River]] facilitating trade, and skilled artisans like [[Derval Ironeater]] contributing to local industry.

### [[Currency]]
[[Gold Coins]] – Standard currency for significant transactions  
[[Silver Coins]] – Common currency for everyday trade  
[[Copper Coins]] – Used for minor purchases  
Barter System – Occasionally employed in local markets, especially for agricultural goods

### History:
- **Founding:** Established as a farming community, Daggerford grew due to its strategic location along the Trade Way and the [[Delimbiyr River]].
- **Ambitions:** The town has long aspired to be seen as an alternative to larger northern neighbors like [[Waterdeep]].
- **Governance:** The ducal family has historically claimed lands extending to areas such as the [[Ambergul Estate]], [[Cromm's Hold]], [[Floshin Estate]] and [[Dragonspear Castle]].
- **Recent Events:** Featured in various adventures, including the module "Under Illefarn," highlighting its role in regional defense and intrigue.
