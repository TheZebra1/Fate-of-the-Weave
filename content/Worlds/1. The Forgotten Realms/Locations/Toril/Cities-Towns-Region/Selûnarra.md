**A bastion of enlightenment and arcane study under the patronage of [[Selûne]].**
**Social Hubs:** [[The Moonlit Library]], [[Hall of Silver Stars]], [[Selûne's Sanctuary]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[The Moonlit Library]]:** Repository of extensive arcane texts and celestial studies.
- **[[Hall of Silver Stars]]:** Center for astronomical observations and Selûnite ceremonies.
- **[[Selûne's Sanctuary]]:** Main temple dedicated to [[Selûne]], serving as a spiritual hub.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Governed by a council of arcanists devoted to the pursuit of knowledge and the worship of [[Selûne]].
- **Security:** Protected by powerful arcane wards and mythallar-powered defenses.
- **Underworld:** Minimal; the enclave maintained a focus on scholarly pursuits over clandestine activities.
- **Altars:** Central temple dedicated to [[Selûne]], reflecting the city's devotion to the Moonmaiden.
- **Emotion:** **Serenity** – A tranquil atmosphere fostering contemplation and scholarly endeavors.
- **Resources:** Renowned for its vast collection of arcane knowledge and magical artifacts.

### [[Currency]]
[[Moon Discs]] – Silver coins embossed with Selûne's symbol (Platinum)  
[[Star Shards]] – Crystalline tokens used in scholarly transactions (Gold)  
[[Silver Slivers]] – Thin silver strips for minor exchanges (Silver)  
[[Lunar Bits]] – Small copper pieces featuring lunar phases (Copper)  
Barter System – Occasionally used for rare tomes and unique components

### History:
- **Founding:** Established as ***Opus***, a floating enclave of [[Netheril]], dedicated to arcane research and the veneration of [[Selûne]].
- **Cultural Focus:** Unlike other Netherese enclaves, prioritized the creation of universities and libraries over personal power accumulation.
- **Exodus:** In the Year of Sundered Webs, −339 DR, the populace was transported to the [[Gates of the Moon]] by the grace of [[Selûne]], escaping the fall of Netheril.
- **Legacy:** Remains a symbol of the harmonious pursuit of knowledge and faith, under the guidance of the Moonmaiden.
