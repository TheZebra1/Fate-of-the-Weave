**A serene and luminous celestial realm governed by the cycles of the moon.**
**Social Hubs:** [[Argentil]], [[Court of Stars]], [[Infinite Staircase]]
***See [[Selûne’s Eye]]***
#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[Argentil]]:** The silvered hall and divine seat of [[Selûne]].
- **[[Brightwater]]:** A realm within the Gates of the Moon dedicated to [[Sune]], radiating love and artistic joy.
- **[[Great Wheel]]:** A swirling constellation of earthmotes devoted to [[Tymora]]'s influence.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Ruled by the moon goddess [[Selûne]], with realms for allied deities like [[Sune]], [[Sharess]], and [[Lliira]].
- **Security:** Guarded by divine entities such as the [[Shards]], [[Lillends]], and [[Eladrin Court]].
- **Underworld:** None—this realm embodies divine peace and harmonious coexistence.
- **Altars:** Major temples include [[Argentil]], as well as sites devoted to joy, fortune, and love.
- **Emotion:** **Tranquility** – The realm’s nature shifts with the moon, evoking serenity and dreamlike wonder.
- **Resources:** A center for divine insight, dream magic, and astral navigation—often accessed via the [[Infinite Staircase]].

### [[Currency]]
[[Divine Favor]] – Platinum-level blessings granted directly by deities  
[[Moonlit Pearls]] – Glowing pearls imbued with Selûne’s magic (Gold)  
[[Starfire Gems]] – Crystalline motes of celestial energy (Silver)  
[[Silver Crescents]] – Basic lunar tokens used by mortals (Copper)  
Barter System – Acts of worship, devotion, or beauty often replace coin

### History:
- **Celestial Significance:** The Gates of the Moon have served as the divine realm of [[Selûne]] since the earliest times.
- **Divine Refuge:** After the Spellplague, deities like [[Sune]], [[Sharess]], and [[Lliira]] relocated here from fallen realms.
- **Planar Nexus:** The realm is closely linked to the [[Infinite Staircase]], allowing travelers of great destiny to reach it through dream, faith, or magic.
