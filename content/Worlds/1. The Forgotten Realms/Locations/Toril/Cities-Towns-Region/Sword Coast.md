**A storied and vibrant stretch of coast famed for its bold cities, rugged wilderness, and frontier spirit.**
**Social Hubs:** [[Waterdeep]], [[Baldur’s Gate]], [[Neverwinter]], [[Daggerford]]
>1300 miles from top to bottom
#### Notable Features:
- **Coastal Clifflands:** White cliffs stretch from the Dessarin River to Baldur’s Gate, giving the region its name.
- **Community Confederation:** The Lords’ Alliance unites the major city-states for mutual defense and commerce.
- **Frontier Wilds:** Dense forests, craggy hills, and mountainous terrain conceal monsters and lost ruins.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** City-states self-govern but coordinate through the Lords’ Alliance and trade groups.
- **Security:** Maintained by city militias, Flaming Fist in Baldur’s Gate, and cooperative defense pacts.
- **Underworld:** Thrives in coastal hubs like Baldur’s Gate and Neverwinter, with factions like the Zhentarim.
- **Altars:** Widespread devotion to deities like Chauntea, Tymora, Helm, Tyr, Lathander, Selûne.
- **Emotion:** **Adventurous** – A land of opportunity, mystery, and exploration, luring travelers to its frontier.
- **Resources:** Rich in timber, gems, leather, arcane lore, and skilled warriors/mercenaries.

### History:
- **Tens of thousands of years ago:** Illefarn, an ancient elven kingdom, dominates the northern reaches.
- **14th Century DR:** The First Dragonspear War, Flaming Fist formed, tieflings rise along the coast.
- **1368 DR:** The Iron Crisis sees bandit sabotage of the iron trade, ending near Dragonspear Castle.
- **1492 DR:** A saprophytic plague spreads through the region; adventurers track its source to the Mere of Dead Men.
