# Cormanthyr (Ruins)
## Overview
Cormanthyr, the ancient elven empire nestled within the [[Cormanthor Forest]], is no more. Once a beacon of magical and cultural achievement, its civilization was shattered by the fall of Myth Drannor in 1487 DR. The city and its mythals lie in ruins, and no centralized government or society remains to unify the forest's scattered inhabitants.

## Current Condition
The Cormanthor Forest is now a wild and dangerous place. Though it was once a haven for elves, it is now sparsely populated and largely abandoned. Key inhabitants include:

- **Drow factions**: Some drow houses exploit the ruins for their own ends.
- **Orcs and goblinoids**: Roving bands occupy parts of the forest.
- **Adventurers**: Treasure seekers and historians brave the dangers for glory and knowledge.

The ruins of Myth Drannor, at the forest's heart, are a haunting reminder of Cormanthyr’s golden age. Magical anomalies persist, remnants of its shattered mythals, making the area unpredictable and hazardous.

## Background
Cormanthyr serves as a poignant symbol of elven heritage. Despite its fall, the memory of its achievements lives on in the hearts of elves across Faerûn. The forest and its ruins remain a place of mystery, danger, and opportunity for those willing to explore its depths.
