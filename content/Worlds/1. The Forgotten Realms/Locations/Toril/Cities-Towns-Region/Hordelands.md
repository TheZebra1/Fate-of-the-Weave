#### Notable Locations and Persons:
- **[[Golden Khanate]]:** Loose confederation of Tuigan warlords.
- **[[Raumviran Ruins]]:** Remnants of an ancient, forgotten empire.
- **[[Flaming Steppe]]:** A volcanic wasteland feared by travelers.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** No central authority; ruled by shifting **Khans and Warlords**.
- **Security:** Tribal warriors, mounted raiders, and supernatural entities roam the land.
- **Underworld:** Scattered; mostly warband-driven crime and territorial disputes.
- **Altars:** [[Tempos]], [[Kossuth]], [[Selûne]], [[The Skyfather]].
- **Resources:** Known for fast warhorses, hardy livestock, and rare spices.

### History:
- Once home to the **Raumathari Empire**, now largely lost to history.
- The **Tuigan Horde**, led by [[Yamun Khahan]], nearly conquered Faerûn in the past.
- Constant conflicts between nomadic tribes, settled peoples, and encroaching factions.
- The Zhentarim seek to establish trade outposts, often clashing with local warlords.
- Known for **vast open steppes**, **ferocious warriors**, and **mystic shamanic traditions**.
