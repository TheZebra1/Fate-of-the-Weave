**A bustling trade city on the Dragon Coast, Teziir thrives amidst commerce and clandestine dealings.**
**Social Hubs:** [[The Gilded Net Inn]], [[Market Square]], [[Temple of Helm]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[The Gilded Net Inn]]:** A renowned establishment offering lodging and information for travelers and merchants.
- **[[Market Square]]:** The heart of Teziir's commerce, where goods from across Faerûn are traded.
- **[[Temple of Helm]]:** The largest religious structure in the city, serving as a center for worship and community gatherings.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** An oligarchy led by the Council of Teziir, composed of influential merchants who prioritize economic prosperity.
- **Security:** Maintained by the Watch of Teziir, a 500-strong guard force, and supported by the mage Azala.
- **Underworld:** The Astorians, a prominent thieves' guild, operate within the city, with known infiltration by Westgate's Night Masks.
- **Altars:** Multiple temples dedicated to major deities, reflecting the city's diverse religious practices.
- **Emotion:** **Ambitious** – Teziir's drive to surpass neighboring trade hubs fuels its growth and complexity.
- **Resources:** A key port facilitating trade between the Sword Coast and the Inner Sea, with a diverse population and thriving markets.

### Currency
Standard Faerûnian currency is accepted; barter is common in local markets.

### History:
- **1312 DR:** Founded by merchants seeking independence from Westgate's corruption.
- **1372 DR:** Population recorded at 10,944; city continues to grow as a trade center.
- **1490s DR:** Estimated population surpasses 40,000, with significant Shou community presence.
- **Recent Developments:** Teziir becomes a protectorate of Cormyr, enhancing its political stability and trade opportunities.
