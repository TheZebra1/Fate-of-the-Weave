# *Ostoria – The Lost Empire of Giants*  
Ostoria was the **greatest empire of the giants**, a vast dominion that once covered much of **northern [[Faerûn]]**. It was a land of **colossal cities, mighty strongholds, and powerful rune magic**, ruled by the greatest of Annam’s children. But the empire fell long ago, shattered by war and time, leaving only **scattered ruins buried beneath ice and forgotten history**.  

## **The Age of Ostoria**  
- **Founded by [[Annam]]’s children**, Ostoria was the **seat of giant supremacy**.  
- Each **type of giant** held dominion over different regions, bound together by the **[[Ordning]]**.  
- Their cities were built with **stormforged stone, rune magic, and elemental power**.  
- The empire thrived for **thousands of years**, rivaling the dragons for dominance.  

## **The War of Dragons and Giants**  
- Ostoria waged **endless war against the dragons**, a conflict known as the **[[Throne Wars]]**.  
- Though mighty, the giants could not withstand the **relentless assaults of dragonkind**.  
- The war culminated in **Ostoria’s downfall**, as its greatest strongholds were **burned, broken, or lost beneath the ice**.  

## **The Fall & Abandonment**  
- With the empire shattered, **giants fractured**, retreating into isolated strongholds.  
- Annam saw his children **fail to rebuild**, and when they rejected **his son, [[Hartkiller]]**, he **withdrew from them completely**.  
- Without divine guidance, **giants lost their unity**, and Ostoria became a **myth of the past**.  

## **The Frozen Ruins of Ostoria**  
- Ostoria’s **once-grand capital lies buried beneath the ice**, untouched for millennia.  
- **Ancient relics, lost knowledge, and powerful rune magic** remain hidden beneath the frozen tomb.  
- At its heart rests the **Throne of the First Storm**, where the last chance for **giantkind’s renewal** still waits.
