**A secluded elven city nestled within a verdant valley, Evereska stands as a bastion of elven culture and history.**
**Social Hubs:** Cloudcrown Palace, Hall of the High Hunt, College of Magic and Arms

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **Cloudcrown Palace:** The residence of the Hill Elders and the political center of Evereska.
- **Hall of the High Hunt:** The seat of power for the Hill Elders, where council meetings and important ceremonies are held.
- **College of Magic and Arms:** An esteemed institution dedicated to the study and practice of magic and martial prowess.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Governed by the Hill Elders, a council of senior elves who oversee the city's affairs.
- **Security:** Protected by elite forces such as the Greycloaks and the Tomb Guard, and safeguarded by a powerful mythal.
- **Underworld:** Nonexistent; the city maintains strict vigilance against external threats.
- **Altars:** Temples dedicated to Corellon Larethian, Sehanine Moonbow, and Rillifane Rallathil are integral to the city's spiritual life.
- **Emotion:** **Reverence** – The city's ancient architecture and serene landscapes evoke deep respect and admiration.
- **Resources:** Renowned for exquisite art, fine wine, and superior elven crafts; imports necessary metals and magical components due to limited local resources.

### Currency
Elven Gold Leafs – High-value currency used among elven communities (Platinum)  
Silver Petals – Standard currency for everyday transactions (Gold)  
Copper Vines – Low-denomination coins for minor purchases (Copper)  
Barter System – Occasionally employed, emphasizing goods and services over currency

### History:
- **Founding:** Established in secret during the Founding Time (-8600 DR) as a haven for elves, particularly moon and sun elves.
- **The Retreat:** Served as a sanctuary for elves during the Retreat, preserving elven culture on the mainland.
- **Phaerimm Assault:** In 1371 DR, Evereska repelled an assault by the phaerimm, with aid from allies including forces from Waterdeep and the re-emerged city of Thultanthar.
- **Fey'ri Invasion:** In 1374 DR, the city withstood an attack by the fey'ri led by Sarya Dlardrageth, with assistance from Seiveril Miritar's crusade.
- **Spellplague Impact:** Post-1385 DR, the Spellplague caused the boundaries between Evereska and the Feywild to blur, affecting the city's planar stability.
- **Recent Events:** Continues to serve as a refuge for elves and a center of elven culture, maintaining its traditions and defenses amidst the changing landscape of Faerûn.
