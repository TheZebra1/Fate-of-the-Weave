# Melvaunt
## Overview
Melvaunt, the "***City of Swords***," is a bustling industrial city located on the northern coast of the Moonsea. Its economy thrives on weapon and armor production, bolstered by its many forges and skilled blacksmiths. There are no safety measures in place at any job site, no state mandated breaks, and the industrial districts run round the clock.

>*"I wouldn't say your 'city never sleeps' as much as I'd point out that your industrial districts have forced insomnia"* -
> ***Open Lord of Waterdeep to Lord Envoy of Melvaunt during a visit to the city.***

## History
Melvaunt has long been a hub of commerce and industry on the Moonsea. Its wealth and power have been shaped by its strategic location and the ambitions of its ruling families.

## Background
Known for its oppressive atmosphere, Melvaunt's citizens endure harsh working conditions in exchange for the city's relative prosperity. Despite this, the city remains a key economic hub in the Moonsea region.
- The working class who function as the engine of Melvaunt's prosperity live in a state of perpetual poverty with a standard 7 hour shift barely allowing for a meager living.
	- *Melvaunt has no fair labor laws nor any government enforced labor safety acts meaning some have chosen to risk and sacrifice health, safety, and sanity taking on double, and even triple shifts working extreme amounts of overtime in dangerous work environments in an effort to claw their way into a more comfortable living condition for their families.*
		- Of note there is one public service program nearly all of the working class takes advantage of known as ***"The Little Gears Act"***
			- From the ages of 6 months - 5 years old children are rounded up and taken to ***Gearhouses*** where they are taken care of while they're parents work, the program is a free service funded by the Government.
				- Fom 5 -14 years old children are eligible for ***Gearshops*** where they begin child labor in much safer work environments than their parents. 
					- At 15 Melvauntians are considered of working adult age.

<small> Of course financially stable households can afford to send their children to trade schools where children are properly educated with skills and may earn scholarships to a University or a Clerical fellowship at the Church of Gond.
