**An ancient elven necropolis, now overgrown and haunted by the remnants of its past.**
**Social Hubs:** None (Ruins)

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **Elven Mausoleums:** Once honored the fallen elven heroes.
- **Decayed Mythal:** The protective magic now corrupts the surrounding area.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Originally governed by Elven High Mages; now abandoned.
- **Security:** Previously protected by a mythal; currently inhabited by undead and dangerous creatures.
- **Underworld:** Presence of undead, including a lich and will-o'-wisps.
- **Altars:** Ruined temples dedicated to elven deities.
- **Emotion:** **Eerie** – The atmosphere is heavy with the remnants of lost glory and pervasive corruption.
- **Resources:** Potential for ancient elven artifacts, though retrieval is perilous.

### [[Currency]]
None – The city is abandoned and holds no active economy.

### History:
- **Establishment:** Built as a tomb city for elven heroes who fell in battles against giants and other formidable foes.
- **Mythal Creation:** Enchanted with a mythal to protect the spirits of the interred and prevent undead manifestations.
- **Decline:** Over centuries, the mythal decayed, leading to unintended consequences such as animating the dead and corrupting the forest.
- **Current State:** Now overgrown and inhabited by undead entities, including a lich and will-o'-wisps, making it a perilous location for explorers.
