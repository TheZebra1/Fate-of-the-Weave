**Abeir, the twin world of Toril, is a realm where primordials and dragons hold sway, marked by elemental chaos and draconic empires.**
**Social Hubs:** [[Shyr]], [[Laerakond]]

### Magic Differences
The influence of the God's on Abeir was minimal as this realm was left to the [[Primordials]]. Because of this [[Mystra]] & her [[Weave]] have a near non-existence role in this planets existence and function. Instead all the effects one might consider to be "***magic***" stem from elemental origins derived from the primoridals.
- Primogens are the equivalent of the "wizards" and "spellcasters" of Toril.
#### Primogens
- Derive power from primoridal energy and can master an element of the world.
- The amount of elemental powers a primogen can wield is not well understand and the only way to know what level they can reach is for them to reach it, but the impact of natural potential talent plays a large role in their power and breeding strong lines of Primogens is a constant fascination of nobility on Abeir.
	- Primogen I's are considered to have mastered one element.
	- Primogen II's are considered to have mastered 3 elements.
	- Primogen Primes can seemingly master infinite elements over their life span and are identified after mastering 5 elements. ***(Extremely rare, 1 in a million***)

### Notable Locations:
- **[[Shyr]]:** A continent dominated by dragon overlords and their dragonborn servitors.
- **[[Laerakond]]:** Known as Returned Abeir when it appeared on Toril during the Spellplague; a land of varied terrains and draconic rulers.

### Key Summary
- **Geography:** Abeir shares its orbit with Toril, including the moon [[Selûne]] and the [[Tears of Selûne]]. Its landscapes are shaped by elemental forces and draconic dominion.
- **Society:** Governed by powerful primordials and dragons, with societies of dragonborn and genasi. Humans exist in servitude or isolated communities.
- **Religion:** Traditional deities are less influential; reverence is directed towards primordials and elemental beings.
- **Emotion:** **Awe** – The omnipresence of colossal dragons and elemental forces instills a sense of insignificance and wonder.
- **Resources:** Rich in elemental materials and ancient draconic knowledge, though access is tightly controlled by ruling entities.

### Currency
[[Draconic Talons]] – High-value currency minted by dragon overlords (Platinum)  
[[Elemental Crystals]] – Common medium of exchange, valued for their innate power (Gold)  
[[Obsidian Shards]] – Used in everyday transactions among common folk (Silver)  
[[Bone Chits]] – Low-value tokens crafted from creature remains (Copper)  
Barter System – Predominant in rural areas and among nomadic tribes

### History:
- **[[Dawn War]] Aftermath:** Following the conflict between gods and primordials, Abeir was granted to the primordials as their dominion.
- **[[Spellplague]] Exchange:** Portions of Abeir and Toril swapped places, introducing regions like Laerakond to Toril and vice versa.
- **[[Second Sundering]]:** The worlds were re-separated, restoring Abeir and [[Toril]] to their distinct existences.
