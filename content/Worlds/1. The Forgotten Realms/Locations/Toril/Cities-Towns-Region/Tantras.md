**A city reborn from divine conflict, Tantras stands as a beacon of faith and resilience along the Dragon Reach.**
**Social Hubs:** [[The Roaring Lion Inn]], [[The Net of Stars]], [[The House of Twilight]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[Temple of Torm’s Coming]]:** Central temple dedicated to Torm, commemorating his avatar's sacrifice during the Time of Troubles.
- **[[The Roaring Lion Inn]]:** Renowned inn claiming to be "the best in town," offering luxurious accommodations.
- **[[The Net of Stars]]:** A highly regarded tavern known for its ambiance and clientele.
- **[[The House of Twilight]]:** Famous nightclub attracting a diverse crowd.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Governed by the High Council, comprising representatives from sixteen merchant families and the high priest of Torm, balancing commercial interests with religious guidance.
- **Security:** Maintained by a **900-strong** City Guard led by Lassalar Ormitar, with the capacity to raise a militia of up to 6,000 in times of need.
- **Underworld:** The Grayclaws operate as a local smuggling and thieving organization, targeting foreign entities and unscrupulous locals, while opposing groups like the [[Zhentarim]] and [[Cult of the Dragon]].
- **Altars:** Multiple temples serve the spiritual needs of the populace, reflecting the city's diverse religious landscape.
- **Emotion:** **Reverent** – The city's history and numerous temples foster a deep sense of spirituality and respect among its inhabitants.
- **Resources:** A hub for trade in metals, livestock, and textiles, with a strong shipbuilding industry and vibrant marketplaces.

### Currency
Standard Faerûnian currency is accepted; barter is common in local markets.

### History:
- **1358 DR:** During the [[Time of Troubles]], Tantras becomes the site of a climactic battle between the avatars of [[Torm]] and [[Bane]],[]resulting in significant destruction but also elevating Torm's status among the faithful.
- **Post-1358 DR:** The city undergoes reconstruction, with the Temple of Torm’s Coming established as a central place of worship and remembrance.
- **1372 DR:** Tantras thrives as a major port city in [[the Vast]], with a diverse population and a strong economy.
- **1490s DR:** The city continues to grow, maintaining its religious significance and commercial prosperity, while navigating the challenges of regional politics and external threats.
