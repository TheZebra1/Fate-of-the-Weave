Since 1385 DR, Dambrath has been a nation of nomadic human clans, primarily horse riders.

- They do not allow non-Dambrath horses into their lands for fear of interbreeding with their purebred horses which are the best in [[Faerûn]].
	- They kill other horses in their lands on sight and might even murder their riders.

Their obsession with becoming [[Lycanthropy Rules|Lycanthropes]] would catch the eye of the [[Blood Hunters]] and cause on ongoing war **since 1385** with neither side being able to strike each other a fatal blow in their home turf, but always fighting each other.
