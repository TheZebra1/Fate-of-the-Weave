**A vast and enigmatic desert, Anauroch holds the remnants of ancient empires and secrets buried beneath its sands.**
**Social Hubs:** Scattered oases and nomadic encampments

#### Notable Locations:
- **[[Black Road]]:** A trade route traversing the desert, historically controlled by the [[Zhentarim]].
- **[[High Ice]]:** A massive glacier in the northern reaches of [[Anauroch Desert]], home to various creatures and ancient secrets.
- [[Elah’zad]]
	- [[House of the Moon]]

#### Key Summary
- **Geography:** Anauroch spans a significant portion of northern Faerûn, featuring diverse terrains from sandy dunes to icy glaciers.
- **Society:** Inhabited by nomadic [[Bedine]] tribes, elusive D'tarig, and formidable creatures like blue dragons; the Underdark beneath is home to the enigmatic Phaerimm.
- **Religion:** The Bedine and other inhabitants follow various deities, with practices adapted to their harsh environment.
- **Emotion:** **Mystery** – The desert's shifting sands conceal ancient ruins, lost knowledge, and untold dangers.
- **Resources:** While largely barren, Anauroch's true wealth lies in its hidden artifacts and the remnants of bygone civilizations.

### Currency
Trade Goods – Barter is common among nomadic tribes  
[[Zhentarim]] Trade Bars – Standardized currency used along the Black Road  
Ancient Coins – Occasionally unearthed from ruins, valued by collectors  
Barter System – Predominant in areas lacking formal currency systems

### History:
- **Formation:** Once a fertile region, Anauroch was transformed into a desert due to the destructive magic of the [[The Karsite Weave. A History of Karsus and the Phaerimm|Phaerimm]] during the fall of [[Netheril]].
- **Netherese Legacy:** The desert is dotted with ruins of Netherese enclaves, including the notable return of Thultanthar, the City of Shade.
- **Zhentarim Influence:** The Black Road served as a critical trade route, with the Zhentarim establishing garrisons to control commerce.
- **Current Status:** Anauroch remains a land of intrigue and peril, attracting adventurers seeking lost treasures and scholars aiming to uncover its storied past.
