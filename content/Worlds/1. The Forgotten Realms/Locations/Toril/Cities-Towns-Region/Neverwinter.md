### History:
- Once known as the "Jewel of the North" due to its beauty and wealth.
- Nearly destroyed in the **Ruining** after the eruption of Mount Hotenow.
- Rebuilt under the leadership of [[Dagult Neverember]], though political tensions remain.
- Threats from former **Many-Arrows Orcs**, elemental cults, and internal factions.
- Home to **The Chasm**, a wound in the city from past magical disasters.
- Use [[Banishment Brand]]s
