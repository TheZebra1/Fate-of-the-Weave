#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[Night Palace]]:** Home of the Syl-Pasha, the absolute ruler of Calimport.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** [[Night Palace]]: Ruled by Syl-Pasha [[Ralan el Pesarkhal]].
- **Security:** Private armies of noble houses, city guards, and hired assassins.
- **Underworld:** [[Shadow Thieves]], [[Rundeen Trade Consortium]], [[Zhentarim]].
- **Altars:** [[Selûne]], [[Ilmater]], [[Tymora]], [[Beshaba]], [[Talos]].
- **Resources:** Major hub of trade for silk, spices, and rare gems; historically known for slavery.

### History:
- Founded as the capital of **Calimshan**, one of the oldest empires in Faerûn.
- Once ruled by **Genies and Djinn**, with remnants of their power still lingering.
- Known for its opulence, corruption, and strict social hierarchy.
- Infamous for the **Rundeen Trade Consortium**, which wields immense power over the economy.
- Slave trade abolished in modern times but still persists in the underground market.
- Constant power struggles between noble families, merchant lords, and criminal syndicates.
- Use [[Banishment Brand]]s
