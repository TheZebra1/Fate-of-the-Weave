**A secluded elven outpost nestled within the mist-shrouded Greycloak Hills, serving as both a settlement and a training ground for Evereska's defenders.**
**Social Hubs:** None (Secluded Outpost)

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **Lord [[Erlan Duirsar]]:** The elven leader of Greyhome, known as the Watcher Over the Hills.
- **Walker in the Mists:** A mist golem created by Lord Erlan Duirsar to defend the settlement.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Led by Lord Erlan Duirsar, Greyhome operates under a traditional elven lordship.
- **Security:** The settlement is protected by the Walker in the Mists, a powerful mist golem, and regular elven patrols known as the Greycloaks.
- **Underworld:** Nonexistent; the settlement maintains a vigilant watch against external threats.
- **Altars:** Temples dedicated to Corellon Larethian, Sehanine Moonbow, and Rillifane Rallathil are present within the settlement.
- **Emotion:** **Serenity** – The pervasive mists and harmonious elven architecture evoke a profound sense of peace and seclusion.
- **Resources:** Renowned for crafting exquisite musical instruments and elven artifacts, though trade is minimal due to the settlement's secluded nature.

### Currency
Elven Gold Leafs – High-value currency used among elven communities (Platinum)  
Silver Petals – Standard currency for everyday transactions (Gold)  
Copper Vines – Low-denomination coins for minor purchases (Copper)  
Barter System – Commonly employed, emphasizing goods and services over currency

### History:
- **Establishment:** Founded as an outpost and training ground for Evereska's defenders, Greyhome was established to secure the Greycloak Hills and provide a retreat in case of an attack on Evereska.
- **Defense Measures:** Lord Erlan Duirsar created the Walker in the Mists, a mist golem, to serve as a guardian of the settlement.
- **Current Status:** Greyhome continues to function as a vital part of Evereska's defense network, maintaining the security of the Greycloak Hills and serving as a sanctuary for elves and trusted allies.
