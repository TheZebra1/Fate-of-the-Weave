#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[High Hall]]:** Government seat and central fortress.
- **[[The Companion]]:** Ever-present radiant sphere that once protected the city.
- **[[Hellriders' Headquarters]]:** Home of the legendary cavalry.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Ruled by the [[Overseer of Elturgard]], backed by a council of paladins.
- **Security:** The [[Hellriders]] and [[Knights of the Companion]] uphold strict law.
- **Underworld:** Limited; strict theocratic rule suppresses crime.
- **Altars:** [[Torm]], [[Tyr]], [[Helm]], [[Lathander]].
- **Resources:** Known for its cavalry, divine artifacts, and strong military presence.

### History:
- Once a thriving trade city, it became the center of [[Elturgard]], a theocracy devoted to **Torm**.
- The **Companion**, a celestial orb, once provided protection against undead and darkness.
- The city was dragged into **Avernus** during the [[Descent into Avernus]], but was later restored.
- After its return from Avernus, governance shifted under the new **Overseer of Elturgard**.
- Known for the **Hellriders**, an elite force of knights who once rode into the Nine Hells.
