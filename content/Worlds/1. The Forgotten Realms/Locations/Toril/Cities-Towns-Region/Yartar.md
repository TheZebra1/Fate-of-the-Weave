**A bustling river city where trade thrives and shadows conceal myriad intrigues.**
**Social Hubs:** [[Fishyard]], [[The Grand Dame]], [[Happy Hall of Fortuitous Happenstance]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[Fishyard]]:** The central market offering a variety of goods, from fresh seafood to exotic imports.
- **[[The Grand Dame]]:** A luxurious riverboat casino owned by Lord Khaspere Drylund, frequented by the city's elite.
- **[[Happy Hall of Fortuitous Happenstance]]:** The temple of Tymora, serving as a haven for adventurers and gamblers alike.
##### [[Beldabar's Inn]]
- Located in Central Market of the city.
	- Rumored Underdark Entrance beneath Inn. 
		- Beware [[Alhoon]]

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Led by Waterbaron Nestra Ruthiol, a shrewd and farseeing Tethyrian noblewoman who values Yartar's membership in the Lords' Alliance.
- **Security:** Maintained by the Shields of Yartar, a mounted fighting force headquartered in the Shield Tower; the city is fortified with walls and a strategic bridge.
- **Underworld:** A hotbed of clandestine activities, with multiple factions like the Hand of Yartar and the Kraken Society vying for influence.
- **Altars:** The Happy Hall of Fortuitous Happenstance serves as the primary temple, dedicated to Tymora.
- **Emotion:** **Vibrant** – The city's lively trade and diverse populace create an atmosphere of constant motion and opportunity.
- **Resources:** Renowned for its barge-building industry, abundant seafood, and as a pivotal trade nexus between major northern and southern cities.

### History:
- **1361 DR:** Waterbaron Alahar Khaumfros, secretly leading the Kraken Society, attempts to incite war; his plot is foiled.
- **1366 DR:** Belleethe Kheldorna becomes Waterbaron, succeeding Khaumfros.
- **1491 DR:** Nestra Ruthiol assumes the role of Waterbaron, navigating the city's complex political landscape.
- **1491 DR:** The Hand of Yartar acquires a devastation orb, leading to increased tensions and covert operations.
- **1492 DR:** A nautiloid ship attacks Yartar, abducting citizens before being repelled by githyanki warriors.
