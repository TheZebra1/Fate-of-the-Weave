**A humble village nestled along the Dessarin River, Trollbridge serves as a vital crossing point and a haven for weary travelers.**
**Social Hubs:** [[The Troll's Rest Inn]], [[River Market]], [[Shrine of the River Mother]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[The Troll's Rest Inn]]:** A cozy inn offering lodging and hearty meals to travelers.
- **[[River Market]]:** A small marketplace where locals trade fish, crafts, and produce.
- **[[Shrine of the River Mother]]:** A sacred site dedicated to Chauntea, tended by the village priestess.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Governed by a village council led by Mayor Harlon Duskwhistle, focusing on community welfare and trade.
- **Security:** Maintained by a local militia and the Riverwatch Rangers, ensuring the safety of villagers and travelers.
- **Underworld:** Minimal; the village maintains a peaceful existence with little criminal activity.
- **Altars:** The Shrine of the River Mother and the Grove of the Wild Heart serve the spiritual needs of the community.
- **Emotion:** **Tranquility** – The village exudes a peaceful atmosphere, offering respite to those journeying through the region.
- **Resources:** Known for its fresh river fish, quality timber, and handcrafted goods.

### Currency
Standard Faerûnian currency is accepted; barter is common in local markets.

### History:
- **Founding:** Established as a ferry crossing point along the Dessarin River, the village grew around the trade and travel facilitated by the river.
- **Development:** Over time, Trollbridge became a modest yet essential stop for merchants and adventurers traversing the Savage Frontier.
- **Recent Events:** The village has remained relatively untouched by major conflicts, maintaining its role as a peaceful waypoint in a tumultuous region.
