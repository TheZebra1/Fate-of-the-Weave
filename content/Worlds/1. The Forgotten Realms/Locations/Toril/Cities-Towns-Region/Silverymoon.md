# Silverymoon
## Overview
Silverymoon is often referred to as the "Gem of the North," renowned for its magical heritage, stunning architecture, and vibrant community. 

### The Moonbridge
![[Silverymoon.png|500]]
<small>*Silverymoon: The Gem of the North, where the enchanting [[Moonbridge]] connects the realms of magic and wisdom, illuminated under a starlit sky.*<big>

## Notable NPCs
- High Mage [[Taern Hornblade]]
- High Lady [[Seven Sisters|Alustriel Silverhand]]
- [[Veloran Vale]]
- [[Vespera Vale]]

## History
Silverymoon has been a significant power in the region, ruled by the powerful High Lady Alustriel. Known for its tradition of magic, it became a center for arcane knowledge and education.

## Points of Interest
- The [[Moonbridge]]: A magical bridge that connects different parts of the city.
- The [[Vault of the Sages]]: The city’s extensive library.
- [[Strixhaven]]: A prestigious university of magic.

## Internal Relationships
Silverymoon enjoys a cooperative relationship among its various guilds and organizations, with the [[Spellguard]] playing a crucial role in maintaining order.

## Outward Relationships
Silverymoon is a central player in the Confederation of the Silver Marches, maintaining alliances with neighboring settlements and cities.

## Background
Silverymoon has a rich history steeped in magic, shaped by its rulers and the various magical institutions that have flourished within its borders.
