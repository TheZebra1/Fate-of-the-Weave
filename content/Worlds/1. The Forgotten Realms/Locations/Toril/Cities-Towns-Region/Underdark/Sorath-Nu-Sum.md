## General Overview
- **Location**: Northdark, former center of kuo-toan politics.
- **Key Features**:
  - Numerous pools for recreation and breeding.
  - Multiple shrines and temples dedicated to **[[Blipdoolpoolp]]**, the Sea Mother.
- **Current Status**: A ruined city, abandoned by kuo-toas, now partially inhabited by **aquatic spiders** and their **drow handlers**.

---

## Historical Events
- **The Coup**:
  - **Leader**: Lu, a cleric of Blipdoolpoolp, led a drow-hating faction.
  - **Action**: Overthrew the ruling council and massacred all drow traders in the streets.
- **Drow Retaliation**:
  - **Response**: Swift and lethal retribution.
  - **Forces**: Drow mercenaries and aquatic spiders attacked the city.
  - **Result**: The city was sacked, shrines left empty, and the kuo-toan faithful were forced to abandon Sorath-Nu-Sum.

---

## Current Inhabitants
- **Aquatic Spiders**:
  - Controlled by drow handlers.
  - Occupy a small corner of the city.
  - **Orders**: Kill any returning kuo-toas on sight.

---

## DM Notes
- **Atmosphere**:
  - Ruined, desolate city with empty shrines and eerie silence.
  - Pools are stagnant and abandoned, adding to the haunting ambiance.
- **Encounters**:
  - Drow handlers and aquatic spiders patrol their section of the city.
  - Any kuo-toan NPCs venturing into the area face immediate hostility.
- **Plot Hooks**:
  - Kuo-toan refugees seeking aid to reclaim their homeland.
  - Forbidden treasures or relics of Blipdoolpoolp hidden within the abandoned shrines.
  - Tensions with drow mercenaries who view the area as a stronghold.

---

## Key NPCs
- **Lu (Optional Survivor)**:
  - The cleric who led the coup might have survived and is now a wandering exile.
  - Possible motivations: regret, vengeance, or redemption.
- **Drow Handlers**:
  - Cold, efficient, and deeply loyal to their mission.
  - Possible factional ties to larger drow political intrigue.

---

## Adventure Seeds
- **Relic of the Sea Mother**: Players hear rumors of a powerful kuo-toan artifact left behind in one of the abandoned shrines.
- **Spider Infestation**: The aquatic spiders have started expanding their territory, threatening nearby settlements.
- **Survivors' Revenge**: A kuo-toan survivor seeks the party’s help in rallying forces to retake Sorath-Nu-Sum.

---

> **DM Tip**: Highlight the stark contrast between the city's former glory and its current desolation. Use the aquatic spiders and the tension with the drow to create a constant sense of danger for your players.
