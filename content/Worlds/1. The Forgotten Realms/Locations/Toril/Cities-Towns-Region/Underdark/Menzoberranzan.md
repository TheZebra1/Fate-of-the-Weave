# Menzoberranzan
## Overview
The legendary drow city of **Menzoberranzan** is a sprawling metropolis carved deep within the vast expanse of the **Underdark**. It is a place where shadows hold secrets, and betrayal is as common as breathing. The city thrives under the ever-watchful eyes of **Lolth** the Spider Queen, whose priestesses & priests command absolute power.

The prayer: "***Elliya Lolthu***" - **Test Me Lolth** is abundant on the lips of the drow.

## Culture
Menzoberranzan's society is built on the worship of **Lolth** and revolves around ***power***, ***deception***, and ***violence***. Key cultural practices include:
- Matriarchal Hierarchy
	- Male are viewed as good for reproduction and grunt work. They are better seen and not heard.
> - *“The male asks why he is small; I ask why he still breathes.”*
> - *“Males serve two purposes: to be useful or to be silent. The wise ones learn to be both.”*
> - *“The silence of a male is the sweetest song in the Underdark. It means he knows his place—and that I won’t have to teach him.”*
> - *“Weakness speaks; strength acts...(Usually violence ensues after this)”*
 
- **Annual Festival of the Spider Queen**: A week-long celebration honoring Lolth, filled with sacrifices and brutal displays of power.
- **Gladiatorial Combat   & **Death Duels** in the **Grand Arena**: Public duels and battles that serve as entertainment and a way to settle grudges.
	- ***Death Duels***: Common between the nobles, these duels are a way to resolve disputes without open warfare.

## History
- *-10,000 DR*: Menzoberranzan founded by **Menzoberra the Kinless**, originally as a refuge for drow escaping the surface.
- *1358 DR*: The Time of Troubles brings chaos, Lolth’s influence wanes temporarily.
- *1489 DR*: The **War of the Spider Queen** shakes the city, leading to increased instability among the noble houses.

## Points of Interest
- **The Bazaar**: The heart of Menzoberranzan’s economy, where slaves, poisons, and rare fungi are traded openly.
- **The Clawrift**: A deep chasm that splits the city, filled with monstrous creatures and dark secrets.
- **[[Arach-Tinilith]]**: The towering academy where drow priestesses are trained in the dark arts of Lolth.
- [[Sorcere]]: The magical academy for Arcane Arts.
- [[Melee-Magthere]]: Academy for Martial Arts.

## Internal Relationships
- Every House is vying to claim the most power, but Baenre's reign has been supreme across the ages.
	- Baenre marriage alliances have deep roots in all 6 Houses in [[Menzoberranzan]] creating internal division in other houses when plots against Baenre are birthed. 
		- Many schemes are stifled before they can even be put into play due to familial ties.
- **Bregan D’aerthe**, while nominally loyal to Baenre, often pursues its own agenda.

## Outward Relationships
- **Hostile** towards **Blingdenstone** (Svirfneblin settlement) due to frequent slave raids.
- A fragile alliance with the **Duergar** of **Gracklstugh**, primarily for mutual defense and trade.

## Background
Menzoberranzan is a city built on treachery, ruled by powerful matron mothers who vie for Lolth’s favor. Its subterranean spires and slave pits are home to countless plots and schemes, where survival is never guaranteed, and the only law is the will of the Spider Queen.
