Gauntlgrym (Gaunt-ul-grim) was an ancient dwarven city and the capital of the [Delzoun](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Delzoun). 

In **1464DR** Drow from House Xorlarrin claimed the city and started a settlement around a forge calling it Q'Xorlarrin.

It was reclaimed and resettled by dwarves under the leadership of King Bruenor Battlehammer in the late 15th century DR, but later lost *again* to a beholder named [Felbris](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Felbris).

Adventuring parties later killed Felbris and a Vampire Dwarf inhabiting the city leaving it abandoned once again.

In **1502DR** reclamation efforts by **House Xorlarrin of the Drow** and **Dwarven forces** led to unending stalemate guerilla warfare.
