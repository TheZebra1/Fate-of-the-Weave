**A bustling cavern-city built atop the Darklake’s northern shore, flourishing through multiracial trade and subterranean intrigue.**
**Social Hubs:** [[Central Market]], [[Nameless Inn]], [[Harborstone Warehouses]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[Central Market]]:** Active trading nexus among fungi stalls and drow spice merchants.
- **[[Nameless Inn]]:** Duergar-run lodging; rooms behind “petrified mushroom” doors and goblin-served ale.
- **[[Harborstone Warehouses]]:** Four fortified storage zones managed by the major merchant factions.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Administered by a merchant-lord council composed of leading drow, duergar, and svirfneblin.
- **Security:** Maintained through arcane wards and hired mercenaries from varied Underdark races.
- **Underworld:** Trade in smuggled goods and discreet passage between surface and Darklake dominates economic currents.
- **Altars:** No public temples, though religious smatterings may exist informally.
- **Emotion:** **Commerce** – The air hums with bartering, negotiation, and hidden deals.
- **Resources:** Renowned for rare Underdark produce, gemstones, and spice; a key node for trade under the surface world.

### Currency
Standard Underdark coinage and trade tokens; spells of barter and services are common.

### History:
- **Early History:** Originally established as a neutral trade port by various [[Underdark]] factions; carved from an ancient cavern network above the [[Darklake]].
- **1350s–1370s DR:** Operated largely in secret, facilitating trade between duergar of [[Gracklstugh]], drow of [[Menzoberranzan]], and deep gnomes.
- **1372 DR:** Site of a secret meeting during the [[Silence of Lolth]] and subsequent struggles with the surface world.
- **1490s DR:** Regains prominence as a neutral stronghold for trade amidst rising tensions in the Underdark.
