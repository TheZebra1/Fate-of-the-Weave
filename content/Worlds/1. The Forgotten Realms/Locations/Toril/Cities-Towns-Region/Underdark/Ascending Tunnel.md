# The Ascending Tunnel: The Endless Current and Magical Fungi

The Ascending Tunnel not only serves as a breathtaking connection between the depths of the Moondeep Sea and the Moonsea above, but it also presents an intriguing challenge for players who choose to navigate it without prior preparation.

---

## Description of the Ascending Tunnel

> **Description to Players:**
> 
> As you peer into the depths of the tunnel, you see a swirling, shimmering current of water cascading upward toward the surface. The light refracts through the magical water, casting enchanting colors along the walls, which are adorned with bioluminescent flora and sparkling gemstones. The flow is strong yet feels gentle to the touch, as if beckoning you to swim upward.

### Key Features:

- **Magical Current**: The current is imbued with magic, allowing for a unique swimming experience. Players can swim up the current at a normal pace without suffering disadvantage. 

- **Oxygen Challenge**: The distance from the depths of the Moondeep Sea to the Moonsea surface is substantial, requiring players to hold their breath for an extended period. It will take approximately **30 minutes of swimming** to reach the surface.

- **Magical Edible Fungi**: Along the tunnel, players will discover patches of magical edible fungi that grant the ability to breathe underwater for a limited time. These fungi are spaced out along the route, offering players a risky but possible way to survive the journey.

---

## Navigating the Current and Finding Fungi

### Constitution Check Mechanics:

The journey is divided into three skill challenges, each representing the players reaching a different patch of fungi.

1. **Initial Patches **: 
   - **DC**: 10
   - **Outcome**: If the player fails the Constitution saving throw, they become unconscious and begin to drown, with rounds until death equal to their Constitution modifier. 

2. **Midway Patches **: 
   - **DC**: 12


3. **Final Patches **: 
   - **DC**: 15

### Drowning Mechanics:

- **Determining Rounds to Drowning**: If a player drowns, they roll a **d8 to determine how many rounds they were from the next fungi patch** when they lost consciousness. 

- **Dragging an Unconscious Player**: If a player notices a party member passing out, they may attempt to drag them to the next fungi patch. The dragging character must roll one more d20, comparing it to their previous Constitution save roll, taking the lower roll as their effective check for reaching the next patch. 

  - **Outcome**: 
    - If the dragging player succeeds in the Constitution check, they manage to pull their friend to the next fungi patch and consume it for water breathing. 
	    - However, if the unconscious player’s **rounds away from the fungi patch** exceed their **Constitution modifier**, they still die regardless of the other player’s efforts.

---

## Overcoming the Oxygen Challenge

**Desperation and Reward**: 
> Players can choose to dive in headfirst, seeking the magical fungi as they swim up. However, this path is fraught with risk. Those who do not plan ahead and rely solely on their luck will face a deadly challenge
### Potential Encounters:

- **Aquatic Guardians**: As players swim upward, they might encounter guardians of the tunnel—magical fish, water elementals, or other aquatic beings tasked with protecting the passage. Depending on the players' approach, this could lead to combat or negotiation.
  - **Outcome**: Successful interactions could yield valuable information about the Moonsea or grants of safe passage.

- **Mysterious Underwater Phenomena**: Players might observe strange phenomena within the current, such as glowing jellyfish, mesmerizing schools of fish, or even remnants of ancient structures. Interacting with these could provide useful lore or bonuses.
  - **Outcome**: Players could learn ancient secrets about the Moonsea or find items that enhance their abilities.

---

## Emergence at the Moonsea

After a prolonged swim, players will finally feel the current easing as they approach the surface of the Moonsea. 

> **Final Description to Players:**
> 
> Breaking through the surface, you gasp for fresh air, feeling the salty breeze wash over you. The shimmering expanse of the Moonsea stretches out before you, inviting you to explore its depths and shores. The city of Melvaunt rises on the horizon, beckoning with the promise of new adventures.
