## Overview
The Underdark is a sprawling and treacherous subterranean realm beneath the surface of Faerûn. It is a dark, perilous expanse filled with labyrinthine caves, hidden cities, and terrifying creatures. Life here is harsh and brutal, ruled by the survival of the fittest. The Underdark houses a complex ecosystem where alliances are temporary and betrayal is common.

The Underdark is also complicated by the fact that most of it's standing water sources have been contaminated with [[diseases.magical contagions#Sight Rot|Sight Rot]] since **1485 DR**, denizens of the Underdark refer to this as [[Underwater]] and the cultures of all Underdark species have shifted to adapt to this inexplicable plague.

## History
The history of the Underdark is as layered as its countless caverns. Wars between the [[Drow]] and Duergar, skirmishes with Mind Flayers, and relentless encroachment by surface adventurers have shaped its treacherous society. This dark world has witnessed eons of bloodshed, worship of cruel deities, and the rise and fall of countless factions.

## Points of Interest
- **[[Menzoberranzan]]**: The legendary city of the Drow, where Matron Mothers reign supreme.
- **[[Gracklstugh]]**: Known as the City of Blades, this Duergar settlement is a hub of trade and a place of constant political tension.
- **[[The Wormwrithings]]**: A dangerous and sprawling network of tunnels infested with purple worms.
- **[[The Labyrinth]]**: A twisting maze of caverns home to minotaurs and fiendish traps.

## Internal Relationships
Relations within the Underdark are complex and highly unstable. Drow houses feud with each other, and the Mind Flayers subtly manipulate other races for their own gain. However, tentative alliances do form when external threats arise.

## Outward Relationships
The Underdark has limited connections to the surface world, often hostile or based on necessity. Trade routes with specific surface organizations exist, though most view surface dwellers as either threats or resources to exploit.

## Background
The Underdark is both a sanctuary and a prison for its inhabitants, isolated by natural barriers and a hostile surface world. Here, ancient deities are worshipped in dark rituals, and societies are shaped by absolute power and fear. The Underdark’s mysteries continue to captivate adventurers and scholars alike.
