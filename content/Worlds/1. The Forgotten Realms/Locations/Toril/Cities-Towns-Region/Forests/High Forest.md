## 1. High Forest

**Location**: Northwest Faerûn, south of the Spine of the World.

**Inhabitants**:
- **Native Creatures**: Elves (wood and wild), treants, centaurs, fey beings, and various magical creatures. 
- **Humblewood Creatures**: Birdfolk (e.g., raptors, owls), humblefolk (e.g., hedgehogs, foxes).

**Theme**: A vast, ancient woodland rich in magic and history, serving as a sanctuary for those seeking to preserve the old ways and natural balance.

**Struggles**: The High Forest has faced numerous threats, including invasions by orcs and demons, and internal conflicts among its inhabitants. These challenges have fostered a strong sense of guardianship and unity among the forest's denizens, who are determined to protect their home from external dangers.
