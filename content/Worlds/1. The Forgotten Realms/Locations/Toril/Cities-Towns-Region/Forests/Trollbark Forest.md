## Trollbark Forest

**Location**: South of the Western Heartlands, west of the River Chionthar, and near the city of [[Elturel]] and [[Daggerford Maps]].

**Inhabitants**:
- **Native Creatures**: Trolls (as the name suggests), goblins, dire wolves, and a variety of monstrous beasts. Legends also speak of shadowy fey and ancient curses tied to the forest.
- **Humblewood Creatures**: Birdfolk (e.g. kenku, corvids, hawks), humblefolk (e.g., foxes, badgers, hedgehogs).

**Theme**: A dark, foreboding forest with a sinister reputation, shrouded in perpetual gloom. The Trollbark Forest is infamous for its dangers and the eerie feeling of being watched. It serves as a haven for outlaws, trolls, and monstrous creatures.

**Struggles**:
The forest is plagued by the constant threat of marauding trolls, who are a menace both within and beyond its borders. The surrounding human settlements view the forest as cursed, rarely venturing within its boundaries. For the inhabitants, this isolation has created a tight-knit, survivalist society where the birdfolk and humblefolk must rely on each other to endure. Many communities take a pragmatic approach to life, favoring resilience, adaptability, and swift action to fend off the forest's many dangers. Tales of cursed ruins hidden deep within the forest serve as both a warning and a source of intrigue for the more daring denizens.
