## Border Forest

**Location**: Near the Anauroch desert, bordering the Dalelands.

**Inhabitants**:
- **Native Creatures**: Elves, fey beings, and various woodland creatures
- **Humblewood Creatures**: Birdfolk (e.g., hawks), humblefolk (e.g., badgers, squirrels).

**Theme**: A transitional forest standing between fertile lands and the encroaching desert, embodying resilience and adaptation.

**Struggles**: The proximity to the Anauroch desert presents challenges such as resource scarcity and threats from desert creatures. Inhabitants have developed a resourceful and hardy nature, striving to preserve their home against environmental encroachment.
