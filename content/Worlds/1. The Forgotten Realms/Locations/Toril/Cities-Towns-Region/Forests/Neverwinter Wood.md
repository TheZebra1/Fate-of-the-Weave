## Neverwinter Wood

**Location**: Near the city of Neverwinter in the Sword Coast North.

**Inhabitants**:
- **Native Creatures**: Elves, fey creatures, and various magical beings.
- **Humblewood Creatures**: Birdfolk (e.g., owls), humblefolk (e.g., foxes, raccoons).

**Theme**: A cold, enchanted forest with strong ties to the Feywild, known for its perpetual winter and mystical ambiance.

**Struggles**: The forest has faced threats from external forces seeking to exploit its magical properties. Inhabitants are often secretive and protective, valuing their seclusion and the forest's enchantments.
