# Cormanthor Forest
## Overview
The Cormanthor Forest, stretching across the Dalelands and beyond, is one of Faerûn's most storied and mysterious wildlands. Once home to the elven nation of Cormanthyr, it is now a shadow of its former glory, filled with overgrown ruins and lingering magic.

## Current Condition
The forest is a dense, sprawling wilderness, characterized by towering oaks, shadowed glades, and hidden dangers. Its once-pristine pathways have been overtaken by nature, making it difficult to navigate without guidance.

### Key Inhabitants:
- **Drow factions**: Hidden enclaves seek control of ancient magical relics.
- **Orcs and goblinoids**: Hostile groups roam the forest, preying on travelers.
- **Adventurers**: Brave souls search for the treasures of Myth Drannor and other ruins.

	- [[Order of the Arcane Blade]]

## Points of Interest
- **Myth Drannor Ruins**: The heart of the forest and a hub of elven history.
- **The Standing Stones**: Sacred sites scattered throughout the forest.
- **Haunted Glades**: Areas of lingering magical anomalies tied to Cormanthyr's fall.

## Background
Cormanthor Forest remains a place of legends, drawing adventurers and scholars despite its dangers. It is a stark reminder of the fragility of even the greatest civilizations and a place where the wilds reclaim what was once lost.
