**A legendary empire of human archwizards that mastered magic, creating floating cities and mythallars—until a cataclysmic spell ended its glory.**
**Social Hubs:** [[Floating Enclaves]], [[Low Netherese Settlements]], [[Tribal Outposts]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[High Netherese Enclaves]]:** Grand floating cities powered by mythallars, home to arcanists and their servants.
- **[[Low Netherese Demesnes]]:** Fertile settlements focused on agriculture, crafts, and supporting the enclaves.
- **[[Angardt and Rengarth]] Tribes:** Rugged frontier clans navigating life outside Netherese control, sometimes allies or foes.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Ruled by elite archmages in High Netheril; Low Netheril governed by appointed administrators.
- **Security:** Floating cities defended by mythallar-powered constructs, wards, and magical armies.
- **Underworld:** Intrigue among archmages, ancient secrets hidden in ruins, and phaerimm plotting vengeance.
- **Altars:** Shrines dedicated to human and elven deities, with many mages seeking power from arcane forces directly.
- **Emotion:** **Awe** – A realm of ambition and brilliance that changed the world… and broke itself trying.
- **Resources:** Mythallars, Nether Scrolls, arcane fish, flying cities, powerful artifacts.

### Currency
Enchanted tokens, spell-scripted notes, mythallar credits, and some standard coinage used among commoners.

### History:
- **−2207 to −1206 DR (Golden Age):** Netheril becomes the greatest arcane civilization in Faerûn, with immense magical progress.
- **−339 DR (Karsus’s Folly):** Archmage Karsus attempts to become a god by casting the spell *Karsus’s Avatar*; Mystryl dies, mythallars fail, and floating cities crash.
- **Post-Fall:** The region becomes Anauroch Desert. Tribes like the Rengarth and Angardt persist, and surviving Netherese fragment or vanish.
- **1372 DR:** The City of Shade (Thultanthar) returns from the Shadow Plane, beginning the Shadovar era.
- **1479–1487 DR:** Shadovar aggression leads to conflict with Myth Drannor. The fall of Thultanthar marks the final end of Netherese resurgence.
