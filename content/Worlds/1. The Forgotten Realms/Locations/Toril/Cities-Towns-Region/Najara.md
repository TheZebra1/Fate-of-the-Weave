#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[Temple of Sseth]]:** Grandest religious structure devoted to the Serpent God.
- **[[The Venom Pits]]:** A network of caverns filled with alchemical toxins.
- **[[Serpent’s Coil Caverns]]:** The hidden capital of Najara, ruled by the Spirit Naja.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** The Yuan-ti rulers, led by the **Spirit Naja**, enforce a strict caste system.
- **Security:** Yuan-ti warriors, naga sentinels, and mind-controlled thralls.
- **Underworld:** Infiltrators and cultist spies operate throughout Faerûn.
- **Altars:** [[Sseth]], [[Merrshaulk]], [[Zehir]], [[Tiamat]].
- **Resources:** Known for venomous alchemy, enslaved captives, and dark magic.

### History:
- Najara has existed for centuries as a hidden **Serpent Kingdom**, controlling secret tunnels and jungle ruins.
- Ruled by the enigmatic **Spirit Naja**, a divine figure said to be an avatar of **Sseth**.
- Infiltrates surface nations using **Yuan-ti spies and shapechangers**.
- Its influence spreads through the **cult of Sseth**, corrupting mortal rulers and organizations.
- Feared for its **powerful psionics, venomous magic, and ruthless enslavement of warm-blooded creatures**.
