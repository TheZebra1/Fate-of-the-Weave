## Overview
- **Location**: Now in Ruins; Previously the sole human settlement in the desolate region of [[Thar]], [[Faerûn]].
- **Terrain**: Surrounded by hills on three sides, providing natural defense.
- **Significance**: [[Red Plume Road]] runs through the heart of these ruins; now a crucial trading post for caravans heading to the Galena Mountains.
	- Once hosted a well known beautiful shrine to [[Selûne]]

## Economy
- **Imports**: Due to its remote location, Glister imports essential resources like food and timber.
- **Exports**: Trades raw ores, including iron, silver, and copper, mined from nearby areas.

## History
- **Establishment**: Founded as the capital of the human kingdom of Thar by Beldoran in 1288 DR after defeating the last ogre Tharkul.
- **Decline**: The kingdom was overrun by ogres in 1303 DR, but Glister endured as a beacon of civilization.
- **Relocation**: The town has moved several times due to permafrost thawing and hostile raids.
- **Destruction**: In the late 1470s DR, Glister was destroyed by orcs of the [[Blood Skull tribe]].

## Notable Features
- **Temples**: Home to three temples established during Beldoran's reign:
  - **Hall of Luck**: Dedicated to Tymora, located near the town's west wall.
  - **House of Swords**: Dedicated to Tempus, a walled abbey outside the town, hosting warrior-priests.
  - **House of Auril's Breath**: Dedicated to Auril, known locally as The Cold House, situated along the main road.
- **Leadership**: Protected by archmage Thusk Tharmuil and religious leaders, contributing to its resilience.
