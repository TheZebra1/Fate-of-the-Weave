The **Red Plume Road** is a major trade route connecting the industrious city of **[[Melvaunt]]** to the resource-rich **[[Galena Mountains]]**, cutting through the treacherous expanse of **[[Thar]]**. Named after the **[[Red Plumes]]**, the mercenary company employed to secure its length, the road is vital for the transport of mined ores, supplies, and workers. 
[[Melvaunt Mining]]
### **Description**
- **Path:** The road is well-maintained but heavily patrolled, featuring fortified outposts every 8–12 miles to provide shelter and defenses against raiders and monsters.
- **Environment:** The route crosses dangerous moorlands plagued by roaming orc bands, trolls, and other predators native to Thar.
- **Caravans:** Daily caravans travel in heavily guarded convoys, carrying workers, mining supplies, and extracted minerals between Melvaunt and the mines.
- **Security:** Artificer-enhanced defenses, including automated ballistae and sending stone communication at outposts are deployed along the route to ensure safety. Regular Red Plume patrols also maintain control.

### **Significance**
- The road is a symbol of Melvaunt's industrial and economic might 
	- The richest mines were thought to be unreachable due to the danger of Thar, however Melvaunt shocked the [[Moonsea]] region by not only securing the mines, but a route to and from the mines through Thar itself. 
- It is a dangerous passage, with constant threats from ambushes and sabotage, making it a proving ground for adventurers and mercenaries alike.

**Traveler Note:** While the road is *relatively* secure under Red Plume protection, anyone traveling unguarded risks falling prey to the dangers of Thar.
