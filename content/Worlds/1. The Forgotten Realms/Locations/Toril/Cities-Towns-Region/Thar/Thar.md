## Overview
- **Location**: A vast, rocky moorland in the [[Moonsea]] North region of [[Faerûn]].
- **Terrain**: Bleak and barren steppes, considered inhospitable by most civilized folk.
- **Inhabitants**: Primarily wild tribes of orcs, ogres, trolls, and other humanoids engaged in endless skirmishes.
- **Notable Settlements**: 
  - **[[Glister]]**: A human settlement serving as a trade post, known for its shrine to [[Selûne]].
- **Economy**: Rich in mineral resources like iron, silver, and copper, traded with neighboring regions. Imports food and timber due to its barren terrain.
- **Significance**: A chaotic and dangerous frontier often avoided by travelers, but prized for its mineral wealth.
