## Overview
- **Type**: Lake
- **Location**: [[Thar]] region, [[Moonsea]] North, Faerûn
- **Significance**: Source of the Tormel River, which runs through [[Melvaunt]]

## Geography
- **Tormel River**: Originates from Bloodmuck and flows southwest, passing by the city of [[Melvaunt]] before emptying into the [[Moonsea]].
- **Nearby Landmarks**:
  - **Fangjaws Hold**: Hills located to the southeast, home to the Skullsmashers ogre tribe.
  - **Nouth**: Ruins of a tower and walled stableyard on the southwestern shore, remnants of King Beldoran's human nation.

## Ecology
- **Wetlands**: Bloodmuck is part of the wetlands of Thar, contributing to the region's marshy environment.

## Significance
- **Strategic Importance**: Serves as a water source and natural landmark within the otherwise harsh and barren landscape of Thar.
