## Overview
- **Location**: A forbidding mountain range North of [[Melvaunt]] and [[Thar]], marking the natural border around [[Vaasa]] in northeast Faerûn.
	- Reachable by [[Red Plume Road]]
- **Terrain**: Rugged, icy peaks with treacherous passes and deep valleys.
- **Geography**:
  - The western arc is often considered an extension of the [[Earthspur Mountains]] and is called the West Galena Mountains.
- **Inhabitants**: Populated by monstrous creatures, bandits, and occasionally adventurers seeking hidden treasures or mining opportunities.
- **Economy**: Rich in minerals, including iron and silver, but mining is dangerous due to hostile wildlife and difficult terrain.
	- [[Melvaunt Mining]]
- **Significance**: A geographical barrier between regions, serving as a challenge for trade and military campaigns.
