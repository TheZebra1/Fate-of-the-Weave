**A modest yet bustling town nestled in the Dessarin Valley, Red Larch serves as a vital waypoint for travelers and traders alike.**
**Social Hubs:** [[The Swinging Sword Inn]], [[Stone Hammer Inn]], [[Town Market Square]]
##### Stone Bridge
***See also [Stone Bridge](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Stone_Bridge) which is nearby crossing the [[River Dessarin]], towards the [[High Forest]] to the East.***

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[The Swinging Sword Inn]]:** A well-known inn reputed for its hospitality and rumored hidden passages.
- **[[Stone Hammer Inn]]:** A popular gathering spot for locals and visitors, known for its hearty meals and local brews.
- **[[Town Market Square]]:** The central hub for trade, featuring a variety of goods from local artisans and farmers.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Managed by a Chamber of Representatives, a council of seven elected officials serving five-year terms, responsible for local governance and community welfare.
- **Security:** Maintained by a local militia and volunteer patrols, ensuring the safety of residents and travelers.
- **Underworld:** Minimal; the town maintains a reputation for honesty and community cooperation.
- **Altars:** Various local shrines cater to the spiritual needs of the diverse populace.
- **Emotion:** **Welcoming** – The town exudes a sense of warmth and hospitality, offering respite to weary travelers.
- **Resources:** Renowned for its red-hued sandstone, known as Red Larch Stone, and its signature dish, crumblecakes.

### Currency
Standard Faerûnian currency is accepted; barter is common in local markets.

### History:
- **Founding:** Established atop a ridge once crowned by red larches, the town's name reflects its origins.
- **Development:** Grew into a trade hub due to its strategic location at the intersection of major trails and its thriving quarry industry.
- **Recent Events:** Served as a starting point for various adventuring parties and has faced challenges such as elemental cult incursions, which have tested the resilience of its community.
