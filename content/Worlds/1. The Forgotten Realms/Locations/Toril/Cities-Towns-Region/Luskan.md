# Luskan War and the Rise of the Two Captains (1492 - 1502 DR)
## Overview
Between **1492 DR and 1502 DR**, Luskan was embroiled in a brutal naval conflict when a powerful pirate fleet from a distant land invaded the **Sword Coast**, seeking a new base of operations. This war reshaped Luskan’s political structure, leading to the consolidation of power under two rival **High Captains**, **Beniago Kurth** and **Blackbeard**. Meanwhile, the **Arcane Brotherhood** used the conflict to rebuild its strength, setting the stage for future power struggles.

## The Invasion (1492 - 1494 DR)
In **1492 DR**, a fleet of **five pirate captains**, known as the **Crimson Fleet**, arrived in the **Trackless Sea**, seeking to carve out a stronghold in **Faerûn**. Led by the fearsome **[[Blackbeard]]**, these seasoned pirate lords hailed from a distant region of **[[Toril]]**, bringing with them ruthless naval tactics, arcane-infused warfare, and an insatiable hunger for plunder.

Upon discovering **Luskan**, the Crimson Fleet saw an opportunity to claim the city’s strategic harbor and rich trade routes. However, Luskan’s established **High Captains** refused to yield, leading to open war on land and sea.

### The Crimson Fleet:
- **[[Blackbeard]]** – The fleet’s warlord, commanding the _Queen Anne’s Revenge_, a terror-inducing warship shrouded in eldritch flame.
- **[[Edward Kenway]]** – A skilled privateer-turned-pirate, known for his deadly accuracy and masterful naval combat tactics.
- **[[Bartholomew Roberts]]** – A cunning and ambitious captain, renowned for his ability to rally fleets and execute lightning-fast raids.
- **[[Jack Rackham]]** – A notorious rogue, famed for his flair and underhanded tactics, specializing in smuggling and espionage.
- **[[Mary Read]]** – A fierce and strategic captain, leading daring boarding assaults and commanding the fleet’s fastest ships.

The **Battle for Luskan** commenced as these rival factions clashed for dominance.

## The War at Sea (1494 - 1498 DR)
For **four years**, Luskan's **five traditional Ships** waged a relentless war against the **Crimson Fleet**. Battles raged across the **Sea of Swords**, with both sides suffering heavy losses. The **Crimson Fleet** introduced **superior maritime tactics**, forcing Luskan's High Captains into a defensive war.

- **1495 DR** – Luskan lost two of its High Captains, **Ship Suljack** and **Ship Rethnor**, to a surprise attack by Blackbeard’s fleet.
- **1496 DR** – **Beniago Kurth**, the most strategic of the High Captains, consolidated power by merging the surviving ships under his command.
- **1497 DR** – The war reached its peak, with Luskan's economy crippled and its once-thriving harbor nearly abandoned.
- **1498 DR** – A costly naval engagement at the **Misty Bay Massacre** left both sides bloodied and seeking alternatives to prolonged warfare.

While the battle raged, the **Arcane Brotherhood**, long thought weakened, used the conflict as an opportunity to quietly **rebuild its arcane power**.

## The Path to a New Luskan (1498 - 1502 DR)
By **1498 DR**, the war had left Luskan's fleet devastated. With no clear winner and mutual exhaustion settling in, a **temporary ceasefire** was brokered. The war's aftermath saw Beniago Kurth emerge as the **sole remaining captain of the original Luskan factions**. 

In **1500 DR**, an **Accord of the Two Captains** was signed, officially recognizing **Beniago Kurth** and **Blackbeard** as Luskan’s **joint rulers**. This agreement **ended the war** and reorganized the city’s leadership structure into a **dual-High Captaincy**.

### **The New Leadership Structure (1502 DR Onward)**
Luskan, once ruled by five High Captains, now operates under the **joint leadership** of:
1. **High Captain [[Beniago]] Kurth** – Leader of the surviving Luskan ships, backed by **Bregan D’aerthe**.
2. **High Captain [[Blackbeard]]** – Ruler of the **[[Crimson Fleet]]**, controlling the newly established pirate strongholds in Luskan’s outer harbor.

Though an uneasy **peace** exists, tension remains **high**, with both captains constantly maneuvering for **greater control** over Luskan’s resources.

## The Arcane Brotherhood’s Resurgence (1502 DR and Beyond)
During the war, the **Arcane Brotherhood** remained neutral, using the chaos to **silently reestablish its dominion** over the **Host Tower of the Arcane**. By the war’s end, the Brotherhood had amassed **unprecedented magical strength**, and its **Overwizards** had returned.

While Luskan’s two High Captains keep each other in check, the **Arcane Brotherhood now lurks in the background**, waiting for the opportune moment to **seize control once more**.

## Aftermath and Future Conflicts
The uneasy truce between Beniago and Blackbeard **may not last**. Several **lingering threats** could ignite **new conflicts**:
- **The Arcane Brotherhood’s Political Intrigue** – With their power restored, will they attempt to reclaim control over Luskan?
- **The Crimson Fleet’s True Intentions** – Does Blackbeard see the accord as a temporary truce, or is he planning a takeover?
- **Bregan D’aerthe’s Secret Agenda** – With [[Jarlaxle Baenre]]’s interests deeply tied to Beniago, how will the drow manipulate Luskan’s fate?
- **The High Captains’ Rivalry** – How long can Luskan survive under two pirate kings before war breaks out again?

The city of sails remains a **powder keg**, and all it takes is a **single spark** to set Luskan ablaze once more.
