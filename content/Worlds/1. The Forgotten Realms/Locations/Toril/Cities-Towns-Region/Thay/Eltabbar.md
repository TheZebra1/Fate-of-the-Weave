**Eltabbar** is a prominent city in the nation of **[[Thay]]**, situated on the western shore of **[[Lake Thaylambar]]**. Historically, it served as Thay's capital and remains a significant center for the **Red Wizards**.

## Geography and Layout

Eltabbar is renowned for its intricate network of broad canals, which traverse the city and contribute to its unique aesthetic. These waterways are adorned with floating flower beds and elegantly designed stone walkways, enhancing the city's beauty. The River Eltar flows through Eltabbar, originating from a waterfall and winding its way via these canals.

The city's layout was meticulously planned to form a massive glyph imbued with protective magic. This design was an innovation of **Jorgmacdon**, the first zulkir of conjuration, and serves to reinforce the city's defenses.

## History

### Founding and Significance

Eltabbar was established in **923 DR**, a year after Thay secured its independence from Mulhorand. Its central location near the Thaymount made it a strategic choice for the nation's capital.

### The Imprisonment of Eltab

A dark secret underlies Eltabbar's foundation. Before the city's establishment, the Red Wizard Jorgmacdon discovered the demon lord **Eltab** beneath the Thayan Plateau. Utilizing ancient rituals, Jorgmacdon released Eltab to aid in overthrowing the Mulhorandi god-kings. However, unable to banish Eltab afterward, the Red Wizards imprisoned him beneath the mouth of the River Eltar. The city's design, forming an arcane glyph, served to seal this demonic prison.

### Release and Subsequent Events

In **1367 DR**, the zulkir of necromancy, **Szass Tam**, released Eltab with the intention of enslaving the demon lord. However, his plan was thwarted, allowing Eltab to escape into a demoncyst below Thaymount. During a confrontation with Szass Tam in **1373 DR**, Eltab was seemingly destroyed. Nevertheless, due to ancient bindings, he was transported to the [[Citadel of Conjurers]] in [[Impiltur]], where he continues to plot his revenge.

## Society and Culture

Eltabbar has long been a haven for the Red Wizards, with its streets lined with grand wizard towers constructed from exotic materials. The city boasts numerous public gardens and parks, meticulously maintained and featuring flora from distant lands. Colorful birds nest within the city's trees, adding to its serene beauty.

The city was also the nexus of Thay's slave trade until the late 14th century DR, with thousands of slaves being shipped into Eltabbar before being auctioned off.

## Governance

Throughout different periods, Eltabbar has been governed by various tharchions. Notably, **Dmitra Flass**, known as the "First Princess of Thay," served as the Tharchioness of Eltabbar during the mid-to-late 14th century DR. She was a powerful figure among the Red Wizards and played a significant role in Thayan politics.

## Notable Locations

- **Council Hall**: This central building was used by the 15th-century zulkirs serving under High Regent Szass Tam.

- **Chapterhouse of Transmutation**: The chamber for the Red Wizards transmuters housed figures like Zulkir Druxus Rhym and the vampire Muthoth.

## Present Day

As of the late 15th century DR, Eltabbar continues to thrive, maintaining its significance within Thay. The city's rich history and enduring legacy make it a focal point of Thayan culture and politics.
