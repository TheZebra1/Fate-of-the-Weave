**Center of [[Thayan]] arcane and military power**
**Social Hubs:** [[The Citadel]], [[Paths of the Doomed]], [[Academy of Shapers and Binders]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[The Citadel]]:** Arcane fortress and throne of [[Szass Tam]].
- **[[Doomvault]]:** Necromantic research complex and magical prison.
- **[[Academy of Shapers and Binders]]:** School for transmutation and conjuration magic.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** [[Magocracy]] ruled by the [[Red Wizards]], with [[Szass Tam]] at its core.
- **Security:** Defended by [[undead]] armies, arcane traps, and terrain hazards.
- **Underworld:** Forbidden laboratories and ancient crypts hidden deep beneath.
- **Altars:** [[Kossuth]], [[Myrkul]], and [[Bane]] dominate religious presence.
- **Emotion:** **Foreboding** – Magic and malice saturate the air like ash.
- **Resources:** Rich in minerals, infused with arcane residue from ancient rituals.

### History:
- A natural volcanic range, warped by centuries of dark rituals.
- Site of immense necromantic and elemental magical experimentation.
- Home to [[Szass Tam]]'s power and the Red Wizards' strongest bastions.
