**Heart of [[Szass Tam]]’s rule and [[Thayan]] domination**
**Social Hubs:** [[Szass Tam’s Throne]], [[Ravensblood Academy]], [[The Last Stop]]

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[Szass Tam’s Throne]]:** Arcane throne chamber from which the lich lord governs.
- **[[Ravensblood Academy]]:** Dark magic school producing loyal necromancers.
- **[[The Last Stop]]:** Trade post for [[slavers]], mercenaries, and magical goods.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** [[Szass Tam]] rules with absolute magical and political authority.
- **Security:** Enforced by [[undead]] armies, elite [[Red Wizards]], and magical surveillance.
- **Underworld:** Everything is controlled—any resistance is quickly crushed.
- **Altars:** Dark temples devoted to [[Myrkul]], [[Bane]], and [[Kossuth]].
- **Emotion:** **Dread** – The chill of necromancy lingers in every hall.
- **Resources:** Home of [[necromantic]] power, arcane vaults, and cursed relics.

### [[Currency]]

Barter System – Used widely for black-market rituals and trade

### History:
- Built atop sarrukh ruins at the peak of the [[Thaymount]].
- Became the arcane capital of [[Thay]] under [[Szass Tam]].
- Center of [[necromantic]] study, planar control, and totalitarian rule.
