## Forge/Smithy Owners: Avi & Endric  
### **Avi (Water Elemental)**  
- **Personality:** Calm, collected, and highly precise. Avi has a deep love for fluidity in craftsmanship, often ensuring that each blade or piece of armor is quenched and tempered to perfection. Their patience balances Endric’s fiery temperament.
- **Appearance:** A humanoid form of swirling azure water, always shifting and shimmering. Their "hands" can take on the properties of mist, ice, or flowing water depending on their mood and work needs.  
- **Skills:** Expert in blade tempering, delicate engraving, and cooling processes for enchanted weapons.  

### **Endric (Fire Elemental)**  
- **Personality:** Passionate, loud, and intensely focused. Endric works in bursts of creativity and emotion, shaping raw metal with an aggressive and artistic flair. They tend to be the voice of the shop, always ready to haggle and charm customers.  
- **Appearance:** A humanoid being of dancing flame, flickering between deep crimson and bright gold. When angered, they burn hotter; when excited, they crackle with sparks of energy.  
- **Skills:** Master of metal shaping, heat-treating, and forging enchanted weapons with raw elemental force.  

## **Their Story**  
Avi and Endric’s love story began in the Elemental Plane of Water and Fire—two realms that should have never intertwined. When Endric was tasked with reforging a legendary blade that had shattered in a cosmic battle, they needed an expert in fluid dynamics to assist in the tempering process. Enter Avi, a meticulous water elemental who found Endric’s brash methods both infuriating and fascinating.  

Over time, their partnership forged something more than just steel. Against all odds, they fell in love and decided to travel together, eventually settling in **Waterdeep**. They opened **Steam & Steel** in the city's Trades Ward, where their combined elemental talents made them one of the most sought-after smithies in Faerûn.  

## **What Makes Steam & Steel Unique?**  
- The **forge itself is a marvel**—a massive anvil suspended between **a fountain of swirling water and a blazing column of fire**. The workshop is divided into two halves: one side constantly steaming with hissing heat from Endric’s flames, while the other remains cool and misty under Avi’s influence.
- When crafting together, they create a mesmerizing **dance of elements**—molten metal kissed by Endric’s fire, only to be quenched in Avi’s liquid embrace, forming pieces of **impeccable craftsmanship**.
- The walls shimmer with **glyphs of binding**, helping maintain the forge’s balance. **Rings of enchanted steam** hover above, cooling weapons gradually, while **embers drift in controlled spirals**, waiting to be summoned for work.
- They are known for **special commissions** that involve elemental magic, making their forge a **destination for adventurers** seeking unique gear.

## **Custom Orders**  
Adventurers can request unique commissions that blend **fire and water magic** into weapons, armor, or accessories. Prices depend on complexity, material rarity, and enchantments.

### **Shop Atmosphere**
- **Heat & Mist**: The shop always smells of scorched metal and cool rain. The air is a **perfect balance of heat and condensation**, creating an **ethereal, sauna-like** ambiance.
- **Ambient Elemental Energy**: Runes glow faintly, binding fire and water in perfect harmony. When Avi and Endric work in tandem, **the air hums with energy**.
- **Music of the Forge**: Hissing steam, crackling flames, and rhythmic hammering create a **hypnotic symphony** that customers often stop to admire.

---

Steam & Steel isn’t just a shop; it’s **an experience**—a blend of raw elemental power, love, and craftsmanship that makes it **one of Waterdeep’s most renowned forges**.
