#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Ruled by **Great Gark**, a powerful hobgoblin wizard seeking to unite all fey goblinoids under his rule.
- **Security:** Patrols of goblin brigands, wolf riders, and a standing army of goblinoid warriors.
- **Underworld:** Limited; the kingdom maintains strict control over internal affairs.
- **Altars:** **Maglubiyet**, **Nomog-Geaya**, **Hruggek**.
- **Resources:** Known for its mercenary forces, agricultural produce, and strategic location in the Feywild.

### History:
- **Great Gark** established Nachtur as a powerful goblin kingdom in the Feywild, aiming to unite all fey goblinoids.
- In **1479 DR**, Gark attempted to expand his kingdom into the **Moonshae Isles** by sending goblins, worgs, and other creatures to the **Dernall Forest** in **Callidyrr**.
- Despite its power, Nachtur is not considered a major threat to the **eladrin** of the **Court of Stars**, though it is kept in check by other races of the Feywild.
