# The History of Prismeer and [[Zybilna]]
## The Land of Illyndor and Queen [[Elyndra]] (The [[Raven Queen]])
- **Illyndor** was a peaceful and radiant kingdom in the Feywild, known for its harmony, beauty, and ethereal inhabitants. Time seemed to slow, and the land hummed with joy and wonder.
- **Queen Elyndra**, ruler of Illyndor, embodied love, balance, and serenity. She was a symbol of purity and beloved by her people.
- Elyndra, unknown to most, was the future Raven Queen. Before her transformation, she was deeply tied to the forces of life and light in the [[Feywild]].

## The Fall of Illyndor: The Shadowfell and the Spellplague
- During the **Spellplague**, Queen Elyndra attempted to ascend to godhood to end the feud between [[Corellon]] and [[Lolth]]. Her ritual was sabotaged by Lolth, leading to her tragic fall into the newly forming **[[Shadowfell]]**.
- The magic sustaining Illyndor’s harmony faded as its queen and her royal elves were pulled into the Shadowfell. Many inhabitants were lost or changed forever.
- Left leaderless, Illyndor began to unravel, its light dimming and its joy replaced by sorrow and uncertainty.

## Tasha’s Arrival in the Feywild
- **Tasha** (Iggwilv, later Zybilna) entered the Feywild during the chaos of the Spellplague, drawn to its fractured state and seeking a foothold in the multiverse.
- She found Illyndor, a kingdom of incredible potential but without a ruler. Seizing the opportunity, she stepped in as a **savior** to restore the kingdom.
- Knowing her dark past as a demonologist would be unacceptable to the people of Illyndor, Tasha adopted the persona of **Zybilna**, an **archfey**, and reshaped the land into a new **Domain of Delight** called **Prismeer**.

## The Creation of Prismeer
- Tasha, now **Zybilna**, established herself as the ruler of Prismeer and implemented laws to maintain order:
  - **The Rule of Hospitality**: All were to be welcoming and kind to guests unless proven unworthy.
  - **The Rule of Ownership**: Theft and taking what is not freely given were forbidden.
  - **The Rule of Reciprocity**: Gifts and gestures required mutual exchange, reflecting fairness and balance.
- While maintaining the appearance of a benevolent ruler, Zybilna's darker tendencies began to subtly influence the land. Prismeer retained traces of its former beauty but showed signs of her manipulative nature, making the domain a mix of whimsy and peril.

## The Struggles of Prismeer’s Inhabitants
- The people of Illyndor, now part of Prismeer, struggled to adapt to their new ruler. Though Zybilna maintained the land’s charm, her influence introduced an undercurrent of unease.
- Her **stepsisters**, the **[[Hourglass Coven]]**, grew jealous of her success and sought to take control. They feigned loyalty but later betrayed her, imprisoning Zybilna and dividing Prismeer into three splintered regions: **[[Hither]]**, **[[Thither]]**, and **[[Yon]]**.

## Themes and Implications
- **The Duality of Zybilna/Tasha/Iggwilv**: Zybilna represents Tasha’s attempt to escape her dark past and reinvent herself as a force for good. However, her demonic history cannot be entirely concealed, and it subtly influences her rule.
- **The Legacy of Elyndra (The Raven Queen)**: The fall of Illyndor reflects the Raven Queen’s own transformation. As Elyndra, she ruled a kingdom of life and joy. As the Raven Queen, she became a deity of death and memory, leaving behind a kingdom struggling to hold onto its identity.
- **The Tragic Irony**: Zybilna’s role as a ruler of a “delightful” domain mirrors her internal conflict. The land’s idyllic appearance hides a deeper sorrow, just as Zybilna hides her true nature.

## Timeline Summary
1. **Before 1385 DR**: Illyndor thrives under Queen Elyndra’s rule in the Feywild, embodying peace and light.
2. **1385 DR**: The Spellplague occurs. Elyndra’s ritual fails, transforming her into the Raven Queen and pulling her and her court into the Shadowfell. Illyndor is left leaderless.
3. **Post-1385 DR**: Tasha enters the Feywild, adopts the persona of [[Zybilna]], and establishes herself as ruler of the now-leaderless Illyndor, renaming it **Prismeer**.
4. **Later Events**: The Hourglass Coven betrays Zybilna, imprisoning her in "[[Tasha's Cauldron]]" and dividing Prismeer into three regions.
