#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[Crystal Spires]]:** Once the most radiant structures of eladrin architecture, now lying silent and empty.
- **[[Lord Kannoth]]:** The eladrin vampire who ruled the city even after its fall.
- **[[Living Gate]]:** A portal to the Far Realm located in the Feydark below Cendriane.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Formerly ruled by an eladrin lordship; now a lawless ruin haunted by remnants of its past.
- **Security:** Lacks formal security; inhabited by undead and other lurking dangers.
- **Underworld:** Presence of undead entities and possible dark fey activities.
- **Altars:** Ruined altars to **Corellon Larethian** and **Sehanine Moonbow**; desecrated sites honoring **Lolth** and **Orcus**.
- **Resources:** Contains ancient artifacts and lost arcane knowledge amidst the ruins.

### History:
- **Cendriane** was once the most radiant city of the eladrin, built to exist simultaneously in both Faerûn and the Feywild.
- The city fell during a vicious battle between the eladrin and the drow, leading to its ultimate destruction and abandonment.
- **Lord Kannoth**, an eladrin vampire, continued to inhabit the city after its fall, ruling over the desolate ruins.
- The **Living Gate**, a portal to the Far Realm, is located in the Feydark beneath Cendriane, adding to the city's mystique and peril.
