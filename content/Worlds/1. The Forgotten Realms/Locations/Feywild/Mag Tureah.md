### History:
- **Mag Tureah** is a vast cavern fortress in the depths of the **[[Feydark]]**, illuminated by precious gems that give the appearance of a starlit night sky.
- The fortress is characterized by its maze-like structure of iron towers and stone edifices, making it an imposing stronghold.
- **King Bres**, a formidable fomorian, rules with an iron fist, maintaining power through fear and dark alliances.
- The fortress contains numerous portals to and from **Toril**, which open and close unpredictably, often trapping unwary travelers who may become enslaved by the fomorians.
- **Baba Yaga** once prophesied that King Bres would be slain by his own son, adding an element of internal tension to the kingdom.
