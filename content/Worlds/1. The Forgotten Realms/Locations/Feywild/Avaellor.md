### History:
- **Avaellor** is an eladrin city that **transitions between the Feywild and the Material Plane**, residing on a mortal island during **spring and summer** before returning to the Feywild as **winter approaches**.
- This cyclical migration allows Avaellor to **engage in trade and cultural exchange** with mortal civilizations but leaves behind an aching emptiness when it vanishes.
- The city is renowned for its **ethereal beauty**, with **gardens that bloom eternally** and architecture seamlessly woven into nature.
- Many who visit Avaellor **feel a deep yearning after leaving**, as if they have glimpsed something just beyond reach—a perfect moment that cannot be recaptured.
- The eladrin who call Avaellor home have embraced this longing, weaving it into their art, music, and magic.
