**Seat of the [[Summer Court]]**

#### Notable Buildings and Persons:
- **[[The Dawnflower Palace]]:** Majestic residence of **Queen Titania**, a radiant wonder entwined with flowering vines and golden light.
- **[[Gossamer Amphitheater]]:** A grand stage where performances of heroism, romance, and fey mischief mesmerize audiences.
- **[[Everbloom Gardens]]:** A vibrant paradise where the Feywild’s most exotic flora remains in perpetual bloom.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Ruled by **Queen [[Titania]]**, presiding over the **[[Court of Stars]]**, where diplomacy, revelry, and fey politics intertwine.
- **Security:** Protected by the **[[Shiere Knights]]**, elite warriors ensuring the safety of the Summer Court.
- **Underworld:** Minimal; intrigue is common among fey nobles, but the city's joyous nature discourages darker dealings.
- **Altars:** **[[Corellon Larethian]]**, **Titania**, **[[Sehanine Moonbow]]**.
- **Emotion:** **Exuberance** – A city of endless festivity and unrestrained joy, where every moment is experienced as if it were the first.
- **Resources:** Renowned for **enchanted textiles**, **feywine**, and **starborn crystals**.

### History:
- **Senaliesse** is the **heart of the [[Seelie Court]]**, radiating warmth and eternal celebration as the embodiment of summer’s essence.
- The city is suspended within the **colossal silver trees** of the [[Feywild]], its avenues formed by massive branches and interconnected by gossamer bridges.
- As the seat of **Queen Titania**, it hosts the **Court of Stars**, a meeting ground for **archfey, emissaries, and adventurers** seeking the favor of the Summer Queen.
- Visitors often find themselves **swept up in endless revelry**, experiencing a heightened sense of **joy, passion, and freedom**.
- Though welcoming to outsiders, mortals must tread carefully—**the fey delight in playful trickery, and time flows differently in Senaliesse**.
