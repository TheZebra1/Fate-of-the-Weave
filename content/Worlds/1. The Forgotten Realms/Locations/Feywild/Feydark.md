#### Notable Locations and Persons:
- **[[Mag Tureah]]:** Dominant fomorian kingdom ruled by King Bres.
- **[[Vor Thomil]]:** Realm of the fomorian Queen Connomae.
- **[[Living Gate]]:** Portal to the Far Realm located beneath Cendriane.

#### Key Summary
- **Government:** Various monarchies, primarily **fomorian**-ruled kingdoms.
- **Security:** Enforced by fomorian armies, cyclopean guards, and dark fey entities.
- **Underworld:** Intrigues among dark fey, rebellious slave factions, and external threats from surface dwellers.
- **Altars:** **The [[Queen of Air and Darkness]]**, **[[Psilofyr]]**, **[[Bane]]**, **[[Loviatar]]**, **[[Torog]]**.
- **Resources:** Rich in **primal mud**, **bioluminescent flora**, and **enchanted crystals**.

### [[Currency]]
Obsidian Shards - High-value currency infused with dark magic
Iron Marks - Standard iron coins stamped with the visage of reigning fomorian monarchs
Fungal Spores - Used in barter among myconid colonies
Barter System - Commonly employed for exotic goods and favors
### History:
- The **Feydark** mirrors the **[[Underdark]]** of the [[Material Plane]], characterized by majestic and fantastic landscapes.
- Unlike the Underdark, the Feydark hosts vibrant ecosystems, including forests and swamps within its subterranean expanse.
- **Fomorians**, deformed giant-kin, dominate the Feydark, ruling vast kingdoms with their cyclopean servants and spriggan allies.
- **Myconid colonies** proliferate, though their aggressive spread often leads to conflicts with other denizens.
- The Feydark is the source of the **life energy** of the [[Feywild]], attributed to the existence of **primal mud**, a potent organic substance nourishing its extensive flora.
