## Lore & History
- **Date:** Observed between the last night of *[[Uktar]]* and the first day of *[[Nightal]]*.
- **Other Names:** Also called *Moonfest* or, in some regions, *Festival of Lovers*.
- **Seasonal Significance:** Marks the beginning of winter.
- **Spiritual Purpose:**
  - A day of remembrance for the dead and to honor ancestors.
  - Stories of heroes, ancestors, and gods are retold.
  - Ceremonies are performed to prevent the unrest of the dead.
- **Religious Role:**
  - Holy day for several faiths including Kelemvor (god of the dead), Sehanine Moonbow (elven moon goddess), and Jergal (scribe of the dead).
  - The *Heralds of Faerûn* may conduct formal genealogical recitations, especially for noble families.

### Regional Variation: Vilhon Reach (Sespech & Turmish)
- Celebrated under the first full moon after *Midsummer*.
- Known as the *Festival of Lovers*.
- Lovers journey separately to prearranged meeting places, such as:
  - Evenstar Vale
  - Lake of Drifting Stars
- Focused on romance, revelry, and moonlit trysts.

## Activities & Customs
### Universal Traditions
- **Ritual of Remembrance:**
  - Blessing the graves of ancestors.
  - Lighting candles or leaving offerings at grave sites.
- **Storytelling Circles:**
  - Local elders and bards recount tales of honored ancestors and legendary figures.
  - Communal sharing of family history and folklore.
- **Bloodsong Ceremony:**
  - The Heralds announce and honor noble genealogies.
- **Feasting & Community Gatherings:**
  - Large, communal meals in town squares or temples.
  - Food shared among all classes and visitors.

## Visual & Atmosphere Ideas
- Candlelit graveyards with glowing spirits.
- Silver moonlight casting long shadows as bards weave tales around a bonfire.
- Flower petals and moon symbols as decorations.
- Quiet reverence mixed with celebration—half a wake, half a festival.
