# Spellplague

## Overview
The **Spellplague** was a catastrophic event that occurred in **1385 DR**, also known as the **Year of Blue Fire**, fundamentally altering the multiverse and the nature of magic itself. It was triggered by the assassination of **Mystra**, the goddess of magic, by **[[Cyric]]** with the aid of **Shar**. This event caused the collapse of the **Weave**, the source of arcane magic, and unleashed widespread chaos across the planes.

---

## Causes
- **The Death of [[Mystra]]**: Mystra's assassination disrupted the Weave, which she governed, leading to its complete collapse. Without the Weave, arcane magic became uncontrollable and volatile.
- **[[Shar]]'s Manipulation**: Shar, the goddess of darkness and loss, orchestrated Mystra's downfall as part of her plan to spread chaos and assert dominance over the planes.

---

## Effects
### **Material Plane**
- Vast regions were destroyed or altered, with landscapes torn apart by wild magic.
- Survivors referred to these areas as **Plaguelands**, where arcane energy warped reality.
- The **[[Chondalwood]]** and the **[[Sea of Fallen Stars]]** were among the most affected regions.

### **Feywild and Shadowfell**
- The Plane of Shadow was infused with necrotic energy, transforming it into the **[[Shadowfell]]**.
- The [[Feywild]], while less directly affected, experienced chaotic surges of magic that disrupted its natural order.

### **Magic and Spellcasters**
- Arcane spellcasters experienced a complete loss of control over their abilities.
- Some spells failed, others backfired, and many practitioners died as a result.
- **[[Spellscar]]s**: Individuals exposed to raw wild magic often developed spellscars, which granted unpredictable magical powers.

### **Deities and the Planes**
- The collapse of the Weave affected divine magic, diminishing the power of many gods reliant on Mystra’s influence.
- The **[[Abyss]]**, the **[[Elemental Chaos]]**, and other planes were destabilized, leading to shifts in planar boundaries.

---

## Aftermath
- **Blue Fire**: The wild magic of the Spellplague often manifested as blue flames that consumed and reshaped everything they touched.
## Legacy
- The Spellplague’s effects lingered for over a century, profoundly changing the history, geography, and magic of the Forgotten Realms.
- It set the stage for the return of Mystra in **1479 DR**, when she was reborn and the Weave stabilized for  time.
	- The instability began to return in 1502 DR with the rise of the [[Cult of the Weave Eater]]

---

## Notable Events
- **Creation of the Shadowfell**: The Plane of Shadow transformed into the Shadowfell, a necrotic mirror of the Material Plane.
- **Rise of [[Plaguewrought Land]]s**: Areas consumed by unstable magic, often dangerous and uninhabitable, dotted the world.

---

The Spellplague remains one of the most significant and tumultuous events in the history of the Forgotten Realms, marking a turning point for the multiverse and the role of magic in the world.
