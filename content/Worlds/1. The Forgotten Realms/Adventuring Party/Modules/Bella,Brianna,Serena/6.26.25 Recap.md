# 🗺️ Session Recap: Shipwrecks & Secrets
## 🧝‍♀️ Faelith-Fae (High Elf Rogue)
- A noble-born princess turned spy, Faelith-Fae is on a personal quest to uncover forbidden tomes that hold secrets of her lineage.
- Born without darkvision, she struck a mysterious pact—her once-bright eyes now inky black spheres, reminiscent of a sugar glider’s gaze.

## 🎵 Tiddles (Halfling Bard)
- Founder of the infamous “FeetFoundeth,” Tiddles stole a merchant ship (name long forgotten) that met its watery grave in the harbor of **[[Calimport]]**.
- A silver-tongued swindler with a flair for dramatics.
- **Background:** [[charlatan-xphb]]

## 🪓 Zackie-Zacchaeus (Dwarf Rogue)
- Petite and wiry, Zackie uses her appearance to her advantage, posing as a sweet little girl to lull strangers into a false sense of security.
- A snake in the grass with a neck tattoo and a blade always close at hand.
- **Background:** [[charlatan-xphb]]

## 🌊 The Shore of Dreams – Disaster at Dock
It all began with a spectacular crash: Tiddles’ stolen vessel plowed straight into a luxurious elven-orc yacht.

- On board were **Grasha** (an intimidating orc woman) and her soft-spoken elven husband **Demetrius**.
- Faelith-Fae, ever the razor-tongued noble, threatened Grasha so fiercely that the orc panicked, scooped up Demetrius, and dove into the sea...
  - Neither of them could swim.
  - They never resurfaced.

With their ship now a memory, the trio found themselves approached by a charismatic stranger named **Jacques**, who extended an offer of adventure and treasure. They accepted — each with their own motives in mind.

## ⛵ Aboard the New Ship
On deck, the crew met:
- **[[Pipque Varns.png]]**
- **[[Pasha Varns.png]]**
- **[[Omayo (Tounge Click).png]]** – a seemingly quiet figure with a hidden dagger.

### Tensions Rise:
- Fae mocked Zackie, making her cry.
- Omayo tried to intervene, mistaking Zackie for a child, only to become the next target of Fae’s cruel words. She accused him of being a slave — which struck a nerve. Hurt and humiliated, Omayo fled to his quarters.

## 🛶 Slave-napping
Zackie and Tiddles hatched a daring escape:

- They discovered lifeboats at the stern and began preparing them.
- Tiddles deftly slipped past Jacques to load the boat.
- They tried to convince Omayo to join them, but fear held him back. He revealed he was bound in service to the **[[Zhentarim]]** — the Black Network.

Unable to sway him, Zackie made a grim choice:
- She **stabbed Omayo with poison**, weakening him enough to push him into the lifeboat.
- Tiddles **cut the rope**, and the escape began.

But their actions didn’t go unnoticed...

- Omayo’s cries roused the ship.
- Jacques and the others rushed to intervene.
- **Fae, now siding with the Zhentarim**, fired bolts of magic down at the fleeing pair.

As a violent **thunderstorm** erupted around them, lightning flashing and waves crashing...
