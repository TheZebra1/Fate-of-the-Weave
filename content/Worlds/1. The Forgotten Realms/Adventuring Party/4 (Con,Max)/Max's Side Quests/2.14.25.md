# D&D Session Summary
## Key Events
### True [[Arcane Conduits. Magic & Material|Silver]] Filament
- Obtained a **True Silver Filament**, which, when attached to the helmet, activates the [Dread Helm](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/wondrous-items:dread-helm).
### [[Safira Watts.webp|Safira]] Arrives
- Overheard **Safira** speaking with **Warren**, promising to deliver "*something*" by "*tonight*".
### **Isolde’s Arrival and Fatal Deception**
- **Isolde**, the fallen cleric retainer, arrived outside, and was directed to provide a distraction.
- She attempted to deceive **Safira** into believing she was from the **[[Arcane Brotherhood]]**, but failed.
  - **Jackford**, realizing Safira’s keen perception, quickly adapted his plan.
    - In a brutal move, he **executed Isolde**, slicing her from head to toe.
    - Gaining **Safira’s complete trust**, he assumed the identity of an *Arcane Brotherhood* representative.
### **Safira's Plans and Jackford's Manipulation**
- **Safira** intended to deliver the **[[Soul Stone]]** to the **[[Arcane Brotherhood]]**.
- She mentioned that her **[[C.L.E.R.I.C.s]]** would meet them en route to the docks for transport to **[[Luskan]]**.
  - **Jackford** insisted they **pack and leave immediately**.
- The distant **howl of Grimjaw** signaled the approach of **Garek, the trainer**.
### **Eliminating Safira’s Forces**
- **Jackford disposed of Safira’s personal guard**, the **C.L.E.R.I.C.s**.
- He then **ambushed Safira** again, this time catching her completely off guard.
### **Chase Through the Streets of [[Waterdeep]]**
- A **drag-out street fight** erupted between **Jackford and Safira**, culminating in her **execution**.
	- After 3 Nat 1's and 3 back to back 9 points of damage the battleaxe has 3 hit points left.
- After the battle, **Jackford deactivated the robots** and **destroyed their remote**.
  - **10 bots and Safira** are **lootable**.
