## 🧪 Session Recap: Breakout at Velkynvelve
### ⚔️ The Battle Begins
- The party battled a Gray Ooze — but it devoured poor Jimjar before being destroyed.

### 🧗‍♂️ Scaling the Falls
- The group climbed the waterfall to break back into Velkynvelve.
- Xalzar crafted a Climber’s Kit from rope and bone to assist the ascent.

### 🔥 Chaos in the Compound
- Demons, Drow, and Quaggoths clashed all around as inmates scrambled to gather supplies.

### 🏃‍♂️ Kick Down the Door!
- Xalzar and Dalinar rushed toward the Shrine to reclaim their gear.

#### 💀 Confrontation with Ilvara
- They encountered Jorlan, Shoor, and Ilvara.
- Xalzar attempted to seduce Ilvara, Jorlan *decidedly* allowed him Xalzar to approach, Shoor *decidedly *did not and Ilvara was unimpressed.
  - ❌ She cast Ray of Sickness without hesitation, dropping him.
- Dalinar, disguised as a Drow, was praised for leading Xalzar back.
- Ilvara ordered Dalinar to take unconcious Xalzar to her quarters.

#### 🏚 Escape and Recovery
- Xalzar, despite his injuries, was determined to continue his path of vengeance.
- Dalinar found a Potion of Greater Healing and revived him.
- Xalzar opened the gear chest but triggered a trap, falling unconscious again.
- Dalinar was left to choose from a pile of magical items.
- Sounds overhead suggested Shoor and Ilvara were repositioning.

> ⏳ Time is running out for our prison breakers...
