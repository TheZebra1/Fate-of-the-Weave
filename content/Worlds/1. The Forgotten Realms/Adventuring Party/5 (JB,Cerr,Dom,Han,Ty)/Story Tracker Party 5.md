# 🐉 D&D Campaign Story Tracker

## 🌌 Long-Term Plot Points
> **Big Questions & Mysteries:**
- **Soul Manipulation:** Who is extracting souls from the House of Healing, and for what purpose?
- **Safira's Plans:** What is Safira Watts' endgame with the **C.L.E.R.I.C. constructs** and the purloined plans?
- **The Man in the Top Hat:** What connection does the man Safira contacted via **Crystal Conferencer** with the password "Nanther" have to ongoing events?
- **Unthril Zond's Secrets:** What other dark dealings did Zond have before Whiskey took over his body?
- **Kenku Persecution:** Why does the **Purification Society** hunt Kenku, and how can Sable escape their grasp?
- **Jaida Scorned:** What is her endgame, and how did she acquire the power to level the Guild building?
- **Mol's Silver Circlet:** Why did Peeta give this to Mol, and what is its significance?

## 🗺️ Main Quest
> **Current Goal:**  
The party has agreed to unite their goals: bringing down the **Purification Society** in Waterdeep, dealing with Jaida Scorned, and uncovering the sinister secrets behind the C.L.E.R.I.C. constructs.

> **Current Location:**  
Waterdeep, transitioning between the House of Healing and the city plaza following a dramatic encounter with Safira Watts.
