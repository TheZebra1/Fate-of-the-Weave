# 🧭 Campaign Recap: *Into the Depths*

It began with four souls drawn together by fate:  
**[[Korrak Stonebreaker.png|Korrak Stonebreaker]]**, **[[Kaelith Shadowsong.png|Kaelith Shadowsong]]**, **[[Elias Thorne.jpg|Elias Thorne]]**, and **[[Cassian Drake.png|Cassian Drake]]**.  
Each walked a different path:  
- 💪 **Korrak**, a goliath bound to honor and the spirits of ice and stone  
- 🗡️ **Kaelith**, a silent knife in the dark  
- 🧠 **Elias**, a strategist shaped by a deadly mentor  
- ✨ **Cassian**, wild magic incarnate

Together, they were unstoppable. Until **Elias vanished.**

---
Their search led deep into the **Underdark**, where they were drawn into a **shared dream**—a vision? A warning?

There, they faced:
- A calculating **[[Liam Fenn.gif|Shadar-Kai alchemist]]**
- A brutal **[[Clockwork.gif|warforged gunslinger]]**
- A **[[Killian Nanther (Clockwork Hand).png|Shadowy Assassin]]** who fought like **Elias** himself

The team was defeated. In the final moment, Elias’s voice echoed:  
> _“Don’t come looking for me!!”_

They awoke to find **Chalito**, a kobold who *Shawshanked* his way out of a drow slave camp.  
Kaelith convinced him (with psychic flair) that he was a **god**. Chalito was released  and fled into the darkness.

Following the tunnel, the party reached **[[Dolblunde.png|Dolblunde]]**, now a ruined drow stronghold.  
At the gates, a **cave fisher** was slain. Inside, they silently took out orcish guards and infiltrated the city.

On the walls, they found a **Waterdhavian strike team** cocooned in webs:
- 🛡️ **Jean Lightshield** – Human Fighter  
- 🐲 **Draven Ironclaw** – Dragonborn Fighter  
- 🎓 **Tasselhoff Burrfoot** – Gnome Wizard

They carried a strange **"Nullification Box"**—purpose unclear, tempers flared, and Korrak *ripped it away*, forcing the squad to retreat, humiliated.

Then came the **spider con**, convincing Lolth’s servants they were her chosen. With spider allies secured, the squad ventured deeper.

They freed **[[Mateus Flutterbuckets.png|Mateus Flutterbuckets]]**, a kidnapped fairy from the **[[Witchlight Carnival]]**, and met a mysterious *dragonborn overseer* lounging before a hoard. He claimed neutrality—paid well by the drow to *look the other way*.

It was here they uncovered a deeper plot:  
🔻 **[[Melvaunt Incorporated]]** was supplying **temple components** via lava-rafted shipments—working directly with the drow.

A **[[Garrick Voss.png|well-dressed agent]]** of Melvaunt was spotted discussing business with a powerful drow woman at the lava docks.

> Elias’s trail rekindled... but so too was the drow threat.  
> One led to answers. The other, to danger.

***They chose danger.***

They hunted the operation through tunnels—killing scouts, ambushing **fungal troglodytes**, stealing weapons.  
Spider allies helped infiltrate the main camp, but the drow retaliated brutally: their spider friends were **slaughtered and impaled**.

After dodging a deadly trap, the squad returned and **crippled the camp’s mobility** by killing some of their beasts of burden.

But then...  
☕ A curious **[[Kaldi Bunna.png|goblin barista]]** stumbled onto the scene.  
Kaelith tricked him into revealing the camp’s defenses—then executed him before he could alert others.

Unfortunately, some guards have noticed the goblin’s corpse.  
An alarm is imminent.

## 🎭 **What will Gloom Squad do?**
Will they **run**?  
Or will they **fight** and make their stand?

# **More History**
### **Korrak’s Past**
Korrak, a former marine in Waterdeep, served under Captain [[Roderick Stormborne]] on the WNS *Feelin’ Nauti*. When [[Blackbeard]] attacked aboard *The Queen Anne’s Revenge*, Korrak led a defense, saving his captain and rallying his crew. Despite a fierce battle, Blackbeard’s reinforcements overwhelmed them. Korrak, refusing to retreat, swore vengeance before being captured by Blackbeard’s living ropes.

### **Cassian’s Past**
At Strixhaven, Cassian engaged in a magical duel with fellow student [[Eldric Thornwell]], debating the merits of chaos magic. Despite Eldric’s experience, Cassian’s unpredictable sorcery secured his victory, earning him a prized staff.

### **Kaelith’s Past**
Kaelith, an ex-assassin, reflected on his path over a fireside talk with Elias. Elias gifted him a medallion as a symbol of their shared struggle to leave behind a life of violence.

### **Encounters & Wild Magic**
- Cassian’s Wild Magic surged, causing him to grow a sixth finger and age twenty years.
- Korrak landed a devastating punch on a kobold, dislodging a fungus from its body.
- Kaelith cleared the walls with precision [[Stealth Kill]]s.
- Solved riddles to gain the favor of local phase spiders, earning recognition as ***Chosen of [[Lolth]]***.

### **Troglodyte Ambush & The Worm’s Vision**
- The party was ambushed by troglodytes; Korrak triggered a tunnel collapse, sealing the path back to [[Dolblunde]].
- A juvenile worm creature burst from a troglodyte, attempting to incapacitate the party with **Hypnotic Pattern**, but was resisted and slain.
- Cassian’s **NAT 20** resistance granted him a vision of the worm’s race suffering extinction, ancient wastelands, and magical cities crashing from the sky.
- They looted a **map** revealing potential dangers and resources in the region.
