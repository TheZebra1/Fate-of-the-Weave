## 🎭 Session Recap: Enter Lidbrani
### 🎤 Strange Encounters
- Lidbrani met:
  - 🎭 Candlefoot the Mime
  - 🧜‍♀️ Palasha the Mermaid
  - 🐦 Kettlesteam the Kenku

#### 🕵️ Uncovering the Mystery
- Through songs, heckles, interrogations, and confessions, Lidbrani learned the carnival hid deeper secrets.
- Suddenly—💥 an assassin attacked, knocking out Kettlesteam!
- Palasha defended Lidbrani and gave him:
  - 🧪 A mermaid tear  
  - 🗺 Directions to the Ascending Tunnel  
  - 🐉 Passage granted by Thermontarax

### 👂 Eavesdropping
- Lidbrani overheard Mr. Witch and Light:
  - ⚠️ They’re in over their heads with the Drow.
  - 🧩 Mention of a "Station", mercenaries, and a greater plot.

### 🏴‍☠️ Flashback: Korrak on the *Queen Anne’s Revenge*
- Years earlier, **Korrak** was captured and imprisoned aboard the *Queen Anne’s Revenge*.
- 🪵 Pirates mocked him through iron bars for what felt like months.
- Day after day, Korrak sharpened the foot of a stool into a deadly point, biding his time.
- ⚡ One day, he lashed out with lightning speed, badly injuring a pirate through the bars.

- Enter **Blackbeard**, who reprimanded his crew for tormenting a guest when ship chores remained undone.
- 🗣 He then sat with Korrak, engaging in a heart-to-heart.
  - Discovered Korrak's obsession with strength and hunger for power.
  - Offered an apprenticeship, promising Korrak the tools to one day rival Blackbeard himself.

- 🤝 A dark accord was struck.
  - Korrak would wait, learn —🔥 *hoping to burn it all down*.

### ⛏ Into the Mines
- The party explored the final mining zone.
- 💀 Attacked by darkmantles!
  - 🔥 Fireball destroyed two.
  - 😵 One latched onto Korrak’s face before being defeated.

### 🧟 Echoes of Dolblunde
- Lidbrani retraced the party’s steps through Dolblunde.
- He met Vel'Zhara T’sarran and her undead gnome Reginald.
- A fight broke out after Lidbrani mentioned Melvaunt and Killian Nanther.
  - 🤝 They momentarily aligned in goals.
  - ⚫ Vel’Zhara warned of a great darkness threatening the surface world.

### 🐀 Other Discoveries
- 🎁 Discovered a Karsite, claimed by Korrak.
- 🐾 Found the Rat Network, but it was too dangerous to descend.
- 🚚 Raided two caravans left behind by followers of Lolth.
- 💔 Left Fessa behind at Camp Kurtel, murmuring:
  - “I am Fessa. I am Fessa. I... I... I am.....”
