### Korrak’s Flashback  
Korrak, once a marine in Waterdeep, served on the WNS *Feelin’ Nauti* under Captain [[Roderick Stormborne]]. During a vicious attack by the pirate Blackbeard and his ship *The Queen Anne’s Revenge*, the *Feelin’ Nauti* fell into chaos. With Captain Stormborne incapacitated, Korrak made sure he’d survive the onslaught, pushing him to safety below decks. Rallying the other marines with cries of *"Never forgive, never forget! Do it once, and do it right!"*, Korrak led a fierce counterattack, even saving his friend James from death.
![[Blackbeard.png|250]]

However, the tide turned when [[Blackbeard]] boarded the *Feelin' Nauti* with reinforcements. As Korrak ordered a retreat to the lifeboats, he stayed behind to confront the pirate king alone. A furious clash ensued, with Korrak lifting Blackbeard by the throat, vowing, *"I don’t care if it’s today, in this life or the next, but I WILL kill you."* But Blackbeard, with flaming dreadlocks and a menacing sneer, answered, *"Lucky for me it isn’t that day,"* as living ropes ensnared Korrak, pulling him aboard *The Queen Anne’s Revenge* to face an uncertain fate in captivity.

### Cassian’s Flashback  
![[Biblioplex.png|150]] ![[Eldric Thornwell.png|150]]

At Strixhaven’s magical college, Cassian faced off against his classmate [[Eldric Thornwell]] in a heated duel over chaos magic’s place in the arcane arts. Eldric argued for disciplined magic, while Cassian saw promise in the unpredictable. Despite Eldric’s experience, Cassian’s own chaotic abilities turned the tide with unpredictable effects that shocked even him, securing his victory. As the duel’s spoils, Cassian won an expensive staff from Eldric, marking a significant triumph in his journey as a sorcerer.

### Kaelith’s Flashback  
![[Elias Thorne.jpg|250]] ![[Shadow Syndicate.png|250]]

In a quiet memory, Kaelith shared a fireside moment with his closest friend, Elias. Both ex-assassins trying to leave behind their pasts, they reflected on the purpose of survival and their difficult journeys away from violence. Elias, in a show of brotherhood, gave Kaelith a medallion from his past, a keepsake to remind Kaelith to stay true to his path and his choice to leave behind a life of darkness.
