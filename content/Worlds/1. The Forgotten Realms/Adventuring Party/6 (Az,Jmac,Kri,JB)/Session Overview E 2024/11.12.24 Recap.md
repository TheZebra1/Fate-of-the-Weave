### Enter [Dolblunde](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dolblunde)

![[Spiders of Dolblunde.png|center|950]]
- Korrak, Cassian, & Kaelith passed the *fungus* covered bodies of Kobolds slain upon the dirt & rock of the [[Underdark]]. 
- Kaelith snuck closer to the gates of the ruined city [[Dolblunde]], but as they approached Korrak strode forward confidently & knocked on wooden doors along the city walls
	- As he did the Cave Fishers dangling above him extruded their filaments at him hoisting him high up into the air. 
		- *In this time Korrak let out a girlish and embarrassing scream, remembering his long-time morbid fear of spiders.*
- The group battled and killed the Cave Fishers, Goblins, Orcs, and Drow carving a path of death
- Finally, they arrived at a stone bridge & a lava river.

### Notable Moments 
![[Cassian's 6th Finger.png|500]]

**Wild Magic, Jean Lightshield a Mysterious Box**
- Cassian grew a *6th Finger* **(Wild Magic)**
	- Became *20 years old* **(Wild Magic)**
- Korrak punched a near dead kobold with such incredible force that it expectorated a piece of *fungus* into the air.
- Kaelith cleared the walls with [[Stealth Kill]]s
- The party met:
	- **Jean Lightshield** - Human Fighter
	- **Draven Ironclaw** - Dragonborn Fighter
	- **Tasselhoff Burrfoot** - Gnome Wizard
		- Jean was grateful for rescue, but took great offense at some "*vigilante*'s" believing they could do the job his fallen comrades could not.
			- He stated his plan was to return to his father and cobble together another strike team to infiltrate the city.
		- Korrak ripped away a small magical box, *of apparent importance*, from Jean.
			- Jean stormed off with his last two men promising to pay back their offenses if he crossed paths with them again.
