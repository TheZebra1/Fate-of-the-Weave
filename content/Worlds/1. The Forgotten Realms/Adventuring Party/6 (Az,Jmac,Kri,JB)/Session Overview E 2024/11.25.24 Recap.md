# Session 5 TLDR Notes
- The party is ambushed by troglodytes, and Korrak causes a tunnel collapse blocking the way back to [[Dolblunde]].
- During the fight, a juvenile worm creature bursts from one troglodyte, attempts a **Hypnotic Pattern**, but is resisted and killed.
- Cassian has a **NAT 20** moment, glimpsing the creature’s disturbing visions of its race and world.
- The party discovers a map after looting and rests before investigating the Drow camp.
- They narrowly avoid a deadly trap laid by the Drow Captain and explore the aftermath of a grim scene.
- Kaelith interrogates a Goblin coffee-maker under a false guise, extracting information before the Goblin is killed.
- Gloom Squad initiates combat as the Drow attempt to flee for reinforcements.

## Session Notes

### Trashy Troglodytes
- The party is ambushed by five stinking monstrosities thirsty for blood.  
- Korrak slams one into the wall, triggering part of the unstable tunnel to collapse, blocking the way back to [[Dolblunde]].  
- Gloom Squad dispatches the troglodytes, though Kaelith accidentally throws a psychic dagger at Korrak mid-fight, causing some tension.  
- One troglodyte behaves erratically, even attacking its allies during the fight.  
	- Upon its defeat, a strange juvenile worm creature bursts forth.  
	- The worm attempts to cast **Hypnotic Pattern** to incapacitate the party, but they all steel their minds against the psychic invasion.  
		- Notably, Cassian rolls a **NAT 20**, not only resisting the attack but briefly glimpsing the creature’s inner thoughts.  
			- He sees visions of:  
				- *Freedom for the creature's race.*  
				- *The life force of ancient regions drained and transformed into barren wastelands.*  
				- *Magical cities crashing down from the sky in catastrophic ruin.*  
	- Ultimately, the juvenile monster is no match for Gloom Squad and is killed. The party harvests it for poison.  
- After looting the monsters, the party rolls a (6) on a d6 and finds a map revealing the layout of a nearby region.  
	- *(Insight checks can be made about the map to infer potential dangers or resources in the region.)*

### A New Day, A New Danger
- The party takes a long rest.  
- Upon awakening, they cautiously approach the Drow camp to assess its condition.  
	- At the camp’s perimeter, they discover the gruesome impalement of their spider "*allies*" on [[Underdark#Underdark Fungi Reference Table|Zurkhwood Trees]].  
	- Pressing forward, they unknowingly trigger a trap set by the Drow Captain for any "*Chosen of Lolth*" seeking to mourn their traitorous friends.  
		- The trap springs, causing the ground to give way!  
			- Incredibly, the party displays extraordinary dexterity, diving for safety and evading what could have been certain death for one or more of them.

### A Cup of Cold [[Kaldi Bunna.png|Kaldi]]
- The party begins systematically assassinating donkeys around the Drow camp, aiming to cripple the movement of the caravan transporting weapons, supplies, and loot.  
	- Their plan proceeds smoothly until...  
- A small, fair-skinned Goblin wearing an apron and carrying a hot pot of coffee notices the mysterious death of a donkey.  
	- The donkey, struck with a psychic dagger, shows no visible wounds—a curious and suspicious death.  
- As the Goblin investigates, [[Kaelith Shadowsong.png|Kaelith]] reaches into his mind and discovers his desire for clean surface water to brew better coffee.  
- Exploiting this, Kaelith assumes the guise of a "***Coffee Master seeking to test a worthy pupil***" to manipulate the Goblin.  
	- The Goblin accepts the "Coffee Test," but there are no coffee-related questions.  
	- Instead, Kaelith interrogates the Goblin about the camp’s layout and security measures.  
	- Gradually, the Goblin grows suspicious, piecing together that he’s in danger. Before he can flee, a psychic dagger pierces his mind, leaving him lifeless on the ground.  

---

### A Waste of Good Kaldi
- A Drow patrolman discovers the Goblin’s unresponsive body and summons another patrolman to investigate.  
	- Together, they lament the waste of good coffee and deduce that Kaldi’s death signals a likely threat.  
	- They sprint off to alert their Captain—but Gloom Squad strikes from the shadows!  
- The party initiates a surprise round. **ROLL INITIATIVE!**
