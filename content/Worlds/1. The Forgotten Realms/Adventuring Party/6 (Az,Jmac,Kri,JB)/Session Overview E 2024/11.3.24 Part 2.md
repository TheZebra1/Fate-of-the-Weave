## **Full Summary**  
*Warning: Lengthy Recap Below!*
### Campfire Reflections & Arena Battle  
Around the campfire, the party discussed their goal of finding Elias, Kaelith’s friend who went missing a month ago, leaving only a blood-stained note: *"Don't come for me."* After talking, they eventually fell asleep.

Suddenly, they awoke in a stone arena, facing three new opponents:
- A mechanized cowboy wielding pistols,
- A shadar-kai throwing gadgets and explosives, and
- A dark, sword-wielding leader with necrotic energy, whose combat style eerily matched Elias’s—despite looking nothing like him.

The battle was fierce and overwhelming, with each hero falling one by one. As Korrak and Cassian went down, visions of Elias appeared, urging them to abandon their search and warning that it would only end in darkness and death. 

In the chaos, Kaelith noticed a glowing chest. He managed to pick the lock and found a picture of a man with eyes identical to Elias’s. As darkness closed in on him, Kaelith was also pulled under, and the party awoke back at camp, disturbed and convinced the man in the photo was related to Elias.

### "Nobody Enslaves Chalito"  
![[Chalito.png|250]]

After the eerie dream, they took turns on watch. On the final shift, a loud crash came from a nearby cavern wall as a kobold with a shovel and a metal bucket-helmet burst through, proclaiming his freedom with, *"Nobody enslaves Chalito!"* Startled to see the party, Chalito froze. Kaelith used his psychic abilities to communicate, and the kobold, believing it was the voice of a god, nervously shared details of his escape from a nearby place he described as a “death trap.” Kaelith released him, and Chalito scurried off into the Underdark.

---

### Discovery of the Ruined City  
![[Dolblunde.png|250]]

Intrigued, the party followed Chalito’s tunnel and eventually reached an opening. There, they saw the ruins of an ancient, ominous city lying in wait just 300 feet away. With curiosity and caution, they prepared to explore the city, ready for whatever dark secrets or dangers it might hold.
