# Szass Tam
## Quick Summary
**Tagline / Descriptor:** The immortal lich ruler of Thay, orchestrating dark plots to expand his dominion.  
**Social Hubs:** [[The Citadel]], [[Thaymount]], [[Surcross]]  
**Affiliations:** [[Red Wizards of Thay]], [[Dread Legions]], [[Thayan Bureaucracy]]

## Personality & Traits
- **Temperament:** Calculating and patient
- **Mannerisms:** Speaks with cold precision; often accompanied by an aura of dread
- **Values:** Power, control, and the pursuit of necromantic knowledge
- **Fears:** Challenges to his authority, failure in his dark rituals
- **Desires:** Ultimate dominion over Faerûn, ascension to godhood
- **Quirks:** Maintains the illusion of his former human appearance; rarely shows his true lich form

## Narrative Profile
### Origin & Background
- **Heritage:** Member of the influential Tam family of Thay
- **Upbringing:** Rose through the ranks of the Red Wizards, mastering necromancy
- **Training/Skills:** Exceptional necromancer with mastery over undead creation and control

### Major Life Events
- **Turning Point:** Achieved lichdom to extend his life and power
- **Current Status:** Supreme ruler of Thay, commanding vast undead armies
- **Hidden Truths:** Allegedly under the influence or command of the ancient lich Larloch

## Abilities
| Power / Trait | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| Master Necromancer | Commands powerful necromantic spells and rituals |
| Undead Legion Commander | Controls vast armies of undead, including liches and vampires |
| Immortality | As a lich, possesses eternal undeath unless his phylactery is destroyed |
| Illusory Appearance | Uses magic to maintain a semblance of his former human visage |

## Relationships
### Allies
- **[[Larloch]]** – Ancient lich rumored to have influence over Szass Tam's actions
- **[[Undead Generals]]** – Loyal commanders leading his undead legions

### Enemies
- **[[Council of Zulkirs]]** – Former ruling body of Thay, many opposed his rise to power
- **[[Velsharoon]]** – Rival necromancer deity, with whom Szass Tam has a contentious history

### Key Connections
[[Dalinar]] - [[Hand of Thay]]
## Hooks & Secrets
- **Rumors:** Whispers suggest Szass Tam seeks to perform a ritual to unmake the world and reshape it in his image
- **Secrets:** His phylactery's location is unknown, making him effectively immortal
- **Adventure Hook:** Players might be drawn into a plot to thwart one of Szass Tam's dark rituals or to find and destroy his phylactery

## Quotes
