**Tav**, or _“The Tav”_, as they came to be known, was a _shapeshifting_ figure of largely unknown origin. Accounts of their _age, background, abilities,_ and even _appearance_ varied so wildly that contemporary historians can only conclude Tav possessed a **cosmic gift**—the power to appear to each individual as **their heart desired**.

While the truth of Tav’s nature remains shrouded in myth, one fact endures across all tellings: Tav led a band of adventurers to [[Baldur's Gate]], where they challenged and ultimately overthrew [[Enver Gortash]], [[Orin the Red]], and [[Ketheric Thorm]]—those who had enslaved an _**Netherbrain**_ through the might of the [[Crown of Karsus]].

Many stood beside this hero in the final battle for [[Baldur's Gate]], the [[Sword Coast]], and perhaps even [[Toril]] itself. Yet, when the last scream faded and the Netherbrain sank into the harbor, **Tav had vanished**.

The bards of the Realm end his tale in a different manner each time they sing it however the most popular are sung by [[Volothamp Geddarm]] who often claims: 
- Tav fell in battle, consumed by the psychic onslaught of the Netherbrain.
- It became a [[mind-flayer-xmm|Mind Flayer]] and chose self-annihilation to protect the realm. 
- Tav **descended willingly into [[Baator#1. Avernus|Avernus]]**, vanishing into the heart of the [[Blood War]] for reasons known only to it.
