# Maligor
## Profile

#### What They Want:
- *To overthrow Szass Tam and rule Thay.*
- *To conquer Aglarond.*

#### What They Fear:
- *Aging and mortality.*
- *Failure in his ambitions.*

#### What They Have Lost:
- *Control over his own fate.*

## History Summary
Born sometime before 1162 DR
### **Rise to Power**
- Became Zulkir of Alteration.
- Amassed an army of gnolls and darkenbeasts.

### **Allies**
- **Asp**: Spirit naga assistant. *Deceased*
- **Jutta**: Trusted apprentice. *MIA, likely deceased by age*

### **Conflict with Szass Tam**
- Plotted to seize mass wealth and ultimate control of Thay.
- Opposed Szass Tam's authority.
- Plans thwarted by Szass Tam and the Harpers.

### **Demise**
- Killed in 1362 DR during his coup attempt.
