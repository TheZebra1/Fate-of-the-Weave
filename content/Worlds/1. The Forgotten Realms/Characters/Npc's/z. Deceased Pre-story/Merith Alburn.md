### 📜 Name:
**Merith Alburn**, Illusionist and Inventor

### 🗺️ Historical Context:
**Brightswords Festival, 1359 DR – [[Waterdeep]]**

### 🎭 Notable Event:
During his stage show *"The Dragon’s Masquerade"*, Merith’s prototype wand misfired:
- Blinded three noble spectators
- Set part of a pavilion on fire

### ⚖️ Aftermath:
- Briefly banned from public performances
- Gained notoriety and cult fame
- Inspired the creation of the **[[Wand of Scalding Ink]]**

### 🧙 Lasting Legacy:
- Known for blending destructive magic with showmanship
- In Castle Ward, flashy magic is still jokingly called  
  **“*pulling a Merith*”**
