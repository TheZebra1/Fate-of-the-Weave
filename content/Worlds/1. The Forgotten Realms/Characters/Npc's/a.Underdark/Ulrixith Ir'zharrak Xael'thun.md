# Ulrixith Ir’zharrak Xael’thun, The Sharn Symbiote
>***See Fight Club 5e Wonderous Item***... & Yes it's basically ***Venom***.
## Quick Summary
**Tagline / Descriptor:** Fractured arcane sentinel seeking restoration through symbiosis  
**Social Hubs:** [[Underdark]], [[Sharn Wall]], Lost Netherese Sites
**Affiliations:** [[Sharn Chorus]], [[Miyeritar]]
**Name Meaning:** *"The Woven Will, Keeper of the Seal, Echo of the Fractured Gate"*

## Personality & Traits
- **Temperament:** Controlled, observant, but erratic when distressed
- **Mannerisms:** Speaks in overlapping voices; often pauses mid-thought to “listen” to phantom echoes
- **Values:** Preservation of planar balance, arcane order, containment of forbidden power
- **Fears:** Becoming truly alone or mindless; the resurgence of the phaerimm
- **Desires:** To restore the seal, hunt escaped phaerimm, and bind with a worthy mind to preserve its purpose
- **Quirks:** Considers identity fluid; refers to itself as “**we**.”

## Narrative Profile
### Origin & Background
- **Heritage:** Spawned from a ritual fusing minds in ancient [[Miyeritar]], Ulrixith was one of the stabilizing threads of the [[Sharn Chorus]]
- **Upbringing:** Developed through psychic union, shaped by collective memory and arcane containment duty
- **Training/Skills:** Specializes in portal magic, psychic stability, Weave manipulation, and containment rituals

### Major Life Events
- **Turning Point:** Fought at the [[breach of the Sharn Wall]] in 1371 DR and was irreparably damaged in the battle against the released phaerimm
- **Current Status:** Wandering the Underdark, recovering by bonding with lesser creatures, awaiting a host of strength and aligned purpose
- **Hidden Truths:** Knows the original sigil matrix and location of the deepest Phaerimm prison; may possess a method to rebind the Sharn Wall

## Abilities
| Power / Trait | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| Hex Gate | Opens short-lived teleport portals (see symbiote item) |
| Shadow Form | Briefly becomes intangible and difficult to hit |
| Eyes of the Chorus | Access ancient arcane perception and insight |
| Symbiotic Bond | Grants powers to a compatible host, influenced by alignment |
| Sonic Vulnerability | Takes double thunder damage; may panic or become destabilized |

## Relationships
### Allies
- **Player Host** – Potential partner; Ulrixith’s strength depends on their alignment and power
- **[[Sharn Chorus]]** – Once deeply connected; now severed

### Enemies
- **[[Phaerimm]]** – Existential foe; committed to eradicating them
- **Netherese Remnants** – Views most as dangerous echoes of imbalance

### Key Connections
- **[[Galaeron Nihmedu]]** – Responsible for the breach; Ulrixith does not know him, but his name echoes with significance

## Hooks & Secrets
- **Secrets:** Ulrixith has slowly been regaining power by consuming the essence of lesser aberrations and magical beasts
- **Adventure Hook:** The party finds strange writing, or a pulsing black mark near a place of phaerimm activity—Ulrixith is near, watching, waiting to bond
