A Red Dragon currently living in the ruins of [[Dolblunde]].

## History
Themberchaud was born in [[Gracklstugh]], the Duergar Capital, serving as their Wyrmsmith. The city always needs a Wyrmsmith to light the fires of it's forges which are the central focus of their economy.

Dragons are the perfect generators of forge flame, however the problem is that eventually they become powerful and ambitious to control and must be executed before that point.

Themberchaud is massively overweight, having been spoiled rotten and overfed since birth. However, his self preservative proved to be greater than his predecessors as he was able to discover the plan to execute and replace him much earlier than them and he escaped alive.

Having fled the city he seeks to rebuild his horde, amass strength, lose some weight and become the Dragon conqueror that the Duergar of [[Gracklstugh]] feared he could be.
