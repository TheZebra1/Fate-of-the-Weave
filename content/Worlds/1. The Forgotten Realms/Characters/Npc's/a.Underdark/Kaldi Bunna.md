# Kaldi Bunna
[[Kaldi's Coffee]]
## Profile

#### TL;DR (For Players):
- **Kaldi Bunna** is a **half-halfling, half-goblin** coffee artisan from the Underdark.
- He brews coffee for the Drow forces.
- Kaldi loves making perfect coffee, and the Drow’s ease of access to rare ingredients helps him do so.

#### What They Want:
- To perfect his coffee brewing skills.
- To eventually leave the Drow's service and return to the surface world.

#### What They Fear:
- Being stuck in the service of the Drow until he dies with no one of consequence appreciating his art.
- The Drow discovering his true feelings of resentment and turning on him.

#### What They Have Lost:
- His mother to a mind flayer. Father to old age.
- His ability to live his life on his own terms without being tied to the violent needs of the Drow.

---

## History Summary

Kaldi Bunna was born in the Deep Grotto, a subterranean oasis hidden deep within the Underdark. His mother, a halfling who had traveled from the surface, and his father, a goblin from a wandering tribe, gave him a unique upbringing. The Deep Grotto is a rare place in the Underdark where a small spring of clean water flows through the caves, surrounded by lush, bioluminescent fungi. It was here that Kaldi's passion for coffee brewing began.

As a young child, Kaldi learned the craft of brewing from his mother, who had once been a well-regarded barista in a surface-world town. When his mother was killed in a mind flayer raid, Kaldi fled deeper into the Underdark into Drow territory.

The Drow, impressed by his skills, forcibly recruited him to brew coffee for their soldiers and leaders. At first, Kaldi resented the Drow for using his passion to fuel their violent ambitions. But as he learned about their control over the Underdark and their ability to provide him with rare ingredients and pure spring water, he found a silver lining: access to the best possible resources to perfect his craft.
