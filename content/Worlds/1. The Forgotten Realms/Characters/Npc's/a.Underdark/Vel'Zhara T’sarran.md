## History Summary
Vel’Zhara was born the secret ***Szarkai/Albino/"Ghost Spider"*** daughter of **Matron Irae T’sarran**, the infamous undead priestess of Kiaransalee who once ruled Maerimydra. A child of prophecy or caution, Vel’Zhara was hidden away during House T’sarran’s final days, raised in a hidden sanctum with whispers of blood and vengeance shaping her mind.

**Irae** was defeated by adventurers before she could summon a revenant army and when **Maerimydra** fell, [[Vel'Zhara T’sarran]] was spirited away by loyal undead servants. Over time, she learned to hide who she was—using makeup, illusion, and false names. She was able to enroll in [[Sorcere]] where she mastered mastered ***shadow sorcery***.

Eventually, she found herself folded into a crucial Lolthite military expedition—disguised, useful, but expendable. So when a surfacer interloper, [[Cassian Drake]], used magic to remove her from the expedition her the dominos of her deception began to fall.
