# Satia Thornwell
## Profile

<small> Mother of [[Eldric Thornwell]], Wife of [[Marin Thornwell]]
#### What They Want:
- *Preserve and elevate the cultural heritage of Silverymoon.*
- *Safeguard magical and historical knowledge in the Silverymoon Archives.*
- *Promote Silverymoon’s reputation as a cultural and intellectual beacon of Faerûn.*
- *Host awe-inspiring public ceremonies, like Moonfest, to strengthen city pride.*

#### What They Fear:
- *Loss of Silverymoon’s cultural heritage to modernization or neglect.*
- *The misuse or theft of arcane knowledge entrusted to the Vault of Ages.*
- *The growing influence of external political factions, especially ones she sees as “unworthy” of Silverymoon’s legacy.*
  
#### What They Have Lost:
- *Faith in purely academic governance; she believes in the need for direct cultural engagement.*
- *Trust in the Thornwell family unity; her husband’s political ambitions often oppose her vision for Silverymoon.*
- *A friend and fellow historian lost in a mysterious “accident” within the Vault, leaving her suspicious of hidden agendas.*

---
### Key Details to Know:

1. **Roles & Influence**:
   - **Lorekeeper of the Vault of Ages**: Protects ancient magical texts and artifacts, ensuring they are used ethically. She's protective and secretive, prioritizing the city’s safety over innovation.
   - **Master of Ceremonies**: Organizes major festivals like **Moonfest**, using her illusion magic to create captivating celebrations that unite the people of Silverymoon.

2. **Personality & Motivations**:
   - **Traditionalist & Protector**: Values Silverymoon’s cultural and magical heritage. She’s wary of external influences that could threaten the city’s autonomy.
   - **Guarded & Cautious**: Her restrictive policies and secrecy have earned her a reputation as a protector who is resistant to change.

3. **Conflict & Relationships**:
   - **Tension with Her Husband, Marin**: They have opposing views—Satia prioritizes the city's independence, while Marin focuses on forging external alliances, even with questionable partners. This creates a rift between them, both politically and personally.
   - **Suspicious of Hidden Threats**: After the mysterious death of a close colleague in the Vault, she’s on high alert for any potential threats, making her more cautious and possibly paranoid.

4. **Potential Hooks for Player Interaction**:
   - **Investigate the Vault**: She may recruit players as allies to help investigate suspicious activities within the Vault, especially if they prove themselves trustworthy.
   - **Event Assistance**: Players might assist her in organizing or safeguarding major festivals, where her illusion magic plays a central role.
   - **Navigating the Thornwell Family Politics**: Players could find themselves caught between Satia’s protective instincts and Marin’s diplomatic ambitions, leading to moral and political dilemmas.
