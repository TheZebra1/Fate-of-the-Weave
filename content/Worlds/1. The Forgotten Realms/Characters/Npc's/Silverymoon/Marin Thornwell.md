## Profile

<Small>Father of [[Eldric Thornwell]], Husband of [[Satia Thornwell]]<big>
#### What They Want:
- *To establish [[Silverymoon]] as a dominant magical power in Faerûn, capable of rivaling Luskan and other major cities. Marin aims to leverage his position as Chairman of the Accreditation Board to forge alliances with influential arcane organizations, facilitate military collaborations, and enhance [[Strixhaven]]’s research capabilities in defensive and offensive magic, ultimately preparing Silverymoon for any threats from both internal and external adversaries.*

#### What They Fear:
- *That Eldric's embarrassing defeat in the duel might not only tarnish the family name but also weaken Marin's position in ongoing negotiations with key factions, including powerful wizards and mercenary guilds. He fears that if his son is seen as weak, it could embolden rivals like Luskan, who may exploit Silverymoon’s vulnerabilities.*

#### What They Have Lost:
- *The advantage of a strong family reputation in diplomatic discussions. Marin understands that a reputation for weakness could unravel the political capital he has built, risking not only their status but also Silverymoon's security in a volatile political landscape.*

## History Summary
Marin Thornwell, the Chairman of the [[Accreditation Board for Magical Institutions]], is a strategic thinker with aspirations to elevate Silverymoon’s standing in the broader magical community. He envisions a future where Silverymoon is not merely a safe haven for scholars but also a formidable political player, capable of defending itself against encroaching threats from cities like Luskan. Marin is focused on forging alliances with influential wizarding factions and securing military partnerships that could ensure Silverymoon's safety. The recent embarrassment involving Eldric's duel is a potential stumbling block that he fears could disrupt his carefully laid plans and political maneuvers.
