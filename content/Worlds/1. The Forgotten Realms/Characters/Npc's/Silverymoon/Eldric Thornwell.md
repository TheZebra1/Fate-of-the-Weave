# Eldric Thornwell
## Profile

<small> Son of [[Marin Thornwell]] & [[Satia Thornwell]]
#### What They Want:
- To reclaim his reputation after losing to [[Cassian Drake.png|Cassian Drake]] in a [[Spell Duel]].  
- Recognition and respect from his peers and mentors.  
- To prove his superiority in magic, particularly in abjuration.  

#### What They Fear:
- Living in the shadow of his father's, [[Marin Thornwell]], accomplishments.
- The possibility of never living up to his family's expectations.  
- The idea that he could end up not being the most powerful and prolific caster in his generation.

#### What They Have Lost:
- His pride and confidence after losing the duel with Cassian.  
- The respect of some peers who may now question his abilities.  
- A sense of security in his own magical prowess, leading to self-doubt.

## History Summary
Eldric Thornwell, the only child of Lord [[Marin Thornwell]] and Lady [[Satia Thornwell]], grew up in a prestigious household in [[Silverymoon]]. His father, the chairman of the [[Accreditation Board for Magical Institutions]], instilled high standards and a disciplined approach to magic. His mother, a renowned enchanter, encouraged creativity, creating a conflicting environment for Eldric.

Expected to excel at [[Strixhaven]], Eldric's entitlement and arrogance grew alongside the pressure to uphold his family's reputation. His confidence faced a severe blow after losing a public duel to Cassian Drake, which damaged his ego and tarnished his family’s name. 

Now, Eldric grapples with a deep-seated grudge against Cassian, a desire to reclaim his status, and the weight of his parents’ expectations, all while navigating the fine line between the disciplined path laid out for him and the chaotic allure of Wild Magic.
