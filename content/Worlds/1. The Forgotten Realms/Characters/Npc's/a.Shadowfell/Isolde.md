# Isolde
## Profile

#### What They Want:
- *To protect the innocent while pursuing justice and vengeance.*
- *To track down and destroy  [[The Caller]], who killed her companions.*
- *To control the growing whispers of [[Nepenthe]], her sentient Sun Blade.*

#### What They Fear:
- *Losing control over Nepenthe’s growing thirst for vengeance.*
- *Failing the memory of her fallen allies and those under her protection.*

#### What They Have Lost:
- *Her original companions*
- *Her past identity as an honorable warrior within the [[Order of the Arcane Blade]].*
- *A sense of clear purpose beyond her hunt for justice and revenge.*

[[Zybilna and Isolde Backstory]]
## History Summary

### **Early Life & Training in the Order**
- Isolde was once a devoted member of the **Order of the Arcane Blade**, excelling in touch-based spellcasting and martial combat.
- As an **eladrin warrior**, she mastered the **Rite of Dawn’s Embrace** and crafted her [[Dawnblades|Dawnblade]] which evolved over time into a [[dawnbringer-oota|Dawnbringer]]

### **Departure from the Order**
- Disillusioned with the Order’s growing **[[Order of the Arcane Blade#Modern Role & Divisions|Sul’ar]] movement**, which sought elven supremacy, Isolde rejected their views.
- She left to forge her own path, dedicated to justice for all, not just elves.

### **The Betrayal & Birth of the Carnival**
- Isolde and her band of warriors became renowned demon hunters.
- This ended in tragedy when **The Caller, acting on [[Zybilna]]’s orders, slaughtered her entire group**.
- Isolde, the sole survivor, was **left adrift, haunted by grief**.

### **The Carnival & Zybilna’s Deception**
- To escape her past, she **became bound to a wandering supernatural carnival** under a pact orchestrated by Zybilna.
- Unbeknownst to her, Zybilna manipulated events, ensuring she would remain **distracted from the truth**.
- Isolde continues to hunt The Caller, unaware of **the deeper deception**.
	- Her [[Dawnbringer]] was turned into **[[Nepenthe]]** giving it vengeful properties that affect Isolde and cause her to produce the [[Twisting]] in a wide radius.

### **Current Status**
- Isolde now leads ** [[The Carnival of Fear]]**.
- **Nepenthe constantly whispers**, urging her toward justice—**or vengeance**.
- She **remains blind to Zybilna’s true nature**, believing her a legend rather than the architect of her greatest tragedy.
