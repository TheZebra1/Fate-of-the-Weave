# Avilda Montrose
## Quick Summary
**Tagline / Descriptor:** Visionary artificer of [[Lantan]] descent, now leading innovation in Melvaunt  
**Social Hubs:** [[Ironlight Academy of Innovation]], [[Gond#Church of Gond|Church of Gond]], [[Melvaunt Inventors’ Circle]]  
**Affiliations:** [[Ironlight Academy of Innovation]], [[Gond#Church of Gond|Church of Gond]]

## Personality & Traits
- **Temperament:** Measured, intense, quietly ambitious  
- **Mannerisms:** Taps fingers in sequence when thinking; often stares into space mid-conversation when a new idea strikes  
- **Values:** Progress, ingenuity, self-determination  
- **Fears:** Obsolescence, being forgotten, losing her influence  
- **Desires:** To surpass Lantan’s legacy and make Melvaunt the new hub of invention  
- **Quirks:** Refuses to use premade tools if she can improve them; speaks to Gond aloud during moments of stress

## Narrative Profile
### Origin & Background
- **Heritage:** Born in Lantan to a minor artificer family with modest social standing  
- **Upbringing:** Apprenticed young in arcane engineering and theological theory under the Church of Gond  
- **Training/Skills:** Master of infused crafting, mechanical golem engineering, divine-artifice hybrids

### Major Life Events
- **Turning Point:** Survived the Spellplague while abroad; believed her people abandoned her  
- **Current Status:** Hierophant of Gond and Headmaster of Ironlight Academy in Melvaunt  
- **Hidden Truths:** Deeply resentful of Lantan’s return and of Danactar’s continued influence

## Abilities
| Power / Trait | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| Divine-Artificer Fusion | Can channel divine magic into mechanical constructs |
| Spelltech Golem Design | Custom-built defenders that patrol Ironlight |
| The Lantanese Lens | A unique monocle that identifies magical flaws instantly |
| Fear of Irrelevance | She’s driven, but sometimes recklessly overreaches to prove her worth |

## Relationships
### Allies
- **[[Ironlight Academy Faculty#Lynn Bora|Lynn Bora]]** – Trusted head of Alchemical Sciences, one of the few who knows Avilda’s inner turmoil  
- **[[Gond#Church of Gond|Church of Gond]]** – Her institutional power base and the origin of her divine authority

### Enemies
- **[[Danactar]]** – The returned Heirophant of Lantan; symbolic of all she feels betrayed by  
- **Traditionalists in Lantan** – Whisper of her heresies and claim she corrupted Gond’s pure faith with innovation

### Key Connections
- **[[Ironlight Academy Faculty]]** – Hand-picked, loyal, and progressive minds shaping the academy's vision

## Hooks & Secrets
- **Rumors:** Some say she’s preparing a device powerful enough to challenge divine will itself  
- **Secrets:** Keeps a locked schematic labeled “Project Rebirth” that references Lantan’s coordinates  
- **Adventure Hook:** A powerful invention of hers has gone missing—possibly stolen by a rival faction with ties to Lantan...

## Quotes

> "_You cannot innovate while clinging to ghosts._"
