## Profile
#### What They Want:
- *To ensure the Witchlight Carnival remains a place of wonder, joy, and mischief.*
- *To keep the carnival’s true **purpose and secrets hidden** from outsiders.*
- *To preserve the delicate balance of fey magic that powers the carnival.*
- *To sustain the **emotional high** he experiences through the carnival’s revelry.*

#### What They Fear:
- *Losing control of the carnival, either through external threats or treachery.*
- *The carnival’s mood darkening, stripping away the **intoxicating emotions** he thrives on.*
- *Being drawn back into the **darker nature of the Shadowfell**, where he once lived devoid of feeling.*
- *The return of **Zybilna**, whose fate is tied to the carnival’s deeper mysteries.*

#### What They Have Lost:
- *His past identity—before he became the **master of the Witchlight Carnival**, he was Urmius Umbrage, a severe and somber figure of the Shadowfell.*
- *A part of his freedom—though he runs the carnival, he is **bound by unseen forces**.*
- *His trust in outsiders, as the carnival has many enemies and hidden dangers.*
- *A true sense of stability, as his **emotional state is tied to the carnival’s atmosphere**.*

## History Summary
### **Origins & Partnership with Mr. Witch**
- **Urmius Umbrage** and **Naeryx Krumple** grew up as orphans in **[[Gloomwrought]]**, a city in the **[[Shadowfell]]**.
- Despite their contrasting personalities—Urmius being severe and Naeryx more reserved—they formed a lifelong friendship.
- Urmius acquired a run-down [[Carnival of Fear.webp|carnival]] operating on the fringes of the Shadowfell and invited Naeryx to manage its operations.
- They encountered **[[Isolde]]**, an eladrin carnival owner from the [[Feywild]]. After a night of revelry, they agreed to swap ownership of their carnivals.
- Embracing this opportunity, Urmius and Naeryx transformed the Fey-themed carnival into the **[[Witchlight Carnival]]**, adopting the personas of **[[Mister Light]]** and **[[Warren Nanther|Mister Witch]]**, respectively.

### **The Witchlight Carnival**
- The carnival is **more than a mere traveling spectacle**—it holds **mystical secrets tied to the Feywild**.
- It acts as a **conduit of emotion**, amplifying joy and wonder while masking sorrow and fear.
- Mr. Light serves as the **face of the carnival**, an exuberant host who ensures every guest is immersed in delight.
- He **feeds off the heightened emotions of the carnival**, feeling a ***near-euphoric high*** when the atmosphere is electric with excitement.
	- Using **subtle magic**, he **enhances the carnival’s joyous moods**, ensuring that visitors experience overwhelming positivity.
- When the carnival’s mood is ***low***, he **suppresses negative emotions**, muting the vibrancy of the carnival until balance is restored.
- He takes **great offense to anything or anyone** who diminishes the carnival’s cheer, reacting with **unexpected intensity**.


### **Allies & Enemies**
#### **Allies**
  - **Mr. Witch** – His trusted partner, who manages the business and watches warily as **Mister Light loses himself in the carnival’s influence**.
  - **Carnival Staff & Performers** – Many are bound to the carnival, willingly or otherwise, and some share **Mister Light’s emotional highs**.
  - **Fey Patrons & Tricksters** – He has alliances with **Feywild lords and mischievous spirits**, who revel in the carnival’s spectacle.
  - **Visitors Who Embrace the Magic** – Those who leave with joyful experiences fuel the carnival’s magic and **sustain Mister Light’s euphoria**.

#### **Enemies**
  - **The Hourglass Coven** – Their **schemes endanger the carnival**, threatening the delicate emotional balance Mister Light has cultivated.
  - **Zybilna (Tasha/Iggwilv)** – A complicated past—he knows more than he lets on, and her return could disrupt everything.
  - **Disruptive Guests & Meddlers** – Those who interfere with the carnival’s **emotional harmony** risk his wrath and may find themselves **lost between realms**.
  - **Cynics & Doubters** – Skeptics who resist the carnival’s magic or reject its wonders unsettle Mister Light, as they threaten his carefully crafted world.

### **Current Status**
- Continues to **travel the multiverse**, bringing the Witchlight Carnival **to new realms**.
- **Guards its secrets well**, ensuring that none **unravel the deeper fey pacts** that sustain it.
- **Elated when the carnival thrives**, yet vulnerable to **its fluctuating moods**.
- **Mister Witch watches with concern**, fearing that Mister Light is **becoming too dependent on the carnival’s magic**.
