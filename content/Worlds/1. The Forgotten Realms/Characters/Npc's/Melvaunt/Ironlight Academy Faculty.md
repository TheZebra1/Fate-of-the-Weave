## [[Ironlight Academy of innovation]]
### Avilda Montrose – Headmaster
![[Avilda Montrose.png|250]]
> [[Avilda Montrose.png|Show Players]]
- **Background:** Born in [[Lantan]], Avilda was away when the **[[Spellplague]]** struck, wiping her homeland from the map. Isolated and alone, she made her way to [[Melvaunt]], where her Lantanese brilliance found fertile ground.
- **Story:** Rising quickly in prominence, she became the **Hierophant of Gond** and later Headmaster. But she hides a bitter wound: when Lantan reappeared, so did **[[Danactar]]**, her rival and spiritual predecessor.
- **Notable Traits:** Visionary, charismatic, privately resentful, fiercely driven.

### Fendril Moonsong – Student Affairs Coordinator
![[Fendril Moonsong.jpg|250]]
[[Fendril Moonsong.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** A tiefling raised in the temples of [[Selûne]], Fendril turned from faith to focus on mental health and social dynamics.
- **Story:** Fendril champions balance in the high-pressure world of arcane academia. After losing a close friend to burnout during his studies, he vowed to reshape how schools support their students.
- **Notable Traits:** Empathetic, insightful, warm but firm.

### Lira Dappledew – Dormitory Matron
![[Lira Dappledew.jpg|250]]
[[Lira Dappledew.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** A halfling from [[Amn]], Lira once ran a popular inn before tragedy pushed her toward a career helping young minds.
- **Story:** Known for homemade treats and an iron sense of fairness, she treats students like her extended family and isn't afraid to confront staff when students are mistreated.
- **Notable Traits:** Nurturing, clever, unshakably calm.

## School of Arcano-Mechanics
### Lynn Bora – Head of Arcano-Mechanics
![[Lynn Bora.jpeg|250]]
[[Lynn Bora.jpeg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** Studied at [[Strixhaven]]. Lynn’s rise was shaped by a powerful vision of a magical catastrophe.
- **Story:** Convinced the **[[Spellplague]]** may return by means of a close [[Eldrin|friend]] of hers, she pushed too hard and lost her best friend. Now she channels that guilt into arcano-mechanical breakthroughs.
- **Notable Traits:** Analytical, burdened by foresight, fiercely protective of students.

## School of Metallurgy
### Eliver Oakenshield – Head of Metallurgy
![[Eliver Oakenshield.jpg|250]]
[[Eliver Oakenshield.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** Hailing from the dwarven halls of [[Citadel Adbar]], Eliver comes from a long line of smiths—until his clan’s forge was bought out by a mercantile syndicate.
- **Story:** Now he teaches future smiths the artistry and soul of metalwork while quietly trying to recover his family’s legacy.
- **Notable Traits:** Stern, proud, a master craftsman with a hidden tenderness.

### Vondal Mossmantle – Forgewarden
![[Vondal Mossmantle.jpg|250]]
[[Vondal Mossmantle.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** A Firbolg druid turned metalwright, Vondal’s unique fusion of nature and industry caused scandal in his homeland.
- **Story:** He believes the forge and forest must coexist and pushes Ironlight toward green innovations.
- **Notable Traits:** Gentle giant, philosophical, protector of balance.

## School of Constructs & Golemancy
### Faelarion Windwhisper – Head of Experimental Constructs
![[Faelarion Windwhisper.jpg|250]]
[[Faelarion Windwhisper.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** Once a high elf courtier in the Feywild, Faelarion left his post to explore the ethics of artificial life.
- **Story:** He once created a sentient golem he saw as a son—but it vanished, leaving him emotionally fractured and obsessed with finding it.
- **Notable Traits:** Elegant, tragic, unwavering in his ideals of life and autonomy.

## School of Thaumaturgy
### Rylor Flametongue – Chief Thaumaturgy Engineer
![[Rylor Flametongue.jpg|250]]
[[Rylor Flametongue.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** Raised in the elemental chaos, Rylor escaped a life of servitude and carved a name in Melvaunt with raw arcane energy and unmatched will.

## School of Enchantment
### Alanna Featherwind – Professor of Enchanted Design
![[Alanna Featherwind.jpg|250]]
[[Alanna Featherwind.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** A once-renowned fashion enchanter in [[Waterdeep]], Alanna burned out under pressure and retreated into academia.

## School of Illusions
### Twilla Starshine – Professor of Illusory Engineering
![[Twilla Starshine.jpg|250]]
[[Twilla Starshine.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** A gnome prodigy from [[Neverwinter]], Twilla believes illusions should serve more than entertainment—they should keep people safe.

## School of Necrotechnology
### Brynn Darkbloom – Lead Research Necrotechnician
![[Brynn Darkbloom.jpg|250]]
[[Brynn Darkbloom.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** Half-orc necromancer who grew up in the **[[Gravebind Monastery]]**, studying spiritual reclamation through bone and sinew.

## School of Transmutation

![[Quirin Stoneshape.jpg|250]]
[[Quirin Stoneshape.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** Earth genasi whose alchemical earthshaping helped build Melvaunt’s new bridge systems.

## Archives & Lore
### Saphira Tomescribe – Archivist and Recordskeeper
![[Saphira Tomescribe.jpg|250]]
[[Saphira Tomescribe.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** An Aarakocra trained in the vast Skyvault Libraries of [[Halruaa]], Saphira came to Ironlight after her previous archive burned down.
- **Story:** She hoards blueprints, schematics, and ancient scrolls like treasure, fearing the loss of knowledge more than death.
- **Notable Traits:** Obsessed with documentation, quietly funny, razor sharp memory.

## Alchemical Sciences
### Olwen Bitterbrew – Potion Lab Director
![[Olwen Bitterbrew.jpg|250]]
[[Olwen Bitterbrew.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** A goblin brewer who once ran a community potion shop before academia recruited her for her results.
- **Story:** Olwen believes magical education should be for all—not just the privileged. Her potions are popular among students, faculty, and the black market.

### Calista Ironquill – Chair of Techno-Historical Studies
![[Calista Ironquill.jpg|250]]
[[Calista Ironquill.jpg|Show Players]]
- **Background:** A dragonborn scholar raised among Netherese ruins, Calista specializes in linking old magic with emerging technology.
