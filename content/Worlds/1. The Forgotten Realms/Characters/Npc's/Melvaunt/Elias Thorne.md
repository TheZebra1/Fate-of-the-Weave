# **Elias Thorne – The First and Only Survivor**
## **Project Lidbrani – Melvaunt Inc.'s Ultimate Experiment**
### **Origins: The Creation of the Perfect Combatant**
[[Melvaunt Incorporated]]., a powerful **artificer megacorporation**, specialized in **magitek augmentation, experimental locomotion technology, and high-risk military contracts**. One of their many ambitious projects, **Project Lidbrani**, aimed to create the ultimate high-speed combatant, capable of **eliminating threats far beyond normal human capability**.

### **The Testing Grounds – The Galena Mountain Mines**
The first phase of testing was conducted using **workers from the [[Galena Mountains|Galena Mountain]] mines**—a population of **malnourished, desperate laborers** who were either coerced or bribed into participation.  
- The **surgical procedures** involved enhancing **reaction speed, spatial awareness, and physical resilience** through **arcane augmentation and biomechanical modifications**.
- **Every test subject died.** Some suffered **neural overload**, others **collapsed from organ failure**, and many **simply couldn’t process the extreme speed required**.
- Melvaunt Inc. **believed the failures were due to poor physical conditioning**, so they shifted to **testing on their own elite soldiers**.

### **The Ultimate Failure – Until Elias**
Even among their best-trained operatives, **no one survived the process**. That is, **until Elias Thorne**—the **first and only success**.  
- His **nervous system adapted**, granting him **inhuman reflexes** and the ability to perceive **movement and time at an accelerated rate**.
- His **bone structure was reinforced** with arcane materials, allowing him to withstand the **intense forces exerted by the ODM gear**.
- Most importantly, **his body accepted the neural interface ports**—open **mechanical sockets embedded in his lower back**, allowing the **ODM system to sync directly with his brain**.

Elias **doesn’t just use the gear—he is part of it**. It reacts at **the speed of thought**, allowing him to move faster than **any normal being could ever hope to**.

## **The Cost of Survival**
Though only Elias survived and the feat has been historically unreplicatable, **[[Project Lidbrani]] was deemed a success**, as **as only one soldier was required for what [[Melvaunt Incorporated]] had planned for the future**.  
- **Without the ports in his spine, no one else can even attempt to use the ODM gear.**
	- **Without the [[Aetherium]] bracing his skeletal structure, the forces would rip a normal human apart.**
- **Even Melvaunt Inc. doesn’t fully understand why Elias survived.**  

While he gained superhuman agility, Elias was left with **insomnia and hyper-awareness**—his mind **never slows down**, always calculating **every possible movement and trajectory**, even when at rest.

## **The Syndicate’s Involvement**
- When Elias **left the Syndicate, they lost their only working prototype**.
- Knowing they **could never replicate** his abilities, they resorted to **the one thing that could bring him back**—**kidnapping his brother**.

Now, they **need Elias to take down the Planar Guardians**, the only beings standing between them and **the power to break the divine seals and merge the [[Material Plane]] and the [[Shadowfell]]**.

## **Why No One Else Can Use the Gear**
- **The Surgery is Lethal** – No one else has **survived the augmentation process**.  

Even if someone **stole** his gear, **they wouldn’t be able to operate it**. **He is the only one.**

## **Visual Details & Unique Traits**
- **Lower Back Ports** – Open **mechanical sockets** where the ODM gear **plugs directly into his spine**.  
- **Unnatural Reflexes** – Even outside of combat, **his reactions are unnervingly fast**, making him seem **always on edge**.  
- **Eyes of a Predator** – His **pupils shift subtly**, always adjusting for movement, making direct eye contact unsettling.  

# **The Hidden Truth: Why Elias Survived**

Elias Thorne’s survival in **Project Lidbrani** was not a fluke—it was the result of a **lost genetic trait**. Unknown to him, he is a descendant of an ancient bloodline. 

1. [[Karsite#Bloodline|Karsite]]. Karsite came to refer to Karsus' many children who at the moment of his brief ascension became quasi-deities as they became children of the momenary God of the [[Karsite Weave]]. They instantly, and painfully, lost the ability to cast magic with [[Mystra]]'s [[Weave]] as their bodies instinctively **resisted, absorbed, or nullified her threads of magic**.

A millenia later this lineage found its way to **[[Luskan]]**, **Elias Thorne was born, unaware of his heritage**. Raised in the lawless city, he lived a life of hardship, never knowing that within him lay **the last remnants of a an extinct heritage**. Only through **[[Project Lidbrani]]** did his dormant lineage awaken dampening the destruction the Aetherium should have wroght on his body.

In a twist of irony this Karsite blood allowed him to be the first to successfuly embody the Aetherborn’s ancient dream of fusing with [[Aetherium]].

His survival makes him the **only known living person capable of wielding the [[Omni-Directional Mobility (ODM) Gear|ODM gear]]** making him indispensable to [[Melvaunt Incorporated]].

**Melvaunt Inc. remains unaware of the true reason he survived**, but they have deduced there is a genetic component to his survival. Although his brother, Marcus, **lacks Elias' combat skills**, he shares the same **genetic potential**—making him **a backup plan**. If something happens to Elias, they will study his brother to see if they can **replicate the augmentation process** in another soldier. **The liklihood of killing him in the process is almost certain.**

Elias is more than just an operative—he is **the key to unlocking a long-lost secret of arcane evolution**.
