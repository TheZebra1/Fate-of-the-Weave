# Dragon Turtle of the [[Moonsea]]
## Brief History
Thermontarax is an ancient dragon turtle rumored to have made his lair in the deepest trenches of the Moonsea long before the rise of [[Zhentil Keep]] or the establishment of the region's major cities. Locals believe that he slumbers for centuries, only surfacing when the Moonsea’s balance is threatened or great treasures pass through its waters.

## Notable Sightings
- **1356 DR:** A merchant fleet bound for [[Melvaunt]] was destroyed by what survivors claimed to be "a storm of fire and scales." Many whispered that Thermontarax attacked to protect the [[Moonsea]] from overexploitation.
- **1370 DR:** Adventurers investigating the ruins of [[Northkeep]] claimed to have glimpsed an enormous, shell-armored beast basking on an underwater ridge. They narrowly escaped its wrath.
- **1402 DR:** The [[Zhentarim]] sought to enlist Thermontarax as a weapon against their enemies, but the emissaries sent to parley with him vanished without a trace.
- **1502 DR:** Rumors surface that [[Melvaunt Incorporated]] my have succeeded where the [[Zhentarim]] failed.

## Rumored Involvements
Some believe Thermontarax may have ties to the destruction of Northkeep, possibly having been lured or controlled by an ancient spellcaster to raze the city. Others argue he acts as a guardian of the Moonsea’s [[Ascending Tunnel|secrets]], and hoards magical artifacts that find their way to the lake's depths.
