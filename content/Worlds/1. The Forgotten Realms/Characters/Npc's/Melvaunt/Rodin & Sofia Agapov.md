## Profile
### Leather Goods

1. **Agapov's Rugged Belt**  
   A sturdy, hand-crafted leather belt with reinforced stitching. Known for its durability, it's a favorite among laborers and travelers.  
   - **Bonus:** Slightly reduces the chance of belt-related equipment failure in strenuous conditions.

1. **Worker's Boots**  
   Well-made leather boots designed for long hours on the factory floor or trekking through the industrial outskirts of Melvaunt. They provide excellent support and protection against harsh conditions.  
   - **Bonus:** Reduces fatigue for workers or travelers over long periods of walking.

1. **Commoner's Bracers**  
   Simple yet durable leather bracers often purchased by those who seek a little extra protection in dangerous work environments.  
   - **Bonus:** Provides minor resistance to abrasions or cuts from daily labor.

1. **Patchwork Cloak**  
   A plain leather cloak pieced together from various scraps, offering decent protection against the harsh wind and soot of Melvaunt's industrial areas.  
   - **Bonus:** Provides slight resistance to environmental hazards like cold or industrial soot.

1. **Agapov's Utility Satchel**  
   A tough and practical satchel with cleverly designed compartments for tools or small personal belongings. A popular item among factory workers and tradespeople.  
   - **Bonus:** Allows the wearer to carry tools or supplies more efficiently.

### Small Goal
**A Better Tomorrow**

- Sofia and Rodin are passionate about improving the lives of the hardworking people in Melvaunt’s poor sector. They aim to create affordable, high-quality goods that offer small yet meaningful benefits to the struggling populace. 
- They might ask adventurers to help gather better-quality leather or materials, as even such resources are scarce in Melvaunt.
- A potential quest might involve convincing a local tannery to lower prices for scraps or stopping a corrupt official who is taxing their small shop unfairly.
- Their ultimate goal is to build a small network of craftspeople in the poor sector, allowing the community to better support one another in the harsh industrial environment.
