# Seraph Duirsar
## Quick Summary
**Tagline / Descriptor:** Cursed celestial warlock carrying a fiendish burden and Evereska’s fragile hope.  
**Social Hubs:** [[Greyhome]], [[High Forest Ruins]], [[Temple of Lathander]]  
**Affiliations:** [[Evereskan Nobility]], [[Servants of Lathander]], [[Moonstone Watch]]

## Personality & Traits
- **Temperament:** Charismatic & optimistic contrasted with moments of intense inner turmoil
- **Mannerisms:** Get's headaches frequently when [[Gargauth]] wrestles for control.
- **Values:** Redemption, protection of the innocent, control over inner darkness
- **Fears:** Losing himself to Gargauth, failing Evereska, becoming a monster
- **Desires:** Freedom from the curse, acceptance, a normal life
- **Quirks:** Speaks fluent Infernal in his sleep; compulsively counts backwards when stressed

## Narrative Profile
### Origin & Background
- **Heritage:** Son of Lady [[Elowen Duirsar]], noble-born of [[Evereska]]; father unknown
- **Upbringing:** Raised in luxury and love until a fateful accident shattered his innocence
- **Training/Skills:** Trained in arcane control and mental fortitude by his grandfather [[Erlan Duirsar]] at [[Greyhome]]

### Major Life Events
- **Turning Point:** At age 10, accidentally disrupted a cult ritual and became the living prison for [[Gargauth]], the Tenth Lord of the Nine Hells
- **Current Status:** Warlock bound to [[Lathander]], walks a razor’s edge between divine light and infernal corruption
- **Hidden Truths:** He is Lathander’s son in spirit—perhaps more; his father may be celestial or otherwise powerful and unknown

## Relationships
### Allies
- **[[Erlan Duirsar]]** – Grandfather and mentor; trains Seraph in secret to prepare him for the weight of his destiny
- **[[Lathander]]** – His celestial patron and spiritual father; a tenuous source of hope and restraint

### Enemies
- **[[Gargauth]]** – Archdevil of corruption; imprisoned within Seraph's soul, constantly whispering and tempting
- **[[Cult of Gargauth]]** – Remnants may seek to reclaim or control Seraph

### Key Connections
- **[[Elowen Duirsar]]** – Loving mother unaware of the full truth about Gargauth’s influence and Seraph’s inner battle

## Hooks & Secrets
- **Secrets:** His infernal possession is hidden from all but Erlan and Lathander’s most trusted agents
- [[Gargauth]] may make a more powerful ally than [[Seraph Duirsar]] for those willing to make a ***deal with the Devil***.
