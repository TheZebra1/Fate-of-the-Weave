# Erlan Duirsar
## Quick Summary
**Tagline / Descriptor:** Esteemed leader and protector of Evereska, known for his wisdom and magical prowess.  
**Social Hubs:** [[Hall of the High Hunt]], [[Greyhome]], [[Halfway Inn]]  
**Affiliations:** [[Hill Elders]], [[Cold Hand]], [[Evereska Military]]

## Personality & Traits
- **Temperament:** Calm and decisive
- **Mannerisms:** Speaks with measured authority; maintains a composed demeanor
- **Values:** Protection of Evereska, preservation of elven culture, strategic alliances
- **Fears:** Threats to Evereska's safety, loss of elven heritage
- **Desires:** A secure and thriving Evereska, fostering strong leadership within his family
- **Quirks:** Often consults with his finch messenger, Manynests, even in the presence of others

## Narrative Profile
### Origin & Background
- **Heritage:** Son of Evindal Duirsar; hails from a noble line dedicated to Evereska's leadership
- **Upbringing:** Raised with a deep sense of duty towards Evereska; trained in both martial and arcane arts
- **Training/Skills:** Skilled fighter and adept wizard; creator of unique magical constructs like the Walker in the Mists

### Major Life Events
- **Turning Point:** Led Evereska through the phaerimm siege in 1371 DR, making critical alliances and strategic decisions
- **Current Status:** Serves as High Lord of the Hill Elders; actively involved in Evereska's governance and defense
- **Hidden Truths:** Secretly trained his grandson, Seraph Duirsar, at the elven outpost [[Greyhome]], preparing him for future leadership

## Abilities
| Power / Trait | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| Mist Golem Creation | Developed the Walker in the Mists, a golem made of solid mist to guard Evereska |
| Dual-Class Mastery | Combines martial prowess with advanced wizardry, making him a versatile leader and combatant |
| Strategic Leadership | Over two centuries of experience in commanding Evereska's defenses and political strategies |

## Relationships
### Allies
- **[[Kiinyon Colbathin]]** – Trusted Tomb Master and military advisor; collaborated during the phaerimm siege
- **[[Manynests]]** – Finch messenger; delivers critical communications across Evereska

### Family
- **[[Luirlan Duirsar]]** – Eldest son; involved in Evereska's affairs
- **[[Elowen Duirsar]]** – Daughter; mother of Seraph Duirsar
- **[[Seraph Duirsar]]** – Grandson; personally trained by Erlan at [[Greyhome]]; his father's identity remains a [[faerunian-lathander-scag|mystery]]

### Enemies
- **[[Phaerimm]]** – Ancient adversaries who threatened Evereska's existence

## Hooks & Secrets
- **Rumors:** Whispers suggest that Erlan is grooming Seraph to take on a significant role within Evereska's leadership
- **Secrets:** The identity of Seraph's father is unknown, even to Erlan; this mystery adds complexity to family dynamics
- **Adventure Hook:** Players might seek an audience with Erlan to gain insight into Evereska's defenses or to uncover hidden truths about Seraph's lineage
