# Drizzt Do'Urden
## Profile

#### What They Want:
- *To live by his own code of honor and never succumb to the cruelty of his kin.*
- *To protect those who cannot protect themselves and fight for what is right.*
- *To remain **true to himself**, despite the expectations and hatred placed upon him.*

#### What They Fear:
- *That his past will catch up with him and he will become the monster many believe him to be.*
- *Losing those he loves—he has already lost too many companions and refuses to lose more.*
- *That no matter how much good he does, he will always be judged by his race rather than his deeds.*

#### What They Have Lost:
- *His home in **Menzoberranzan**, cast out for refusing the ways of the Spider Queen.*
- *Many dear friends, including members of the **Companions of the Hall** over time.*
- *The hope that he will ever be truly accepted, even among some surface dwellers.*

---

## History Summary

### **Exile from Menzoberranzan**
- Born into **[[House Do’Urden]]**, one of the noble houses of **[[Menzoberranzan]]**.
- Unlike other drow, he rejected **Lolth’s cruelty**, believing in honor and mercy.
- Trained under **[[Zaknafein]] (his father), the greatest drow swordsman**, and inherited his ideals.
- Fled **Menzoberranzan** rather than become part of the city’s treachery.

### **Wandering the World**
- Spent years in the **[[Underdark]]**, surviving on instinct and developing his **Hunter** persona.
- Eventually found refuge on the **surface**, though he faced **fear and prejudice**.
- Formed bonds with surface-dwellers, proving himself through **deeds rather than words**.

### **The Companions of the Hall**
- Found true friendship with **Bruenor Battlehammer, Catti-brie, Wulfgar, and Regis**.
- Became **one of the greatest heroes of the North**, defending Icewind Dale, Mithral Hall, and beyond.
- His bond with **Catti-brie** eventually deepened into **love**, forging a new path forward.

### **Allies & Enemies**
- **Allies**
  - **[[Bruenor Battlehammer]]** – His closest friend, a father figure and ally in many battles.
  - **[[Catti-brie]]** – His beloved wife and a formidable warrior in her own right.
  - **[[Guenhwyvar]]** – His mystical panther companion, bound to him in deep trust.
  - **[[Jarlaxle Baenre]]** – A complicated ally; they often stand at odds, yet have a mutual respect.
  - **The [[Harpers]]** – He has worked with them, though he prefers his independence.

- **Enemies**
  - **[[Artemis Entreri]]** – Once a bitter rival, now something more akin to a dark reflection.
  - **Drow Nobles & Matron Mothers** – Many still seek to claim or kill him for his betrayal.
  - **Worshipers of [[Lolth]]** – He is an anathema to Lolth’s doctrine, and they hunt him.
  - **Xenophobic Surface-Dwellers** – Even after all he has done, many still refuse to see past his dark skin.

### **Current Status**
- Still **wanders the world**, seeking adventure, purpose, and justice.
- Remains **one of the most skilled swordsmen alive**, wielding **[[Icingdeath]] and [[Twinkle]]** with unmatched finesse.
- Continues to fight for those in need, knowing that while he cannot change the world alone, **he can be an example that defies its expectations**.
