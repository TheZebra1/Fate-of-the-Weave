## Profile

#### What They Want:
- Redemption for his past actions, particularly the sandstorm he accidentally unleashed that devastated his village.  
- A sense of belonging, as he struggles with the pain of exile and loneliness.  
- Control over his wild magic, which he fears will hurt others again if left unchecked.  
- Protection for his goblin companions, who have become his family in exile.  

#### What They Fear:
- Losing control of his wild magic and causing destruction or hurting his loved ones.  
- Facing rejection or hostility from his mother and the Sandspeakers if he attempts to return.  
- The possibility that the Shard of the Skyfire (his glowing blue stone) may corrupt him or attract dangerous enemies.  
- Abandonment by his goblin friends, who are the only ones who have shown him compassion.  

#### What They Have Lost:
- His home and his place among the Sandspeakers.  
- The trust of his mother, Cleopatra Zayah, who banished him despite her love for him.  
- His innocence, as his exile has forced him to survive through hardship and fend for himself in a hostile world.  
- A sense of control, as his wild magic is both a blessing and a curse that defines his life.

## History Summary
Zahur is the son of Cleopatra Zayah, leader and diviner of the Sandspeakers, a nomadic tribe descended from ancient Mulhorandi settlers. As the heir to her mantle, Zahur was expected to carry on her legacy of wisdom and leadership. However, his inherent wild magic, likely tied to the Shard of the Skyfire—a celestial artifact gifted to him at birth—manifested chaotically, terrifying the tribe.  

During a sacred rite to invoke Horus-Re’s blessings, Zahur lost control, unleashing a massive sandstorm that devastated the tribe's encampment. This act was deemed sacrilege, and Cleopatra was forced to banish her son to uphold the tribe's laws, despite her deep sorrow.  

Left to wander the hostile deserts of Calimshan, Zahur was on the brink of despair when he encountered three goblin outcasts—Ruk, Bik, and Nim. Cast out from their own tribe for showing mercy to humans, these goblins became Zahur’s unlikely companions. Together, they roam the deserts and scrublands, scavenging to survive and supporting each other through their shared exile.  

Zahur is torn between his guilt and his desire for redemption. His control over his wild magic is improving, but the Shard of the Skyfire around his neck remains a mystery—its power both a blessing and a potential danger. Zahur’s fate, and the impact he may have on others, is in the hands of the adventurers he meets on his journey.

# Full Story: Zahur, the Banished Boy
## Appearance:
Zahur is a scrappy teenager of wiry build with sun-kissed skin that speaks of a harsh upbringing under a blazing sun. His clothing is sparse: a loincloth and a short leopard-spotted cape tied around his neck by a string that holds a blue, faintly glowing stone at its center. His body is adorned with rough bandages wrapping his hands, wrists, thighs, and abdomen, giving him a patched-together appearance. A wooden mask with a rough-hewn, tribal look covers the upper part of his face, obscuring his features but leaving his glowing, cerulean eyes visible. He carries a gnarled staff topped with a curl resembling a shepherd's crook, and his presence is eerie yet strangely vulnerable.

---

## Background:
Zahur hails from a remote desert tribe, the **Tethyrian Sandspeakers**, who are descendants of ancient Mulhorandi settlers in Calimshan. His mother, **Cleopatra Zayah**, was a powerful diviner and tribal leader who held sway over the clan's destiny through her connection to the divine. The tribe worshipped the Mulhorandi god **Horus-Re**, and Zahur was expected to follow in his mother’s footsteps as her heir.

However, Zahur was born with an unusual gift—or curse. From an early age, he showed signs of latent **wild magic**. Spells manifested chaotically around him, bending reality and terrifying his kin. Some claimed the glowing blue stone around his neck was a fragment of a fallen star imbued with arcane power, gifted by his mother to protect him. Others whispered it was a tether to something far darker. During a sacred rite to channel Horus-Re's blessings, Zahur accidentally summoned a destructive sandstorm that obliterated half the village. The act was seen as sacrilege, and Cleopatra, heartbroken but bound by her role as leader, decreed his banishment.

---

## Current Situation:
Exiled into the wilderness, Zahur wandered the Calim Desert, enduring hunger, predators, and isolation. In his darkest hour, he stumbled upon three red-haired goblins—**Ruk, Bik, and Nim**—picking through scraps in the sands. Rather than attack him, they took pity on the boy and offered him food and companionship. These goblins, cast out by their own tribe for their unusual compassion, have become Zahur's unlikely family.

Zahur and his goblin friends now wander the deserts and scrublands of **Amn**, **Tethyr**, and the outskirts of **Calimshan**, scavenging to survive. The boy has begun to gain control over his magic, aided by the enigmatic blue stone, but he fears its power. He is haunted by guilt over what he did to his people and torn between returning to earn redemption or forging a new identity.

- **Naïve but Determined**: Zahur has a childlike innocence but an iron will to protect those who have shown him kindness.
- **Inner Turmoil**: Zahur wrestles with guilt over the destruction he caused and fears losing control of his magic again.

## Role in the Campaign:
Zahur can be a multi-layered NPC who introduces several plot hooks to your campaign:

### Initial Encounter
The party encounters Zahur and his goblins while traveling through the deserts or forests. Perhaps they find him
- Being accused of theft by a caravan or town guard. 
- Stumble upon him defending his goblin friends from overzealous adventurers mistaking them for a threat.

### Plot Hooks
1. **The Shard of the Skyfire**: The blue stone Zahur wears is an artifact known as the **[[Shard of Skyfire]]**, a fragment of a celestial being's essence. Its presence fuels Zahur's wild magic. However, it has also begun attracting attention—bandits, rival mages, or even the Zhentarim want the artifact for their own ends. The party may have to protect Zahur or decide whether to claim the stone for themselves.
   
2. **Return to the Sandspeakers**: Cleopatra Zayah is tormented by her decision to banish her son. A tribe elder approaches the party, offering a reward to track down Zahur and bring him home. However, his return might not be welcomed by all; some members of the tribe might see him as a threat and seek to stop him from reclaiming his place unless he proves he can control his power.

3. **The Curse of Wild Magic**: Zahur's wild magic has a connection to an ancient Mulhorandi curse or a forgotten entity tied to the god **Set**. A rival diviner might seek to manipulate Zahur’s powers for a nefarious purpose, and the party must decide whether to help Zahur control his abilities or sever his connection to magic entirely.

4. **The Goblins' Redemption**: Ruk, Bik, and Nim have tragic pasts of their own. They were exiled from their goblin tribe for showing mercy to a human village and the Sandspeakers will not let them live in the village due to fear of them due to their nature. Zahur is fiercely loyal to them and may ask the party for help in clearing their names or finding a safe haven where they won’t be hunted.

---

## Alignment & Morality:
- **Neutral Good**: Zahur strives to do what he feels is right, though his inexperience and insecurities can lead to mistakes.
- **Open to Influence**: The party’s actions could shape Zahur’s future. Compassion and mentorship might inspire him to become a hero, while betrayal or cruelty might drive him to dark alliances.

---

## Abilities:
- **Wild Magic Sorcerer**: Zahur has unpredictable magical abilities that occasionally surge uncontrollably. His staff serves as a focus, and the Shard of the Skyfire amplifies his power.
- **Survivalist**: Years in the wild have made Zahur adept at foraging, tracking, and navigating harsh environments.
- **Goblins' Assistance**: Ruk, Bik, and Nim act as comic relief but can aid Zahur and the party with clever traps, stealth, or unexpected acts of bravery.
