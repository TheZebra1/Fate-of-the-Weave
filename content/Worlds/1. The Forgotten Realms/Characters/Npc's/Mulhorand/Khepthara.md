## History Summary
Her dreams always come true. The nature of the dream is based on her emotions the prior day. It started at the age of 12. She was angry at her sister one day. That night she had a dream that her sister died drowning in a river of blood.

The next day it happened: the Nile River was plagued with blood, and the sister, who was bathing there, went into a panic attack and drowned in the blood.

[[Khepthara]] had several bad dreams in the coming weeks, each followed by destructive plagues on Egypt. 

Finally, one night she dreamed of her father, ***Sorrabeck***, the highest general of Pharaoh’s army, being killed by miraculously large waves while chasing escaping Egyptian slaves. In the dream, he turned at the last moment to see [[Khepthara]] cresting a hill, too late to save him.

She told her father about the vision in the morning. He took this to heart and vowed that if the moment came, he would heed his daughter's warning about charging into the water.

However, when the day came, [[Khepthara]], while doing her chores carrying in water from a well, tripped and struck her head, falling unconscious. While unconscious, she immediately had a vision that the current Pharaoh would die at the Red Sea and, if her father survived, he would take over as a corrupt and despotic Pharaoh, bringing violent and oppressive rule to the land.

She regained consciousness and chose, at the last minute, to fetch a horse and sprint out to the Red Sea. 

She arrived moments before the waves were about to come crashing down on the army of Egypt. 

[[Khepthara]] chose to make a selfless sacrifice by charging headlong into the seabed. As intended, this prompted her father to charge in after her in a rescue attempt.

**Sorrabeck** died in the waves... but [[Khepthara]] was shunted from Earth to [[Toril]] by the gods [[Khepri]] and Thoth as an additional aid against [[egyptian-set|Set]].

[[Khepthara]] now lives in [[Mulhorand]] and practices every day, trying to control her powers by focusing on good thoughts and emotions so that only good things will happen in her prophetic dreams.  
- Unbeknownst to her, the dreams are not the cause of the future. They are simply a foretelling of events should all things proceed as they are. Not dreaming of the future does not stop it from coming. True control would mean mastering her emotions to see both good and bad outcomes when needed.
