## History Summary
Safira was born in [[Elturel]], but her fate was sealed when a pirate attack separated her from her parents as a child.

Drifting alone on a piece of wreckage, she was discovered near Lantan by gnomish artificers, who raised her among their kind. She quickly became a prodigy under the mentorship of High Artificer [[Danactar]]. Due to her fiendish nature no one ever recognized her brilliance or ***Listened to her ideas***.

Feeling invisible and undervalued she became disillusioned and wanted to create a world where she was **respected** and **listened** to.

Stealing [[Lantan|Lantanese]] schematics for advanced medical [[C.L.E.R.I.C.|automatons]], she absconded with them to a shadowy organization in [[Melvaunt]]. 

Instead of healing, these constructs were modified to siphon [[Soul Stone|soul energy]], feeding it back to her employers. Safira personally brokered the sale of these "enhanced" automatons to the [[House of Healing (Waterdeep)]], turning one of the city’s most trusted institutions into a feeding ground for her dark patrons.

Now, she operates within [[Waterdeep]]’s [[House of Healing (Waterdeep)]], rich and dangerous, but always watching her back for the consequences of her treachery.
