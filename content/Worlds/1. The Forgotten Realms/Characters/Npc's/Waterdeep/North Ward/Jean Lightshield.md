## History Summary
*"Respect is earned, not inherited. I may carry the Lightshield name, but I'll make my own mark, even if it means breaking a few bones along the way."* - ***Jean Lightshield**

Jean Lightshield, the son of the legendary Erevan Lightshield, has big shoes to fill as the potential future leader of [[W.A.S.P]]. Unlike his father, Jean is known for his quick temper and aggressive tactics, which have both gained him victories and caused friction within the ranks.

While he possesses remarkable combat skills and a fierce determination to protect [[Waterdeep]], his leadership style is often questioned. Many wonder if he can truly embody the calm, strategic mind of his father or if his impulsiveness will be his downfall.

Despite the doubts, Jean is determined to prove himself, and with the backing of some loyal younger operatives, he may yet become the next captain of [[W.A.S.P]]—if he can learn to control his temper and inspire the same respect his father commands.
