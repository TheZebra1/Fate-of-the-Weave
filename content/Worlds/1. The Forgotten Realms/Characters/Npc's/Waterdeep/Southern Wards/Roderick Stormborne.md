## **History & Legacy**  
Captain Roderick Stormborne is a decorated officer of the [[Order of Tye|Waterdeep Navy]], known for his unshakable discipline and tactical brilliance. Rising through the ranks from a deckhand, he dedicated his life to safeguarding the seas from pirates and enemy fleets, earning the respect of his peers and subordinates alike.  

Beyond his duties at sea, Roderick held an unusual habit—**an annual trek into the [[Spine of the World]].** He claimed it was a **personal** **challenge**, a test of endurance, but whispers among the crew suggested **deeper** motives.  

On one such journey, he was nearly claimed by the brutal elements, but fate intervened—he was saved by a goliath warrior, **[[Korrak Stonebreaker.png|Korrak]]**. Grateful, Roderick offered Korrak a place in his crew, training him in the ways of the Waterdeep Marines.  

Yet, those closest to Roderick noted an uncharacteristic silence regarding that fateful journey. He spoke little of why he had ventured so far or what he had sought in the frozen peaks. Some claim he searched for an ancient relic, others that he was following the echoes of a forgotten oath. Whatever the truth, only he knew why meeting Korrak seemed to bring an end to his wandering.
