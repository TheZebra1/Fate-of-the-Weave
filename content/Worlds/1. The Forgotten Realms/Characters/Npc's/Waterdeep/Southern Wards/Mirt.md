# **Mirt’s Rewards for Finding Laeral Silverhand**
## **Rare Magic Items from Mirt’s Collection**
Mirt is a **former adventurer and powerful noble**, so his collection includes **valuable magic items** suited for investigation, diplomacy, or combat.
### **Rare Magic Items**
- **Cloak of Protection** (*Dungeon Master’s Guide*)  
  - *Grants +1 AC and saving throws.*  
  - A stylish **Waterdhavian noble’s cloak**, enchanted for defense.  

- **Ring of Mind Shielding** (*Dungeon Master’s Guide*)  
  - *Prevents mind-reading and conceals alignment.*  
  - Mirt insists it's **“just a precaution”** for dealing with Waterdeep’s schemers.  

- **Hat of Disguise** (*Dungeon Master’s Guide*)  
  - *Allows disguise at will.*  
  - A perfect tool for **an investigator or assassin** seeking discretion.  

- **Dust of Disappearance** (*Dungeon Master’s Guide*)  
  - *Grants invisibility for 2d4 minutes when thrown.*  
  - "Just in case you need a quick exit," Mirt says with a grin.  

### **Very Rare to Legendary Items (For True Loyalty)**
- **Amulet of the Planes** (*Dungeon Master’s Guide*)  
  - *Allows teleportation between planes.*  
  - If Laeral’s disappearance involves **otherworldly forces**, this is invaluable.  

- **Tome of Leadership and Influence** (*Dungeon Master’s Guide*)  
  - *Grants +2 Charisma after 48 hours of reading.*  
  - "You’ll need charm to sway the right people," Mirt quips.  

- **Ioun Stone of Mastery** (*Dungeon Master’s Guide*)  
  - *Increases proficiency bonus by +1.*  
  - A relic **once held by a former Open Lord**.  

- **Rod of Security** (*Dungeon Master’s Guide*)  
  - *Creates a private demiplane for rest and safety.*  
  - “If you ever need to vanish for a while, this’ll do the trick.”  

## **Boons & Favors from Mirt**
Mirt’s **true power lies in influence, wealth, and connections**. His favors are worth more than gold.
### **Political & Social Boons**
- **Waterdeep Residency**  
  - Full legal status as a **noble or protected citizen** of Waterdeep.  
  - Comes with **perks like access to noble circles** and reduced legal trouble.  

- **Access to the Lords' Court**  
  - Grants the adventurer a **private audience with Waterdeep’s ruling council**.  

- **Personal Contact with the Harpers**  
  - Provides **exclusive missions, intelligence, and secret resources**.  

- **Masked Lord Candidacy** (*Requires loyalty to Waterdeep*)  
  - If the adventurer proves **exceptionally useful**, they may be **invited to become a Masked Lord**.  
### **Criminal & Underworld Connections**
- **Pardon for Any Past Crimes**  
  - Mirt can **erase criminal records** and ensure the City Watch looks the other way.  

- **Secret Harper Safehouses**  
  - Access to **discreet locations throughout the Sword Coast**.  

- **Zhentarim Black Market Access**  
  - Though not an ally, Mirt can **broker deals with the Zhentarim** for rare items or favors.  

- **Control Over a Waterdeep Trade Guild**  
  - If the adventurer wants **influence in commerce**, Mirt can **arrange for them to take over a trade guild**.  

## **Unique Personal Artifacts (Only for Trusted Allies)**
If the adventurer **proves invaluable**, Mirt might part with **some of his most treasured items**.
### **Mirt’s Coin of Favor** (*Homebrew, Legendary*)  
*A single golden coin engraved with Mirt’s laughing face.*  
- **Once spent, Mirt will personally intervene in a crisis.**  
- This might mean:
  - **Calling in political favors.**
  - **Hiring assassins.**
  - **Bribing enemies.**
  - **Magically rescuing the adventurer.**
### **Mirt’s Old Cutlass** (*Sword of Sharpness, Dungeon Master’s Guide*)  
*A +2 cutlass that deals additional slashing damage and can sever limbs on a natural 20.*  
- **Mirt’s personal weapon from his adventuring days**.  
- Well-maintained but only gifted to someone **he fully trusts**.  
### **The Ledger of Waterdeep** (*Homebrew Artifact*)  
*A book containing the true debts, favors, and secrets of Waterdeep’s elite.*  
- Knowing what’s inside is **worth more than gold**.  
- Mirt would **never part with it lightly**, but for a **trusted ally**, he might share **select pages**.
