## History Summary
*"Those of you who wish to flee, you may do so with my blessing... but those of you who wish to fall, so that history says you **stood** follow me into this hell.*" - **Erevan Lightshield**, [[Siege of Waterdeep]] 1450 DR*

- Sir Erevan Lightshield, born into a noble paladin family, rose to lead [[W.A.S.P]]. 
- Father of [[Jean Lightshield]]
- A legend in Waterdeep, known for unwavering dedication, revered for his bravery and tactical brilliance.

### [[Order of the Arcane Blade]]
- Erevan left the Order when the Sul'ar began gaining more ground in the Order. Their ideology made him realized he could use these gifts to protect his home of [[Waterdeep]] and so he returned shortly after completing the ***Rite of Passage***.
