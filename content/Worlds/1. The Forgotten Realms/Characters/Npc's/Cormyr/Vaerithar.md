## Backstory:

Long ago, a young man from [[Cormyr]], restless and yearning for a destiny beyond the familiar fields and courts of his homeland, set sail across the seas of Toril. His quest for purpose led him to distant shores, where in a land of war and wonder, he crossed paths with a Knight Dragon — a rare breed of Warden Dragon who led not by greed, but by the gathering of people as their hoard.

The Black Knight Dragon, known as **Tazmyrion the Iron Wing**, saw great potential in the young Cormyrean. Over time, the man earned his place within Tazmyrion’s hoard — not as a mere follower, but as a trusted captain and confidant. In time, Tazmyrion named him **Vaerithar**, meaning **Prince**, a title that carried weight beyond mere rank; it was a declaration that he would inherit the *dragon's very essence* upon his death, gaining a fragment of the dragon’s strength, memories, and form.

Fate, however, sped this destiny along before anyone anticipated. In a brutal defeat, Tazmyrion's forces were overwhelmed. As his life ebbed away, the dragon's spirit surged into the chosen Prince. In the chaos of defeat, the newly anointed heir fled aboard a battered ship bound for [[Faerûn]].

It was during the voyage that the transformation began. Scales the color of deep royal purple sprouted across his body; wings unfurled from his back; his human form melted away into that of a wyrmling dragon. The crew, awestruck and terrified, nevertheless recognized the intelligence and nobility behind the young dragon’s gaze, and delivered him safely back to familiar shores.

Adopting the name **Vaerithar**, he spent the next decades joining adventuring bands, gathering experience, allies, and knowledge — steadily growing in size and strength. His goal was simple yet profound: to build a new hoard, not of gold, but of people — a loyal force bound by duty and shared destiny.

---

By **1492 DR**, Vaerithar had matured into a proud and powerful adult dragon. Returning to Cormyr, the land of his birth, he offered his service to the Crown. Queen Regent [[Raedra Obarskyr]], recognizing both the dragon’s potent military genius and his Cormyrean heart, named him **Grand War General** of Cormyr.

Under Vaerithar’s leadership, the training, discipline, and tactics of the Purple Dragon Knights were revolutionized. By **1502 DR**, the armies of Cormyr stood among the finest fighting forces in all Faerûn — a gleaming testament to their draconic general’s vision. It was in that year that he marched his forces to the lands south of [[Cormyr]], beginning a conquest with ***world defining goals.***
