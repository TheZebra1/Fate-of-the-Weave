## Profile

#### What They Want:
- To escape the **Far Realms** and return to the **Material Plane** to reclaim power.
- To **reverse ceremorphosis** using the **Chrysalis Codex**, restoring the **Adversary Mind Flayers** to their **original Gith forms**.
- To overthrow **Ilsensine** and the Illithid Empire, leaving it a ruin while establishing their own vision of a new empire.

#### What They Fear:
- **Ilsensine’s return** and the **rebuilding of the Elder Brain** could undo all their progress.
- Being trapped in the **Far Realms** forever without a way back to the Material Plane.
- Losing the connection to the **Adversary Mind Flayers** and weakening their hold.

#### What They Have Lost:
- The physical form of the original **Gith**, now replaced with that of a **Mind Flayer**.
- Control over the **Adversary Mind Flayers** after Ilsensine’s sacrificial banishment.
- The unity of the **Gith race**, as their people now remain divided between the **Githyanki** and **Githzerai** factions.

## History Summary
Gith'Zerath was once a Gith warrior, enslaved and transformed into a **Mind Flayer** through the process of **ceremorphosis**. However, Gith'Zerath retained his soul and will, using this unique resistance to lead a rebellion against the **Illithid Empire**. His psionic powers allowed him to create the **Adversary Mind Flayers**, a faction of **Illithid** that served as extensions of his own mind. After the rebellion and the fall of the **Illithid Empire**, Gith'Zerath was banished to the **Far Realms** along with his followers, where they remain trapped. However, the **reincarnation of Ilsensine** in **1502 DR** has reopened a **psionic link**, to [[Toril]] and Gith'Zerath seeks to return and: 
1. Wipe out any vestiges of the [[Illithid Empire]]
2. Use the **[[Chrysalis Codex]]** to restore himself and his followers to their original Gith forms.
