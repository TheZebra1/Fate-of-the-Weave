## Profile

#### What They Want:
- To restore the Illithid Empire to its former glory.
- Reunite the Elder Brain network and regain their original power.
- The return to a physical form capable of ruling the multiverse once again.

#### What They Fear:
- Permanent fragmentation and loss of its consciousness.
- The return of the [[Gith'Zerath|Adversary Mind Flayers]] and the destruction of the Illithid race.
- Rebellion from the Gith races.

#### What They Have Lost:
- It's original powerful Elder Brain form.
- Control over the Illithid Empire following the rebellion of Gith'Zerath
- The unity of the Elder Brain network and the ability to guide the Illithids as before.

---

## History Summary
Ilsensine was once the supreme ruler of the Illithid Empire, an immense psionic force that spanned the multiverse. However, after the rebellion of Gith'Zerath and the eventual downfall at the hands of Gith’s forces in -642 DR, Ilsensine sacrificed its physical form to protect its race. The Elder Brain's consciousness was scattered across the planes, leaving the Illithid scattered and leaderless.
