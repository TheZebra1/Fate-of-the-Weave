## Profile

#### What They Want:
- *To amass power and wealth without ever being truly bound by responsibility.*
- *To prove himself superior to the rigid drow society that sought to control him.*
- *To remain **unpredictable**, ensuring that no one can ever truly outmaneuver him.*

#### What They Fear:
- *Losing control of Bregan D'aerthe or seeing it manipulated by outside forces.*
- *Being drawn back into the political machinations of Menzoberranzan, especially House Baenre.*
- *Boredom—Jarlaxle thrives on chaos and the thrill of the unpredictable.*

#### What They Have Lost:
- *His place in traditional drow society, though he views this as a liberation rather than a loss.*
- *True trust—his life is built on deception, and even his closest allies know he always plays the long game.*
- *Any lingering attachment to his noble lineage—he has no interest in serving House Baenre or Lolth.*

## History Summary

### **Origins & Rebellion Against Drow Society**
- Born into **House Baenre**, one of the most powerful noble houses in **Menzoberranzan**.
- Instead of submitting to the will of the **matron mothers**, he carved his own path, forming **Bregan D’aerthe**, a band of drow mercenaries made up of outcasts, exiles, and opportunists.
- Refused to follow **Lolth’s religious dogma**, making him a dangerous anomaly in drow culture.

### **Rise of Bregan D’aerthe**
- Turned **Bregan D’aerthe** into a powerful and feared faction, operating **in and beyond the Underdark**.
- Unlike drow nobles, he **embraced diplomacy, trickery, and alliances**, using non-drow mercenaries when it benefited him.
- Expanded into **Luskan**, gaining influence over its pirate lords and seedy underbelly.

### **Allies & Enemies**
- **Allies**
  - **Bregan D’aerthe** – His elite band of spies, assassins, and mercenaries.
  - **[[Drizzt Do’Urden]]** – A complicated relationship; admiration and rivalry in equal measure.
  - **Luskan Pirates** – Many serve his interests, willingly or otherwise.
  - **Various Lords & Power Brokers** – He is a master of cutting deals and playing both sides.

- **Enemies**
  - **Drow Matriarchs** – Especially those of **Menzoberranzan**, who see him as a dangerous wildcard.
  - **House Baenre** – Despite his name, he refuses to serve them.
  - **Zealots of Lolth** – Jarlaxle’s independence is seen as **heresy**.
  - **Anyone Foolish Enough to Double-Cross Him** – Betrayal is rarely forgiven.

### **Current Status**
- Continues to **expand his influence**, moving freely between **Luskan, the Underdark, and the surface world**.
- Remains a **wildcard**, allying with heroes, villains, and everyone in between—so long as it suits his goals.
- Always **one step ahead**, ensuring that no one, not even the gods, can predict his next move.
