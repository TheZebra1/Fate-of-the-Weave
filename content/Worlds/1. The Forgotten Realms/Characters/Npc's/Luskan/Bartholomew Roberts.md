## Overview
Bartholomew Roberts is a **zealous and ruthless pirate lord**, who believes that piracy is **divine destiny**. He is a **strict enforcer of the pirate code**, brooking no weakness or hesitation. His vision is not one of survival, but of **supremacy over the seas**.

## Appearance
- Wears an **immaculate crimson coat** with gold trim, always pristine despite battle.
- **Cold, piercing eyes**, always analyzing, always judging.
- Carries **two finely crafted rapiers**, which he wields with precision.

## Background
- Originally a **naval officer**, he was **captured by pirates** and forced into their ranks.
- Instead of resisting, he **embraced piracy**, seeing it as **the true calling of the strong**.
- He **slaughtered his captors**, taking their ship and **creating a new fleet** under his iron rule.
- Unlike others, he did not see **Faerûn as an escape**, but as **a battlefield for his holy war**.

## Motivations
- **Pirates as the True Kings of the Sea** – He believes that piracy is **the natural order**, and those who resist **must be broken**.
- **Destroying the Arcane Brotherhood** – He sees Luskan’s wizards as **false gods**, hoarding power meant for the sea’s chosen.
- **No Weakness Allowed** – He has no patience for diplomacy or compromise, only **total victory**.
