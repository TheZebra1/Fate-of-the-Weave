## Overview
Mary Read is a **fierce and deadly raider**, known for her **unmatched speed in battle** and ability to **disappear without a trace**. A warrior born into piracy, she is both feared and respected by her crew.

## Appearance
- A **slender, yet muscular woman**, often mistaken for a man due to her combat prowess.
- Wears a **hooded cloak** over **practical leathers**, built for agility.
- Dual-wields **curved cutlasses**, striking with unmatched speed.

## Background
- Born into piracy, Mary **disguised herself as a man** to survive in a world dominated by ruthless men.
- She fought her way to the top, proving herself **faster and deadlier than any rival**.
- She **despises the old High Captains of Luskan**, blaming them for past betrayals against her former crew.

## Motivations
- **Revenge Against Luskan’s Old Guard** – She wants to **bury the past** in **blood and fire**.
- **Protecting Rackham** – Though she trusts few, she sees **Rackham as her only true ally**.
- **Freedom Above All Else** – Unlike Roberts, she believes **piracy should be about choice, not tyranny**.
