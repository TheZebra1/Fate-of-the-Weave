## Overview
Edward Kenway is a **brilliant strategist** and **masterful navigator**, known for his **charismatic leadership** and **reluctant outlaw status**. Originally a privateer, he turned to piracy after betrayal left him with no allegiance. Though he thrives on the open sea, he struggles with the morality of his chosen path.

## Appearance
- A tall, athletic man with **weathered skin** and **blond hair**, often tied back.
- Wears a **long, dark captain’s coat** lined with red and adorned with trophies from past victories.
- His belt carries **multiple flintlock pistols** and a **curved saber** at his side.

## Background
- Once a **privateer**, Edward was **betrayed by his own employers** and left for dead.
- Realizing the futility of serving fickle masters, he turned to piracy, quickly **rising through the ranks** due to his cunning and combat prowess.
- It was **his idea to sail to Faerûn**, believing it to be **a land of opportunity** where pirates could carve out a free existence.
- Though he embraces the pirate life, he **seeks purpose beyond simple plunder**.
- Kenway's wife Elizabeth Scott bore him a daughter before passing away from smallpox.
	- Jennifer Scott, now tags along with an very protective Kenway who wants his daughter to enjoy the spoils of his adventures without leading such a life herself.

## Motivations
- **A New World, A New Beginning** – He sees Faerûn as a **chance to build a true pirate haven**, free from tyrants.
- **Opposing Blackbeard’s Rule** – While he respects Blackbeard, he sees **his methods as too brutal**.
- **The Arcane Brotherhood’s Secrets** – He believes that understanding **Luskan’s hidden magic** could be key to securing the pirates' future.
