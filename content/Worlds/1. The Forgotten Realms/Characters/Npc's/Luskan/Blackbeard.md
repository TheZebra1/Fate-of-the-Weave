## Profile
#### Usable Quotes

#### What They Want:
- *To find a true, glorious death.* For years, Blackbeard has sought a battle worthy of ending his life, but he’s grown too powerful for mortal foes. Only an extraordinary encounter or supernatural force can give him the end he craves.
- A grand, legendary final clash that would be remembered through the ages, securing his name in myth.
  
#### What They Fear:
- *Eternal, meaningless existence.* Blackbeard loathes the idea of never finding a worthy adversary, fearing he’ll drift endlessly across seas and realms without purpose.
- *Dying in obscurity.* For a man of such notoriety, fading into the unknown would be the ultimate shame.
  
#### What They Have Lost:
- *Mortality.* He no longer knows if he’s truly alive or some cursed entity bound to wander forever. While he retains flesh and blood, his connection to life and death is frayed.
- *Connection.* His wife magically transformed into a Revenant Ship is lost to him and remains in spirit alone. The men who once swore loyalty are gone and the men who serve him now are contract bound. Blackbeard truly has nothing to live for.

## History Summary
Once a mortal pirate lord who cut a swath of terror across the seas, ***Edward Teach***, known as ***Blackbeard***. In ***1385 DR***, the outbreak of the destructive [[Spellplague]] caused Blackbeard to be swept up in a magical storm along with his ship the "***Queen Anne***," named after his **wife,** "***Anne Teach",*** who served as his first mate. 

When the storm was over, Edward found himself changed, experiencing a mythical level power and immunity to age. 

Unfortunately, the appreciation for this new power was soured by the discovery that his wife **Anne**, had been irreversibly twisted into the mast and sails of the ship in an *un-survivable* fashion.

From then on the tortured spirit of his wife seemed to live on inside the ship, which Blackbeard renamed ***The Queen Anne's Revenge.***

As the years passed, Blackbeard became the most feared pirate on all seas across [[Toril]]. He quickly grew weary of spoils & pillaging  only longing  for a ***worthy death*** so that his soul may join his wife at rest.

His body & soul are tied to the [[Weave]] itself and as long as it exists he can never truly die. He searches for a [[2. Korrak Captured#The Karsite Solution|solution]] that can give him the death he craves.
