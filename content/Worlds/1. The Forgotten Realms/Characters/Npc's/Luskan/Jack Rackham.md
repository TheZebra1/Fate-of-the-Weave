## Overview
Jack Rackham is a **master manipulator, smuggler, and con artist**, preferring to win battles with **cunning over brute force**. A true opportunist, he controls **a vast black market network**, trading secrets as easily as stolen goods.

## Appearance
- A **lean, sharp-dressed rogue**, always wearing **ornate rings** and **a silk cravat**.
- **Smug smile**, always hiding a deeper scheme.
- Armed with **hidden daggers** and a **pair of exquisite dueling pistols**.

## Background
- Unlike many pirates, Jack was never **a soldier or a noble**—he was **a street rat** who conned his way onto a ship.
- He **rose through deception**, talking his way into power **without ever needing brute force**.
- While others waged **open war**, Jack **played both sides**, making sure he’d **always profit** no matter who won.

## Motivations
- **Control Luskan’s Black Market** – He doesn’t need a throne, he just needs **control of the city’s gold**.
- **Secret Deals with the Arcane Brotherhood** – He plays a **dangerous game**, selling information to wizards and pirates alike.
- **Survival at All Costs** – Unlike the others, he **plans for the long game**, ensuring he **always has an escape route**.
