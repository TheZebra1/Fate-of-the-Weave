## The Valencrests: A Brief History
Once a proud noble house of [[Neverwinter]], the **Valencrests** were healers, scholars, and city patrons. When the **[[Wailing Death]]** struck in 1372 DR, they refused to flee, sealing their estate and transforming it into a quarantine refuge.

When all the clerics of the city could not help with the plague, mercy turned to madness.

**Lord [[Vallan Valencrest]]**, desperate to save his family and the people of his city, turned to necromancy—trying to preserve their souls through ***forbidden rites*** where healing failed. The magic worked… imperfectly.

- [[Vallan Valencrest#Miriam Valencrest|Miriam Valencrest]], his wife always disagreed with the plan.

The estate became a tomb. None who entered were seen again. Over the course of the [[Spellplague]], the manor's wards twisted, anchoring the dead souls inside.

Now, the house stands quiet—but ***never empty***.

> Easy Summary:
> “The [[Vallan Valencrest]] tried to save [[Neverwinter]] from the [[Wailing Death]] with necromancy. Ultimately he couldn't beat death, he just created undeath and kept the souls of the dead  from leaving the [[Material Plane]].”
## Valencrest Children
### Lira Selvine Valencrest *(Older Daughter)*
- A graceful soul, fond of music and poetry.
- Played violin near the hearth and in the gallery.
- Left her initials “L.S.” carved into the living room hearth stone.
- Possession effect: brings calming visions of music and starlight if not resisted.
### Sara Bella Valencrest *(Younger Daughter)*
- Mischievous, bright-eyed, and bold.
- Sketched maps and drew on the furniture when no one watched.
- Believed the pantry was a secret fort and hid her “treasures” there.
- Left behind a stuffed owlbear named ***Pip*** in the daughters’ room.
### Thomlan “Thom” Valencrest *(Youngest, Son)*
- Earnest and brave, idolized knights and stories of valor.
- Practiced swordplay with a dulled broomstick and read tales of heroes.
- His wooden griffon and chalk map still lie untouched in his room.
- If possessed, he attempts to help the players—especially if they are “heroes.”
