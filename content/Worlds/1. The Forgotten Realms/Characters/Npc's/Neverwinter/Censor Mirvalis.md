# Censor Mirvalis
## Profile
#### What They Want:
- To maintain meticulous control over land rights and noble holdings in post-crisis Neverwinter.
- To protect the fragile legal ecosystem of the city from exploitation by predatory organizations (like AI).
- To uphold her personal reputation as incorruptible, efficient, and infallible.

#### What They Fear:
- Exposure of her past affiliations with now-defunct adventuring guilds.
- Losing bureaucratic control to foreign interests or infernal loopholes.
- Magic beyond her understanding disrupting the delicate balance she maintains through paper and law.

#### What They Have Lost:
- Colleagues and family to the Wailing Death—trauma which she has buried beneath procedural rigor.
- Faith in traditional adventuring guilds after their failures during the crisis.
- A lover who vanished under suspicious contract-related circumstances—she suspects AI involvement but has no proof.

## History Summary

Censor Mirvalis is a high-ranking official within the **Neverwinter City Records Office**, charged with arbitrating land disputes and title claims. She is precise, eloquent, and notoriously unflinching in her interpretation of civic law. She moves through the ruins of bureaucracy like a ghost, enforcing contracts and records with a steel spine and a fountain pen.

Though outwardly a neutral party, she is *deeply suspicious* of **Acquisitions Incorporated** and their rapid territorial acquisition across Faerûn. She views their contracts as dangerously exploitative and believes that the fragile social fabric of Neverwinter could unravel under their influence.

Mirvalis carries no weapon. She doesn't need one. Her presence, ink-stained gloves, and cold stare are enough to silence most rooms. She *can be bargained with*, but never openly bribed—she'll accept favors, precedent, or ironclad legal citations over gold.

Whether the players alienate her or earn her wary respect, she is a **powerful recurring force**—a watchdog, witness, and reluctant player in the infernal game building beneath the city’s surface.
