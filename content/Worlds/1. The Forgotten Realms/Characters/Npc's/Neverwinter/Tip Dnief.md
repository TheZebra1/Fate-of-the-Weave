# Tip Dneif
## Profile
#### What They Want:
- Ensure the branch meets monthly soul-acquisition targets
- Identify weaknesses in protocol and exploit them for “correctional leverage”
- Report directly to Mr. [[Azmo Diaz]] with weekly audits

#### What They Fear:
- Breaches in contract integrity
- Being *seen* for what he truly is by divine agents or seers

#### What They Have Lost:
- Patience for “inefficiency” and disorder
- Respect for mortal autonomy

## History Summary
Tip Dneif is the charmingly terrifying ***compliance auditor*** for [[Acquisitions Incorporated]]. Appearing as a broad-shouldered, bald human man in a tailored black vest, he speaks in a deliberate and clipped **Ukrainian accent** and never seems to blink. His presence is both bureaucratic and oppressive, yet never overtly hostile—unless numbers are missed.

Though “assigned to *temporarily assist*” new branches, Most branches find that he becomes an ever present feature of their facility. He is a living ledger, and everything he sees—he remembers.
