# Mr. Azmo Diaz
## Profile
#### What They Want:
- Rapid multi-realm expansion of AI franchises

#### What They Fear:
- Exposure before the plan reaches critical scale

## History Summary
Little is known about Azmo Diaz before the founding of [[Acquisitions Incorporated]] in the wake of the [[Spellplague]]. Some claim he appeared in [[Calimport]] seemingly overnight, rising from obscurity to become one of the city’s most well-connected figures. Others whisper that he was already a power in some other form, simply stepping into the spotlight when the time was right.

What is certain is that Mr. Diaz moves with uncanny poise and foresight. ***Charismatic***, ***unflappable***, and always dressed for the next opportunity, he presents himself not as a conqueror or hero—but as a facilitator of potential. He doesn't promise salvation; he offers *contracts*. And in a world in need of rebuilding, that’s often more valuable.

He is rarely seen in person. Most interactions occur through illusion, sending stone, or elegant arcane projections. When he does appear physically, it is often aboard the airship **[[battle-balloon-ai|The Hard Cloud]]**, or from the breathtaking heights of the **[[Tower of Emerald Winds]]**, a private estate suspended over Calimport and sealed by law and magic.

Some say his success comes from uncanny business sense. Others speculate about pacts, powers, or older truths buried under clever branding. But nothing has ever been proven. What matters, in the end, are the results—and Mr. Diaz always delivers results.

He is a ***benefactor, employer, and visionary***. A mentor who offers **tools** and **opportunities**… for those willing to ***sign***.
