## Profile

#### What They Want:
- *To dismantle corruption while maintaining his cover as a businessman.*
- *To uncover the truth about his father, Valtheris Nightmeadow—whether he is truly dead or still alive.*
- *One day free his mother from servitude to [[Szass Tam]]*
#### What They Fear:
- *Losing control of his dhampiric nature and becoming a monster.*
- *Being discovered by Szass Tam’s agents and used as a pawn or destroyed outright.*
- *Betrayal—he trusts very few and keeps others at arm’s length.*

#### What They Have Lost:
- *His mother, **[[Shantra Modar]]**; raised in private and had to leave her behind in [[Thay]]*
- *A sense of normalcy—his entire life has been one of secrecy, subterfuge, and survival.*
- *Never could have a relationship with his father: **Valtheris Nightmeadow**, secret operative from [[Evereska]].

## History Summary
### **Origins & Heritage**
- **Born in secret**, hidden from the watchful eye of **Szass Tam**.
- His mother, **Shantra**, was a **true vampire** and former leader of the **Thay Resurrectionists**.
- His father, **Valtheris Nightmeadow**, was a **Moon Elf Bladesinger and spy**, presumed dead.
- **Raised by his mother** for a time, but her exile and imprisonment left him **alone** to survive.

### **Life in the Shadows**
- Grew up on the streets, learning the art of **stealth, deception, and survival**.
- Became a **bounty hunter**, tracking those who deserved justice in the underworld.
- Over time, he **built a network** and gained influence, allowing him to establish *[[Transfusion]]*, a lounge that serves as both a business and a front for his operations.

### **The Creation of Transfusion**
- **A high-end lounge, gambling den, and dance hall**, frequented by nobles, criminals, and wanderers alike.
- **Secretly a hub for information brokerage**, where deals are made in whispers.
- Staffed by **trusted allies**, each playing a role in **keeping Percy’s operations running smoothly**.

### **Allies & Enemies**
- **Allies**
  - **Bromm (Orc Bouncer)** – A steadfast guardian, intimidating troublemakers.
  - **Bronn Stonehammer (Dwarf Pit Boss)** – A keen observer who handles gambling operations and reads people with ease.
  - **Lilith Nightshade (Purple Tiefling Dancer)** – A performer who gathers secrets and has ears in every dark corner.
  - **Various informants and underworld contacts** – Tied into the city’s veins.

- **Enemies**
  - **Garrus (Tabaxi Floor Manager)** – Secretly working against him, with unknown backers.
  - **Crime Lords & Corrupt Nobles** – Many have been unknowingly manipulated by him, or seek to control his business.
  - **Agents of Szass Tam** – Some suspect his heritage, and rumors of Shantra’s past resurface.

### **Current Status**
- **Maintains his business** as a cover for his vigilante operations.
- **Walks the fine line** between the world of criminals and the world of the law.
- **Haunted by whispers** of Szass Tam’s reach, but continues to defy the powers that would claim him.
