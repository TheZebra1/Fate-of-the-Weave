# Vallan [[Valencrests]]
## Profile
#### What They Want:
- To preserve the remnants of his family—even if only in memory or undeath
- To be understood, or at least not blamed for what became of the estate

#### What They Fear:
- That everything he did was for nothing
- That Miriam was right

#### What They Have Lost:
- His wife’s love, his children's lives, and ultimately—his humanity

## History Summary
Vallan Valencrest was a respected noble and scholar in [[Neverwinter]], known for his patronage of medical research and his progressive philosophies. When the **[[Wailing Death]]** consumed the city, Vallan refused to flee. He sealed the Valencrest Estate and tried desperately to develop a cure. With divine magic proving ineffective and alchemy failing, he turned to necromancy—not out of malice, but survival.

His experiments in soul-binding, preservation magic, and spirit anchoring were intended to “pause death” until a solution could be found. But death waited for no one. His work slowly corrupted the estate, fusing souls to the walls, the floors, the echoes. Vallan remains now as a spectral imprint—perhaps still conscious, perhaps only replaying his final regrets.

## Miriam Valencrest
Miriam was Vallan's wife and moral compass—known for her fierce compassion, spiritual discipline, and deep faith in [[Selûne]]. She nursed the wounded personally during the early days of the plague. When Vallan turned to necromancy, Miriam stood against him. Their final argument—etched into the soul of the house—was her last act of defiance before she locked herself away and let the end come quietly. Some say her soul still lingers in the estate, untouched by the corruption, watching.
