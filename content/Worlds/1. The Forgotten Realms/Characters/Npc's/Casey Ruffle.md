# Casey Ruffle
## Quick Summary
**Tagline / Descriptor:** Inventive dreamer turned arcane entrepreneur, shadowed by past sins  
**Social Hubs:** [[Strixhaven]], [[Silverymoon]], [[Ebon Chain HQ]]
**Affiliations:** [[Ebon Chain]], [[Gargauth]], [[Strixhaven Alumni]]  

## Personality & Traits
- **Temperament:** Quietly driven, empathetic but guarded
- **Mannerisms:** Tends to adjust her glasses when nervous; speaks with precise, deliberate diction
- **Values:** Discovery, Recognition, Redemption
- **Fears:** Her secret being revealed, losing control of her company, facing [[Cassian Drake.png|Cassian]]
- **Desires:** To prove her worth, be seen as a visionary, be loved for who she is
- **Quirks:** Talks to her familiars like they’re people (and believes they understand)

## Narrative Profile
### Origin & Background
- **Heritage:** Mid-class Silverymoon family with minor magical lineage
- **Upbringing:** Studious and introverted; often overlooked, except by professors impressed by her focus
- **Training/Skills:** Specialized in summoning and arcano-mechanics, developed prototype [[Glimmerbind]]s
- [[Casey Ruffle Full Backstory]]
### Major Life Events
- **Turning Point:** Sabotaged Vespera’s portal experiment in secret, leading to her disappearance
- **Current Status:** Founder of The Ebon Chain, still building legitimacy and facing mounting scrutiny
- **Hidden Truths:** Her investor is the archdevil [[Gargauth]], and her company is spreading [[Ebon Chain Contract|infernal contracts]] via magical fine print

## Abilities
| Power / Trait         | Description |
|-----------------------|-------------|
| **[[Glimmerbind]] Crafter** | Can create magical containment spheres capable of binding Fey spirits |
| **Arcane Portal Specialist** | Stabilized a working Feywild gateway (based on stolen research) |
| **Familiar Link** | Shares sensory and emotional connection with bonded Fey |
| **Blind Spot: Guilt** | Emotional blocks tied to Vespera, limits objectivity in high-stress moral debates |

## Relationships
### Allies
- **[[Cassian Drake]]** – Former classmate and unknowing muse; Casey is still emotionally attached
- **[[Gargauth|Master Kaelion]]** – Her shadowy investor; manipulates her from the sidelines

### Enemies
- **[[Feywild Courts]]** – Increasingly enraged over the trafficking of Fey beings
- **[[Harpers]]** – Investigating The Ebon Chain's true motives and fine-print soul binding

### Key Connections
- **[[Vespera Vale]]** – Missing former rival; Casey secretly caused her accident
- **[[vondal mossmantle.png|Vondal Mossmantle]]** – Groundskeeper from [[Strixhaven]] who suspects Casey’s company is built on lies.

## Hooks & Secrets
- **Rumors:** Some say The Ebon Chain's tech is Fey-forged or infernally tainted… Others claim Casey talks to her devices like they’re alive.
- **Secrets:** She sabotaged her rival Vespera and unwittingly made a pact with Gargauth.
- **Adventure Hook:** Players might be hired to audit Ebon Chain's soul contracts, escort a young Familiarist into the Gloaming Glade, or track down one of the Forbidden-tier familiars that escaped containment.

## Quotes
>_“It's not about controlling them… if you work hard enough they will come to love you!”_  
>— **Casey Ruffle**, founder of the Ebon Chain
