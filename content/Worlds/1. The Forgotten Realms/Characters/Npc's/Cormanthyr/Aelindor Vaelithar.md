# Aelindor Vaelithar
## Profile

#### What They Want:
- To uphold the ancient traditions and purity of the Order of the Arcane Blade.
- To serve as an unyielding protector of Cormanthor and the Weave.
- To inspire the [[Order of the Arcane Blade#Modern Role & Divisions|Ilaer]] through his steadfast devotion to the Order's ideals.

#### What They Fear:
- The rise of the [[Order of the Arcane Blade#Modern Role & Divisions|Sular]] and perversion of the Order's ideals.
- Failing to fulfill his sacred duty and losing the balance of the Weave in Cormanthor.

#### What They Have Lost:
- His mortal life, including the ability to pass on to [[Olympus-Arborea-Arvandor|Arvandor]]
- Relationships with those who left for Evermeet, as his refusal to join them strained bonds of kinship and trust.

---

## History Summary 
Aelindor Vaelithar was not the most powerful caster of his time, but his unwavering faith in the Order’s principles made him a figure of profound influence. A zealot for the Order’s ideals, Aelindor viewed the world in stark terms of right and wrong, refusing to accept compromises or practical solutions. When the Retreat to Evermeet began, Aelindor’s unshakable devotion to the Order and its purpose led him to stay behind, despite the logical and practical advantages of leaving with his kin.

Unable to accept the nuance of balancing duty with pragmatism, Aelindor stood firm, vowing to protect Cormanthor and the Dawnspire at all costs. 

Seeing his resolve, the other High Mages, though reluctant, honored his sacrifice and performed the **Fhaor'Akh'Tel'Quess** ritual to bind him to the forest. This act transformed Aelindor into an eternal dryad, tethered to the Weave and the forest, ensuring the protection of Cormanthor through his existence.

### [[Elven High Magic]]
- His presence anchors the **[[Elven High Magic#Ritual of the Myriad|N’Maernthor]]** spell. If he leaves the forest, the spell is nullified.
- Transformed into an Eternal Dryad by the **[[Elven High Magic|Fhaor'Akh'Tel'Quess]]** spell.
	- Spell is permanently broken if he leaves [[Cormanthor Forest]], returning him to mortal form and restoring his elf soul.
### Roleplay Hooks
- **Unyielding Devotion**: Aelindor’s black-and-white perspective often clashes with others’ more nuanced or pragmatic views. NPCs and players may challenge his inability to adapt to changing times or the complexity of modern threats.
- **Vulnerabilities in Faith**: His rigid devotion can be exploited by those who point out the cracks in his logic, especially in moments where his principles lead to inaction or harm.
- **Conflict with Reformers**: Younger members of the Order, or reformist NPCs and players, could highlight his inflexibility as a danger to the Order’s future, creating tension between tradition and adaptation.

## Personality and Traits
Aelindor embodies the serene wisdom of a timeless leader, yet his serenity hides the blind faith that drives him. He is calm, measured, and deeply connected to the Weave, serving as a spiritual anchor for the Order. However, his unwillingness to compromise or accept the need for change makes him both a revered figure and a source of frustration for those around him.

- **Appearance**: Bark-like golden skin, silver leaf-like hair, and glowing golden eyes that reflect the essence of the Weave.
- **Personality**: Wise, serene, but inflexible; a paragon of tradition and belief, bordering on naïveté
