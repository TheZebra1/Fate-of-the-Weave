# [[faerunian-bhaal-scag|Bhaal]]
## Overview
**Summary**:  
The **Church of Bhaal** is a clandestine cult devoted to **Bhaal, the Lord of Murder**, one of the [[Dead Three]]. Its followers believe in **ritualistic killing** and the **ascendancy of murder as a divine art**. Worshipers operate in **secret, infiltrating cities, noble courts, and criminal underworlds**, waiting for the time when Bhaal’s dominion over death will be absolute.

#### What the Organization Wants:
- **To spread murder and assassinations** as a divine sacrament.  
- **To ensure Bhaal’s resurrection** through mass killings.  
- **To infiltrate Waterdeep, Baldur’s Gate, and other major cities.**  
- **To eliminate rival deities and their followers.**  

#### What the Organization Fears:
- **Exposure**: Bhaalists thrive in the dark—**open revelation would bring destruction**.  
- **The [[Harpers]] and the [[Flaming Fist]]**: Organizations that actively hunt them.  
- **Bhaal’s Wrath**: Failure to kill in his name can result in **being forsaken or slain by fellow cultists**.  

#### What the Organization Has Lost:
- **Their former temples**—the faith was **weakened when Bhaal was slain** during the Time of Troubles.  
- **The first generation of Bhaalspawn**—hunted down and eradicated.  
- **Trust among the Dead Three**—Bane and Myrkul’s followers have different agendas.  

## Etymology
The name **Bhaal** originates from **ancient Netherese dialects**, meaning *“The Bloodied One”* or *“The True End”*. His followers refer to themselves as **Children of Bhaal** or **Murderborn**.

## Activities
### **Primary Activities**
- **Contract killings and assassinations** as religious rituals.  
- **Spreading fear** through well-placed murders.  
- **Gathering forbidden knowledge** related to undeath and resurrection.  
- **Corrupting noble courts** by embedding assassins and spies.  
- **Performing dark rituals** to bring Bhaal closer to full resurrection.  

---

## Society
### **Beliefs**
- **Murder is not a crime—it is a divine act.**  
- **Every person has a preordained death, which Bhaalists seek to fulfill.**  
- **Only the strongest and most ruthless are fit to serve Bhaal.**  
- **To kill without passion is meaningless—murder must be felt.**  

### **Culture**
- **Bhaal’s followers operate in cells**, rarely knowing more than a few fellow cultists.  
- **Assassins, mercenaries, and political saboteurs** often double as Bhaalists.  
- **Initiation rites involve ritualistic killings**—sometimes of loved ones.  
- **The cult rewards cruelty and cunning over loyalty.**  

### **Religion**
- **Bhaal is worshipped through blood sacrifice.**  
- **Prayer is silent; only death should speak.**  
- **The most devout can receive “The Gift”**—a brief glimpse into how and when they will die.  

## Possessions
- **Hidden shrines and temples** buried beneath major cities.  
- **The Crimson Codex**—a ledger of every high-profile assassination ever carried out in Bhaal’s name.  
- **Blessed murder weapons**, including the infamous **Daggers of the Crimson Oath**.  
- **A network of spies, informants, and assassins**.  

## History
### **Founding**
- Bhaal’s worship dates back to **ancient [[Netheril]]**, where he was **first revered as a god of executioners and assassins**.
- He became one of **The Dead Three**, along with **[[Bane]] and [[Myrkul]]**.
- **During the [[Time of Troubles]]**, Bhaal was **slain by [[Cyric]]**, but not before leaving behind his essence in the **Bhaalspawn**.

### **Key Events**
- **The Bhaalspawn Crisis (1372 DR)**: The last remnants of Bhaal’s divine power were hunted and eradicated.  
- **The Rise of the [[Church of Bhaal]] (1482 DR)**: A resurgence of Bhaalists began infiltrating **Baldur’s Gate** and **Waterdeep**.  
- **The Night of Long Shadows (1496 DR)**: Over 100 Waterdhavian nobles were assassinated in a single night—Bhaal’s work.  
- **Present Day (1499 DR)**: Bhaal’s influence is spreading once more, and his **chosen champions prepare for the next age of blood.**

## Rumors & Legends
- **The Crimson Saint**: A rumored immortal assassin, said to be Bhaal’s direct hand in [[Faerûn]].  
- **The Whispering Blade**: A cursed weapon that speaks the names of those fated to die.  
- **The Bleeding Moon Prophecy**: A hidden scripture predicting Bhaal’s full return when the sky turns red for seven days.  
- **The Betrayer’s Bane**: A secret ritual said to turn a traitor’s own blood into a sentient assassin.
