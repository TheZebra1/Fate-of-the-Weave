# Set/Zehir: God of Darkness, Poison, and Serpents
## Overview
- **Name**: Set (original Egyptian name), Zehir (Faerûnian god name).
- **Portfolio**: Darkness, poison, serpents, betrayal, assassins.
- **Alignment**: Chaotic Evil.
- **Domains**: Shadowfell, serpents, poison, yuan-ti worship.

## History
- **Origin**: Set was a god of storms, chaos, and treachery in the Egyptian pantheon.
- **Arrival in Faerûn**: During the [[Spellplague]] (1385 DR), Set crossed into [[Faerûn]], finding Sseth ruling over serpents and the yuan-ti.
- **The Usurpation of [[Sseth**]]:
  - Set discovered Sseth's dominion over serpents and yuan-ti and deemed it his rightful place.
  - He discovered that Sseth was already weakened from previous battles with other Gods and struck.
  - Struck a deal with [[Shar]] that allowed him to enter [[The Towers of Night]] & assassinate Sseth in his domain.

### The Confrontation and Sseth’s Death
The final confrontation between **Set** and **Sseth** unfolded in **Sseth’s shadowy domain**, a vast, dark jungle intertwined with ancient stone temples and serpentine totems. Sseth, was attempting to regenerate in a hibernation state, a fact Set exploited to its fullest and bound him.

Set closed in for the **killing blow**, wielding a **blade forged of pure shadow and poison**—a weapon he had crafted for this very moment. As Sseth struggled against the bindings, **Set drove the blade into his heart**. The weapon didn’t just wound Sseth physically; it **unraveled his divine essence**, consuming it and **binding it to Set’s will**.

With Sseth's death the domain was wrestled under Set's control, transforming the center of the jungle into Set's new throne: A large pyramid that oozed slick poison out of every crevice. The top of the pyramid was flat instead of pointed, making room for a Jade Throne that crested up in the shape of a cobra arcing over the seat. 

Upon his inauguration from Lord [[Ao]] Set took the name [[Zehir]].

From that moment, all the prayers that previously went to Sseth were channeled into Zehir instead. Most followers were compleltely unaware that their God was dead, but at least one cult was formed in Zehir's name doing his personal bidding: [[Fangs of Zehir]]
