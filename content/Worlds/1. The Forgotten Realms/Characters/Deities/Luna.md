# Luna
## Profile

#### What They Want:
- To provide a safe haven for travelers and worshippers of Selûne.
- To guide and aid those who seek Selûne's light.

#### What They Fear:
- The spread of Shar's influence and darkness.
- Harm coming to her patrons and followers.

#### What They Have Lost:
- A portion of her divine power while manifesting as an avatar.
- The ability to intervene directly in godly conflicts while in mortal form.

## History Summary

Luna is the avatar of [[Selûne]], the goddess of the moon, rumored to live among mortals in [[Waterdeep]]'s **North Ward** & **Dock Ward**. She manages the [[Selûne’s Smile]] tavern, serving as a beacon of hope and guidance for those who seek the ***Moonmaiden's*** light. Luna has played a pivotal role in various events, including aiding adventurers against dark forces and providing sanctuary to those in need. Her presence in Waterdeep symbolizes Selûne's enduring commitment to the mortal realm.

## **Extended History:**

- **Early Life and Establishment of Selûne's Smile:**
  - In the 1350s DR, Luna managed Selûne's Smile, a newly opened inn in Waterdeep's Dock Ward.
  - She hired Vajra Valmeyjar as a bouncer, and they developed a strong friendship.

- **Association with Vajra Valmeyjar:**
  - Vajra worked at Selûne's Smile and maintained a close relationship with Luna even after leaving her employment.

- **Encounters with Conner and the Kittenlord:**
  - In the late 1340s DR, Luna healed Conner, a conman left for dead in the desert.
  - She later guided him to protect the kittenlord from Abon Duum, leading to Conner becoming the guardian of the heir to the great cat lord.

- **Involvement in the Song of Selûne:**
  - Luna recounted the "Song of Selûne," revealing her past as the goddess who imprisoned the monstrous Imgig Zu within the Selûne's Eye gem.

- **Battle Against Imgig Zu:**
  - In 1357 DR, Imgig Zu escaped from Selûne's Eye and sought to release his people.
  - Luna observed as adventurers, including Vajra and Priam Agrivar, confronted Imgig Zu.
  - She intervened by directing moonlight to blind the ogres, aiding in Imgig Zu's defeat.
  - After the battle, Luna placed Selûne's Eye on a shelf over the bar at Selûne's Smile.

- **Time of Troubles and Confrontation with Shar:**
  - During the Time of Troubles in 1358 DR, a false avatar of Selûne appeared in Waterdeep.
  - Luna confronted this impostor, who was actually the goddess [[Shar]].
  - After a fierce battle over Waterdeep,  Shar retreated.

- **Legacy:**.
  - [[Selûne’s Eye]] remained forgotten on the shelf over the bar of [[Selûne’s Smile]] for decades awaiting it's true purpose as part of the [[Ritual of the Waxing]] which can bring back [[Selûnarra]] from the [[Gates of the Moon]] in [[Ysgard]] to save [[Toril]] in a time of great [[Darkening|Darkness]]
