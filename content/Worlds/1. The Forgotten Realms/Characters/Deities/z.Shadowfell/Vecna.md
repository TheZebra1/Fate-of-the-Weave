Vecna is a once-human king and lich from the world of [[Oerth]] who ascended to godhood. Known as the **Master of All that is Secret and Hidden**, Vecna is considered one of the **most powerful liches** to have ever existed. His influence spans the multiverse, and despite his many defeats, he remains an enduring threat, always gathering strength to rise again.

- [[exandria-vecna-egw|Vecna]]/[[greyhawk-vecna-xdmg|Vecna]]

## Origins and First Defeat  

Vecna began as a mortal wizard on Oerth who mastered necromancy and sought ultimate power. Through his dark studies, he ascended to lichdom and established a vast empire ruled through fear, undeath, and forbidden knowledge. His most trusted lieutenant, **[[Strahd von Zarovich|Kas]]**, served as his right hand, wielding the **[[sword-of-kas-xdmg|Sword of Kas]]**, a weapon forged by Vecna himself.

However, Kas betrayed Vecna, leading to a legendary battle that shattered Vecna's empire. During this confrontation, Vecna lost his hand and eye, two relics that would later become infamous artifacts. Though seemingly destroyed, Vecna’s essence persisted, and his story did not end there.

## Rise and Fall as a God  

Vecna's mastery of necromancy and forbidden knowledge allowed him to transcend his lichdom and ascend to godhood, expanding his influence across the multiverse. His portfolio came to include **secrets, undeath, and dark magic**. As a deity, Vecna attempted to assert his dominance over the planes, including a bold move to invade **[[Sigil]]**, the City of Doors.  

Vecna's ambition, however, often led to his downfall. His schemes to rewrite reality and establish ultimate control were thwarted by powerful mortals, rival deities, and planar forces. Despite these defeats, Vecna's essence remained intact, retreating to secret places to plot his return.

## Presence in the Forgotten Realms  

Vecna’s direct influence on *[[Toril]]* is minimal but profound. Artifacts tied to him, such as the **[[Hand of Vecna]]** and **[[Eye of Vecna]]**, have been discovered in the Realms, causing devastation among those who wield them. His cults operate in secrecy, spreading his worship and working to further his schemes in this new world.  

Vecna’s arrival in the Forgotten Realms may represent another phase of his cyclical rise and fall. Following defeats in Oerth and other planes, Vecna could be seen as gathering his strength in the Realms, seeking to establish a new power base from [[Cavitius]] and [[Barovia]] to reclaim his former glory.

## Cyclical Nature of Vecna’s Story  

Vecna’s story is defined by a recurring pattern:
1. **Ambition and Rise**: Vecna grows in power, often achieving new heights such as lichdom or godhood.  
2. **Defeat**: His schemes are thwarted, often by betrayal (e.g., Kas) or opposition from powerful forces (e.g., in Sigil).  
3. **Survival and Gathering Strength**: Vecna’s essence persists, retreating to rebuild and plot his next return.

This cycle mirrors the nature of undeath itself: perpetual, relentless, and nearly impossible to destroy. Like **Sauron**, Vecna represents a persistent threat that never truly vanishes, always working from the shadows to rise again.

## Artifacts and Symbols  

Vecna is closely tied to several cursed and powerful artifacts:
- **The [[hand-of-vecna-xdmg|Hand of Vecna]]**: A severed hand that grants immense power to its wielder but corrupts them in the process.  
- **The [[eye-of-vecna-xdmg|Eye of Vecna]]**: A severed eye with similar effects, requiring the user to replace their own eye with it.  
- **The [[sword-of-kas-xdmg|Sword of Kas]]**: A weapon forged by Vecna and later used by Kas to betray him.

These artifacts serve as both tools of Vecna’s influence and reminders of his legendary defeats.
## Worship and Cult  

Vecna’s worshippers operate in secretive cells across the multiverse. Temples dedicated to him have been uncovered in [[Highport]], while his *cult is structured in a strict hierarchy where each position is named after a **body part***—symbolic of Vecna’s own dismemberment. His worshippers seek to uncover forbidden knowledge, spread his influence, and hasten his inevitable return.
