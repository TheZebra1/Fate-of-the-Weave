# Church of Gond 
[[faerunian-gond-scag|Gond]]
## Hierachy
[[Lantan]] was the original focal point of worship for the worship of Gond, however near the start of the [[Spellplague]] a great disaster befell Lantan wiping it off the face of the map. 

A Lantanese Master in the church of Gond named [[Avilda Montrose]] escaped the disaster and being the last of the Lantanese she brought the brillance of Lantan with her into [[Faerûn]] ultimately restarting the church in [[Melvaunt]] as it's Heirophant inspiring mechanical innovation like never before seen on the continent.
