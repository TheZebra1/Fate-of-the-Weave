# **Ao, the Overgod**  
![[AO.webp|500]]
***"All that is, was and will ever be—every god, every plane, and every star in the void— all are echoes within the dream of Ao... By his will, even the grandest eternity may fade as if it never was."***
## **Overview**  
Ao is the **Overgod**, a supreme entity that stands above all deities. He enforces **cosmic balance** and ensures that no god exceeds their station or abandons their duty. Though rarely seen, his will shapes the laws that even the gods must obey.  

## **Core Tenets**  
- **Supremacy** → No god stands above Ao; he alone governs the divine order.  
- **Balance** → He ensures the equilibrium of **law and chaos, good and evil, mortal and divine.**  
- **Non-Intervention** → Ao does not meddle in mortal affairs—he **enforces**, not favors.  
- **Judgment** → Gods who forsake their roles or disrupt the balance **face his wrath.**  

## **Known Acts**  
- **The [[Time of Troubles]]** → Stripped the gods of their power, forcing them to walk the mortal world.  
- **The Punishment of [[Annam's Burden|Annam]]** → Bound the [[Annam|All-Father]] into the [[Spine of the World]] for abandoning his children.  
- **Divine Order** → Maintains the hierarchy of gods, preventing any from rising beyond their intended role.  

*"The gods rule mortals, but Ao rules the gods."*
