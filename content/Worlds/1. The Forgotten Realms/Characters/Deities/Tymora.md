## Profile

#### What They Want:
- *To spread fortune and reward boldness among mortals.*
- *To encourage adventure, risk-taking, and the pursuit of destiny.*
- *To oppose tyranny and those who would impose rigid order on fate.*

#### What They Fear:
- *The loss of free will and chance in the world.*
- *The rise of gods or forces that would seek to control or manipulate luck for evil ends.*
- *The return of Beshaba’s influence, corrupting fate and twisting fortune into misfortune.*

#### What They Have Lost:
- *Her original unity with Beshaba before the **Twinning of Tyche**, when they were one goddess.*
- *Some direct influence over fate, as it remains unpredictable even to her divine sight.*
- *The trust of those who blame her for their misfortunes, despite her impartial nature.*

---

## History Summary

### **Origins & The Twinning of Tyche**
- Tymora was born from the **divine split of Tyche**, the original goddess of fortune, when she was **corrupted by Moander**.
- The good essence of Tyche **manifested as Tymora**, embodying **luck, courage, and fortune**.
- The darker half, **Beshaba**, became the goddess of misfortune and calamity.

### **Role in Faerûn**
- Tymora is **worshiped by adventurers, gamblers, and the bold**.
- Her clergy teaches that **"Fortune favors the bold,"** rewarding those who take risks with divine luck.
- She is associated with **coin flips, lucky charms, and acts of daring**.

### **Rivalry with Beshaba**
- Tymora is in constant **opposition to Beshaba**, who brings bad luck and curses upon the world.
- The two sisters **rarely interact directly**, but their followers often clash in matters of fate and chance.

### **Current Status**
- Tymora continues to **watch over risk-takers and the daring**, granting luck to those who live boldly.
- Her divine influence is felt **whenever fate turns unexpectedly in someone's favor**.
- She remains **a beloved deity**, with shrines in **many gambling halls, adventurers' guilds, and wayfarers' temples** across Faerûn.
