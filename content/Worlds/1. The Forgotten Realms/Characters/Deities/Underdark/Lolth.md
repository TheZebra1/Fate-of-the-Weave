## The Spider Queen, Queen of the Demonweb Pits  
![[Lolth.webp|500]]
[[drow-lolth-scag|Lolth]]
**Goals:**  
- The [[Darkening]]
- [[Demon Weave]]
Lolth enjoys absolute dominion over nearly all drow in the Underdark and requires unwavering worship and obedience. She thrives on chaos, betrayal, and strife, ensuring that only the most ruthless and cunning of her followers rise to power. Her ultimate ambition is to extend her influence beyond the Underdark, corrupting and subjugating other civilizations.  

**Personality:**  
Lolth is **cruel, manipulative, and calculating**, delighting in deceit and betrayal. She fosters **paranoia and competition** among her followers, believing that only the strongest and most cunning deserve her favor. She is also **vain and vengeful**, never forgetting a slight and punishing disloyalty with eternal torment.  

**Abyssal Layer:**  
Lolth rules the **Demonweb Pits (66th Layer of the Abyss)**, a vast, shifting network of web-choked corridors and extradimensional pathways. The plane resembles a massive spider's web spun between the void, with bridges leading to various other planes under Lolth’s control. It is a nightmarish realm filled with **massive spiders, drider servants, and the tormented souls of those who failed her**.  

**Feuds:**  
- **[[Corellon Larethian]]:** Once a powerful elven goddess, Lolth was cast down by Corellon for her ambition and betrayal. She harbors an **eternal hatred** for him and seeks vengeance against all elvenkind.  
- **[[Abyss|Other Demon Lords]]:** Though a powerful demon queen, Lolth does not easily ally with other demon lords, viewing them as **lesser beings** unworthy of her status. She manipulates them when needed but discards them just as quickly.  

Lolth’s influence is felt throughout the Underdark, where her priestesses enforce her will, and her spiderkin terrorize those who oppose her. Only through **deception, strength, and unwavering devotion** can one hope to survive under her rule.
