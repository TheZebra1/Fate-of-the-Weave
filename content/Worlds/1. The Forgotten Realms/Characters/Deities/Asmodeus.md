# Asmodeus
[[Asmodeus' Truth]]
## Profile
[[faerunian-asmodeus-scag|Asmodeus]]
#### What They Want:
- **The fulfillment of contracts and oaths** sworn under his domain.  
- **The continued function of Baator**, ensuring it remains the most efficient cosmic force of law and punishment.  
- **The enforcement of the Pact Primeval**, which grants devils legal authority over the souls of the damned.  
- **To uphold order across the planes**, in opposition to the forces of chaos.

#### What They Fear:
- The **collapse of law**, particularly if mortals or deities attempt to dissolve the structures that uphold Baator’s authority.  
- The **rise of unchecked chaos**, whether through demonkind, rogue deities, or rebellious mortal factions.  
- **Divine overreach**, where the gods themselves violate cosmic balance and nullify agreements that have held since the Dawn War.

#### What They Have Lost:
- **Baator’s original role as a celestial realm**, now forever associated with tyranny and damnation.  
- **His place among the lawful pantheon**, having been cast into exile under unclear circumstances.  
- **The trust of the celestial hosts**, despite his insistence that he only fulfills the duties assigned to him.

## History Summary  

The origins of Asmodeus are debated, with scholars, theologians, and infernal cults offering conflicting accounts. No single version is considered absolute truth, though three primary theories dominate historical records:

### **1. The Serpent of Law (Oldest Theory)**
> *"Asmodeus is not merely an archdevil, but a fragment of a greater primordial force—A being of Law who, alongside a counterpart, shaped the cosmic order. But in their dispute over the center of creation, they clashed, and Asmodeus was cast down into Baator, his power diminished but his will unbroken."*
- This theory suggests Asmodeus predates even the gods and is a **lawgiver force bound to Hell** rather than an ordinary deity.  
- Some infernal cults claim that **one day, he will reclaim his lost status** when the time is right.  

### **2. The Fallen General (Church-Sanctioned Version)**
> *"Asmodeus was once the greatest of the celestial generals, fighting against the Abyss. But over time, his means became too ruthless. Tried by his divine peers, he was cast down, yet struck a bargain: he would rule Baator and keep the demons at bay in exchange for governing the damned."*
- This version is the **most widely accepted** among divine scholars, as it frames Asmodeus as a **necessary evil** rather than a force of pure malice.  
- The **Church of Asmodeus** uses this story to justify their worship, arguing that **he is a protector of cosmic balance, even if his means are harsh**.  

### **3. The Betrayal and the Ruby Rod (A Forbidden Theory)**
> *"Asmodeus was once the right hand of a god known as *He Who Was*, a now-forgotten supreme deity of law. At the urging of a demon lord, he stole a fragment of raw chaos and forged the **Ruby Rod**, a weapon capable of rewriting cosmic order. With this weapon, he slew his own master and was cast into the pit as punishment."*
- This theory is **largely suppressed**, with few scholars daring to entertain it.  
- It **suggests that Asmodeus' power is tied not to his godhood, but to the Ruby Rod itself**, which acts as his **binding contract with reality**.  

### **Modern Role and The [[Pact Primeval]]**
- **Following the Dawn War**, the gods, fearing their own power, agreed to **self-regulation** and signed the **Pact Primeval**.  
- This pact granted Asmodeus **legitimate legal dominion over Baator**, recognizing it as a necessary part of cosmic balance.  
- **Article VII of the Pact**, known as the **Divine Armistice Clause**, is a rare clause allowing the gods to **temporarily cease all divine interference** under certain conditions.
	- [[Ao]] looked and saw that this was agreeable to the balance of the universe and signed applied his own seal to the Pact.
- While rarely invoked, it remains one of the few laws that **Asmodeus has absolute power to defend this arrangment**, as one of the **two signatories required to undo it.**  

### **Modern Perception**
- To most mortals, **Asmodeus is the personification of tyranny and law**, feared and revered in equal measure.  
- To his followers, **he is the ultimate arbiter of order**, rewarding those who uphold their contracts and punishing those who defy structure.  
- Among the gods, he is seen as **dangerous but essential**—for as long as the Nine Hells function, chaos remains contained.  

## Current Status  
- **Asmodeus rules from [[Nessus]]**, the deepest layer of [[Baator]], where he **governs the hierarchy of devils with absolute control**.  
- He **fulfills his legal obligations flawlessly**, ensuring that no celestial or divine force can question his role.  
- He remains **a careful, patient ruler**, choosing his words and actions with precision, never giving his enemies cause to act against him.  
- **The Pact remains intact. The law is upheld. And Hell endures.**
