## The Prince of Deception  
![[Fraz-Urb'luu.jpg]]

**Goals:**  
Fraz-Urb’luu seeks revenge on those who imprisoned him and desires to spread lies, illusions, and paranoia across the planes, eroding all sense of reality.  

**Personality:**  
He is a master of deception, preferring manipulation and illusion over brute force. Arrogant and vengeful, he delights in making mortals and demons alike question their own sanity.  

**Abyssal Layer:**  
Fraz-Urb’luu rules **Hollow’s Heart**, the 176th layer of the Abyss. It is an empty, ever-shifting wasteland where illusions become reality, and nothing can be trusted.  

**Feuds:**  
- **[[Zuggtmoy]]:** Once tricked him into imprisonment, for which he has never forgiven her.  
- **[[Graz’zt]]:** A frequent rival in the game of deception and power plays.
