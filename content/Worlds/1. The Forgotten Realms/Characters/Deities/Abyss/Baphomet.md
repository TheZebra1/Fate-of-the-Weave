## The Horned King, Demon Lord of Beasts  
![[Baphomet.webp|500]]

**Goals:**  
Baphomet seeks to spread chaos, savagery, and instinct-driven rule over civilization, ultimately leading the world into an age of brutal, bestial dominance.  

**Personality:**  
Brutal, cunning, and bloodthirsty, Baphomet despises civilization and intellect, preferring raw instinct and dominance through physical might. However, he is not a mindless brute—he possesses a sinister cunning.  

**Abyssal Layer:**  
Baphomet rules **The Endless Maze**, the 600th layer of the Abyss. It is a massive, ever-changing labyrinth filled with deadly traps, minotaurs, and demonic beasts.  

**Feuds:**  
- **[[Yeenoghu]]:** A savage rivalry between the demon lords of bestial fury, each claiming dominance over primal slaughter.  
- **[[Graz’zt]]:** Due to past manipulations, Baphomet harbors a deep hatred for the Dark Prince.
