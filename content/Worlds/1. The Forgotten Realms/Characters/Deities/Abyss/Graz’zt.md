## The Dark Prince of Deception & Pleasure 
![[Graz’zt.webp|500]]

**Goals:**  
Graz’zt seeks to extend his influence across the multiverse, corrupting rulers, seducing mortals, and building a vast empire of deception and pleasure.  

**Personality:**  
Charismatic and calculating, Graz’zt is one of the most intelligent and patient demon lords. He thrives on manipulation, controlling others through desire rather than brute force.  

**Abyssal Layer:**  
Graz’zt rules **Azzagrat**, a triple-layered domain covering the 45th, 46th, and 47th layers of the Abyss. It is a decadent realm of pleasure and deception, filled with shadowy cities and demonic courts.  

**Feuds:**  
- **[[Demogorgon]] & [[Orcus]]:** Both are his rivals in the eternal war for Abyssal supremacy.  
- **[[Zybilna|Iggwilv]] (Tasha):** Their relationship is complicated—once lovers, now rivals.

This Demon prince tried to unite the Demon Lords of [[Abyss]] against a plot of [[Zybilna|Iggwilv]] to rule the Abyss.

Unfortunately this brief alliance left them all open for a Plot of [[House Baenre#2. ** Gromph Baenre.png Gromph Baenre **|Gromph Baenre]] to pull in several Demon Lords to the Underdark to wreak chaos and overthrow [[Lolth]]'s influence on [[Menzoberranzan]].
