## Demon Queen of Fungi  
![[Zuggtmoy.webp|500]]

**Goals:**  
Zuggtmoy seeks to spread fungi across all planes, aiming to transform living creatures and environments into fungal entities under her control. She desires complete **domination** through the slow, creeping corruption of organic matter.  

**Personality:**  
She is patient and cunning, preferring subtle methods over brute force. Zuggtmoy delights in decay and corruption, believing fungi to be the ultimate form of life.  

**Abyssal Layer:**  
She rules over **Shedaklah**, the 222nd layer of the Abyss, a realm of fungal growth and rot. However, she shares this domain with **[[Juiblex]], the Faceless Lord**, and the two are bitter enemies, constantly vying for dominance over the plane.  

**Feuds:**  
- **[[Juiblex]]:** A constant rival for control over Shedaklah, as both seek total domination of the layer.  
- **[[Urdlen]]:** The gnome god of greed and murder, against whom she has a historical enmity. This conflict resulted in a disease affecting Urdlen's realm, believed to be Zuggtmoy's doing.
