### Spawn of Demogorgon: Bagromar & Tetradarian
> Both are “spawn-clones,” created magically or biologically to serve Demogorgon’s will, reflecting his fractured psyche.
- **[[Bagromar]]**  
  - Clone of Demogorgon’s **right head** (**Hethradiah**)  
  - Embodies **rage, ferocity, and instinct**  
  - Acts as a **brutal general** of Demogorgon’s armies  
  - Resides in the Gaping Maw, often unleashed during invasions  
- **[[Tetradarian]]**  
  - Clone of Demogorgon’s **left head** (**Aameul**)  
  - Embodies **cunning, calculation, and strategy**  
  - Serves as **master tactician and vizier** to Demogorgon  
  - Schemes within the twisting towers of Abysm  

**Personality:**  
A being of dual minds, Demogorgon’s two heads—Aameul and Hethradiah—are constantly at odds, yet equally intelligent and ruthless. He is cunning, unpredictable, and driven by an insatiable thirst for destruction.  

**Abyssal Layer:**  
Demogorgon rules over **[[Gaping Maw]]**, the 88th layer of [[Abyss]]. It is a desolate ocean filled with monstrous horrors, jagged rock spires, and abyssal predators, reflecting his savage and chaotic nature.  

**Feuds:**  
- **[[Orcus]] & [[Graz’zt]]:** Two of his biggest rivals in the eternal struggle for dominance over the Abyss.  
- **[[Dagon]]:** An ancient demon lord with whom he has an uneasy alliance but often comes into conflict.
