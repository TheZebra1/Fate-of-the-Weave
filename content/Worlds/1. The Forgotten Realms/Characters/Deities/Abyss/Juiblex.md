## Faceless Lord, Demon Lord of Slime and Ooze  
![[Jubilex.webp|500]]

**Goals:**  
Juiblex seeks to **consume** and corrupt all forms of life, **dissolving** everything into his gelatinous essence. His ultimate desire is to flood existence with his pestilent ooze, reducing all to mindless, amorphous sludge under his control.  

**Personality:**  
Juiblex's mind is alien and incomprehensible, even by demonic standards. He acts with unknowable purpose, driven by a malevolent hunger to destroy and consume. Unlike other demon lords, he cares little for intrigue or schemes, focusing solely on the act of dissolution.  

**Abyssal Layer:**  
Juiblex resides in **[[Shedaklah]]**, the 222nd layer of the Abyss, a fetid, decaying swamp of slime, filth, and oozes. Though he shares this realm with **Zuggtmoy**, the Demon Queen of Fungi, the two are in constant conflict, each attempting to consume and overtake the other. His domain within Shedaklah is an ever-shifting, toxic mire filled with writhing, sentient slimes and acids that dissolve any who enter.  

**Feuds:**  
- **Zuggtmoy:** Juiblex shares the 222nd layer with Zuggtmoy, and their mutual hunger has made them mortal enemies. Each seeks to dominate Shedaklah by completely consuming the other.  
- **All sentient life:** Juiblex is an enemy of all creation, seeking only to break down and dissolve everything into his own oozing filth. Unlike other demon lords, he has no alliances—only endless hunger.
