## The Beast of Butchery  
![[Yeenoghu.jpg]]

**Goals:**  
[[Yeenoghu]] desires nothing but endless slaughter, seeking to drown the world in blood and turn all creatures into ravenous, bloodthirsty predators.  

**Personality:**  
Completely mad with bloodlust, Yeenoghu is unpredictable and relentless. He cares nothing for strategy or power plays—only destruction and carnage.  

**Abyssal Layer:**  
Yeenoghu rules **The Seeping Woods**, the 422nd layer of [[Abyss]]. It is a rotting, corpse-strewn hunting ground where gnolls and other ravenous beasts endlessly pursue prey.  

**Feuds:**  
- **[[Baphomet]]:** A constant rival, as both seek to claim dominance over savagery and beasts.  
- **All gods of civilization:** He despises order and seeks to eradicate any form of structured society.
