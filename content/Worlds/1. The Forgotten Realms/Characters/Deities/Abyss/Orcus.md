## Demon Prince of Undeath  
![[Orcus.webp|500]]

**Goals:**  
Orcus seeks to transform all life into undead servitude under his rule, ultimately desiring to replace all other forms of existence with his own necrotic will.  

**Personality:**  
Orcus is brooding, cruel, and nihilistic. Unlike other demon lords, he prefers to act indirectly, using necromantic magic and his cultists to achieve his goals while he broods in his dark fortress.  

**Abyssal Layer:**  
Orcus rules **Thanatos**, the 113th layer of the Abyss. It is a bleak, corpse-strewn wasteland filled with undead horrors, frozen cities, and tomb-laden mountains.  

**Feuds:**  
- **[[Demogorgon]] & [[Graz’zt]]:** Both are his enemies in the power struggle for Abyssal dominance.  
- **[[Kiaransalee]]:** The drow goddess of undeath, who once tried to usurp his rule over Thanatos.
