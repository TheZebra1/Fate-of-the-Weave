# Shar: The Mistress of Night
***Home: [[The Towers of Night]]***
[[faerunian-shar-scag|Shar]]
## Overview
- **Portfolio**: Darkness, loss, secrets, despair.
- **Alignment**: Neutral Evil.
- **Domains**: Trickery, Death.
- **Symbol**: A black disk with a deep purple border.
- **Worshipers**: Those who seek to conceal truths, embrace despair, or wield darkness as a weapon. Includes spies, assassins, and outcasts.

## Description
- Shar is one of the most ancient gods in the Forgotten Realms, representing the eternal void and the loss of light.
- She is cloaked in **flowing black robes** that shimmer with starlight, with a face partially obscured in shadow. Her glowing **purple eyes** are a window to the infinite mysteries of the cosmos.
- Her **Palace of Loss**, located within the **Towers of Night**, is the epicenter of her dominion, a place of impossible geometry and shadow.

## Mythology
- Shar is the twin sister of **Selûne**, born from the primordial chaos at the dawn of creation.
- Together, they shaped the universe but fell into conflict when **[[Selûne]] brought light** into existence, which Shar despised as it disrupted her perfect void.
- Their eternal battle resulted in the creation of **Mystryl (Mystra)**, goddess of magic, born from a fragment of their conflict.

## Personality
- Shar is cunning, manipulative, and ruthless, embodying despair and secrecy.
- She desires absolute control and loathes the light her sister brought into existence.

## Worship
- Followers honor her through rituals of loss and secrecy, often carried out in darkness. Her clergy are known to spread despair and manipulate others to serve her hidden agendas.
