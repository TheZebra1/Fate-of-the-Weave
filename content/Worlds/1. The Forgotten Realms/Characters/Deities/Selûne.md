# Selûne: The Moonmaiden
- [[faerunian-selune-scag|Selune]]
	- [[Selûne's Song.wav|Selûne's Song]]
## Overview
- **Portfolio**: Moonlight, stars, navigation, love, and renewal.
- **Alignment**: Chaotic Good.
- **Domains**: Light, Life.
- **Symbol**: A pair of eyes surrounded by a circle of seven stars.
- **Worshipers**: Sailors, travelers, lovers, seekers of hope, and those who honor the moon.

## Description
- Selûne is a radiant and ethereal figure, cloaked in **silver and white robes** that shimmer like moonlight. Her eyes glow with the soft light of twin moons, reflecting peace and wisdom.
- Her presence is accompanied by an **aura of silvery light**, and her hair flows like a **river of starlight**.
- She resides in a celestial domain of peace and beauty, surrounded by rolling clouds and glowing stars.

## Mythology
- Selûne is [[Shar]]’s twin sister, born with her from the primordial chaos at the dawn of time.
- Their conflict began when **Selûne created light**, an act that Shar saw as a betrayal of their shared nature.
- The battle between the sisters resulted in the creation of **Mystryl ([[Mystra]])** and the cosmic balance of light and dark.

## Personality
- Selûne is compassionate, protective, and wise, embodying hope and renewal.
- She values love and guidance, serving as a beacon to mortals in times of darkness.

## Worship
- Followers honor Selûne through moonlit ceremonies and acts of kindness. Her clergy are healers, guides, and defenders of the light.
