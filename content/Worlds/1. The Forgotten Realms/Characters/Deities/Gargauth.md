# Gargauth – The Banished Lord
![[Gargauth.webp|500x500]]
[[Gargauth-Symbol.webp|Symbol]]
## Identity & History
- Once an **archdevil of [[Baator]]**, cast out for extreme treachery.  
- Lives primarily in the [[Material Plane]] and banned from [[Baator]].  
	- Was trapped in the [[Shield of the Hidden Lord]] beneath the [[Fields of the Dead]] near [[Baldur's Gate]]
		- Currently trapped inside [[Seraph Duirsar]] after a failed ritual to escape the shield.
- A master of **corruption, deception, and political infiltration**.  
- Patron of the [[Ebon Chain]]
	- ***Alias: Master Kaelion***

## **Weaknesses & Flaws**
- **Silver is Gargauth’s Achilles’ heel** – It disrupts his essence and prevents his magic from functioning properly.  
- **Divine opposition** – Gods of **justice, freedom, and the natural order** will intervene if they learn of his intentions.  

## **The Curse of the Forsaken Gate (Why He Cannot Use Conjuration)**
- Gargauth was once **a master of conjuration**, able to summon entire legions.  
	- **The Lords of Baator sealed away this power**, shattering **[[The Forsaken Gate]]**, his planar keystone locking him off from the entire [[list-spells-school-conjuration|Conjuration]] school of magic.
- Now, he **relies entirely on mortal warlocks and the Ebon Chain** to build his forces.  

## **The Argent Betrayal (Why Silver Harms Him)**
- Gargauth **betrayed celestial allies**, nearly leading a divine rebellion against Asmodeus.  
- When his plan failed, his **renegade angel followers were cursed into silver statues**.  
- As punishment, **his very essence was tied to silver**, making it the ultimate weapon against him.
