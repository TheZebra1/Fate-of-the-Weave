### Overview
**Planar Guardians** are powerful entities that emerge to protect the balance between the planes of existence. They are not physical beings in the traditional sense but are metaphysical manifestations summoned by the **[[Tablets of Fate]]** whenever someone attempts to alter or merge the planes. Their sole purpose is to prevent the disturbance of the natural order of the multiverse.

### Connection to the [[Tablets of Fate]]
The **[[Tablets of Fate]]** are ancient artifacts created by the overgod **Ao** to define the boundaries and laws of the planes. These tablets contain the fundamental laws that govern the multiverse, ensuring that no single plane can dominate or merge with another. A lesser-known subtext in the tablets reveals that any significant breach in the separation of planes triggers the creation of a **Planar Guardian**. These Guardians are bound by the ancient laws written on the tablets and exist solely to restore the balance that has been disrupted.

### Characteristics of Planar Guardians
- **Appearance**: Planar Guardians are not bound to any one form. Their shape and abilities are determined by the nature of the planar disruption. For example, a Guardian summoned to defend against an encroachment of the **[[Shadowfell]]** may take on a shadowy, ethereal form, while one defending against the **[[Feywild]]** may appear as a being of pure, vibrant energy.
  
- **Powers and Abilities**:
  - **Adaptive Combat**: Each Guardian is uniquely suited to counter the specific threat they are summoned to oppose. They possess powers that directly counteract the invading plane’s influence.
  - **Planar Seals**: Guardians can create magical seals to prevent further merging of the planes, temporarily halting the encroachment.
  - **Resilience**: These entities are incredibly tough, requiring immense strength or powerful magic to defeat.

### Known Types of Planar Guardians
1. **Shadow Guardians**:
   - Spawned when the **Shadowfell** encroaches on the Material Plane.
   - Can drain light and energy from their surroundings.
   - Often appear as towering figures with armor made of shadows, wielding weapons that drain the life force of their foes.

2. **Fey Guardians**:
   - Manifest when the **Feywild** is threatened or its balance is disturbed.
   - Use nature-based magic, such as entangling roots and blinding bursts of light.
   - Typically take the form of majestic, otherworldly creatures like stags, unicorns, or treants.

3. **Elemental Guardians**:
   - Emerge if the [[Elemental Planes]]s (Fire, Water, Air, Earth) are disrupted.
   - Each is attuned to their respective element, wielding control over elemental forces.
   - Appear as colossal beings made of their respective element, such as a fire giant or water serpent.

### Guardian Summoning Conditions
- The **[[Tablets of Fate]]** dictate that the **Planar Guardians** are bound to respond in the following conditions:
  - **Massive Planar Shifts**: Large-scale summoning or manipulation of planar energies.
  - **Permanent Alterations**: Attempts to permanently collapse, combine, or create planes.

### Known Prophecies
Legends speak of a prophecy hidden in the subtext of the **[[Tablets of Fate]]**:
> “When shadows consume the land and balance is torn asunder, the Guardians retribution will rise to restore wonder. But if so rises a *thorn* of greater will, the dark path will be fulfilled.”

### Historical Sightings
- **The Sundering**: Historical records suggest that Planar Guardians appeared during the Sundering, when the realms were reshaped by divine forces.
- **The Time of Troubles**: During this period, various Guardians were said to have materialized to restore the chaos caused by the gods walking the earth.

### Legends
- **Artifacts**: There may be relics scattered across [[Faerûn]] capable of strengthening, weakening or banishing the Guardians, offering unique opportunities to turn the tide of battle with a Guardian in a desired direction.
