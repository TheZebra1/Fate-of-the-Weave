## Overview
- [[Zybilna and Isolde Backstory]]
Zybilna is a cunning and enigmatic archfey who rules the Domain of Delight known as **Prismeer** in the Feywild. Beneath her benevolent façade lies a manipulative and calculating nature, traits she carefully conceals from her subjects. Originally the infamous demonologist **Tasha (Iggwilv)**, Zybilna adopted her fey persona to reshape the fallen kingdom of [[Prismeer|Illyndor]] into her own realm during the chaos of the [[Spellplague]].
### Special Abilities 
- Can open up to 5 portals to anywhere in the [[Gaping Maw]] which allow **100,000 medium** creatures each to enter the realm of [[Demogorgon]].
## Origins and Rise to Power
Zybilna arrived in the [[Feywild]] following the collapse of **Illyndor**, the once-radiant kingdom ruled by **Queen [[Elyndra]]** (later the [[Raven Queen]]). The kingdom had been left in turmoil after Elyndra's fall into the [[Shadowfell]] during her ill-fated attempt to ascend to godhood. Zybilna seized the opportunity to present herself as a savior to the displaced inhabitants. 

Taking on the guise of an archfey, she restructured Illyndor into **[[Prismeer]]**, a domain blending beauty with a subtle sense of unease. She established **The Rules of Hospitality, Ownership, and Reciprocity** to maintain order and foster the illusion of fairness in her realm.

## Manipulative Nature
Zybilna's true strength lies in her ability to manipulate those around her. She uses charm, wit, and cunning to exploit the vulnerabilities of her allies and enemies alike. While she portrays herself as a kind and whimsical ruler, her control over Prismeer reveals her darker tendencies:
- **Psychological Manipulation**: Zybilna crafts intricate plots that position herself as indispensable. She thrives on subtle coercion and emotional influence.
- **Power Consolidation**: She ensures her authority remains unchallenged by eliminating potential threats before they arise, often through indirect means.
- **Duality of Prismeer**: While Prismeer reflects her fey charm and creativity, its underlying dangers mirror Zybilna’s hidden ruthlessness.

## Prismeer: A Domain of Whimsy and Peril
Under Zybilna's rule, Prismeer retained much of Illyndor’s ethereal beauty but acquired an unpredictable and dangerous edge. Though its inhabitants are bound by her rules, the land itself reflects her enigmatic nature, blending wonder with subtle menace.

### The Rules of Prismeer:
1. **Hospitality**: Guests must be treated with kindness unless they prove themselves unworthy.
2. **Ownership**: Taking what is not freely given is forbidden.
3. **Reciprocity**: Acts of kindness must be returned in equal measure.

## Conflict and Betrayal
Zybilna's rule was eventually undermined by her jealous **stepsisters**, the **[[Hourglass Coven]]**. Under the pretense of loyalty, they betrayed her, sealing her in magical stasis and dividing Prismeer into the regions of **[[Hither]]**, **[[Thither]]**, and **[[Yon]]**. This act not only fractured the domain but also revealed the growing resentment Zybilna had fostered among her allies.

## Key Traits
- **Titles**: Ruler of Prismeer, Archfey of Whimsy and Peril, formerly Tasha/Iggwilv.
- **Personality**: Charismatic, manipulative, and calculating beneath a façade of whimsy and charm.
- **Allies and Enemies**: Once trusted her stepsisters (the Hourglass Coven), who betrayed her. Maintains a complex relationship with the denizens of Prismeer.
- **Notable Creations**: Prismeer itself, the [[Demonomicon]], and numerous magical artifacts, including the legendary sentient blade **[[Nepenthe]]**.

Zybilna remains a figure shrouded in mystery, her every action calculated to maintain her power and influence, even in her absence.
