#### **Overview & Naming of the Sun**  
Lathander, known as the Morninglord, is the god of dawn, renewal, and rebirth in the Faerûnian pantheon.
- Previously named **Amaunator**, for which the **Sun** over [[Toril]] is named **"Amaun"**

#### **Domains**  
He embodies the principles of hope, creativity, and growth, often associated with the sun and the renewal of life. Lathander promotes the triumph of light over darkness and the potential for new beginnings.

#### **Personality**  
Lathander is depicted as a vibrant, youthful figure, radiating warmth and optimism. He encourages his followers to pursue their dreams and to bring joy and prosperity to the world.

#### **Worshippers**  
Clerics, paladins, and artists frequently worship Lathander, seeking his blessings for inspiration and guidance. His followers often conduct rituals at dawn, celebrating new beginnings and the promise of a new day.
