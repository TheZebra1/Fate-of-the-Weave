# Annam
## Profile

#### What He Wants:
- To see the giants redeemed and unified under a worthy heir.
- To pass on his burden and regain divine agency through worship and legacy.

#### What He Fears:
- Eternal stillness—existing without presence, without influence, unheard and unseen.
- That his people will fade entirely, leaving nothing behind of his creation.

#### What He Has Lost:
- His voice, his freedom, and the last hope of reconciliation with his children.
- His divine presence in the world, now reduced to a dormant anchor holding the planes together.

## History Summary
Annam All-Father is the creator of giantkind and founder of the **[[Ordning]]**, the divine hierarchy that once governed the empire of **[[Ostoria]]**. His children ruled vast territories in harmony—storm, fire, frost, stone, and cloud giants working under a shared divine purpose.

When Ostoria fell during the wars against dragons, it fractured giantkind. But it wasn’t their defeat that broke Annam’s faith—it was the **rejection of his mortal-born son, [[Hartkiller]]**, who had come with a vision of peace, restoration, and unity. The giant lords, bound by pride and tradition, refused to follow him. Some even raised blades.

Disillusioned, Annam **withdrew from the world**. But [[Ao]], Overgod of the Realms, declared this abandonment a divine failure. As punishment, Annam was **bound into the [[Spine of the World]]**, transformed into a living anchor stabilizing the **[[Material Plane]], [[Feywild]], and [[Shadowfell]]**.

Before his silence, he forged **five [[Marks of Annam]]**—divine sparks meant to preserve his legacy and offer a path to redemption. These marks, once granted to newborn giants, eventually began appearing among **giantkin** as Annam’s power dwindled.

**Only one Mark remains.** Should it be extinguished, Annam will lose his final link to the world. With no agency left, he will face an eternity of voiceless existence. But if pushed to desperation, he may rise once more, calling out to the last of his people in one final plea to be remembered.

### Avatar 
Annam's avatar appeared as a giant of massive size, standing at least 100 feet (30 meters) tall if not taller. He had white hair and a large full beard, and was clad in robes of midnight blue.

Giant's never stop growing and Annam's avatar is no different, although it hasn't been seen in thousands of years it is likely that it would be large as large as a mountain by now. It's whereabouts are a mystery to most, however some legends say that it is one with the mountains themselves *supporting the inner planes.*
