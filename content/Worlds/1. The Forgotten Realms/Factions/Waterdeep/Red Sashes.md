# Red Sashes
## Overview
**Summary**:  
The Red Sashes began as an elite vigilante group in [[Waterdeep]], founded by [[Durnan]], tasked with protecting the city from threats emerging from [[Undermountain]]. Though their presence has dwindled in the south, splinter cells endure—such as the one now operating in [[Neverwinter]]. This cell, answering to a mysterious figure known only as *The One*, has adapted the original Sash creed into a mercenary model suited for a city on the brink.

#### What the Organization Wants:
- To uncover and bring to justice evil in Waterdeep.
- To secure a permanent foothold in the [[Protector’s Enclave]] as a legitimate guild
- To execute covert orders issued by *The One*, whose goals remain opaque even to local members

#### What the Organization Fears:
- Being seen as mercenary thugs instead of a noble order

#### What the Organization Has Lost:
- Several promising recruits lost during estate-clearing contracts in Neverwinter

## Etymology
The “Red Sashes” refers to the ***crimson sashes*** worn across the body by members—a visual badge of their station. Originally a mark of honor and duty in Waterdeep, the sash remains a symbol of shared history, but has become more stylized in newer cells. Neverwinter Sashes often decorate theirs with kill-marks or job tokens.

## Activities
**Primary Activities (Neverwinter Cell):**  
- Taking dangerous jobs and hunting dangerous creatures to facilitate a reborn Neverwinter.  
- Artifact retrieval and “containment”  

## Society
### Beliefs
- “Hold the line, no matter where the line is.” Adaptability is now their code.
- Loyalty to the people before the law
- The One sees farther than any captain—orders are followed, not questioned

### Culture
- Ranks exist, but chain of command is looser than tradition dictates
- Combat excellence is respected above all else  
- Members form tight-knit pairs or trios rather than platoons

### Religion
- *The One* is respected with almost spiritual reverence, though no one admits it aloud

## Possessions
- A shared safehouse in Neverwinter’s [[Blacklake District]]
- A single enchanted blade of Waterdeep origin, passed to the most senior duelist
- Crimson sashes with minor magical warding (resist detection, mild defense enchantments)
- A sealed letter bearing *The One’s* sigil, untouched since their cell was founded

## History
**Founding (Original Order):**  
Founded by **Durnan** in **Waterdeep** to combat threats from **Undermountain**, the Red Sashes served as elite guardians and strike forces. Over time, their influence waned as peace returned to the city, and some members left in search of new purpose.

**Neverwinter Cell Formed (~1387 DR):**  
Following the chaos of the **[[Wailing Death]]** and early **[[Spellplague]]**, a handful of disillusioned Sashes splintered north, seeking purpose in a city in need of strong blades. They claim to act under the guidance of *The One*, a mysterious figure who issues orders and funds the group remotely.

**Key Events:**
- Assisted with refugee control in Blacklake—allegedly accepted payment to “quietly relocate” resistant groups
- Entered the Valencrest Estate in competition with [[Acquisitions Incorporated]]—only one survivor returned

## Rumors & Legends
- *The One* is said to be an exile from the Waterdeep order—or a founder who never died
- If the safehouse sigil is broken, it supposedly sends word to Waterdeep... or to somewhere deeper
