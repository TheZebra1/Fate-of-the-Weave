# Grey Force
## Overview
**Summary**:  
*Grey Force is Waterdeep’s elite adventuring force, operating under the Blackstaff to eliminate high-level threats and enforce stability.*

#### What the Organization Wants:
- Defend Waterdeep from **supernatural and existential threats**.
- Execute **covert and high-risk missions** beyond city law.
#### What the Organization Fears:
- **Internal corruption** undermining their mission.
- Losing control over **dangerous magic and artifacts**.
#### What the Organization Has Lost:
- **Public trust**, due to their destructive reputation.
- **Key members**, fallen in battle against overwhelming foes.
