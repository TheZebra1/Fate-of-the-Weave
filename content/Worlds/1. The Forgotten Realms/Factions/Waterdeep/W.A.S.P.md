# Waterdahvian Arcane Shield Platoon (W.A.S.P.)
The Waterdahvian Arcane Shield Platoon (W.A.S.P.) is an elite military force formed by the Lords of Waterdeep in response to the [[Siege of Waterdeep]] in 1450 DR. The group is comprised of skilled warriors and arcane practitioners dedicated to defending Waterdeep from magical and physical threats.

#### What the Organization Wants:
- Protection of Waterdeep from external threats.
- Maintenance of peace and order within the city.

#### What the Organization Fears:
- The resurgence of powerful enemies like those involved in the [[Siege of Waterdeep]].
- Loss of public trust and support from the [[Lords of Waterdeep]].
- Internal corruption or betrayal within their ranks.

#### What the Organization Has Lost:
- N/A

---

## Etymology
"***Waterdahvian Arcane Shield Platoon***" reflects their dual focus on magical defense and martial prowess, symbolizing their role as the city's **Mage Slayers**.

## Activities
**Primary Activities**:  
- Conducting covert missions to neutralize magical threats.
- Training recruits in advanced combat and arcane techniques.
- Use HQ portals to respond to [[Battle Bell]]'s
---
## Possessions
- Arcane-infused weapons and armor tailored for their operatives.
- Access to the Waterdeep Vaults for powerful magical artifacts.
- A fortified headquarters 
---
## History
- **1450 DR**: Established by the Lords of Waterdeep to bolster the city's defenses against magical and physical threats.
	- Ended the [[Siege of Waterdeep]].
- **1452 DR**: Expansion of the group to include arcane specialists.
- **1455 DR**: Successfully thwarted an assassination attempt on the Open Lord of Waterdeep.
---
## Rumors & Legends
- Some say the W.A.S.P. has secret ties to the [[Harpers]], using their influence for covert operations.
- Whispers around the city speak of a hidden artifact within their headquarters that amplifies their arcane power.
- It is rumored that [[Erevan Lightshield]], the current leader, possesses a sentient sword that guides his decisions.

# Handbook
## 1.1 Wages
### **Operative (Full-time)**
- **Annual Salary**: 120 gold pieces per year
- **Monthly Salary**: 10 gold pieces per month
- **Per Mission Bonus**: 10 gold pieces per mission
- **Payment Method**: Direct deposit (magical) – gold appears in the possession of the operative at the appropriate times.
### **Collateral (Per Diem)**
- **Annual Salary**: None
- **Per Mission Payment**: 20 gold pieces per mission
- **Payment Method**: Direct deposit (magical) – gold appears in the possession of the operative after mission completion.
---
## 7.1 Shore Leave
- **Eligibility**: All members get 7 days of PTO after each mission.
- **Start Date**: PTO starts immediately after mission debriefing unless there's an emergency.
### 7.2 Accumulation
- **No Banking**: PTO should be taken promptly; it can't be saved up.
- **Carryover**: If missions prevent PTO, unused days can be used within 3 months.
### 7.3 Emergency Recall
- **Recall**: Members can be recalled during major threats. Interrupted PTO will be compensated later.
### 7.4 Conduct During Leave
- **Contact**: Stay reachable for emergencies.
- **Behavior**: Maintain professionalism, avoiding inappropriate leveraging of W.A.S.P authority.
