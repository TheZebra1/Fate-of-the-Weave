# The Silent Choir
## Overview
**Summary**:  
A secretive order that reveres the Wailing Death not as a tragedy, but as a divine whisper—an unspoken message delivered through suffering. Believed to recruit exclusively from the Wail-born, the Silent Choir teaches that silence is the language of truth, and that the dead still speak—if you know how to listen.

## Core Beliefs
- The [[Wailing Death]] was a **message**, not a punishment
- Words are corrupted; only **silence** carries truth
- The Wail-born are **“Echo Children”**, spiritual conduits between realms

## Symbols & Practices
- Members wear **silver-threaded veils** or jaw masks to vow silence
- Ceremonies include **breath-based mantras**, **soundless hymnals**, and **collective dreamwalking**
- Their sanctums are soundproofed, sealed, and inscribed with **echo glyphs**

## Rumors & Hooks
- Some say the Choir can awaken Wail-born potential—at a cost
- They may possess **records or magical remnants** of the original plague
- A Choir chapter may be secretly **advising a noble faction or cult within AI**
