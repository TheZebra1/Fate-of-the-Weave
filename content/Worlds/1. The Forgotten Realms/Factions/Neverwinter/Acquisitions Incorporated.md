**Summary**:  
Founded in the late 1380s DR, this early incarnation of Acquisitions Incorporated began as a planar-scale adventuring and contract-for-hire service under the charismatic leadership of Mr. [[Azmo Diaz]]. 

*Note: This version predates the widely known modern Acquisitions Incorporated of the 15th century by nearly a hundred years, and may have inspired it—though no public record confirms a connection.*

#### What the Organization Wants:
- To dominate the post-[[Spellplague]] economic and magical recovery period
- Establish branches in ***every major city*** and ***across planes*** before resistance grows
- Secure ***soulbound contracts*** across all classes and cultures, using service-based employment

#### What the Organization Fears:
- Discovery of its founder’s true identity
- Interference by divine powers or the Harpers

#### What the Organization Has Lost:
- Several early city footholds due to public outcry or “unexpected divine audits”
- The original legal department (*now a wailing node of souls in **Head Office***)

## Etymology
The name "Acquisitions Incorporated" was deliberately chosen for its ***corporate blandness***—designed to fly under the radar. "Acquisitions" refers both to the retrieval of items and to the acquisition of people, souls, and territory.

## Activities
**Primary Activities**:  
- Contract adventuring and "problem resolution"  
- Strategic franchise deployment  
- Magical binding of clients via fine print  

## Society
### Beliefs
- Control through structure, contract, and convenience
- Power comes from systems, not swords
- There’s ***no such thing as an uninformed signature***

### Culture
- Professional, ambitious, and outwardly pleasant
- Franchisees are encouraged to be flexible, efficient, and likable
- Internally strict, with ruthless performance metrics and “compliance audits”

### Religion
- Article III of the [[Pact Primeval]] is mentioned in the [[Acquisitions Incorporated Service Contract|Service Contract]]

## Possessions
- The **[[Tower of Emerald Winds]]**, [[Calimport]] – opulent and secure, floating above the city
- **[[battle-balloon-ai|The Hard Cloud]]**, Diaz’s luxury skyship used for onboarding elite branches
- **Sigilstones** – minor soul-branding relics placed in every franchise location

## History
**Founding (1388 DR)**:  
In the aftermath of the [[Wailing Death]] and early [[Spellplague]] instability, Mr. [[Azmo Diaz]]  launched Acquisitions Incorporated as a legal-adventuring alternative. While traditional guilds floundered, AI flourished with speed, efficiency, and soul-binding charm.

**Key Events**:  
- Takeover of three [[Neverwinter]] guild halls within six months.
- Rapid Expansion in major cities leading to controversial and violent climax at each juncture.
- Attempted cross-planar expansion into [[Sigil]]; ended in mass contract collapse  

## Fate & Legacy
**Collapse (Predicted ~1391 DR):**  
AI’s expansion led to public lashback, divine interference, and unstable franchisees along with cosmic lawsuits, mass breaches, and “spiritual reallocation.” The true nature of the Company was buried, erased, or archived under oath.

**Legacy:**  
Decades later, a new Acquisitions Incorporated rose—clean, comedic, and completely unassociated with its dark predecessor. Or so they claim. Rumors persist that the new founders discovered fragments of the original’s paperwork... and chose to ignore the ink stains.

## Rumors & Legends
- The original AI's logo is still visible on certain cursed estates when the moon is full
