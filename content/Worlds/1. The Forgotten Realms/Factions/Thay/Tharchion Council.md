## 📜 Tharchion Council of [[Thay]]
- **Purpose:** Serves as the administrative and martial governing body of Thay’s provinces, known as ***Tharches***; each ***Tharchion*** governs a region on behalf of the [[Zulkir|Zulkirs]].
- **Formation:** Established by [[Szass Tam]] after declaring himself High Regent in the late 14th century DR to consolidate civil authority and reward loyal military and political operatives.
- **Function:** Executes policy, enforces law, controls local militias, collects taxes, and manages public projects within each Tharch]\; subservient to the [[Zulkir|Council of Zulkirs]] but wields real power regionally.
- **Meetings:** The full Council gathers in [[Eltabbar]] twice annually—once during the spring thaw and once after the autumn harvest—to report to the [[Zulkir]]s and the ***[[Szass Tam|High Regent]]***.
- **Internal Politics:** Though meant to be functionaries, many ***Tharchions*** scheme to expand their influence, often acting as pawns or proxies in Zulkir rivalries.
- **Current Climate:** The Council was recently rebuilt after [[Szass Tam]] reorganized the government of [[Thay]].
	- [[Zulkir]] [[Dar’lon Ma]] is trying to enchant several members into loyalty
	- [[Zulkir]] [[Sirikhan]] is pushing for new oaths of arcane obedience to be made by all civil leaders.
