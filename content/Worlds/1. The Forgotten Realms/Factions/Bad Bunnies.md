# The Bad Bunny’s
[Bad Bunny Playlist](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/0GySbGb5cheIVzWtiDowaM?si=c7ab4467f3664431)
## Overview
**Summary**:  
The Bad Bunny’s are a flamboyant, fast-talking gang of kobold criminals who execute flashy, music-blasting heists across the realm. More performance troupe than professional thieves, they’re led by the egotistical and beloved Benito "Bad Bunny" Scales.
### Bad Bunny 
- Benito
- Benji
- Chunky
- Chacho
#### What the Organization Wants:
- To become legendary folk criminals whose names echo across taverns.
- To throw the most stylish, dramatic heists in Faerûn.
- To prove that kobolds are *way cooler* than anyone gives them credit for.

#### What the Organization Fears:
- Becoming irrelevant or forgotten.
- Serious consequences—like jail or injury (they *hate* pain).
- Anyone ruining their vibe (they fear buzzkills).

#### What the Organization Has Lost:
- A sense of shame.
- Several heists’ worth of loot due to over-the-top escape plans.
- A rogue member who “took the act too far” and now works for a real crime syndicate.

## Etymology
The name is both a tribute and a warning: "Bad Bunny" is Benito’s stage name, and calling his kobold crew the **Bad Bunny’s** reinforces their playful, chaotic aesthetic—with a toothy edge.

## Activities
**Primary Activities**:  
- Planning and executing high-profile, low-success-rate heists.  
- Stealing stylish items, not necessarily valuable ones.  
- Dramatic entrances and getaways.  
- Public stunts and distractions in the name of crime-as-performance-art.

## Society
### Beliefs
- Style over substance.  
- Every crime should be fun.  
- No one gets hurt (unless it's funny or they're villains).  
- Kobolds deserve the spotlight.

### Culture
- Extremely tight-knit, like a traveling troupe.  
- All wear rabbit masks and refer to each other with stage names like “Lil Hopper,” “Flopsy,” or “DJ Twitch.”  
- Friendly to outsiders *unless* they’re guards, boring, or rude.

### Religion
- Some venerate **Brandobaris**, halfling god of trickery and adventure.  
- Most worship **Benito** like a living folk hero.

## Possessions
- The “Warren Wagon”: a mobile burrow-cart full of stolen goods, makeup kits, stage props, and confetti.  
- Custom enchanted rabbit masks that hide their identities and provide minor glamours.  
- A scroll of **Major Image** (well-worn and often used mid-escape).  
- A playlist crystal that magically plays a rotating selection of Bad Bunny (™) beats.

## History
**Founding**:  
Formed after Benito Scales was kicked out of a kobold warren for “excessive musical interruptions during stealth raids.” He declared, “If crime can’t be fabulous, I don’t want it,” and recruited like-minded kobolds.

**Key Events**:
- The “Hop Stop Heist,” where they robbed a noble’s carriage by pretending to be a pop-up dance troupe.
- “The Great Glitter Escape,” when they vanished in the middle of a fight under a rain of sparkles.
- Ongoing feud with The Silent Fang, a rival kobold gang who considers the Bad Bunny’s an embarrassment.

## Rumors & Legends
- Some say Benito once stole a dragon egg and returned it with a glitter bow and apology note.
- A few believe they’re secretly working for a bard college as an experimental performance piece.
- There's talk of a “Final Heist” so grand it will shut down a whole city block—and they’ve been rehearsing for months.
