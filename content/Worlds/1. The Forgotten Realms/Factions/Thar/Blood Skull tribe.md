## Overview
- **Name**: Blood Skull Tribe (also known as the "Bloody Skulls")
- **Type**: Orc tribe
- **Location**: [[Thar]] region, [[Faerûn]]
- **Base of Operations**: [[Bloodskull Keep]]
## History
- **Origins**: Formerly known as the Black Spears, the tribe rebranded as the Blood Skulls in the late 14th century DR.
- **Activities**: Engaged in raids and warfare, notably destroying the settlement of [[Glister]] and attacking [[Hulburg]] in the late 15th century DR.

## Bloodskull Keep
### Description
- **Structure**: A formidable fortress named for the tribe's tradition of painting the skulls of fallen enemies and comrades red, displaying them prominently in the great hall.
- **Collection**: The great hall houses hundreds of skulls, including those of humans, dwarves, orcs, giantkin, and wyverns.

### Location
- **Geography**: Situated in central [[Thar]], northwest of [[Bloodmuck]].

### Significance
- **Role**: Serves as the central stronghold and symbol of power for the [[Blood Skull tribe]]
