#### **DM Quick Reference**
- **Skabatha:** Disciplinarian, obsessed with order.
- **Endelyn:** Sarcastic fatalist, loves prophecy.
- **Bavlorna:** Sluggish and insecure, **easy target for mockery.**
[[Fey Pact]]
## **Overview**
- Trapped [[Zybilna]] in her own [[Tasha's Cauldron]] & took over [[Prismeer]]
	- Betrayed Zybilna to [[Isolde]].
		- [[Tasha's Cauldron]] displayed prominently as the center piece of [[The Carnival of Fear]]. 

## **Coven Members**
### **1. Bavlorna Blightstraw** (The Slackjaw)
>**Weakness: Allergy to *Widdershins"(The act of moving counterclockwise)"***
- **Role in Dynamic:** Frequently berated by her sisters for stating obvious or "dumbed-down" ideas.
- **Personality Traits:**
  - Lazy, reclusive, and gross.
  - Obsessed with secrets and trinkets.
  - Insecure, especially about her appearance (hates being called "bloated").
  - Creates tiny [[quickling-vgm|Quickling]] versions of herself called *"Lornlings"*
- **Domain:** Hither (swampy region).
**Magic Item: [[helm-of-telepathy-xdmg|Helm of Telepathy]] w/ spell save DC = 8 + Primary Stat Mod + [[proficiency-bonus-xphb|Proficiency Bonus]]**

### **2. Skabatha Nightshade** (The Toy)
>**Forgets the first creature she sees each day/Has a wind up spinner on **
- **Role in Dynamic:** Berates Bavlorna for being disorganized or foolish.
- **Personality Traits:**
  - Strict, controlling, and methodical.
  - Treats others like tools or puppets.
  - Obsessed with order and punctual routines.
- **Domain:** Thither (decaying forest).
**Magic Item: [[Marionette Crown]](In Custom FC5 Compendium)**

### **3. [[Vespera & Endelyn Moongrave.png|Endelyn Moongrave]]** (The Thespian)
>**Captured [[Vespera Vale]] after Vespera was juanted into [[Prismeer]] by [[Casey Ruffle]].**
- **Role in Dynamic:** Ridicules Bavlorna with sarcastic and theatrical jabs.
- **Personality Traits:**
  - Morbid, dramatic, and obsessed with tragic endings.
  - Fatalistic and cryptic in speech.
  - Delights in prophecy and manipulating outcomes.
- **Domain:** Yon (stormy mountains).
**Magic Item: [[Robe of Threaded Fate]](In Custom FC5 Compendium), [[084-04-010-endelyns-orrery-of-tragedies.webp|Orrery of Tragedies]]**

## **Example Interaction**
- **Skabatha:** "The meddling adventurers are clearly planning to storm our domains, so we must fortify our defenses!"
- **Endelyn:** "Precisely! The threads of fate are unraveling in their favor—unless we cut them short."
- **Bavlorna:** "Yeah, uh... so, we should, uh... stop them from doing stuff."
- *(Awkward pause.)*
- **Skabatha:** "That’s *exactly* what I just said, you toad-brained oaf!"
- **Endelyn:** "Oh, Bavlorna, you’re a marvel. Truly. The marvel of stating the obvious."
- *(They bicker briefly, then move on.)*
