# Order of the Arcane Blade
#### Creed
- **Elvish**: *"Anar'Quessir'Quel'Dorai, Tel'Andune'Ara, Tel'Nael'Eryn."*
- **Common**: *"For the People of the Sun, the Light Eternal, and the Sacred Forest."*
## Overview
**Summary**:  
The Order of the Arcane Blade is an ancient organization dedicated to [[Arcane Blade Casting]], created to protect **Nature,** **Magic,** and the **Balance** after the end of the [[Crown Wars]].
### Core Tenets of the Order of the Arcane Blade
1. **Balance**: *"The blade and the spell are one—wield them in harmony to protect the Weave and natural order."*  
2. **Guardianship**: *"Strike not in anger or haste but only when the light demands your hand to act."*  
3. **Discipline**: *"Master yourself before you seek to master others, for the reckless hand undoes even the greatest purpose."*
#### What the Organization Wants:
- To preserve and protect the **[[Weave]]** and the **natural** balance of [[Faerûn]].
- To reclaim Cormanthor Forest from corruption.

#### What the Organization Fears:
- The spread of **destruction** and **corruption** of **magic** and the **natural world**.
- The harm that **reckless** **action** can create, even if well-intentioned.

## Society
#### Structure
- **Initiates**: Apprentices undergoing rigorous training in both martial and arcane disciplines.
- **Bladesworn**: Fully initiated members who have undergone the Rite of Dawn's Embrace.
- **[[Dawnbringer]]s**: Senior masters who lead the Order in its missions and training efforts.
- **High Dawnbringer**: The leader of the Order, currently [[Aelindor Vaelithar]].



#### Rite of Passage
- **Purpose**: To prove the acolyte’s readiness to join the ranks of the **Bladesworn**, demonstrating martial skill, arcane mastery, and devotion to the Order’s ideals.
- **Phases**:
  1. **The Trial of Balance**:
     - A test to harmonize arcane magic with martial combat. The acolyte must face a magically enhanced opponent or scenario where they must show equal mastery of **spellcraft** through **bladework**.
  2. **The Trial of Light**:
     - The acolyte must venture into a place of darkness or corruption, such as a desecrated part of Cormanthor, to recover an object of significance (e.g., an ancient artifact, a shard of a broken Sun Blade) or cleanse the area of evil.
  3. **The Trial of Devotion**:
     - A test of faith and selflessness, where the acolyte must sacrifice something of great personal value (this could be material, such as a cherished heirloom, or abstract, such as letting go of an unresolved emotional burden or ambition).

- **Completion**: Once all trials are completed, the acolyte undergoes the *Rite of Dawn's Embrace* at the temple, expending all their magical energy in the creation of their Sun Blade. This process not only bonds the blade to them but signifies their ascension to the rank of **Bladesworn**.
#### Sun Blades
- **Symbol of the Order**: [[Dawnblades]]s are radiant weapons bound to their wielders through the sacred *"Rite of Dawn's Embrace"*. These weapons are not forged in the traditional sense but brought into being through an ancient [[Elven High Magic]] ritual.
- **The Ritual**: (***DC 26 Arcana Check***)(**NAT 20 SUCCESS DOES NOT COUNT, MUST BE NATURAL)
  - Upon completing a **rite of passage**, the **initiate** returns to the [[Dawnspire]] and **expends all their remaining spell slots**, contributing their personal energy to a collective High Magic ritual.
	  - Guided by the temple’s Elders, this process creates a **unique Sun Blade**, spiritually and magically tied to the wielder, possessing [[Dawnblades#Special Sun Blades|Dawnblades]]
	  - Anyone that tries to attune to a [[Dawnblades#Special Sun Blades|Dawnblades]], created in this way, must still succeed on the ***DC 26 Arcana*** to attune and activate the blade, however they do not have to do any ritual.
		  - A creature can attempt to attune to these weapons no more than once per day.
## History
- **Origins**: Founded during the height of elven civilization to defend against Elvish Civil war after the end of the  [[Crown Wars]]; also has stifled extraplanar and fiendish incursions.
- **Connection to Evermeet**: The entire Order retreated to [[Evermeet]] alongside the **majority of elvenkind**, but the [[Dawnspire]] temple in [[Cormanthyr]] was established as their mainland bastion.
	- **Post-Retreat**: After the Retreat, the Order in Faerûn opened its membership to all races, though elven High Magic remains central to its practices.

## Modern Role & Divisions
- **[[Dawnspire]] in [[Cormanthor Forest]]**: The **last** temple on the mainland of [[Faerûn]], protected by the **[[Elven High Magic|N’Maernthor]]** spell. Serves as a sanctuary and rallying point for efforts to restore balance to the forest.
- **Internal Tensions**:
  - **[[Ilaer.webp|Ilaer]]** (Traditionalists): Advocate for patience and preserving the Order’s ancient methods (led by figures like Aelindor).
  - **[[Sul'ar.png|Sul'ar]]** (Reformers): Push for direct action and adaptation to modern threats, often led by younger members or non-elves
	  - Inspired by ideology of [[Sythrak Gurthanor]]
---

## Current Mission
The Order seeks to:
1. Reclaim [[Cormanthor Forest]] from the corruption spreading through the forest.
	1. Traditionalists and Reformers have different ideas about when and how this should be done.
2. Preserve the ancient traditions of [[Elven High Magic]] while adapting to a more inclusive future.
3. Serve as protectors of the Weave, Nature and guardians of balance across Faerûn.
