# 🐀 The Rat Network (1502 DR)
> *"The roads above belong to kings and coin. But beneath them, we reign."*  
> — Skivven, the Regent Below

## 📜 Core Lore

- **Scope**: A vast underground system of tunnels, sewers, and magical burrows linking major Faerûnian cities.
- **Leadership**:
  - **The True Rat King**: Whoever wears the *[[Crown of the Rat King]]*.
  - **The Regent Below**: *[[Skivven.png|Skivven]]*, a powerful wererat steward awaiting the next King.

## 💎 Benefits to Allies
- **Fast Travel**: Magical underground network enables near-instant travel between cities with rat guides.
- **Information Network**: Equivalent of a permanent *[[legend-lore-xphb|Legend Lore]] / [[commune-xphb|Commune]] / [[scrying-xphb|Scrying]]* hub.
- **Safehouses**: Hidden bunkers, caches, and warrens only the rats can navigate.
## 🔐 Access & Restrictions
- **Denied to All** unless:
  - Wielder of the *Crown of the Rat King*
  - Personally approved by Skivven
  - Member of an approved faction (see below)

- **Repelled by Force**: Unapproved entrants are swarmed by rats until ejected or killed.
## 🏛️ Factions with Conditional Access

| Faction               | Purpose                                  | Access Conditions |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Zhentarim**        | Smuggling, blackmail, assassination       | Tribute of info + 10% cut |
| **Harpers**          | Surveillance, coordination                | Share intelligence monthly |
| **Xanathar Guild**   | Crime, slavery, illegal trade             | Non-aggression pact |
| **Red Wizards**      | Espionage, artifact movement              | Arcane knowledge tribute |
| **Eilistraee’s Drow**| Sanctuary, movement                       | Defend nests from surface threats |

## 🛠️ Network Structure
- **Burrows**: Safehouse tunnels linking major cities.
- **Veins**: Primary transport routes, sometimes enchanted with minor teleportation magic.
- **Nests**: Local HQs guarded by Whisperers or Awakened rats.
## 🧠 Rat Lore Access
Once allied:
- **Whisper Wall**: Psychic info web — grants localized rumors or *Commune*-like answers.
- **Boons**:
  - Advantage on local knowledge checks
  - Access to hidden areas and rumors
  - 1 piece of critical, DM-provided info per long rest

## 🎯 Becoming Allies
### Trust-Building Quests:
- Defend a Nest from invaders (cultists, aberrations, etc.)
- Rescue or heal an Awakened Rat Seer
- Wear the [[Crown of the Rat King]]
- Root out a traitor Whisperer working with the Hells
### Travel Cost:
- Must offer tribute: food, magic trinkets, or secrets
- Failure to pay results in blacklisting from the Network

## 📦 Hooks to Introduce
1. **"The Rat With a Crown"** – Mysterious rat delivers a gold-inscribed key.
2. **"Tunnel to Nowhere"** – Party discovers a rat-guarded door with glowing sigils.
3. **"The Siege Below"** – Party stumbles upon rats fighting aberrations in the Veins.
