# Bregan D'aerthe
## Overview
**Summary**:  
Bregan D'aerthe is an elite **drow mercenary and mercantile group** founded and led by **[[Jarlaxle Baenre]]**. Based in **[[Menzoberranzan]]**, they operate both in the **[[Underdark]]** and on the surface, trading in secrets, soldiers, and wealth.

#### What the Organization Wants:
- **Profit** through mercenary work and trade.  
- **Influence** within Menzoberranzan and the surface world.  
- **Autonomy** from the matriarchal rule of drow society.  

#### What the Organization Fears:
- **Rival groups** such as [[Jaezred Chaulssin]].  
- **Exposure** of secrets that could undermine their power.  
- **Internal dissent** or betrayal among members.  

#### What the Organization Has Lost:
- Key members like **Rai-guy Bondalek** and **M'tarl**.  
- **Temporary loss of leadership** when Jarlaxle was influenced by [[Crenshinibon]].  
- **Resources** during conflicts with other drow houses and organizations.  

---

## Etymology
The name *Bregan D'aerthe* originates from the Drow language, though its exact meaning remains ambiguous.

## Activities
**Primary Activities**:  
- **Mercenary Work** – Selling skilled soldiers and assassins to the highest bidder.  
- **Trade** – Acting as intermediaries between the **Underdark** and the surface.  
- **Espionage** – Gathering and selling **intelligence** to influential groups.  

---

## Society
### Beliefs
Bregan D'aerthe operates on **pragmatism and profit** rather than loyalty to drow traditions. Unlike most drow factions, they embrace a **meritocratic structure**, where skill and results outweigh noble lineage.

### Culture
- Members rely on **mutual respect** and **shared ambition**.  
- They are **deceptive, yet reliable**—they honor agreements but twist them to their benefit.  
- Most **surface dealings** involve disguised or proxy representatives.  

### Religion
While some members follow **[[Lolth]]**, Bregan D'aerthe itself is **secular**, focusing solely on power and wealth.

---

## Possessions
- **Secret Headquarters** in **the [[Clawrift]]**, [[Menzoberranzan]].  
- **Fleet of ships** for trade and smuggling.  
- **Surface holdings** in [[Luskan]] and [[Calimport]].  

---

## History
**Founding**:  
Jarlaxle Baenre founded Bregan D'aerthe to **empower houseless drow males**, granting them protection and purpose in a society where they were otherwise expendable.

**Key Events**:  
- **1366 DR** – Established a **base in Calimport**, expanding surface influence.  
- **1377 DR** – Secured a foothold in **Luskan**, using Ship Kurth as a front.  
- **1484 DR** – Hired by **[[House Baenre]]** to garrison [[House Do'Urden]], embedding themselves deeper into Menzoberranzan’s politics.  

---

## Rumors & Legends
- Some claim Bregan D'aerthe has **infiltrated many surface organizations**, placing disguised agents in key positions of power.  
- **Jarlaxle himself** is rumored to have multiple identities, subtly **manipulating** events across [[Faerûn]].  
- A whispered legend suggests they control an **underground network of portals**, allowing them to move swiftly between cities undetected.
