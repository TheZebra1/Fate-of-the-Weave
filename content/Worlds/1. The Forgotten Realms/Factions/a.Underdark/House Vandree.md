## **House Vandree**  

Led by a Matriarch, like all Drow Houses. The Father is not considered in the legitimacy of Drow Heirs and all that matters is the bloodline of the Matriarch.  

### **House Information**  
- **Leader**: Fiirnel'ther Vandree  
- **Primary Focus**: Maintaining the slave trade and supplying critical surface-world resources. House Vandree controls certain rare materials from the surface and traffics in surface slaves, making them influential within Menzoberranzan's economy.  
- **Reputation**: Known for their fascination with the surface world, members of House Vandree secretly sympathize with surface dwellers. However, they mask this heresy with ruthless behavior in drow society. While venturing above, they sometimes masquerade as surface elves, enjoying the natural beauty of the surface world before resuming their predatory hunts for slaves.  

## **Living Members**  

### 1. **[[Fiirnel'ther Vandree.webp|Fiirnel'ther Vandree]]**  
   - **Role**: Matron Mother of House Vandree  
   - **Description**: Cunning and highly calculating, Fiirnel'ther leads House Vandree with a firm hand. Her subtle fascination with the surface world has influenced the house's policies, but her ability to maintain secrecy ensures her survival in Menzoberranzan's cutthroat politics.  

### 2. **Dia'ndra Vandree**  
   - **Role**: Priestess of Lolth  
   - **Description**: A devout cleric of Lolth who acts as a key enforcer of her mother's will. Dia'ndra is outwardly loyal to Lolth, but she secretly questions some tenets of drow religion, though she would never admit this openly.  

### 3. **Relonar Vandree**  
   - **Role**: Weapon Master of House Vandree  
   - **Description**: A skilled fighter tasked with training the house's soldiers. Relonar is fiercely loyal to his family and takes pride in protecting House Vandree's interests in both the Underdark and surface excursions.  

### 4. **Imrith Vandree**  
   - **Role**: Spy and Diplomat  
   - **Description**: A talented negotiator and covert operative. Imrith often ventures to the surface to handle trade deals and reconnaissance missions. His charm and resourcefulness are valuable assets to the house.  

### 5. **Asha Vandree**  
   - **Role**: House Vandree Representative at [[Velkynvelve]], *Wizard*
   - **Description**: Stationed at the slave outpost of Velkynvelve, she studies arcane magic. Her curiosity about surface magic puts her at odds with more traditional drow *clerics* and *paladins*.  
### 6. [[Liriel Baenre]](Born Vandree)
Daughter of [[Sosdrielle Vandree]] &  "[[House Baenre#2. ** Gromph Baenre.png Gromph Baenre **|Gromph Baenre]]".

## **Deceased Members**  

### 1. **Troken'ther Vandree**  
   - **Role**: Former Matron Mother of House Vandree  
   - **Description**: A feared and respected leader who solidified House Vandree's position in Menzoberranzan's hierarchy. Troken'ther's fascination with the surface was more open, leading to her assassination by a rival house.  

### 2. **Narviela Vandree**  
   - **Role**: Former High Priestess of House Vandree  
   - **Description**: Became a lover of [[Minthara Baenre.png|Minthara Baenre]] for some time until Minthara was ordered to assassinate her. 
	   - Minthara mournfully carried out the poisoning.

### 3. **Sosdrielle Vandree**  
   - **Role**: Consort of  "[[House Baenre#2. ** Gromph Baenre.png Gromph Baenre **|Gromph Baenre]]" 
   - **Description**: Beautiful mother of Liriel Baenre(Vandree). Killed by Gromph's golem so that Gromph could take the gifted Liriel to be part of [[House Baenre]]. Gromph modeled the shrine to Lolth at House Baenre in Sosdrielle's likeness.

---

House Vandree walks a dangerous line between their fascination with the surface world and their ruthless façade in Menzoberranzan. Their influence in the slave trade and access to surface goods ensures their place within the city's political and economic structures, but their hidden sympathies leave them vulnerable to discovery and destruction.
