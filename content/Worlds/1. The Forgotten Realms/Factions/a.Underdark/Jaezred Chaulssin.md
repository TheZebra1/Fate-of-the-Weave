# Jaezred Chaulssin  
[[Princes of the Apocalypse Plot|DM Only]]  
> *“Only by fracturing Lolth’s chains do we free our kind.”*  
> — **[[Mauzzkyl Jaezred]]**, Patron Grandfather & Founder

## Overview  
The **[[Jaezred Chaulssin]]** is a secretive, patriarchal order of shadow-dragon‑blooded [[Drow]] assassins founded in [[792 DR]] in the ruined city of [[Chaulssin]]. Their goal: dismantle [[Lolth]]’s matriarchy and reshape drow society under male-led dominion and shadow-draconic bloodlines.

## Draconic Racial Ranks  
The society is structured by draconic purity:  
- **[[Zekyl]]** — Half-shadow dragon drow (common, elite assassins)  
- **[[Zar’ithra]]** — Rare draconic-blooded drow with stronger magical talents  
- **[[Draa’zekyl]]** — True drow-dragon hybrids; lead the order and serve as Patron Fathers

## What the Organization Wants  
- To overthrow [[Lolth]]'s clergy and rebuild drow civilization under their leadership  
- Expand fosterage cells across all major Underdark cities  
- Reclaim ancient power lost after the death of [[Vhaeraun]]

## What the Organization Fears  
- The complete loss of draconic bloodlines through dilution or assassination  
- Exposure to [[Lolth]]’s followers and retaliation  
- Remaining fractured and weakened due to planar disruptions

## What the Organization Has Lost  
- Many members during the [[Spellplague]] and [[Vhaeraun]]’s death (~1375 DR)  
- Their overt influence in cities like [[Ched Nasad]] and [[Eryndlyn]]  
- Shadowfell stability after the unraveling of the Weave  

## Strongholds & Locations  
- **[[Chaulssin]]** – Home base in the Shadowfell/Underdark overlap; former shadow dragon city  
- Fosterages in:
  - [[Ched Nasad]]  
  - [[Eryndlyn]]  
  - [[Maerimydra]]  
  - [[Sschindylryn]]  
  - [[Dusklyngh]]  
  - [[Karsoluthiyl]]  
  - [[Jhachalkhyn]]  
- Outposts in [[Skullport]] and [[Sshamath]]

## Society & Policies  
- Ruled by a council of eight: the **[[Patron Grandfather]]** and seven **[[Patron Fathers]]**  
- Operatives hold titles like:
  - **Velve** (Blade) – Ranks 1st through 8th  
  - **Anointed Blade** – Assassins empowered to act autonomously  
- Promotion is meritocratic, often through assassination, sabotage, or arcane mastery

- Surgical strikes and magical assassination  
- Shadow portal access between fosterage nodes  
- Long-term infiltration of rival noble houses  
- Subversive propaganda to erode trust in priestesses

## History  
- **–221 DR**: [[Chaulssin]] is conquered by shadow dragons; drow enslaved and bred with dragons  
- **634 DR**: [[Zekyl]] uprising liberates the city and kills the dragons  
- **792 DR**: [[Mauzzkyl Jaezred]] formalizes the organization after purging the malaugrym  
- **1136 DR**: Return to the Material Plane; begin planting fosterages  
- **1372 DR**: Exploited [[Silence of Lolth]] to assassinate key matron mothers  
- **1375 DR**: Death of [[Vhaeraun]] during the [[Spellplague]] nearly dismantles the order  
- **Post-Spellplague**: Reduced to ~140 members; they retreat and rebuild in secret

## Current Situation  
The order is once again growing in strength, reclaiming [[Shadowfell]] strongholds and reinvigorating its bloodlines. Operatives are quietly undermining [[Lolth]]'s clergy in [[Menzoberranzan]], [[Sschindylryn]], [[Eryndlyn]], and [[Skullport]] while seeking new alliances—especially among **Shadar-kai** created by the [[Shadow Plague]] and [[Drow]] disillusioned with matriarchal rule.

## Rumors & Legends

- One [[Draa’zekyl]] vanished into [[Menzoberranzan]] with a shard of the [[Shadow Weave]]... and may still be plotting from within.
