Led by a Matriarch, like all Drow Houses. The Father is not considered in the legitimacy of Drow Heirs and all that matters is the bloodline of the Matriarch.
## **Living Members**

### 1. [[Quenthal Baenre.png|Quenthel Baenre]]  
   - **Role**: Matron Mother of House Baenre & Most powerful member of the [[Menzoberranzan#Ruling Houses|Ruling Council]].  
   - **Description**: Second daughter of Yvonnel. . She is cunning and pious.
   - Bceame [[Chosen|Chosen]] of [[Lolth]] after sacrifcing her sister Triel Baenre to Lolth.
   - Tiefling Half-blood Heritage

### 2. [[Gromph Baenre.png|Gromph Baenre]]  
   - **Role**: Archmage of [[Menzoberranzan]]  
   - **Description**: Eldest son of Yvonnel, a master of arcane magic. Holds significant influence despite his status as a male in drow society. Known for his arrogance, pragmatism.
	   - Uses the [[Demon Weave]] for sorcery rather than Mystra's [[Weave]].
   - Tiefling Half-blood Heritage

### 3. [Jarlxle Baenre](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Jarlaxle)
- **Role**: Mercenary Leader of [[Bregan D'aerthe]]
- **Description**: Feared by many as a favored of Lolth after surviving a sacrifice to Lolth as a newborn.
### 4. **[[Nalfein Baenre.png|Nalfein Baenre]]**  
   - **Role**: Spy and Assassin  
   - **Description**: Cousin to the main branch. A skilled covert operative specializing in espionage and assassination. Works in the shadows to protect and expand House Baenre's influence.

### 5. [[Minthara Baenre.png|Minthara Baenre]] 
   - **Role**: Former Head of Security for House Baenre (now estranged)  
   - **Description**: Cousin to the main branch, paladin of Lolth, and former enforcer for the house. She was brainwashed by the Absolute and served as a general in *Baldur's Gate 3*.
### 6.. [[Narissin Baenre.png|Narissin Baenre/Duskryn]]
- **Role:** Newest Head of Security for House Baenre, after nearly a decade of failed heads being assassinated or executed for failure.
	- Brought over from [[House Duskryn]], taking on the Baenre name while serving the House, per custom.

### 7. Sos' Umptu Baenre
- **Role:** Mistress of [[Arach-Tinilith]]

### 8. [[Shynara Baenre]] 
- **Role**: Most prominent High Priestess of House Baenre
- **Description:** Consorts directly with Sos'Umptu & Quenthel.

### 9. Veylith Baenre
- **Undercommon Meaning:** "Vey" translates to "veil" or "shadow," symbolizing secrecy and authority. "Lith" means "power" or "strength," reflecting leadership and control.  
**Meaning:** *"Power of Secrets."*

### 10. Zestra Baenre
- **Undercommon Meaning:** "Zes" signifies "sharpness" or "spark," representing wit and energy. "Tra" means "essence", "life", or " "soul," reflecting intrinsic nature.  
**Meaning:** *"Spark of Life."*


​## Deceased Members
### 1. **Yvonnel Baenre**  
   - **Role**: Former Matron Mother of House Baenre  
   - **Description**: Ruled the house for over 2,000 years. A legendary figure of cunning, power, and ruthlessness. Her death marked a significant shift in the house's leadership.
	- Died during in attempt at her ultimate goal of conquering [[Mithral Hall]]. see the [[Battle of Mithral Hall]] 
### 2. [[Triel Baenre.png|Triel Baenre]]  
   - **Role**: Forme Matron Mother of House Baenre (Successor of Yvonnel)  
   - **Description**: Eldest daughter of Yvonnel Baenre, devoted cleric of Lolth, known for her ruthless efficiency and unwavering ambition. Assisnated and sacrificed to Lolth by Quenthel Baenre prompting Lolth to maker Quenthel her new [[Chosen]]

### 3. Dantrag Baenre
   - **Role**: Former Weapons Master of House Baenre  
   - **Description**: A skilled fighter and duelist who wielded dual scimitars. He was killed by Drizzt Do'Urden.

### 4. Bladen’Kerst Baenre  
   - **Role**: Former Daughter of Yvonnel  
   - **Description**: A priestess of Lolth killed during internal house struggles. Her death illustrates the deadly nature of drow politics.

### 5. Unnamed Parents of Minthara & Nalfein
   - **Role**: Likely former minor members of House Baenre’s extended family  
   - **Description**: Their deaths left Minthara and Nalfein to rise to prominence through merit and devotion to House Baenre.
### 6. Drakahn Baenre 
- Father of Xalzar Baenre 
- Sacrificed to Lolth by his wife, Shynara Baenre.
	- He was a high quality Drow male, an incredibly pleasing sacrifice propelling Shynara into the inner circles of [[Menzoberranzan]]'s most elite and honored worshipers of Lolth.
