# The Ebon Chain
[[Plot of Gargauth|DM Only]]
> *“It's not about controlling them… if you work hard enough they will come to love you!”*  
> — **Casey Ruffle**, founder of the Ebon Chain
## Overview
The Ebon Chain is the **premier provider of magical familiars**, offering expertly trained companions to **mages, scholars, and nobles** across the realms. With **a reputation for excellence and strict ethical standards**, they ensure every familiar finds a **stable and loving home**.

#### What the Organization Wants:
- To provide **high-quality, well-trained familiars** to responsible magic users.  
- To **expand operations into all major cities**, becoming the trusted name in arcane companionship.  

#### What the Organization Fears:
- The **misuse or mistreatment** of magical familiars.  
- The **unregulated familiar trade**, which they claim endangers both familiars and magic users alike.  
- [[Unfamiliar]] attacks.

#### What the Organization Has Lost:
- The **original writings of their founder**, rumored to hold forgotten secrets of familiar bonding.  

## **Store Locations**
- [[Waterdeep]] – Prime market for nobles and archmages, with hidden facilities in Undermountain
- **[[Baldur's Gate]]** – A center of trade and power, making it a perfect distribution hub.  
- **[[Silverymoon]]** – Embedded within the arcane academia, influencing future spellcasters.  
- **[[Neverwinter]]** – Ties to elemental forces, ideal for acquiring rare familiars.  
- **[[Calimport]]** – Exotic creature trade, thriving in a city of intrigue and wealth.  
- **[[Thay]]** – Openly deals with Red Wizards, providing creatures for experimentation.  
- **[[Menzoberranzan]]** – A stronghold of dark elf nobility, catering to drow ambitions.  
>Each location operates under the strict quality standards of **The Ebon Chain**, ensuring that all familiars are **properly placed in the right homes**.  
>- [[Ebon Chain Contract]]

## **Society & Policies**
The Ebon Chain operates with **a structured hierarchy**, where members earn titles such as:
1. **Grand Leashmaster**
2. **Chain Warden**
3. **Harvester**
4. **Acquisitor**

- Stores uphold **the highest standards of care**, and every customer must sign a **Familiar Custodianship Agreement**, ensuring a **safe and responsible home** for their new companion.  
>- 🔺 **[[Currency|Silver]] is strictly prohibited in all locations**, a long-standing policy that remains unexplained.

### Tier Notes
- **Basic Tier**: Perfect for students and new [[Familiarist Class|Familiarists]]. The creatures are minor spirits or magically imbued mundane animals.
- **Special Tier**: These familiars have a mystical nature or extraplanar origin. Often come with more autonomy and personality.
- **Mystical Tier**: True magical beasts or planar creatures. Rare and usually bonded through special permissions or rituals.
- **Exalted Tier**: Highly dangerous and powerful beings. These are *not* offered to the public and usually require black-market dealings or special influence.
- **Forbidden Tier**: These familiars often involve corrupted souls or infernal pacts. Illegal in most cities and highly controversial.

> ⚠️ *Only the Basic and Special tiers are offered openly in Ebon Chain storefronts. Mystical and above are accessed for select customers through secretive means, without Casey Ruffle's knowledge or approval.*
