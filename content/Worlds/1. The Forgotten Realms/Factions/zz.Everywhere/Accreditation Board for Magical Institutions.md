## 🧭 Purpose & Goals
> Ensure standardized magical education and ethical practice across Faerûn.  
> Promote responsible use of arcane power and inter-institution collaboration.  

- Maintain a registry of approved magical colleges  
- Issue, revoke, or suspend accreditations  
- Review magical curricula for safety & ethics  
- Investigate arcane misuse by institutions

## 📜 Policies & Guidelines

<details>
<summary><strong>🔒 Accreditation Criteria</strong></summary>

- Must employ at least 3 credentialed mages (Archmage or equivalent)
- Curriculum reviewed every 5 years
- Compliance with the Ethical Spellcasting Charter
- Annual public record of magical incidents

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>🕵️ Investigation Protocols</strong></summary>

- Tiered response based on severity of infraction  
- Probationary reviews before suspension  
- Use of independent Arcane Auditors  
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>📘 Curriculum Guidelines</strong></summary>

- Minimum of 3 schools of magic taught  
- Required ethics coursework (yearly)  
- Historical arcane context & Weave theory  
</details>

## 🕰️ Historical Summary
- Founded post-Time of Troubles to bring order to wild arcane growth  
- Supported by the Lords' Alliance & major arcane circles  
- Headquarters established in [[Silverymoon]] due to arcane neutrality and reputation  

## 📌 Miscellaneous Notes
- Board includes members from different nations (e.g., [[Halruaa]], [[Thay]], [[Waterdeep]], [[Silverymoon]], [[Amn]], [[Calimshan]], etc.)  
## ⚠️ Disputed Institutions
These schools exist but are **not recognized** by the Board due to ethical or political concerns:
- [[Hexcidon]] — The only known hexblade-focused college in Faerûn. Trains elite warlock bodyguards and generals, primarily in service to [[Thay]].  
  > *Reason for Rejection:* Direct involvement with extraplanar patrons of questionable intent; frequent violations of binding consent protocols.
- [[The Hidden Path]] — A network of secretive magical enclaves advocating unregulated Weave experimentation.  
  > *Reason for Rejection:* Refuses oversight or transparency; suspected links to magical insurgency cells.
