# Church of Bhaal
[[Plot of the Church of Bhaal|Dm Only]]
## Overview
**Summary**:  
The Church of Bhaal is a ruthless, decentralized cult dedicated to the Lord of Murder. Once thought destroyed after the Time of Troubles, the church has re-emerged stronger in the 15th century DR, operating through hidden temples and assassin networks led by [[Grandfather Rynzhar.Skin.webp|Grandfather Rynzhar]]

#### What the Organization Wants:
- To spread worship of Bhaal through ritual murder.
- To re-establish Bhaal as one of Faerûn's most feared deities.
- To eliminate rival deities and their followers.
- To ensure that death remains a sacred, violent act.

#### What the Organization Fears:
- Exposure and destruction by rival faiths and authorities.
- Internal power struggles weakening their influence.
- The failure of Bhaalspawn bloodlines to empower the church.

#### What the Organization Has Lost:
- Their once-powerful standing before Bhaal's death in 1358 DR.
- Influence over many assassin guilds taken by Cyric and Mask.
- Sacred relics lost in the centuries of decline.

---

## Etymology
The name "Church of Bhaal" signifies its dedication to the Lord of Murder. While some refer to its followers as "Bhaalists" or "Bhaalyn," most outsiders simply call them "Murder Priests."

## Activities
**Primary Activities**:  
- Training assassins in Bhaal’s name.  
- Conducting ritual murders to honor their god.  
- Spreading influence within criminal and noble circles.  
- Uncovering and protecting relics of Bhaal.  

---

## Society
### Beliefs
- Murder is a sacred act, and every death is an offering to Bhaal.
- Bhaal's will is best enacted through bloodshed and assassination.
- The strong have the divine right to slay the weak.

### Culture
- Initiates must commit their first murder to join the clergy.
- High-ranking priests are referred to as "Murder Lords" or "Bloodborn."
- Many members operate in secrecy, masquerading as mercenaries, nobles, or common folk.

### Religion
- The faithful revere Bhaal above all, viewing other deities as lesser beings.
- Ritual killings are performed on high holy days such as the Feast of the Moon.
- Some sects believe in the return of a new Bhaalspawn to lead them.

---

## Possessions
- Hidden temples in major cities and wilderness regions.
- Sacred daggers and murder-themed relics.
- Elite assassin cults trained in divine killing arts.

---

## History
**Founding**:  
The church thrived before Bhaal’s death in 1358 DR, only to fall into chaos after the Time of Troubles. It was later revived following Bhaal’s resurrection in the late 15th century DR.

**Key Events**:  
- **1358 DR** – Bhaal is slain during the Time of Troubles.  
- **Late 14th century DR** – Bhaal’s resurrection is rumored, and cult activity increases.  
- **1480s DR** – The church resurfaces in Baldur’s Gate and beyond.  
- **1502 DR** – The church is now a hidden but formidable force across Faerûn.  

---

## Rumors & Legends
- A powerful assassin, rumored to be a Bhaalspawn, may soon take control of the church.
- Secret alliances exist between the church and influential noble families.
- Bhaal’s return was incomplete, and some priests seek a final blood ritual to restore him fully.

**Objective:** Climb the **ranks of Bhaal’s cult** through **senseless murder** using a ritual dagger to earn power, weapons, and divine favor.
