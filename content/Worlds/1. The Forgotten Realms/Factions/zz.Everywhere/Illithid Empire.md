## Overview
**Summary**:  
The Illithid Empire was a vast, interplanar civilization ruled by the Elder Brain [[Ilsensine]]. Though the empire once controlled much of the multiverse, their power was shattered following the rebellion of [[Gith'Zerath]], and again after the **[[Gith]] Rebellion** in **-642 DR**. Though the empire has since fallen, remnants of the Illithids remain scattered across the planes, awaiting the eventual return of Ilsensine.

#### What the Organization Wants:
- Rebuild the Illithid Empire to its former glory.
- The return of [[Ilsensine]] full power and reunite the Elder Brain network.
- **The Chrysalis Codex**: A powerful artifact said to be able to reverse ceremorphosis or restore the Elder Brain’s full form. It is believed to be hidden in a Githyanki fortress within the **Astral Plane**.

#### What the Organization Fears:
- Continued rebellion from the Gith races (Githyanki, Githzerai).
- The permanent loss of Ilsensine’s consciousness.
- Full extinction of the Illithid race.

#### What the Organization Has Lost:
- The vast empire once spanning countless planes.
- The majority of its population and the Elder Brain network following Gith'Zerath’s rebellion.
- Physical form of Ilsensine, rendering the Elder Brain in fragmented, weakened states.

## Etymology
The name "Illithid" derives from ancient psionic rituals used by their ancestors to merge and control minds. The name **Ilsensine** comes from the primordial language of the Illithid and signifies “The Infinite Mind,” referencing the Elder Brain’s vast psionic reach and ultimate power.

## Activities
**Primary Activities**:  
- Seeking out ancient psionic artifacts, like the **Chrysalis Codex**, to restore their power.
- Using psionic influence to infiltrate societies and subtly rebuild influence.
- Searching for a way to resurrect or reforge the Elder Brain’s full consciousness.

## Society
### Beliefs
Although the Illithid do not worship traditional deities, they revere **Ilsensine**, whom they consider the divine entity that guides their race. They believe Ilsensine to be the source of their psionic abilities and their right to dominate the multiverse, seeing it as a being of pure intellect that transcends physical form.

In reality, **Ilsensine was the original Elder Brain**, now divided and scattered across the planes. The Illithids' religious beliefs have become an echo of its true, fragmented nature, and their devotion to Ilsensine as a deity is, in essence, a misunderstanding of the Elder Brain’s true identity.

### Culture
Illithid society is centered around psionic hierarchy, where power is derived from mental strength and influence. The Elder Brain commands its subjects through a hive-mind connection, though some individuals, like Gith'Zerath, seek autonomy. The reverence for Ilsensine has shaped their culture and their strict belief in the supremacy of intellect. 

Members of the Illithid race believe that achieving greater power in their psionic abilities brings them closer to Ilsensine. The more powerful an Illithid becomes, the more they are seen as an extension of Ilsensine's design.

### Religion
Ilsensine is revered as the divine and eternal consciousness of the Illithids. The Unseen Brain is understood to be a transcendent being, guiding the race’s survival and expansion across the planes. While they do not worship a traditional pantheon of gods, Ilsensine's influence over all things psionic is considered a sacred truth.

The main practical function of this belief in Ilsensine is that when Mind-flayer "adventuring parties" called "Inquisitions" and "cults" were away from the hive they would eventually regain their independence from the Elder Brain. Their understanding of the design of Ilsensine is often a strong incentive for them to return to their hive's where they may best be part of his design.

## History
**Founding**:  
The Illithid Empire was founded by the first Elder Brain, **Ilsensine**, who created a cohesive empire of unimaginable power across multiple planes.

**Key Events**:  
- **-11,000 DR**: The rebellion of **Gith'Zerath**, who led the **Adversary Mind Flayers** against the Illithid Empire. Gith'Zerath successfully destabilized the Illithid power structure.
- **-10,500 DR**: **Ilsensine’s Last Gambit**: To save the Illithids, Ilsensine sacrificed its physical form, casting a spell to banish Gith'Zerath and the Adversaries, but its consciousness was scattered across all surviving Mind Flayers.
- **-642 DR**: **The Gith Rebellion**, led by the warrior **Gith**, resulted in the final downfall of the Illithid Empire as the Githyanki freed their people from Illithid enslavement.
- **Post-642 DR**: The Illithids remain a defeated race, with some scattered across the multiverse. Ilsensine’s consciousness is fragmented, though there are whispers of its eventual return.

## Rumors & Legends
- **Ilsensine’s Return**: Some devout Illithid worshipers of Ilsensine believe that Ilsensine will one day manifest himself in physical form to **restore** the Illithid Empire. 
  
- **The Adversary**: A terrifying bedtime tale passed down among Elder Brains and Ulitharids, the "**[[Ilsensine|Adversary]]**" is said to be a ruthless and powerful entity that would destroy the entire Illithid race. 
	- The fear of this “Adversary” is deeply ingrained in Illithid culture, with many believing that its arrival would mark the end of their kind.

- **The Chrysalis Codex**: An ancient artifact believed to have the power to reverse ceremorphosis and possibly even restore Ilsensine to its full form, hidden within a Githyanki fortress in the Astral Plane. Whispers suggest that the Codex may be the key to either the revival of Ilsensine or the final undoing of the Illithid race.

- **Gith'Zerath’s Rebirth**: Legends tell of Gith'Zerath’s return from the Far Realms to reclaim his followers and lead a second rebellion against the Illithid Empire, though many fear that this will only serve to hasten the end of the Illithid race.
