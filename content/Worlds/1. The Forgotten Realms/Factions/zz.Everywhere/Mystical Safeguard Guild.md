# Mystical Safeguard Guild
## Overview
The Mystical Safeguard Guild is a powerful guild responsible for enchanting and securing shops in major cities across the Forgotten Realms. Known for their expertise in arcane protections, the Syndicate ensures that every sanctioned shop benefits from magical security systems.

## Origins
Founded in 1356 DR by Archmage Elthorian Silvermantle in [[Waterdeep]], the Guild quickly expanded to other major cities like [[Baldur's Gate]] and Neverwinter.

## Structure
The Syndicate is organized into branches in each city, each led by a High Warden. Members are known as Wardens or Mystics, depending on their specialization. The guild offers various tiers of protection, tailored to the needs of each shop.

## Services
The Syndicate provides enchantment services, including:
- **[[arcane-lock-xphb|Arcane Locks]]**: Enhanced locks with a DC 20 Dexterity check to pick.
- **[[alarm-xphb|Alarm]] Spells**: Alerts the owner and the City Watch of any intrusion.
- **[[glyph-of-warding-xphb|Glyphs of Warding]]**: Magical traps with effects like teleporting intruders to a city jail (DC 25 Dexterity save).
- **Warding Circles**: Protective barriers against specific creatures or spells (DC 22 Wisdom save).

These services ensure shops are well-protected against theft or attack.
