## Welcome
Ansur’s Arcane Aegis (AAA) is **[[Faerûn]]’s premier travel security and protection service**. Whether you are a **merchant, noble, adventurer, or common traveler**, AAA ensures **safe passage along the trade roads and beyond.**

With outposts in **every major city** along the Sword Coast, AAA offers a variety of travel assistance packages tailored to meet your needs.

## 🏠 AAA Outposts – What to Expect
AAA maintains **well-staffed, professional outposts** in all major cities and key trade hubs. When you arrive at an AAA location, expect:
- **A sturdy stone or wood structure, often adorned with a dragon insignia.**
- **A welcoming front office** with a **large contract board** listing caravan departures and available escorts.
- A **waiting lounge** for clients to arrange their travels.
- **A security office** where armed escorts and mercenary assignments are handled.
- **Storage rooms** for securing traveler valuables before departure.

🔎 *Rumors have circulated about restricted back rooms in AAA outposts, but staff members insist these areas contain only logistical records and security operations.*
