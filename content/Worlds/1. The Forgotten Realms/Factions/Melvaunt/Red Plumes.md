## Overview
- **Type**: A mercenary organization serving as the military arm of [[Melvaunt]] and [[Hillsfar]].
- **Duties**:
  - Enforce the will of the First Lord of Hillsfar.
  - Maintain order and secure the city's borders.
- **Behavior**: Known for extortion, intimidation, and harassment of non-human inhabitants under Hillsfar's xenophobic policies.
- **Structure**: Loyal to the First Lord but largely operate as an independent military force with some autonomy.
- **Reputation**: Feared for their brutality and unwavering loyalty to the First Lord, but also known to avoid actions that would provoke rebellion or complaints from powerful factions.
