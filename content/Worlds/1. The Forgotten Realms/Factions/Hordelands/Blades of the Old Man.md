# Blades of the Old Man
[[Plot of the Blades of the Old Man|Dm Only]]
## Overview
**Summary**:  
The Blades of the Old Man are a traditionalist sect of [[Bhaal]] worshipers who adhere to the original doctrines of their deity. Operating from the secluded Fortress of the Old Man in the Sentinelspire Mountains, they view murder as a necessary and sacred act to maintain cosmic balance, opposing the chaotic and indiscriminate killings promoted by modern cults.
- Led by [[High Priestess Talieth.webp|High Priestess Talieth]]

#### What the Organization Wants:
- **Preservation of Traditional Beliefs**: Uphold Bhaal's original teachings, emphasizing murder as a ritualistic act that serves a higher purpose.
- **Restoration of Bhaal's Honor**: Counteract the chaotic interpretations of Bhaal's worship by other sects, aiming to restore the deity's esteemed position in the pantheon.
- **Selective Recruitment**: Attract and train individuals who understand and respect the sanctity of their mission.

#### What the Organization Fears:
- **Dilution of Doctrine**: The spread of chaotic and mindless violence undermining their sacred practices.
- **Extinction**: The potential demise of their order due to dwindling numbers and external threats.
- **Exposure**: Discovery by external forces leading to persecution or eradication.

#### What the Organization Has Lost:
- **Divine Favor**: The direct blessings and powers once granted by Bhaal before his death during the Time of Troubles.
- **Influence and Numbers**: A significant reduction in members and sway over regional affairs.
- **Sacred Artifacts**: Relics imbued with divine power, lost over centuries of conflict and upheaval.

## Etymology
The name "***Blades of the Old Man***" originates from their founder, **Alaodin**, known as the "*Old Man of the Mountain*." The term "Blades" signifies their role as precise instruments of Bhaal's will, executing calculated and purposeful assassinations.

## Activities
**Primary Activities**:  
- **Ritualistic Assassinations**: Conducting murders that align with their belief in maintaining cosmic order.
- **Preservation of Lore**: Safeguarding ancient texts and teachings that reflect Bhaal's original doctrines.
- **Training Disciples**: Educating new members in the traditional practices and philosophies of their faith.

## Society
### Beliefs
The Blades believe that murder, when performed with purpose and ritualistic precision, serves to prevent overpopulation, curb potential tyrants, and eliminate threats to societal stability. They view themselves as custodians of balance, ensuring that each death serves a greater good.

### Culture
Members engage in rigorous training, both physically and intellectually, to master the art of assassination and understand its philosophical underpinnings. They practice asceticism, valuing discipline and restraint, and gather regularly to discuss doctrine, perform rituals, and plan missions.

### Religion
Their worship of Bhaal is solemn and structured, focusing on ceremonies that honor the calculated nature of death. They reject the chaotic and wanton slaughter associated with other Bhaalist cults, striving instead to embody the deity's original intent.

## Possessions
- **[[Sentinelspire]]**: A secluded stronghold serving as their base of operations, equipped with training facilities and archives of sacred texts.
- **Ancient Weaponry**: Blades and tools passed down through generations, each symbolizing their commitment to tradition.
- **Sacred Texts**: Manuscripts detailing Bhaal's original teachings and the philosophies of measured assassination.

## History
**Founding**:  
Established by Alaodin, the "Old Man of the Mountain," the Blades were created to serve as Bhaal's elite assassins, executing missions with precision and purpose. They operated from the Fortress of the Old Man, hidden within the Sentinelspire Mountains.

**Key Events**:  
- **1358 DR**: The Time of Troubles leads to Bhaal's death, causing a crisis of faith and loss of divine powers among the Blades.
- **1369 DR**: Leadership turmoil results in Alaodin's death; Chereth assumes control, leading to internal strife and a departure from traditional practices.
- **1374 DR**: A significant schism occurs, with traditionalists led by Talieth retreating to preserve original doctrines.
- **1502 DR**: Under High Priestess Talieth, the Blades continue as a small, clandestine group dedicated to Bhaal's ancient teachings.

## Rumors & Legends
- **The Hidden Prophecy**: Whispers of a prophecy foretelling the rebirth of Bhaal's true essence through the Blades' unwavering devotion.
- **The Lost Relic**: Legends speak of a powerful artifact, the "Dagger of Equilibrium," said to enhance the wielder's ability to execute Bhaal's will with unparalleled precision.
- **The Chosen Successor**: Some believe that a direct descendant of Alaodin exists, destined to lead the Blades to a new era of influence and restore the sanctity of their mission.
