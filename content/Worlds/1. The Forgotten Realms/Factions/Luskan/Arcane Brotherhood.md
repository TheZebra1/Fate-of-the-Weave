# Arcane Brotherhood
## Overview
**Summary**:  
The **Arcane Brotherhood** is a secretive and ruthless organization of wizards based in Luskan. They seek to amass magical power, control trade, and extend their influence across the Sword Coast.
#### What the Organization Wants:
- Discover and hoard magical knowledge and artifacts.  
- Establish dominance over [[Luskan]] and surrounding regions.
#### What the Organization Fears:
- Rival magic factions like the **[[Red Wizards]] of [[Thay]]**.  
- Internal betrayal and power struggles among its ranks.
#### What the Organization Has Lost:
- The **[[Hosttower of the Arcane]]** (destroyed and later rebuilt).  
- Control over Luskan due to external conflicts and infighting.
---
## Etymology
"Brotherhood" signifies their exclusive, secretive nature, while "Arcane" reflects their pursuit of magic above all else.
## Activities
**Primary Activities**:  
- Magical research, artifact collection, and spell experimentation.  
- Political manipulation and trade control, especially in Luskan.
---
## Society
### Beliefs
- **Magic is power**—knowledge is hoarded, not shared.  
- The **strongest wizard should rule**—hierarchy is based on ability.
### Culture
- Intrigue and betrayal are common; members constantly scheme for dominance.  
- Outsiders are seen as tools, obstacles, or threats to be eliminated.
### Religion
- No unified faith, but members often revere **Mystra** (magic), **Bane** (tyranny), or **Vecna** (secrets).
---
## Possessions
- **The Hosttower of the Arcane**, a rebuilt magical fortress in Luskan.  
- **Ancient tomes and artifacts**, some dating back to Netheril.
---
## History
**Founding**:  
Established centuries ago by ambitious wizards seeking to control Luskan and the North through magic.

**Key Events**:  
- **Arklem Greeth’s reign**—turned the Hosttower into a stronghold of dark magic.  
- **Destruction and reformation**—the Brotherhood collapsed multiple times but always resurfaces.  
- **Recent resurgence**—present in Icewind Dale (*Rime of the Frostmaiden*), seeking lost magic.
---
## Rumors & Legends
- **The Ghost of Arklem Greeth**—some say his spirit still haunts the Hosttower.  
- **Forbidden Spells**—rumors of powerful magic hidden within their vaults, too dangerous to use.
