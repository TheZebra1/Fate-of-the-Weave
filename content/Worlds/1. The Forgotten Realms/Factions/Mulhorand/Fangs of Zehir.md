# The Fangs of Set/[[Zehir]]
## **History**
- **Origins**: Established in the early 1400's DR as a cult devoted to [[Zehir]].
- **Goal**: Make Set/Zehir a dominant god of the Faerûnian pantheon.
- **Reputation**: A secretive and ruthless organization manipulating events to serve Set/Zehir’s interests.

## **Why [[Zahur]] and the Shard of the Skyfire Matter**
1. **Prophecy of the Celestial Scarab**:
   - The Shard holds a fragment of **[[Khepri]]’s divine essence** and serves as a "gateway" to channel Set/Zehir’s power.
   - Zahur is prophesied as the **"Scarred Heir of the Sands"**, a mortal tether critical to the Shard's destiny.

2. **The Name Connection: Zahur and Zehir**:
   - The Fangs claim Set/Zehir shaped Zahur’s birth, marking him as the destined vessel for Set’s power.
   - Zahur’s wild magic is seen by the Fangs as evidence of Set’s influence.

3. **Conflict Between Divine Influences**:
   - **Khepri’s Goal**: Zahur represents renewal and balance, with the potential to heal the lands ravaged by Set.
   - **Set/Zehir’s Goal**: Corrupt Zahur’s power to plunge the world into chaos.

## **The Fangs’ Current Activity**
1. **The Ritual**: 
   - Preparing the **[[Binding of the Skyfire]]** ritual to sacrifice Zahur and bind the Shard’s power to Set/Zehir’s chosen **[[Ankharet]]**, breaking Khepri’s influence.
   - **Key Elements**: Zahur (the catalyst) and the Shard (the focus).

2. **Key Locations**:
   - **[[Temple of Eternal Shadows]]**: A hidden desert temple dedicated to Set/Zehir, accessible during specific celestial alignments.
   - **Desert Ruins**: Scattered bases used for relic storage and planning.

3. **Active Agents**:
   - **[[Ankharet]], the Voice of Set/Zehir**: A yuan-ti high priestess overseeing operations and seeking to ensure Zahur becomes Set’s vessel.
   - **The Fang**: A masked assassin and master manipulator leading efforts to capture Zahur and the Shard.

## **Themes: Corruption vs. Redemption**
- **Corruption Attempts**: The Fangs exploit Zahur’s insecurities, claiming:
  - Khepri abandoned him.
  - Set is his true father.
  - Embracing chaos is his destiny.
- **Promises**: Power, control over his magic, or undoing the destruction of his village.

## **The Big Reveal: Zahur’s Birth and Connection to Set/Zehir**
1. **Hints**:
   - Fangs refer to Zahur as the **“Scarred Heir.”**
   - Relics and visions reveal Zahur’s divine significance.
   - Fangs will injure Zahur to subdue him, but respect him greatly as Zehirs son and will not allow him to die before the sacrifice.
   - When killed Fangs will kneel and cast a one turn **bless** on Zahur before dying.

2. **Full Truth**:
   - Zahur’s birth is the result of a celestial contest between **Khepri** and **Set/Zehir**, both influencing his creation.
   - His wild magic reflects the internal struggle between the two gods, making him pivotal to their plans.
   - Khepri wants to do more as Set’s eternal rival, but he was never brought over from Earth with the rest of the Pantheon into [[Faerûn]]. The best he could do within the laws of the Multi-verse was send the **[[Shard of Skyfire|Shard]]** and influence Zahurs birth and even that was pushing it.

## **Summary of Campaign Stakes**
- **Fangs’ Goal**: Corrupt Zahur and use the Shard to bring Set/Zehir into the world.
- **Khepri’s Goal**: Protect Zahur and use the Shard to stop Set/Zephir. The ***shard*** when mastered can also stabilize and renew the desert.
