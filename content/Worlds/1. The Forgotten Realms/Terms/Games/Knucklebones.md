# Knucklebones (D&D Poker)
## Overview
Knucklebones is a simple dice game of chance and bluffing, often played in taverns and gambling dens. Players roll **three** dice and bet on who has the best hand.
## Requirements
- **Players:** 2 or more
- **Dice:** Each player needs 3d6
- **Starting Ante:** Each player places an agreed-upon amount into the pot before each round.
## Gameplay
1. **Ante Up**  
   - Each player antes up by placing the agreed bet into the pot.

2. **Rolling the Bones**  
   - Each player rolls 3d6 in secret.

3. **Betting & Bluffing (Optional)**  
   - Players may raise, call, or fold based on their confidence in their roll.
   - If all but one player fold, the remaining player wins the pot.

4. **Reveal & Compare**  
   - All remaining players reveal their dice.
   - The highest-ranking hand wins the pot.
## Hand Rankings (Highest to Lowest)
1. **Three of a Kind** – All three dice show the same number (e.g., 5-5-5).
2. **Straight** – The dice show a sequence (e.g., 2-3-4 or 4-5-6).
3. **Two of a Kind** – Two dice match (e.g., 3-3-5).
4. **Highest Die Wins** – If no one has the above, the highest single die determines the winner.
   - If tied, compare the second highest, then third.
## Variant Rules
- **"Dead Man’s Bones"** – Rolling three 1s means an automatic loss.
- **"The Gambler’s Curse"** – Players may re-roll one die, but rolling a 1 results in instant loss.
- **"The Tavern Special"** – The house (or bartender) takes a small cut of every pot.
## Example Round
1. Three players ante up (2 gold each, pot = 6g).
2. Each rolls 3d6 in secret.
3. Player 1 raises the bet by 2g. Player 2 calls, Player 3 folds.
4. Players 1 and 2 reveal their dice.
5. Player 1 has 4-4-6, Player 2 has 2-3-4 (a straight).
6. Player 2 wins the pot!
