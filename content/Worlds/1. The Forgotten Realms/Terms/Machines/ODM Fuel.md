# **Fueling the ODM Gear: Elemental Distillate & Thundercore Gas**  

Elias Thorne’s **ODM Gear** requires an advanced **arcane propulsion system**—something beyond traditional combustion or alchemical reactions. Over the years, **two viable fuel sources** have been developed, though both are **incredibly rare and tightly controlled**:  

## **1. Elemental Distillate (Melvaunt Inc.’s Primary Fuel Source)**
- A highly volatile liquid derived from the **essence of bound air elementals**, refined through **magitek compression chambers**.  
- It provides **exceptional propulsion power**, allowing rapid bursts of movement **without traditional combustion**.  
- Used exclusively by **Melvaunt Inc.**, which operates **secretive extraction facilities** in remote locations.  

🔹 **Strengths:**  
✔ Produces the **most stable and efficient propulsion**, minimizing risk to the user.  
✔ Melvaunt’s engineers have **optimized it for sustained use**, making it the **ideal fuel for the ODM system**.  
✔ Near-invisible exhaust makes it **perfect for stealth operations**.  

🔹 **Weaknesses:**  
✖ **Highly controlled supply**—Elias cannot legally or easily obtain it outside of Melvaunt’s operations.  

## **2. Thundercore Gas (The Arcane Brotherhood’s Alternative)**
- A **lightning-infused vapor**, extracted from **storm-charged Aetherium veins**.  
- Stored in **pressure-sealed canisters**, it releases **short but powerful bursts of propulsion**, though **less stable than Elemental Distillate**.  
- The **Arcane Brotherhood of Luskan** has experimented with it for use in **stormforged weapons and energy-based engines**.  

🔹 **Strengths:**  
✔ Can be used as an **emergency fuel** if Elias cannot acquire Elemental Distillate.  
✔ Generates **raw kinetic force**, allowing for even **faster acceleration**, though at a **higher risk**.  
✔ Available in **Luskan’s black market**, where the **Arcane Brotherhood might be willing to trade—if the price is right**.  

🔹 **Weaknesses:**  
✖ **Far more unstable** than Elemental Distillate—misuse can result in **shock discharges or uncontrolled propulsion**.  
✖ Operation is loud and **never stealthy.**
✖ The Arcane Brotherhood **does not easily part with its stock**, meaning Elias must either **steal it, barter for it, or work for them**.  

---

## **Which Fuel is Better?**
- **Melvaunt Inc. uses Elemental Distillate as the primary and most reliable fuel source.**  
- **Elias, if cut off from Melvaunt’s supply, may have to turn to the Arcane Brotherhood’s Thundercore Gas, though doing so could come at a steep price.**  

---

### **Summary**
The **ODM Gear relies on Elemental Distillate**, a **controlled and stable fuel source** created from **bound air elementals**, but in desperate situations, **Thundercore Gas**—a volatile, lightning-infused alternative—can be used as a substitute, though it comes with **risks and political entanglements**.
