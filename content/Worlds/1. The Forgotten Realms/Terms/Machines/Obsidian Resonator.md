**Obsidian Resonator**  
> A towering device of Mind Flayer origin, the **Obsidian Resonator** serves as a powerful psionic communication hub and energy amplifier. It channels and magnifies mental signals, allowing long-distance telepathic contact between Illithid settlements. When fully operational, the Resonator can also boost psionic abilities, create mental barriers, and project powerful waves of psionic energy across vast distances. Its core, an obsidian crystal, stores and focuses the immense power required for these tasks.

- *DC 25 INTELLIGENCE to operate without training*
- *DC 20 INTELLIGENCE with Psionic abilities.*
- *DC 15 INTELLIGENCE with ADVANTAGE for mindflayer or related creatures.*
