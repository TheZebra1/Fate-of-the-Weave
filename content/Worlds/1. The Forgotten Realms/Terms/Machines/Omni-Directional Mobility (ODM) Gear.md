#### **Item Type:** Wondrous Item (Very Rare), requires the [[Project Lidbrani]] Surgery

This custom piece of equipment allows its user to move rapidly in three dimensions and attack massive enemies effectively.
- Runs on  [[ODM Fuel]]
### **Features & Mechanics**
#### 1. Gas-Powered Boost

- The ODM gear has **5 charges** of compressed gas, the user can expend **1 charge** to:
    - ***Bonus Action:*** Walking speed is set to 0 for the round. Then fly in a straight line up to 60 ft. in any direction towards a solid surface or heavier creature using a grappling hook(*range 100 ft*.)
    - ***Reaction:*** Take the **Dodge** action and move 10 ft. in any direction without provoking oppurtunity attacks.
    - ***Reaction:*** Change Directions/Turn in the air.
- The gas supply canisters can be changed over a **short or long rest**.

#### 2. Wall Running/Momentum
- **Wall Running:** The user can move along vertical or horizontal walls without falling **for up to 1 rounds** before needing to land or use another boost.
	- User does not fall if ending it's turn in the air after using a Boost.

#### **3. Grappling Hook Mechanics**
- If targeting a **creature**, the user makes a ranged weapon attack using Dexterity.
	- If the user is airborne and has hit a target bigger than itself with it's grappling hook, they can make an **Athletics or Acrobatics check (DC 14)** to reposition themselves to that enemy's flank, gaining **advantage on their next attack against that creature**.

### **Combat Example: ODM Gear in Action**

1. The user **fires a grappling hook into a floating [[planar guardian]]**. **(Bonus Action, 1 gas charge)**
2. **They launch forward 30ft (movement) , spinning mid-air to reposition behind their massive foe **(Acrobatics/Athletics DC 14)**.
3. **They slash twice (Attack Action)** with their twin blades, striking the enemy’s weak point.
4. **They change direction to retreat**, firing a second grappling hook to a nearby building and using last 30ft of aerial movement **(Reaction + 1 charge)**, setting up for the next attack.
