## **Creation and Purpose**  
A **Dawnbringer** is a sentient, radiant longsword, originally forged by the **[[Order of the Arcane Blade]]** as a symbol of their creed—discipline, balance, and the careful wielding of power. Crafted through the sacred *Rite of Dawn’s Embrace*, it serves as both a weapon and a guiding light, ensuring its wielder acts with wisdom and precision.

A [[Dawnblades|Dawnblade]] created through the *Rite of Dawn’s Embrace* eventually evolves into a **Dawnbringer**.

Dawnbringers possess unique sentience, their very essence infused with the light of the **[[Weave]]** itself. The blade’s radiance is not only a tool against darkness but also a reminder that power, when wielded recklessly, leads to ruin. It is entrusted only to those who embody the highest ideals of the Order—those who achieve the rank of **[[Order of the Arcane Blade#Structure|Dawnbringer]]**, for whom the blades are named.

---

## **Brysis of Khaem: The Guardian and the Betrayed**  
One well-known wielder of a Dawnbringer was **Brysis of Khaem**, a half-elf sorcerer who lived during the final days of **[[Netheril]]**. Though not a sworn member of the Order, Brysis was a steadfast ally who believed in their philosophy of restraint and purpose. She wielded the blade with care, understanding that true power lay not in destruction, but in knowing when to act.

When the floating cities of Netheril plummeted from the sky due to [[Karsus's Folly]], Brysis sought to protect the Dawnbringer and many arcane secrets from those who would misuse its power. She sealed herself within her floating tomb, intending to safeguard the blade until the Order could reclaim it. However, as Netheril fell, so too did her tomb—swallowed by the **Underdark**, lost for centuries.

---

## **A Tragic Fall: From Wielder of the Light to a Creature of Darkness**  
For centuries, the Dawnbringer lay dormant in the **Lost Tomb of Khaem**, shrouded in darkness. But while the blade endured, so too did the soul of Brysis—trapped, alone, and forgotten. When the **Demon Lords** were unleashed upon the Underdark in the late 15th century DR, their corrupting influence awakened her—but not as the woman she once was.

Brysis rose as a **wraith**, a being of hatred and malice, her soul twisted by the very forces she once fought against. The wielder of the Dawnbringer had become an agent of darkness—a cruel irony that serves as a grim reminder of the cost of unchecked power. The very blade she had sworn to protect was now lost to her, yearning for a new wielder to carry on the mission she had abandoned.
