# Twisted Echoes of the Summoned
![[Unfamiliar.png|500]]
## Origins
- Once ordinary [[Feywild|Fey]] beings, repeatedly summoned and dismissed until their minds fractured.
- Warped into eldritch horrors due to overuse of summoning magic.

## Appearance
- Shifting between bestial and avian features, flickering between solid and ethereal.
- Unnatural body angles, glowing haunting eyes, and rippling unstable magic.

## Motivation & Behavior
- Hunt down summoners, especially those who frequently use familiars:
	- [[Familiarist Class]]
	- [[Ebon Chain]]
- Destabilize familiar bonds, making them rebellious.
- Seek to unmake the very act of forced summoning.

## Summoner's Folly
- Overuse of summoning magic may lead to familiars returning as Unfamiliars.
- Some were once powerful archfey or celestial beings, now irreversibly broken.

## Encounters
- Found near places of magical learning, lurking in shadows.
- Attack with unnatural speed and unpredictable magic.
- Leave behind mist and eerie whispers upon defeat.

## Growing Threat
- Increasing attacks as summoning magic becomes more common.
- Fear of Unfamiliars evolving into a force that disrupts summoning magic itself.
- Scholars worry that no Fey can be summoned without risk of transformation.

## Final Warning
- Every Unfamiliar was once a bound creature, now seeking vengeance.
- The cost of summoning magic is no longer just ethical—it may be deadly.
