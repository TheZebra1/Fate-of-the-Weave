**The primary world of the Forgotten Realms setting, teeming with magic, gods, empires, and adventure.**
**Social Hubs:** [[Waterdeep]], [[Baldur's Gate]], [[Neverwinter]]

#### Notable Locations:
- **[[Waterdeep]]:** “City of Splendors,” a cultural and magical epicenter.
- **[[Baldur's Gate]]:** A powerful port city and trade hub.
- **[[Neverwinter]]:** Known for its resilience and masterwork crafting.

#### Key Summary
- **Geography:** Toril is the third planet in Realmspace, home to diverse continents, climates, and magical phenomena.
- **Satellites:** Orbited by [[Selûne]] and the [[Tears of Selûne]], both playing roles in myth and magic.
- **Society:** Features civilizations ranging from tribal to highly advanced magical empires.
- **Religion:** Worshipped by an expansive pantheon influencing all realms of existence.
- **Emotion:** **Wonder** – A world of limitless exploration, secrets, and celestial design.
- **Resources:** Brimming with magic, divine influence, enchanted artifacts, and natural wealth.

### History:
- **[[Abeir]]-Toril Split:** The world was originally united with Abeir, later separated by the overgod [[Ao]].
- **[[Time of Troubles]]:** The gods walked Toril in mortal form, reshaping the divine order.
- **[[Spellplague]]:** Resulting from Mystra’s death, the Spellplague wrought havoc on Toril’s magic and geography.
- **[[Second Sundering]]:** Recent planar realignment restoring lands, deities, and balance across the world.
