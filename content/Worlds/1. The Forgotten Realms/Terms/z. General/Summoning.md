## General Rule:
All creatures summoned from other planes, including the [[Feywild]], retain their full awareness and consciousness.
### Key Aspects:
- **Sentient Summons**: **Every** summoned creature is **aware** of its summoning and possesses its own thoughts and consciousness.

- **Varied Control**: The **level of control** a summoned creature maintains depends on its **power** and **willpower**. More powerful beings might act independently, while **weaker creatures are less likely to regain control** until they are dismissed or killed.

- **Return to Home Plane**: Upon being "**killed**" outside of their **Home Plane**, summoned creatures return to their home plane, carrying the memory of their summoning experience.

- **Ethical Implications**: This practice can create ethical dilemmas and interplanar tensions, as the use of sentient beings as **tools** can foster **resentment**, particularly from planes like the Feywild.

## The Feywild's Perspective:
- **Frequent Summoning Source**: The Feywild is a common source for summoned creatures, making its inhabitants particularly aware and resentful of this practice.
### Rise of Unfamiliar's
- The resentment of summoned familiar's has begun to manifest has new aberrated fey called [[Unfamiliar]]s.
