### Kaldi's Coffee
**Wondrous Item (Uncommon)**

#### Description:
A small flask of **Kaldi’s Coffee** brewed by the goblin coffee maker, [[Kaldi Bunna]]. This coffee is a dark, rich brew with deep earthy flavors, offering a brief moment of respite in the harshness of the Underdark. When consumed, the drinker feels an immediate surge of energy and focus. It clears the mind, replenishes physical strength, and restores the body’s natural resilience, temporarily alleviating the weariness that plagues many travelers in this grim place.

#### Special Considerations:
- **Exhaustion Relief:** Drinking Kaldi’s Coffee **removes one level of exhaustion**. This effect can only be triggered **once per long rest**. If the drinker is not suffering from exhaustion, they gain a small but potent boost of **temporary hit points** equal to their **character level** (*maximum 10*), which lasts for 1 hour.
- **Once Per Long Rest:** Kaldi’s Coffee can only be used once per long rest, regardless of how many cups are consumed.
- **Brewed in the Underdark:** The coffee's effects are uniquely tied to the Underdark's energies. It may not function the same way in different environments. In places of extreme magic or different planar origins, the DM may choose to have it be less effective or have altered effects.
### Kaldi's Coffee: Brewing Conditions

Kaldi Bunna, the eccentric goblin coffee maker, has a deep respect for the art of coffee brewing. To prepare the perfect cup of **Kaldi's Coffee**, certain ingredients and conditions must be met. This special brew requires rare components that can only be found under specific circumstances in the Underdark and beyond.

#### Ingredients & Conditions:

1. **Pure Clean Water**  
   - **Description:** Kaldi believes that only the purest water can unlock the full potential of his brew. He requires clean water preferably from a **spring** in the Underdark, however clean water freshly collected will do. The water should be untainted by the **Sight Rot**.

2. **Deep Cavern Mushrooms (Fungal Beans)**  
   - **Description:** Deep in the Underdark grow mushrooms that, when ground and roasted, create a distinctive flavor for the coffee. These fungi are dark, rich, and pungent, and they have been cultivated by the myconids for generations.
   - **Obtaining the Mushrooms:** The players will need to venture into the Underdark’s more dangerous regions to gather the rare **Fungal Beans** from these mushrooms, often guarded by creatures or difficult terrain.

3. **Underdark Honey (Secret Ingredient)**  
   - **Description:** Kaldi’s secret ingredient, a rare honey harvested from glowing, dangerous Underdark bees, gives his coffee a smooth, almost buttery flavor. The honey is found in hives that reside in high caves, sometimes guarded by giant spiders or even driders.
   - **Obtaining the Honey:** To collect this ingredient, the players may have to outwit or battle the dangerous protectors of the hive. Kaldi insists that only a small amount is necessary to truly perfect the brew, so they won’t need to harvest too much.

4. **Magic Fire**  
   - **Description:** Kaldi has a specific method of brewing his coffee, but it requires a certain type of fire. He needs **magical flames** to get the temperature just right, neither too high nor too low. This can be achieved using enchanted items, magical fire spells, or by drawing from fire-based elemental magic.
   - **Obtaining the Magic Fire:** Players could acquire magical flames in a number of ways—by harvesting **components** from **fire elementals**, finding **enchanted coals**, or through a **DC 15 arcana** check by a **spellcaster** with **fire magic** in the party.

5. **Time & Space**  
   - **Description:** The brew requires patience. It is not the kind of coffee that can be rushed. The right atmosphere is key: a peaceful moment where time slows down and the party can gather together. Kaldi insists that the coffee be brewed **during a long rest**, giving him the time he needs to perfect it.
   - **Obtaining the Atmosphere:** The players need to ensure they are in a place where they can truly relax for a long rest, ideally away from danger or conflict. The coffee's full potential won’t emerge if brewed in haste.

#### The Brewing Process:

Once Kaldi has gathered all the required ingredients, the brewing process takes approximately **one hour**. During this time, Kaldi becomes completely absorbed in his work, muttering about the purity of the water, the freshness of the mushrooms, and the magical qualities of the fire. If the players are in a rush, they may try to hasten the process, but doing so will reduce the effectiveness of the brew. Kaldi will refuse to compromise on his craft.

#### Special Considerations:
- **Once per week:** Kaldi can only brew his special coffee once per week, due to the rarity of the ingredients and the care needed to make it properly.
- **Dangerous Ingredients:** Some of the ingredients are dangerous to collect, and certain creatures may see the players as rivals for these resources.
