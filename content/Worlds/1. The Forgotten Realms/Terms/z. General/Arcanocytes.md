# Arcanocytes

Arcanocytes are microscopic, magic-conducting organisms found within the cells of almost all creatures. They play a crucial role in an individual’s magical potential and the differences in magical abilities across races and individuals.

## Origins

When mortals were created, Mystra is believed to have imbued each creature with Arcanocytes, enabling a biological connection to the Weave.

## Key Characteristics

1. **Density**: The more Arcanocytes an individual has, the stronger their innate magical potential. Higher density often correlates with greater magical aptitude.
   
2. **Manifestation**: Arcanocytes vary by race and individual, shaping their magical affinity. For example, elves may have Arcanocytes tuned to nature, while humans exhibit broader versatility.

3. **Sensitivity**: Some individuals are more attuned to their Arcanocytes, showing early magical talent even without training—common in sorcerers.

4. **Training**: Those with moderate Arcanocyte density, like wizards, rely on rigorous study to enhance their magical abilities.

## Exploitation of Arcanocytes

1. **Life-Draining Spells**: These spells drain the Arcanocytes from the victim, siphoning their magical potential, often leaving the target weakened or dead.

2. **Temporary Effects**: Stolen Arcanocytes degrade quickly outside their host, explaining the temporary impact of life-draining spells.

## Summary

Arcanocytes are central to the magical ecosystem, explaining why magical abilities vary across races and individuals. This biological explanation is widely accepted in scientific and arcane circles alike.
