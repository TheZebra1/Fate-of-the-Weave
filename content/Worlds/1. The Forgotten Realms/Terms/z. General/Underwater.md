# [[Underdark]] Water Challenges
- Most water found in the underdark is tainted and the likelihood of [[diseases.magical contagions#Demon Fever|Zuggtomoy Fever]] is high.
	- Related to [[Zuggtmoy]]
## 1. **Sources of Clean Water**
   - **Natural Springs**: Rare underground springs, usually coveted by creatures or factions.
   - **Stalactite Drips**: Small, clean water droplets from condensation that.
   - **Bioluminescent Fungi**: Mushrooms that grow near clean water, indicating its presence.
   - **Divine Rituals**: Clerics or shamans use magic or rituals (e.g. *Locate Object*) to find safe water.

## 2. **Purification Methods**
   - **Alchemical Filters**: Rare devices or powders developed by alchemists to purify water.
   - **Magical Purification**: Spells like *[[purify-food-and-drink|Purify Food and Drink]]*, essential for communities or adventurers.
   - **Crystal Radiance**: Crystals that neutralize *Sight Rot*, but lose potency over time.
   - **[[Underdark#Underdark Fungi Reference Table|Underdark Fungi]]**: Barrelstalk absorbs and neutralizes contaminants after soaking.

## 3. **Water Access & Conservation**
   - **Control and Trade**: Powerful factions (e.g. drow, duergar) control and trade clean water.
   - **Rationing**: Communities store and ration purified water carefully.
   - **Fermented Drinks**: Local brews (like mushroom ale) stretch small amounts of clean water.
   - **Surface Rain Catching**: Caverns with surface openings collect **"Stalactite Drips"** for use.
   - **Artificial Springs**: Magic-powered or mechanically constructed springs to siphon clean water.

## 4. **Roleplaying and Adventure Hooks**
   - **Water Conflicts**: Disputes over clean water rights lead to political tensions or adventures.
   - **Herb Quests**: Dangerous quests to gather rare herbs for filtration or remedies.
   - **Sacred Water**: Water purification rituals may be sacred, with clerics revered for their powers.
   - **Pollution Foes**: A creature or artifact causing *Sight Rot*, which must be dealt with to restore water.
   - **Tribute for Water**: Communities or travelers perform tasks or pay tribute for access to clean water.
