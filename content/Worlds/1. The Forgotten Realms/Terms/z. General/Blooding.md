# Blooding (Drow Coming-of-Age Ritual)

**Overview:**
- The Blooding is a traditional drow coming-of-age ritual.
- It marks the transition from adolescence to adulthood.

**Procedure:**
- A young drow must hunt and kill an intelligent or dangerous creature from the surface world.
- This often occurs during surface raids; preferred targets include surface elves.
- Alternatively, captives may be brought into the Underdark specifically for this purpose.

**Purpose:**
- Demonstrates the individual's prowess and readiness to serve drow society.
- Reinforces the values of strength, cunning, and dominance over surface dwellers.

**Cultural Significance:**
- Successful completion is a rite of passage, granting full status within drow society.
- Failure or refusal can result in punishment or death, as it reflects weakness or disobedience.
