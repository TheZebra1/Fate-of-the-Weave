The Weave is how most people are able to perceive and interact with magic. It was created by the Goddess [[Mystra]].

The Weave flows through nearly every aspect of the known universe.

Importantly, the Weave was is not **pure magic**, but rather a structure by which that original magic can be channeled.  

Other systems for calling upon pure magic have always existed within the Realms, but the Weave is by far the most widely used and best understood.

The Weave was also linked to fate, and a rare few individuals could manipulate such connection to alter time istelf to their advantage.
