A **Fey Pact** is a magical agreement or binding contract between a mortal and a powerful Fey being (such as an Archfey, hag, or Feylord). These deals are often sealed through riddles, clever wordplay, or symbolic gestures rather than traditional legal terms.

- **Nature of Fey Contracts**: Fey pacts often rely on **intent, wording, and emotional investment** rather than legality.
- **Balance of Power**: While a mortal may gain magic or influence, the Fey almost always hides a greater cost or ironic twist.
- **Breaking a Pact**: Breaking the terms—especially unknowingly—often results in curses, madness, or the fey "collecting" what is owed.

## Examples of Fey Pacts
### 🎭 Pact of the Final Curtain
**Offer:** Resurrection of a fallen loved one  
**Cost:** The character must perform in the Feylord’s "theater" when summoned, risking humiliation or death.

### ⌛ Pact of Stolen Time
**Offer:** A decade of youth restored  
**Cost:** The Fey steals a decade from the character's future. They age rapidly at an unpredictable time.

### 🪞 Pact of the Double
**Offer:** A perfect doppelganger to complete dangerous tasks  
**Cost:** If the doppelganger dies, the character loses that piece of themselves—memories, abilities, or emotions.

### 🐚 Pact of the Whispering Tide
**Offer:** The ability to speak and understand any language  
**Cost:** The character must whisper every word they say into a seashell and toss it into running water—or lose their voice the next dawn.

### 🔥 Pact of the Kindled Heart
**Offer:** The character is protected from fire and heat  
**Cost:** They feel constant cold in their heart and can no longer feel warmth—physically or emotionally.

### 🐇 Pact of Endless Flight
**Offer:** A magical token that lets the character teleport once per day  
**Cost:** A Fey creature starts hunting them slowly, never stopping. It only needs to catch them once.

### 🧸 Pact of the Lost Name
**Offer:** Great magical power (spellcasting, artifact, Feywild travel)  
**Cost:** The character’s true name is erased. They are unrecognizable to loved ones and immune to divination.

### 💀 Pact of the Glass Grave
**Offer:** Immunity to physical death until a fate foretold  
**Cost:** A metaphysical doom chases the character—like a slow-moving, unkillable threat that can strike only once.

### 🌙 Pact of Night’s Favor
**Offer:** The ability to never sleep and always remain vigilant  
**Cost:** Dreams become real. Nightmares occasionally manifest in the real world as temporary phantasms or curses.

## Notes for DMs
- Use **Fey speech patterns**: poetic, ambiguous, and playful.
- Boons should be tempting but flawed.
- Pacts should **advance the story**, not simply grant power.
- Allow clever players to negotiate, twist, or even loophole the deal.
