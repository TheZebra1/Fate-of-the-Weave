[[Order of the Arcane Blade]]
#### Arcane Casting Through Touch
- **Philosophy**: The Order of the Arcane Blade believes in ***measured discipline***. 
	- To embody this, all members undergo a **sacred ritual** upon becoming Initiates that alters their **connection** to **magic**, **restricting** their **spells** to **touch range**. 
		- This philosophy enforces the idea that magic should never be cast recklessly or at a distance but delivered only when the **threat is immediate** or a **perfect, decisive** strike has been identified.

- **The Ritual of Binding**:
  - Upon initiation, acolytes undergo a ritual that permanently **binds** their magical essence to their **body** and **blade**, severing their ability to cast spells at range.
    
- **Casting Mechanics**:
  - **Touch Spells**: Delivered via *Unarmed Strike* or through the [[Dawnblades]], dealing **physical** damage, **spell** damage, and effects **simultaneously**.
  - **Non-Touch Spells**: Automatically **converted** to **single-target** spells delivered with a strike. Area-effect spells (e.g., *Thunderwave*) project their effect behind the target in a **cone** equal to the spell’s range.
	  - Without a Sun Blade, members can still cast spells via **touch** but lose the seamless synergy of the blade’s focus.

- **Connection to the Sun Blade**:
  - The [[Dawnblades]], created during the ***Rite of Dawn’s Embrace***, serves as the magical focus that channels touch-based casting. This reinforces the philosophy of harmony between blade and spell.
	  - The blade’s growth in power mirrors the caster’s increasing discipline and mastery, further symbolizing their commitment to the Order’s ideals.

- **Effect on the Order It Must Be This Way**:
  - This philosophy ties directly to [[Aelindor Vaelithar]]'s belief in waiting for the right moment to reclaim [[Cormanthor Forest]]—**measured patience** over impulsive violence is the heart of the order's identity.
