The **Material Plane** is the primary plane of existence in the Forgotten Realms, where most mortal beings reside. It is a realm of both magic and mundane elements, serving as a bridge between other planes like the [[Shadowfell]] and [[Feywild]]. 

This plane features diverse landscapes, from vast ***oceans*** and towering ***mountains*** to bustling ***cities*** and ancient *forests***.** The Material Plane is defined by its **balance**, existing at the intersection of elemental forces and cosmic influences, making it the center of many planar conflicts and adventures.
