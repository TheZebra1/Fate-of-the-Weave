# **Aetherium: The Arcane Alloy**
![[Aetherium.png|250]]
## **What is Aetherium?**
Aetherium is a rare, magical crystal known for its ability to stabilize and amplify arcane energy. It is prized for powering [[mythallar]]s, constructing enchanted devices, and reinforcing advanced magical structures. Its unique properties make it both incredibly valuable and dangerous to handle.
>Antithesis of [[Karsite]]
### Special Effect 
- A creature can hold a shard of Aetherium during a short rest and restore one Spell Slot up to level 3 once per day. 
	- A creature may not gain this benefit from Aetherium more than one per day even if they use a different shard.

### Sources
Aetherium is found in a few rare locations:
1. **The [[Underdark]]:** Veins of raw aetherium crystal form in areas of high magical density but are often guarded by dangerous creatures.  
2. **Netherese Ruins:** Ancient caches of refined aetherium remain hidden in the ruins of Netherese cities.  
3. **Planar Rifts:** Crystals form near rifts to the Feywild, Shadowfell, or other planes, though harvesting them requires advanced tools.  
4. **Meteoric Deposits:** Rare meteors composed of aetherium occasionally fall, guarded by powerful creatures like dragons.

### Aetherium (Weapon or Focus Modifier)  
*Weapon (any) or Spellcasting Focus, rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*

Aetherium-infused weapons or focuses are laced with shimmering veins of stabilized arcane crystal. Crafted by arcanists and artificers who dare to harness the raw energy of the [[Weave]], these items are highly sought after by spellcasters across [[Faerûn]].

When a weapon or focus is modified with Aetherium, it gains the following properties:

- **Arcane Amplification.** While attuned, your spell save DC and spell attack bonus each increase by **+1**.  
- **Channel Surge.** Once per turn when you cast a spell of **1st level or higher**, you can choose to deal **extra force damage** equal to your **proficiency bonus** to one target of the spell.  
- **Weave Battery.** The item stores ambient magical energy. When you finish a long rest, choose one 1st-level spell you know or have prepared. Once that day, you may cast it **without expending a spell slot**. This resets on a long rest.

> **Volatile Arcana.** When you roll a **natural 1** on a spell attack or saving throw to maintain concentration, the Aetherium item pulses with wild energy. Roll on a **Wild Magic Surge** table. Even if you're not a sorcerer, the effect still occurs.

**Variant:** Aetherium can be used to craft or modify **spellcasting focuses** (rods, staves, orbs) with the same effects. These variants often glow softly with ambient light and hum when near ley lines or powerful enchantments.

### Aetherium (Armor or Clothing Modifier)  
*Armor (any) or Robes, rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*

This armor or robe is inlaid with flowing strands of Aetherium, humming faintly with stored power. While it offers no unusual protection against mundane threats, its value to spellcasters lies in the way it stabilizes the magic of the wearer.

A creature wearing Aetherium-enhanced armor or robes gains the following effects:

- **Arcane Amplification.** While attuned, your spell save DC and spell attack bonus each increase by **+1**.  
- **Weave-Tuned Reflexes.** You have advantage on saving throws to maintain concentration on a spell.  
	- *If you are a **War Caster** or have **advantage** from another source add +2 to your **concentration** save.**(This effect does not stack with multiple instances of itself***
- **Spellweave Skin.** Once per long rest, when you are hit by a spell that targets only you, you can use your reaction to **halve the damage** or **dispel the spell** as if you had cast *[[counterspell-xphb|Counterspell]]* at 3rd level.
