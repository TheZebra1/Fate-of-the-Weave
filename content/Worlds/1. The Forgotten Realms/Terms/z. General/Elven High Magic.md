[8:58](https://youtu.be/jhYrfC4cogM?si=21AVm5tSjeml0wh9&t=538) ***N'Quor'Khaor*** - At least 9 [[Elvish High Mage]]s **permanently** **banish** an entity and **imprisons** them. *One of the only spells that can ONLY be broken by [[MYSTRA]], [[CORELLON]], or [[AO]]*. The bound entities powers and influence are removed from the world.
- The keys to unlocking the bindings are created along with the ritual and must be used at the binding site, not possible from a distance. *(Keys are usually arbitrary and impossible to satisfy like "Tears of [[Kossuth]]")*
- This is how "***Prophecies***" are created. The specific conditions become remembered as a prophecy passed down.
	- *"Ex. If the [[Kiira N'Vaelahr]] and [[Kiira N'Vita]] are united then the binding shall be broken"*
- **All Mages involved in the casting are killed, and more after that may be required to tribute their life as well. HP requirement.**
	- At least 400 HP is required for a Lesser Extraplanar
	- 800 HP for a Demi-god
	- 1200 HP for a Lesser god. (1000+ followers)
	- 1600 HP Intermediate god. (100,000+ followers)
	- 2000 HP for Greater gods. (1 million+ followers)
- Variant of this spell banished the [[drow]] from the surface, cursing them.
