In this world, certain precious metals and stones act as conduits for the Weave, enhancing or altering magical effects. Purer materials offer stronger connections to specific types of magic.

| **Material** | **Arcane Properties**  | **Resonance**                                                                                        | **Primary Location**                                                                                |
| ------------ | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gold**     | Universal Conductor    | Superior purity ensures stable magic flow.                                                           | **The Sunspire Mountains:** Rich veins of pure gold hidden deep within the peaks.                   |
| **Platinum** | Supreme Conduit        | Exceptional for transmutation and protective magics. Essential for high-level rituals and artifacts. | **The Frostpeak Mines:** A remote, frigid region where platinum is interwoven with ice crystals.    |
| **Silver**   | Life and Death Balance | Repels undead & lycanthropes                                                                         | **Silverlight Vale:** A misty valley with ancient silver mines said to be blessed by lunar deities. |

## ⚠️ Special Considerations:
- **Purity Matters:** Higher purity metals conduct magic more efficiently. Impurities can cause spell failures or unintended effects.  
- **Obsidian's Volatility:** While powerful, obsidian’s connection to time and psionics makes it unpredictable. Only skilled mages can safely channel its power.  
- **Stone Synergy:** Combining different stones with spells may create unique effects or increase their potency. For example, combining lapis lazuli and moonstone could create a healing barrier.
