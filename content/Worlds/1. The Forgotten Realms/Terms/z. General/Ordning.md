# 🗿 Giant Pantheon (Ordning) – Post-[[Annam]]
## Supreme Creator (Absent)
- **Annam the All-Father** *(missing/departed)*
  - Creator of the giants and the original source of the Ordning.
  - No longer present, leaving the pantheon in flux.

## Current Hierarchical Structure
### 🌩️ 1. **[[Stronmaus]]** – *King of the Pantheon (in Annam’s absence)*
- **Domain:** Sky, sun, weather, joy
- **Giants:** Storm & Cloud Giants
- **Notes:** Favored son of Annam; embodies leadership and power among the gods.

### 🌿 2. **[[Hiatea]]** – *Matron of Strength and Nature*
- **Domain:** Nature, agriculture, hunting, family
- **Giants:** Fire & Stone Giants
- **Notes:** A daughter of Annam; also worshipped by some humans.

### 🎭 3. **[[Memnor]]** – *Lord of Pride and Deceit*
- **Domain:** Pride, persuasion, subtlety
- **Giants:** Cloud Giants (especially those of a scheming nature)
- **Notes:** Charismatic and manipulative; rivals Stronmaus in influence.

### ❄️ 4. **[[Thrym]]** – *God of Cold and War*
- **Domain:** Ice, strength, winter
- **Giants:** Frost Giants
- **Notes:** Embodies conquest, cold, and brutal power.

### ⛰️ 5. **[[Skoraeus Stonebones]]** – *The Silent Watcher*
- **Domain:** Earth, stone, artistry, knowledge
- **Giants:** Stone Giants
- **Notes:** Reclusive and wise; rarely speaks, but deeply revered.

### 🪓 6. **[[Grolantor]]** – *Brute of the Wilds*
- **Domain:** Hunting, combat, gluttony
- **Giants:** Hill Giants, Ettins
- **Notes:** Dull-witted but strong; feared more than respected.

## Notes
- The hierarchy mirrors the social stratification of giants in the Ordning.
- Stronmaus is the de facto leader while Annam is absent.
- Each deity retains authority over their own sphere and giant followers.
