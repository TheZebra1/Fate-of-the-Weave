# The Mark of Annam
![[Mark of Annam.png|center|350]]
[[Mark of Annam.png|Show]]
> *"When the All-Father could no longer guide us, he left behind no commands—only sparks."*  
> — Fragment from a lost [[Rune-Song of Ostoria]]

The **Mark of Annam** is a divine symbol—an ancient spark of the All-Father's fading power—placed within chosen descendants of giantkind. It is not a tattoo, scar, or visible rune, but a **living brand of divine purpose**, woven into the soul and passed down across generations.

There were once **five Marks**, each representing a fragment of [[Annam]]’s divine agency in the world after his punishment. Now, **only one remains**.

## Origin
After the rejection of his son [[Hartkiller]], and before his binding within the [[Spine of the World]] by [[Ao]], Annam created the Marks as a **final tether to the world**. They were meant to preserve his legacy, empower the worthy, and offer the chance—however slim—that giantkind could one day restore what was lost.

## Traits of the Mark
- The Mark chooses **giants or giantkin** at birth—it cannot be transferred or forged.  
- When a bearer **dies naturally**, the Mark eventually **reincarnates** in a newborn somewhere in the world.  
- When a bearer is **killed**, the Mark is **extinguished forever**—a flame snuffed out.

## Significance
To bear the Mark is to carry a divine ember of [[Ostoria]]'s legacy.

It grants more than power—it marks the bearer as a potential **heir to the All-Father**, a bridge between the past and future of giantkind. Though the bearer may not feel different at first, **ancient magic**, **forgotten runes**, and **divine places** will recognize the Mark... and sometimes, **respond** to it.

## Current Bearer

The last known bearer of the Mark is [[Korrak Stonebreaker|Korrak]], a goliath from the [[Spine of the World]]. Though unaware of his full significance, his existence alone **maintains Annam’s last tether to the world**.

***If the Mark is lost, so too is the All-Father’s ability to ever return as a Deity of the Giants.***
