# ("Test in Her Honor")

## Overview
Elliya Oloth (*El-LEE-ya Oh-LOTH*), meaning "Test in Her Honor," is a sacred and brutal dueling tradition in drow society. This ritual, always to the death, is invoked to resolve disputes, defend honor, or assert dominance. While public duels are rare in drow culture, Elliya Oloth stands apart as an audacious act, inherently tied to Lolth’s chaotic and cruel nature.

## Purpose
- **Honor**: To settle disputes where intrigue or subterfuge has failed.
- **Faith**: To offer bloodshed to [[Lolth]] as a sign of devotion.
- **Dominance**: To demonstrate power and strength in a public spectacle.

## Ritual Process
1. **Challenge**: The challenger invokes the duel with the words:
   *“By Lolth’s will, I call for Elliya Oloth. Let her name witness our trial.”*
   - This is a brazen declaration, as it risks public shame or worse if the challenger fails.
2. **Conditions**: A priestess of Lolth oversees the duel, setting:
   - Weapons (e.g., daggers, whips, or magic).
   - Location (often in a ceremonial chamber or temple arena).
3. **Blood Offering**: Both combatants spill their blood on a web-like sigil to symbolize their devotion and willingness to serve Lolth, even in death.
4. **The Duel**: The combatants fight to the death. No rules exist beyond survival.
5. **Outcome**:
   - **If the Defender Loses**: They are killed outright, their weakness punished with death.
   - **If the Challenger Loses**: Their failure is met with humiliation:
     - Their tongue is cut out, rendering them unable to cast spells with verbal components.
     - They are enslaved to the victorious defender, marked as subjugated and disgraced.
     - This punishment ensures that issuing a challenge is as dangerous politically as it is physically.

## Cultural Significance
- **Religious Importance**: Elliya Oloth is sacred to Lolth, who is believed to watch these duels. Victors are often seen as favored by the Spider Queen.
- **Social Risks**: Issuing a challenge is a public declaration of power. Failure brings humiliation, subjugation, and a lifetime of suffering.
- **Cruelty and Cunning**: The system ensures that only those with absolute confidence in their superiority will risk invoking Elliya Oloth. The punishment for failure reflects the drow ethos of dominance and control.

## Notes
- The punishment of challengers emphasizes the drow disdain for public displays of weakness. The act of calling Elliya Oloth is seen as an audacious gamble, where only the cunning and strong thrive.
- Priestesses of Lolth play a pivotal role, not only officiating the duel but ensuring the consequences align with Lolth’s chaotic will.
