### Overview
The **Tablets of Fate** are ancient, divine artifacts created by the Overgod **[[Ao]]** to maintain the cosmic order of the multiverse. These tablets define the boundaries and fundamental laws governing the planes, ensuring that no single realm can dominate, merge, or collapse another. The Tablets were famously broken during the **[[Time of Troubles]]**, causing widespread chaos until they were eventually restored.

### Hidden Subtext
A lesser-known subtext within the **Tablets of Fate** reveals a critical safeguard: any attempt to merge, alter, or collapse a plane of existence will automatically trigger the creation of a **[[Planar Guardian]]**. These entities are manifestations of the cosmic balance, designed to counteract any force that threatens the separation of the planes. The intensity and power of the summoned Guardian are proportional to the scale of the planar breach, making larger disruptions far more dangerous.

### Historical Impact
The presence of the **Tablets of Fate** has been a key factor in several major events in [[Faerûn]]'s history, most notably:
- **[[The Sundering]]**: The reshaping of [[Toril]] and [[Abeir]] led to the appearance of Planar Guardians, summoned to stabilize the newly formed realms.
- **The Time of Troubles**: During this chaotic period, breaches in planar boundaries caused multiple Guardians to appear, driven by the need to restore cosmic balance.
