## Overview
Spell duels are a formalized method for mages to settle disputes or prove their magical prowess in a controlled environment. These duels allow participants to showcase their skills without causing serious harm.

## General Rules
1. **Participants**: Duels are held between two spellcasters.
2. **Setting**: Duels take place in a designated arena under the supervision of faculty or experienced mages.
3. **Spectators**: A limited number of spectators may observe, provided they maintain silence and do not interfere.

## Duel Structure
- **Preparation Phase**:
  - Participants have a 10-minute preparation period before the duel begins to select their spells and strategize.

- **Duel Duration**: 
  - Each duel lasts until a participant concedes, is bound for long enough that they could be executed were the duel lethal, or is rendered unconscious*(Usually one round, but conditions may apply)*.

## Spellcasting Rules
1. **Spell Selection**:
   - Both participants must choose their spells before the duel begins. Only spells they are familiar with may be used.
   - Lethal spells are prohibited.

## Win Conditions
- **Concession**: A participant may concede at any time, acknowledging the other's superiority.
- **Victory**: A participant wins if they incapacitate or bind their opponent without causing serious harm.

## Post-Duel Protocol
- **Respectful Conduct**: Participants must show respect to each other regardless of the outcome.
- **Prize Wager**: If a prize or wager is involved, the winner may claim it immediately following the duel.

## Special Considerations
- **Judges**: An appointed judge will oversee the duel, ensuring compliance with rules and making decisions on disputes.
- **Magical Interference**: Outside magical influences or assistance during the duel are strictly forbidden and may result in disqualification.
