## What is Karsite?
*Weapon (any), rare (requires attunement by a non-spellcaster of Mystra's [[Weave]])*
![[Karsite.png|250]]
Forged or infused with crystalline fragments harvested from a place flowing with [[Karsite Weave]], such as the [[Plaguewrought Land]]s, a **karsite** item hums with anti-magical resonance. These rare materials disrupt the natural flow of the [[Weave]] and are despised by spellcasters across [[Faerûn]].
>Antithesis of [[Aetherium]]

### Karsite (Weapon or Ammunition Modifier)
When a weapon or piece of ammunition is modified with karsite, it gains the following properties:
- **Spellripper.** When you hit a creature ***concentrating*** on a spell, that creature makes its Constitution saving throw to maintain concentration with ***disadvantage***.
- **Antimagic Sting.** If you hit a creature with this weapon and that creature is capable of casting spells via [[Mystra]]'s [[Weave]], it takes an additional **1d4** force damage. This extra damage increases to **2d4** if the creature is currently benefiting from an active spell (such as *mage armor*, *shield of faith*, or *haste*).
- **Consumptive Magic.** When you roll a **natural 20** on an attack roll with this weapon against a creature that can cast spells, the creature must succeed on a **DC 15 Constitution** saving throw or lose one **spell slot** of it's **lowest level**.

> **Cursed Material.** If a spellcaster attempts to attune to a karsite weapon, the attunement fails and the caster takes 4d6 psychic damage. Karsite weapons resonate in the presence of spellcasters, shedding dim crimson light in a 5-foot radius when within 30 feet of an active spell.

**Variant:** Karsite ammunition (*arrows, bolts, bullets*) is consumed on use but retains all the above properties.
- ***Ammunition does not require attunement.***

### Karsite (Armor Modifier)
*Armor (any), rare (requires attunement by a non-spellcaster of Mystra's Weave)*

This armor is inlaid with shimmering veins of karsite crystal, harvested from a place flowing with [[Karsite Weave]], such as the [[Plaguewrought Land]]s. The presence of karsite disrupts arcane flows, creating a defensive shell that interferes with spellcasting—both helpful and hazardous.

A creature wearing karsite armor gains the following benefits and drawbacks:
- **Arcane Wardbreaker.** While wearing this armor, you have advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
- **Weave Disruption.** If a creature you can see casts a spell within **30 feet** of you that **targets you**, you can use your **reaction** to force that creature to make a **Constitution** saving throw ***(DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier)***. On a failed save, the spell fizzles and has no effect, expending the spell slot. ***This property can be used once per long rest***.

> **Cursed Material.** Karsite armor cannot be worn or attuned to by a creature that can cast spells using [[Mystra]]'s [[Weave]]. If this type of caster attempts to attune to karsite armor, they take **6d6** psychic damage and are **blinded** until the end of their next turn. While within 10 feet of a spellcaster, the armor emits a low hum and thin, red energy fractures the air around it.

## Bloodline
>Nemesis of [[Aetherborn]]
>- [[Aether.Karsite Shadow Wars]]

Karsites are the mortal descendants of [[Karsus]], the archwizard whose attempt to ascend to godhood shattered the [[Netheril|Netherese]] Empire. Marked by their unnatural resistance to magic, Karsites cannot cast spells but instinctively absorb arcane energy, using it to enhance their physical prowess. Trained as [[mage-slayer-xphb|Mage Slayer]]s, they turned their curse into a weapon, waging a centuries-long shadow war against magic-wielders called the [[Aetherborn]]. Though thought extinct after the ***[[Aether.Karsite Shadow Wars#The Sundering of Shaethra (ca. 157 DR)|Sundering of Shaethra]]***, whispers of their bloodline persist in the veins of battle-hardened warriors and spell-resistant individuals across [[Faerûn]].
