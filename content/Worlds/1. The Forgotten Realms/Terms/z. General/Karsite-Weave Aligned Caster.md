# [[Karsite]]-Weave Aligned Caster
*A spellcaster who has converted to the Karsite Weave—a sheath of arcane dominance that overwrites the structures of the Weave and Shadow Weave alike.*

## Overview
The Karsite Weave is not a thread to be pulled or a path to be walked. It is a **membrane**, a **sheath**, a **rewriting** of magical law. Born from the failed godhood of Karsus and refined through the will of his spiritual successor, Karthus, the Karsite Weave offers spellcasters a brutal, sovereign form of magic—untethered to divine favor or latticework tradition.

To convert is to stand apart, to reshape the arcane as a tool of **ambition**, not balance. It offers power—but not without cost.

## Mechanical Changes
### **1. Arcane Supremacy**
*Your magic dominates other magical frameworks.*

- When you cast **dispel magic** or **counterspell** against a spell cast via the Weave or Shadow Weave, treat the spell as if cast using a spell slot **one level higher**.
- You have **advantage on saving throws against spells** cast by Weave- or Shadow-aligned casters.

### **2. Ambition-Fueled Focus**
*Concentration is not maintained through balance—it is imposed through sacrifice.*

- When you take damage while concentrating on a spell, you may choose to **automatically succeed** on your concentration check by losing HP equal to **twice the damage taken**.
- You must decide to use this ability **before** rolling a concentration save.
- You can use this ability **a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest**.

### **3. Arcane Dissonance**
*You are fundamentally incompatible with cooperative magic.*

- **Healing** spells cast on you by non-Karsite-aligned sources restore **reduced HP**, subtracting a number equal to your **total levels in Karsite-aligned casting classes**.
  - *Example: A 10th-level Karsite caster heals 15 HP from a spell that would normally heal 25.*

- When affected by **non-healing beneficial spells** from non-Karsite sources (e.g., *bless*, *shield of faith*, *haste*), you must choose one of the following for each spell:
  - **Halve its duration**
  - **Halve its mechanical effect** (rounded down)

- These penalties do **not** apply to Karsite-aligned magic, or to spells you cast on yourself.

#### Difficult Detection *(Most things are connected through the [[Weave]], you are not)* 
- Detecting others via [[list-spells-school-divination|Divination Spells]] is very difficult. Interactions are skewed and inaccurate often times.
- You are difficult to detect via [[list-spells-school-divination|Divination Spells]]. Interactions are skewed and inaccurate often times.

## Aesthetic & Flavor Notes
- Your presence creates a subtle **psychic pressure** on nearby casters. Mystran or Shadow-aligned casters instinctively find you **"wrong."**
- Arcane effects from you may appear as **crimson fractal lines**, **whispering echoes**, or **cold geometric sigils** burned into the air.

## Summary: The Karsite Trade

| Benefit                              | Cost                                    |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Dominate enemy casters               | Loss of support magic traditional magic |
| Reshape the rules of concentration   | Must bleed for magical endurance        |
| Become immune to arcane surveillance | Become isolated from divine/Weave magic |
