- **Magic Null Zone:**
  - Area with varying size where magic is nullified or destabilized.
  - Renders spells, wards, machinery, persons, or creatures with magical components unstable.

### Karsite Weave Surge Table (d20)
When a character casts a spell of **1st level or higher** within a [[Karsite Weave]] region, roll a d20.

| d20 | Surge Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-2 | **Spell Fizzles.** The spell is consumed but fails entirely. No effect.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 3   | **Arcane Rebound.** The spell targets the caster instead (or nearest ally if self-targeted).                                                                                                                                             |
| 4   | **Mana Drain.** The spell works, but the caster loses one additional spell slot of the same level (or next lowest if unavailable).                                                                                                       |
| 5   | **Veil Shatter.** Roll on the [Wild Magic Surge Table](wild-magic-zone-xdmg.md) (PHB or custom), regardless of class.                                                                                                                    |
| 6   | **Arcane Echo.** The spell duplicates itself and goes off a second time with random targets in range.                                                                                                                                    |
| 7   | **Reality Fracture.** The caster and all within 10 ft must make a DC 15 Wisdom save or become stunned until the end of their next turn.                                                                                                  |
| 8   | **Displacement Surge.** The caster is teleported 30 feet in a random direction.                                                                                                                                                          |
| 9   | **[[Mystra]]’s Lament.** A ghostly projection of Mystra appears, silently screaming. All creatures within 15 feet must make a DC 13 Wisdom save or be frightened until the start of their next turn.                                     |
| 10  | **Karsite Pulse.** All magic items within 30 feet temporarily deactivate for 1 round.                                                                                                                                                    |
| 11  | **False Flow.** The spell appears to work but its effect is delayed by 1 round. No one realizes this until the delay passes.                                                                                                             |
| 12  | **Corrupted Channeling.** The caster takes 2d6 force damage as raw magic burns through their nervous system.                                                                                                                             |
| 13  | **Eye of Ambition.** The caster has a brief vision of [[Karthus]] and must make a DC 14 Charisma save or suffer the [Grappled](Conditions.md#Grappled) for 1 minute (can retry each round).                                              |
| 14  | **Surge of Insight.** The spell works normally and the caster gains advantage on their next Intelligence or Arcana check.                                                                                                                |
| 15  | **Sympathetic Surge.** The spell is redirected to a new target, but the caster can choose.                                                                                                                                               |
| 16  | **Magical Gravity.** The spell's effects are altered: area spells pull rather than push, and projectiles curve unnaturally. DM determines effect.                                                                                        |
| 17  | **Weaveburn.** The caster’s veins glow red. They gain resistance to spell damage for 1 minute and their spells damage is doubled, but all spells cast during this time deal **1d6** psychic damage to the caster **per level of spell.** |
| 18  | **Silent Weave.** The spell functions at one level higher automatically expending a lower level spell slot *(Player's choice of slot)*                                                                                                   |
| 19  | **Karsite Echo.** The spell functions at one level higher (without using a higher slot)                                                                                                                                                  |
| 20  | **Weave Surge Mastery.** The spell functions at one level higher (without using a higher slot), and the caster may cast one more prepared or known spell for free this turn.                                                             |
