# **Rune Brand of Exile (Cursed Condition)**
**Type:** Magical Curse  
**Trigger:** Entering a forbidden city or remaining within its walls  
**Effect:** Immediate magical retribution upon entry and persistent burning pain over time.  
## **Overview**
The **Rune Brand of Exile** is an ancient and powerful magical punishment used by various city-states across the Forgotten Realms to permanently banish criminals, traitors, and those deemed enemies of the realm. Those branded with this magic suffer searing pain and eventual death if they remain within the city's walls for too long. 

#### ***Cities known to use this branding include:***
- **[[Waterdeep]]**, where the [[Lord’s Court]] punish high crimes against the city with exile enforced by arcane means.
- **[[Baldur's Gate]]**, where the [[Parliament of Peers]] may brand dangerous dissidents.
- **[[Neverwinter]]**, where Lord Neverember’s enforcers use it against traitors.
- **[[Calimport]]**, where the [[Pasha]]s brand former assassins who betray their masters.
- **[[Elturel]]**, where the [[Hellriders]] have been known to exile criminals with this brand.

## **Game Mechanics**
### **Retribution upon Entering the Forbidden City**
- When a branded individual crosses into the city's walls, the rune flares violently, dealing an immediate **6d6 fire damage** (no save).  
- The branded creature must make a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw**:
  - On a failure, they are **Stunned** for 1 minute.
  - On a success, they suffer only searing pain but no additional effects.
- This effect triggers **every time** they re-enter after leaving.

### **Persistent Pain Effect**
- Once inside the city, the brand continues inflicting pain at increasing intervals.
- While outside the city, the effect persists at a slower rate.

| **Time & Location** | **Effect** |
|---------------------|------------|
| **Inside the forbidden city (Every 10 minutes)** | 2d6 fire damage |
| **4+ hours inside the city** | Increases to 4d6 fire damage every 10 minutes. |
| **8+ hours inside the city** | Increases to 6d6 fire damage every 10 minutes. |
| **Outside the city (Every 30 minutes)** | 2d6 fire damage |

- If the branded individual reaches **0 HP from this effect**, they cannot be stabilized by non-magical means.
- If they remain in the city for **24 consecutive hours**, the brand ignites in a final, lethal surge, instantly killing them.

### **Additional Features**
- **Magical Tracking:**  
  - While within the city’s limits, authorities can sense the branded individual's presence via *Locate Creature* at will.
  - Arcane enforcers may possess higher-tier divinations (such as *Scrying*) to track them.

- **Suppression & Removal:**  
  - *[[remove-curse|Remove Curse]]* or *[[dispel-magic|Dispel Magic]]* can **suppress** the brand for **1 hour**, but cannot remove it.
	- Dispelling magic on the curse, alerts the forbidden city that the connection is *offline*, but beyond that their ability to automatically [[scrying|scry]], [[locate-creature|locate]], or otherwise monitor the branded creature is suspended for the duration.
  - Permanent removal requires:
    - A **high-level ritual** performed by an archmage or divine authority.
    - **Divine intervention** from a god of freedom, redemption, or trickery.
    - A **quest of atonement** assigned by an authority of the city.
