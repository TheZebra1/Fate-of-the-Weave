# The Hand of Thay
## Overview
The **Hand of Thay** is a position of immense prestige and mystery within the annals of Thayan history. Known only to the highest-ranking Red Wizards, the Hand is an unparalleled agent of Szass Tam, the lich-lord and supreme ruler of Thay. Tasked with duties of the utmost significance, the Hand serves as an extension of Szass Tam's will, wielding the power to act across the realms in pursuit of Thay's three paramount objectives:  

1. **The Gathering of Knowledge**  
2. **The Execution of Enemies**  
3. **The Eternal Survival of Thay**  

## Origins and Purpose
The Hand of Thay is said to be a singular entity, appointed by Szass Tam himself. Unlike other agents or Red Wizards, the Hand is believed to embody a mastery of all three core tenets of Thay’s grand design. This unique versatility allows the Hand to rise above all other operatives, serving as the ***ultimate enforcer*** of Thay's interests.

It is written that the Hand is called upon only in times of great need, when even the combined strength of the Zulkirs cannot accomplish Szass Tam's designs. Once an individual ascends to this role, they are granted unparalleled authority, second only to Szass Tam, to carry out their master's will.

## Records of the Hand
Historical records suggest the position of the Hand has existed for over a millennium, and many Hand's have been appointed over the years. Curiously, no hand has ever been dismissed, killed or publicly relinquished of their position.  Yet at pivotal moments in history [[Szass Tam]] makes a grand display of naming a new ***Hand***. 

Descriptions of the Hand vary, with accounts speaking them has having shifting identities across eras. This has led to speculation among scholars about whether there ***really*** is only ***one*** Hand of Thay at a time.

The role of the Hand, is shrouded in secrecy, only Szass Tam himself knows the full truth of the Hand’s origins and purpose.

## Legacy
The Hand of Thay stands as a symbol of Szass Tam's brilliance and ruthlessness. Whether the role is fulfilled by one person or many across the centuries, its impact on the survival and supremacy of Thay is undeniable. In the eyes of Thayan scholars, the Hand serves as a reminder that loyalty, power, and sacrifice are the keystones of Thay's enduring legacy.
