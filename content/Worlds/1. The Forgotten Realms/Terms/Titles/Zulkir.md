## Council of Zulkirs of Thay – Late 15th Century DR
### [[Szass Tam]] – [[Necromancy]] & High Regent of [[Thay]]
- **Appearance:** Desiccated lich in crimson robes; sunken red-glow eyes
- **Personality:** Cold, methodical, plays the long game across centuries
- **Specific Goal:** Find the [[Nether Scrolls]] & create spell that can turn all of Toril into an undead wasteland under his complete domination, along with an enormous controlled sector for farming ***humanoids*** as a continuous re-supply for undead agents.
- **Faction:** [[The Black Flame Cabal]] – inner circle of elite necromancers loyal only to Tam
- **Agent/Servant:** [[Thaladra One-Eye]], a banshee assassin who serves as Tam’s voice in courts and councils
- **Reward:** [[Mark of Unlife]] – a necromantic brand that prevents aging and allows the wearer to [[animate-dead-xphb]] ***(5th-level-1/day without spell slots)***
### [[Akhara Nihil]] – [[Conjuration]]
- **Appearance:** Pale skin veined with silver; eyes like black mirrors
- **Personality:** Curious to the point of recklessness, fascinated by extraplanar ecosystems
- **Specific Goal:** Open a permanent gateway to the Far Realm to harness the raw potential of its alien matter. She needs a stable anchor point in the Prime Material Plane—ideally deep under [[Eltabbar]], where planar boundaries thin near the prison of [[Eltab]].
- **Faction:** [[The Crystal Mandible]] – conjurers and planar researchers seeking to tap forbidden realities
- **Agent/Servant:** [[Kyrion Suth]], an aaracokra void-channeler mutated by exposure to Far Realm spores
- **Reward:** [[Ossuary Seed]] – a palm-sized, living glyph that can summon a skeletal guardian from any corpse within 60 ft, once per long rest

### [[Sirikhan]] – [[Abjuration]]
- **Appearance:** Tall, armored, faceless mask of polished gold
- **Personality:** Rigid, paranoid, devoted to the defense of Thay's magical supremacy
- **Specific Goal:** Finish building the [[Aegistower]], a massive arcane defense array capable of sealing Thay against extraplanar intrusion. Needs a set of ancient mythal-infused sigils stolen from ruined [[Myth Ondath]].
- **Faction:** [[The Warded Hands]] – elite abjurers who protect Thayan artifacts, borders, and secrets
- **Agent/Servant:** [[Master Stoneheart]], a stone golem imbued with sentience and ancient runes, acts as Sirikhan's enforcer
- **Reward:** [[Sigil of Null]] – allows the bearer to suppress all teleportation and planar travel within 60 ft, usable once per day

### [[Damond]] – [[Divination]]
- **Appearance:** Young with silver irises; glows slightly in moonlight
- **Personality:** Mysterious, gentle tone, always speaks in calculated vagueness
- **Specific Goal:** Complete the [[Chronovault]], a device that can predict not just possible futures but absolute certainties. Missing a piece called the [[Thirteenth Eye]], once worn by a long-dead Netherese prophet.
- **Faction:** [[The Quiet Hall]] – oracular seers who rarely speak aloud, communing instead through magical dreamstones
- **Agent/Servant:** [[Ilari Vos]], a changeling spy who secretly infiltrates other Zulkirs' courts under multiple identities
- **Reward:** [[Glimpse of Infinity]] – a single use boon that allows the PC to declare the outcome of one roll, spell, or scene (DM-approved), once per campaign arc

### [[Dar'lon Ma]] – [[Enchantment]]
- **Appearance:** Perfectly symmetrical face; eyes sparkle unnaturally
- **Personality:** Disarming, narcissistic, passive-aggressive mastermind
- **Specific Goal:** Seize mental control of the [[Tharchion Council]] using a new ritual that weaves permanent enchantment into bureaucratic contracts. Requires the lost [[Sigil of Consent]]—rumored to be in the possession of a rogue paladin exiled to [[Narfell]].
- **Faction:** [[The Velvet Glove]] – a secret society of courtiers, artists, and bards acting as charm-speakers and seducers
- **Agent/Servant:** [[Mirrel Dane]], a former Cormyrean noble turned enchantress spy, currently embedded in the Eltabbar court
- **Reward:** [[Chalice of the Second Voice]] – lets the drinker cast *suggestion* once per day without components or verbal component

### [[M’Weru]] – [[Evocation]]
- **Appearance:** Bronze skin, flame-shaped tattoos, fiery eyes
- **Personality:** Proud, quick-tempered, theatrical
- **Specific Goal:** Create a sentient firestorm that can “cleanse” undead from Thay’s outer districts, challenging Tam’s grip. Needs the [[Heart Coal]] from a fire primordial long-buried beneath the [[Yuirwood]].
- **Faction:** [[The Ember Sash]] – evokers obsessed with divine fire and destructive purification
- **Agent/Servant:** [[Ashbinder Vuros]], a flame genasi war-cleric loyal to [[Kossuth]], embedded within the Temple of the Flaming Brazier
- **Reward:** [[Cindercall Gauntlet]] – enchanted bracer that casts *fireball* as a bonus action once per day and lights any weapon with magical flame for 1 hour

### [[Hlarkus Baltreyo]] – [[Illusion]]
- **Appearance:** Constantly shifting appearance; only eyes remain constant (deep violet)
- **Personality:** Snarky, sinister, loves layers of secrets and falsehoods
- **Specific Goal:** Craft the [[Veil of Realms]], a metaphysical spell-structure that lets him place an entire city into a false reality. Needs the psychic essence of 1,000 sleeping minds bound within the [[Tapestry of Slumber]].
- **Faction:** [[The Gilded Veil]] – illusionist cabal that manipulates dreams, memories, and entire historical records
- **Agent/Servant:** [[Qell Varn]], a doppleganger playwright and memory-forger whose “shows” rewrite real memories in the minds of audiences
- **Reward:** [[Mask of Many Faces (True)]] – a unique magical mask that lets the wearer assume any form, voice, and even alignment aura, undetectable by *true seeing*
