## *"The Restorer"*
### The Breaking of [[Sythrak Gurthanor]]
> “I almost let it go. I almost let *them* go. But the world does not show restraint, and neither shall I.”

- **Exile and Wanderings**: Sythrak, disillusioned with the [[Order of the Arcane Blade]], leaves and roams [[Faerûn]] seeking purpose.

- **Return to [[Evermeet]]**: He reluctantly returns to Evermeet, expecting disappointment but finds unexpected beauty and tradition.

- **Temporary Peace**: Sythrak integrates into Evermeet's society, mentoring young elves and almost finding contentment.

- **Catalyst for Change**: A personal tragedy—a final message from his mentee, ***Elrienna***, who perished in Faerûn—shatters his peace.

- **Renewed Resolve**: Feeling betrayed by Evermeet's inaction, Sythrak departs, vowing to restore elven prominence in Faerûn.

- **Transformation**: He becomes **Envinyatar,** "The Restorer," determined to lead elves back to Faerûn as conquerors, not refugees.

# Evermeet – The Night Everything Changed

The moonlight poured through the open balcony, silver against the polished marble of Evermeet’s great halls. The air smelled of sea spray and night-blooming jasmine, a place untouched by war, untouched by ruin.

And yet, Sythrak Gurthanor could feel it creeping in.

A messenger stood before him, silent, hesitant.

**“My lord… it is from Faerûn.”**

Those words chilled him.  
It had been years since Elrienna left. He had told himself she would return, that she would see her folly, that she would—

He took the scroll with careful hands.  
The seal was broken. Rushed. Urgent.

His breath came slow, even as the world tilted beneath his feet.

---

## The Final Message

The ink was smudged. Bloodstained.  
The hand that wrote it had trembled.

He read:

> “I tried, my lord. I tried to do as you taught me. To be swift, to be sharp, to be decisive.”  
>  
> “But they were too many.”  
>  
> “I was fast. I was strong. But I was alone.”  
>  
> “Evermeet will not send aid. They do not care. They do not believe we belong here anymore.”  
>  
> “So I must ask you—”  
>  
> **“Why did you teach me, if not to act?”**

The message ended there.  
No farewell.  
No request for vengeance.  
Only a question, cutting sharper than any blade.

Sythrak’s fingers clenched around the parchment.  
Slowly. Silently.  

It crushed beneath his grip as his breath left him in a shuddered exhale.

The messenger shifted, uneasy. *“My lord…?”*

Sythrak did not respond.  
He turned from the balcony. From the hall. From the illusion of paradise.

---

## The Silent Gathering
> “But the world did not stop. It did not become kinder. It only forgot us.”
He did not mourn.  
Mourning was for those who accepted loss.

Instead, he moved.

He walked the halls of his Evermeet residence, a place he once called home, and gathered his things.
- **His [[Nepenthe]]**, untouched for years, now felt heavy with purpose.
- **His cloak**, folded at the foot of his bed, settled around his shoulders like a shroud.
- The **sigil of Evermeet** carved into the arch above his doorway, a mark of shame.

Step by step, he descended into the darkened streets, unseen, unheard.  
Not a single soul stopped him.
They coul not feel what he felt.  
But in time they would all understand 

---

## The Final Look Back

At the edge of the harbor, where the sea met the stars, Sythrak paused.  
He turned his head, looking back at Evermeet.

This was supposed to be the last great sanctuary of the elves.

But it was not a sanctuary.  
It was a tomb for the living.

His hands curled into fists.  
*"And so be it,"* he thought.  

*"If they would rather die on this isle, let them. But I will not. I will not let her die for nothing."*

He stepped onto the waiting ship.  
Not once did he look back again.

---

# The Death of Sythrak Gurthanor, and the Birth of "Envinyatar"

That night, he ceased to be the man Evermeet had allowed him to become.  
The man who had almost let go.  
Who had almost forgotten.

No longer.

From the shadows of exile, he would rise.  
From the ashes of his greatest mistake, he would build a new order, a new war, a new world.

The time of **Envinyatar,** "*The Restorer,*" had come.

---

## Final Thought

Elrienna never asked for vengeance in her final words.  
She only asked him why he had taught her to fight, only to tell her not to act.

She did not ask for him to wage war.  
She did not ask him to burn Faerûn.  
She did not ask him to force Evermeet back into the world.

**But that is what he will do.**  

Because it is all he has left.  
Because he cannot undo what has been done.  
Because he refuses to let her death be meaningless.

And so, the fire that had once died in him is reborn.  
And this time, nothing will put it out.
