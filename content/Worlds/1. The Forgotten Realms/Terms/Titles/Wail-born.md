# Wail-born
## Overview
**Summary**:  
The term **Wail-born** refers to individuals born during or shortly after the **[[Wailing Death]]** plague of 1372 DR in [[Neverwinter]]. Though many died during childbirth or infancy due to exposure, those who survived are widely believed to carry **residual magical signatures** from the plague’s lingering necrotic aura. Now in their early 20s to 30s, the Wail-born form a quiet, unsettled generation within the city—marked by both suspicion and reverence.

## Common Traits
- **Psychic Instability**: Prone to dreams, night terrors, or hearing voices when near sites of death
- **Arcane Echoes**: Untrained magical flares, especially tied to necromancy, abjuration, or illusion
- **Resistance to Disease or Magical Sickness**: Many Wail-born are naturally immune to minor plagues
- **Unusual Auras**: Divine casters report a “blurring” when scanning Wail-born with divination magic

## Social Standing
- **Mistrusted by Elders**: Some view Wail-born as cursed or “half-dead” due to their connection to the plague
- **Quietly Feared**: Rumors spread that some Wail-born can communicate with the dead—or even the plague itself
- **Used by Factions**: Some groups, such as secret cabals or state sorcerers, recruit Wail-born for their unique magical potential

## Cultural Impact
- A minor cult known as **The [[Silent Choir]]** reveres Wail-born as prophetic remnants of the “Weeping Spell”
- Many Wail-born wear ***bone, jet, or plague-etched silver charms*** to suppress their symptoms or hide their origin
- Bards and playwrights often portray Wail-born as tragic figures caught between life and death

## Variant Wail-born Features (for PCs or NPCs)
*These may be used as background traits, bloodline quirks, or DM permission-based feats:*
- **Plague-Touched Resilience**: Advantage on saves vs. disease and magical exhaustion
- **Necrotic Feedback**: When targeted by a necromancy spell, roll a d6—on a 6, it rebounds harmlessly
- **Whispers of the Wail**: Once per long rest, cast *Speak with Dead* without components (target must have died during plague years)

## Rumors & Legends
- Some believe Wail-born are living anchors for **the soul-echoes left behind by the Wailing Death**
- It’s whispered that in times of high emotion, Wail-born can involuntarily summon **plague wraiths**
- A masked noble in Neverwinter is said to secretly fund Wail-born orphanages, possibly to train them—or study them

## Plot Hooks
- A Wail-born NPC begins hearing messages that lead to an **unsealed crypt** from the plague’s origin
- An arcane guild wants to **harvest Wail-born blood** for experimental spellcraft
- A PC or contact discovers they are Wail-born… and something is calling them from beneath the city
