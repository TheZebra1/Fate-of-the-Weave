### Key Level-Specific Features
You have joined the ***[[Order of the Arcane Blade]]***, a prestigious enclave of spellcasters that infuse their hands with arcane magic and can channel it into Arcane Weapons. 
### **Level 1: Spellcasting** 🧙
- Prepare *Wizard* spells.
- Wisdom is your spellcasting ability 🧠.
- You have undergone a ritual that causes arcane magic to flow directly through your hands; all spells you cast are touch range ✋
- **Touch Spells**: If you use a spell with a **range of touch**, the spell can be delivered along with an "**Unarmed Strike**" 🥊 causing it to deal physical damage plus spell effects.
- **Non-Touch Spells**: These spells are *converted* to **single-target** spells and can be delivered along with an "**Unarmed Strike**" causing it to deal physical damage plus spell effects.
	- <small> (*Spells with an area effect are projected behind the target in a cone; range of cone is equal to effect range indicated in the spell; DM judicates odd interactions; **Ex. Thunderwave = 15ft cone extends behind target when struck*** ) <big>

#### 📖 Changing Prepared Spells:
- After a long rest, you can change your prepared spells by spending ***1 hour*** **studying** your spellbook 📚 and **practicing** the movements 🧘‍♂️ and techniques associated with casting your spells. This allows you to adapt your magical abilities to the challenges you might face.

### Level 1: Weapon Mastery, Sunblade 🏅
#### Weapon Mastery
- You may use your Wisdom modifier for melee attack and damage rolls, instead of Strength or Dexterity.
- Choose 2 weapons to apply mastery properties.

#### [[Dawnblades]]
- It assumed that you have completed or are about to complete your "[[Order of the Arcane Blade#Rite of Passage|Rite of Passage]]" at the "[[Dawnspire]]" and therefore have completed or will complete the "Rite of Dawn's Embrace"
	- This will grant you the ability to wield a Sunblade, and you may gain access to one if the DM determines it is appropriate.
<small>*Sunblades are most often long swords, but it is not taboo for the order to have fashioned one of any melee weapon type(Besides weapons with the "Heavy" property) on your request.<big>

### Level 2: Fighting Style, Spell Surge**
#### Fighting Style  🥋
- Gain a Fighting Style feat.

####  Spell Surge ⚡
- At level 2, you gain **Spell Surge:** When activated, you may choose one of the following:
	- Convert one weapon attack you are about to make into a **[[Spellstrike]]**.
	- Regain a Level 1 Spell Slot.

### Level 3: Arcane Armament, Arcane Blade Fea, Blade Summon, Deflect Projectiles
You have become proficient at infusing your blade with arcane magic. **Once per [[short-rest-xphb|Short Rest]]**, when you take the ***Attack action***, you can use a ***bonus action*** to activate **[[Spellstrike]]** and infuse one of your melee attacks with a **spell** or **cantrip** without requiring the use of a **Spell Surge**.

- You can *attune* to a weapon over a short rest to make it an arcane focus ✨
- **Touch Spells**: If you use a spell with a **range of touch**, the spell is delivered along with a weapon attack ⚔️, dealing both weapon and spell damage, plus spell effects.
- **Non-Touch Spells**: These spells are *converted* to **single-target** spells and then delivered along with a weapon attack ⚔️, dealing both weapon and spell damage, plus spell effects.
	- <small> (*Spells with an area effect are projected behind the target in a cone; range of cone is equal to effect range indicated in the spell; DM judicates odd interactions;* 
		- *Ex. Thunderwave = 15ft cone extends behind target when struck* ) 
	- DM judicates odd interactions; 
*Ex. If Hunger of Hadar was somehow known then perhaps the DM may rule that it can be cast in a semi circle starting at the target.*<big>

> **Tip:** The **Bonus Action** applies to one strike. This means if you have **Extra Attack** 💥, only one of your weapon strikes can be empowered by **Spellstrike**, unless you use **Spell Surge** ⚡ to empower a second **Spellstrike**.

#### Blade Summon ✨
- As an action ⏱️ you can summon a [[Dawnblades]] to your hand if it is within 30 feet of you and not currently wielded by or attached to another living creature.
#### Deflect Projectiles
- As a reaction while **wielding a sun blade**. When a ranged projectile attack or spell attack roll hits you, and **no enemy is within 5 feet of you,** **reduce** the attack's total damage against you. The reduction equals 1d10 plus your **Wisdom** modifier and **Arcane Blade level**.
	- If you reduce the damage to 0, you can expend 1 spell slot to redirect some of the attack's force. If you do so, choose a creature  within **60 feet** of yourself that isn't behind Total Cover. That creature must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take damage equal to the total damage you reduced. The damage type is the same as what was directed at you.

### Level 8: Dimension Hop ✨
Once per **Short** or **Long** rest you may spend a ***bonus action*** to teleport up to **20 feet** to an unoccupied space you can see.  
- **Teleportation Distance**: This increases to **30 feet** at 14th level and **40 feet** at 18th level.
- **Optional Spell Slot Enhancement**: The first time you teleport in a round with this ability, you may choose to expend a spell slot for additional benefits:
  - **1st-level slot**: Gain advantage on the next weapon attack you make this turn.
  - **2nd-level slot**: You can cast a 1st-level abjuration spell you know as part of this bonus action.
  - **3rd-level slot**: Your teleportation leaves behind a burst of arcane energy, dealing 2d6 force damage ⚡ to all creatures within 5 feet of your original location **OR** adds 2d6 force damage to your next weapon attack.
  - **4th-level slot or higher**: You can teleport again once more before the end of your turn.
<small>Further upcasting adds an extra d6 of force damage per spell slot level to your next weapon attack.

You have reached the pinnacle of your arcane and martial training.

- You gain 2 Dimension Hops per **Short** or **Long Rest** 🔄.
- Your **Dimension Hop** grants resistance to all damage types until the start of your next turn when used with a spell slot of **4th level or higher**.

## **Arcane Blade Feats** 🎖️

### **Available at 4th Level**  
#### **Feat: Blade Ward Mastery** 🛡️  
You can cast **Blade Ward** as a bonus action once per short rest.

#### **Feat: Arcane Wardancer** 💃  
When you take the **Dash** action, you can cast a cantrip as a bonus action.  
- You may use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier.

#### **Feat: Arcane Defense** 🛡️  
When you are targeted by an attack, use your reaction to gain a +3 bonus to AC. If the attack misses, you gain temporary hit points equal to your **Wisdom** modifier. You can use this feature a number of times per day equal to your Wisdom modifier.  
- At **Level 10**, this ability merges with "[[Arcane Blade Class#Deflect Projectiles|Deflect Projectiles]]" and "[[Arcane Blade Class#**Level 10 Arcane Aegis** 🛡️|Arcane Aegis]]", stacking together which is called: **Superior Aegis**.  
	- **Superior Aegis:** A dodged attack using your Aegis will yield Temp HP equal to 5 + 2x your Wisdom modifier.  
	- This is a special reaction that can be taken at the same time as "[[Arcane Blade Class#Deflect Projectiles|Deflect Projectiles]]" and "[[Arcane Blade Class#**Level 10 Arcane Aegis** 🛡️|Arcane Aegis]]"

#### **Feat: Arcane Fist** (Path Feat) 🥊  
- **Prerequisite**: Your unarmed strikes now deal **1d4 + your Wisdom modifier** as force damage. Additionally, you gain proficiency with unarmed strikes if you don't already have it, and unarmed strikes count as magical for overcoming resistance or immunity.

#### **Feat: Bulwark of the Arcane** (Path Feat) 🏰  
- You gain proficiency with **heavy armor**. When you use **[[Spellstrike]]**, you can choose one friendly creature within 30 feet of you. That creature chooses either to gain temporary hit points equal to your **Wisdom** modifier (minimum 1) or add 1d4 elemental damage of their choice to their next weapon attack.

#### **Feat: Arcane Cavalier** (Path Feat) 🏇  
- You gain the Mounted Combatant Feat. You gain Animal Handling proficiency. In addition, when you use **[[Spellstrike]]** while mounted, you deal **extra force damage** equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1) on a hit.

### **Available at 8th Level**  
#### **Feat: Dimensional Warrior** ✨  
- Gain resistance to force damage.  
- **Dimension Hop**'s range is increased by an additional 10 feet.

#### **Feat: Mystical Parry** 🛡️  
When a creature you can see attacks you with a melee weapon, you can use your reaction to add your Wisdom modifier to your AC for that attack. If this causes the attack to miss, you can cast a spell targeting the attacker as part of the same reaction.

#### **Feat: Arcane Fist Mastery** (Path Feat) 🥊  
- **Prerequisite**: Arcane Fist, Level 8  
- When you [[Spellstrike]] with an unarmed strike, deal an additional **force damage** equal to 3 times the level of the spell slot spent. This damage counts as magical force damage.

#### **Feat: Arcane Sentinel** (Path Feat) 🛡️  
- **Prerequisite**: Bulwark of the Arcane, Level 8  
- You can now use **Dimension Hop** as a **reaction** to intercept damage for a creature within 20 feet of you. You teleport to an unoccupied space adjacent to the creature and take the damage in their place.

#### **Feat: Dimensional Rider** (Path Feat) 🏇  
- **Prerequisite**: Arcane Cavalier, Level 8  
- **Effect**: While mounted, your mount can teleport with you when you use **Dimension Hop**.

### **Available at 12th Level**  
#### **Feat: Battle Focus** 🧠  
You can concentrate on one additional spell. If you take damage, you must make a concentration check at disadvantage, losing both on a failure.

#### **Feat: Arcane Fist Ascendant** (Path Feat) 🥊  
- **Prerequisite**: Arcane Fist Mastery, Level 12  
- **Effect**: Your unarmed strikes now deal **1d6 + your Wisdom modifier** force damage. Additionally, once per short rest when you [[Spellstrike]] using an unarmed attack, you may force the target to make a save against your Spell Save DC or suffer the **blinded** or **deafened** condition for 10 turns, making a save at the end of each of their turns.

#### **Feat: Arcane Bastion** (Path Feat)🛡️  
- **Prerequisite**: Arcane Sentinel, Level 12  
- When you use your **[[Spellstrike]]**, you can expend a use of **Arcane Aegis** to project an **arcane shield** that grants you and all friendly creatures within 15 feet **resistance** to a damage type of your choice. The shield lasts until the start of your next turn.

#### **Feat: Arcane Battlecharger** (Path Feat) 🏇  
- **Prerequisite**: Arcane Warhorse, Level 12  
- **Effect**: When you [[Spellstrike]] while mounted, after moving more than 20 ft in a line, you may force a creature to make a saving throw against your Spell Save DC or become prone, if the target is already prone make an additional melee attack against that target. Additionally, if you are within reach of a beasts head you can now attempt to succeed on a DC 18 Animal Handling check to **mount a beast** regardless of the creature's temperament or training, and it will obey your commands until you dismount.

### **Available at 15th Level**  
#### **Feat: Arcane Overload** 🔋  
Once per day, you can spend a **Spell Surge** to infuse your next **[[Spellstrike]]** with two different spells you ***know***, using spell slots as if casting both spells. You cannot cast a leveled spell on your next turn.

### **Available at 20th Level**  
#### **Feat: Arcane Resonance** 🎶  
Once per short rest, when you use **Dimension Hop**, you can immediately cast any spell you ***know*** without the normal restrictions on **Non-Touch** spells. The spell must have a casting time of one action or bonus action.

#### **Feat: Steel Wind Strike** 🌀  
Once per day, as an action, you can activate this ability to attack as if casting the **Steel Wind Strike** spell.

#### **Feat: Bladesinger’s Elegance** ✨  
You gain an additional use of **Spell Surge** per short rest, and your **Spellstrike** critical hit range is increased to 19-20.
