The **Familiarist** class is a versatile class that allows players to build around their bond with magical familiars. Whether commanding **multiple familiars** as a swarm, **riding** a powerful mount, or **merging** with a familiar for enhanced abilities, the **Familiarist** offers a range of unique features. This class encourages strategic play and customization, making it ideal for players who want to create a deep connection with their summoned allies.![[Familiarist(Light).png|500]]![[Familiarist(Dark).png|500]]
## Class Features

### Level 1: **Spellcasting & Basics**
#### Spellcasting
- **Cantrips:**
  - You know **three cantrips** of your choice from the **Wizard** spell list. When you reach **levels 4** and **10**, you learn an additional cantrip. You can replace one cantrip you know with another from the Wizard spell list when you finish a long rest.
  
- **Training Manual:**
	- Your **Training Manual** contains the level 1+ spells you know. It starts with six level 1 Wizard spells of your choice.
	- When you gain a Familiarist level, add two Wizard spells to your **Training Manual**. These spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots.

- **Prepared Spells:**
	- Each of your **familiars** can train/prepare 4 spells. You can change your prepared spells after a long rest.

- **Spellcasting Focus:**
	- Your familiar acts as your arcane focus.
	- Without an active familiar, a Familiarist only has access to **cantrips** or non-Familiarist spells from other sources (e.g., Feats, Multiclassing) if they meet the requirements for those abilities.

- **Familiar Spellcasting Rules:**
	- As the Familiarist your connection is critical to your familiars and you must be within 100 feet to issue new orders to them and within 45 feet for them to cast a spell.
	- Any Familiar belonging to you that takes an **action** or a **bonus action** to cast a spell must consume ***your*** actions and spell slots to do so.
	- Familiars cast **touch-range** spells as part of a **melee attack**.
	- AoE spells originate from the target struck, adjusting their effect (e.g., cones, spheres).
	- The DM adjudicates unusual interactions.
	- Concentration checks are triggered by the familiar ***or*** the caster taking damage but the check is made by the caster/trainer.
#### Basics
- **Choosing A Familiar:**
	- Choose and name your first familiar from the “[[Familiarist Class#Familiar List & Special Actions (WORK IN PROGRESS)|Special Familiar List]]” or homebrew one with your DM. 
- **Summoning A Familiar:**
	- **Bonus Action:** Choose one of your owned familiars to appear within 30 feet of you at a designated location.
		- Summoning a familiar dismisses any previously summoned familiar *(Unless otherwise stipulated by subclass or DM)*
	  - **Fey or Fiend:** Familiars are either **Fey** or **Fiend** (player's choice).
	- **Dismissal:** If the Familiarist drops to 0 HP all of it's familiars are dismissed instantaneously. 
	  - Dismissing a familiar is a free action. To re-summon, it takes a bonus action. A familiar cannot be re-summoned on the same turn that it was dismissed.
- **HP:** All familiars starts with HP =(*Your Constitution Modifier*) + (*Your Spellcasting Modifier*)
	- Every Familiarist level after 1, your Familiar's Max HP increases by *(Your Spellcasting Modifier)*
		- When dismissed, a familiar retains its HP. If the familiar drops to 0 HP, it cannot be re-summoned until a **short rest** is completed.
- **Your HP**
	- As the Familiarist your connection to your familiars is so intense that you take half the damage your familiars take, as psychic damage.
### Level 2: **Run Away**
- **Reposition:** Once per long rest, after you are attacked or targeted by an enemy, regardless of whether the attack missed or not you may move up to half your movement speed in a desired direction. *(Unless you are under movement impairing effects)*
- **Evasion** Once per long rest, when you succeed on a saving throw that allows you to halve the damage, you can instead use a reaction to take no damage.  
  *(You must narrate how you evade the damage, if you cannot reasonably do so you must take the halved damage and the ability is still open for use later.)*

### Level 3: **Familiarist Subclass**
You gain a **Familiarist Subclass** of your choice. Choose from:
- **Mega**  
- **Swarm**  
- **Symbiote**  

You gain each of your subclass's features that are of your **Wizard level** or lower. Below are the features provided by each subclass at level 3:

#### **Mega Subclass:**
![[Familiarist(Mega).png|250]]
- **Familiar’s Size:** Your familiar's size becomes one size larger than yours.
- **Mounted Combat:** While mounted, both you and your familiar gain **+2 to AC and Saving Throws**.
	- While mounted your familiars "Special Actions" do not dismount you and you are included in any special movement or physics bending actions.
- **Familiar HP:** Your familiar’s max HP is equal to **Familiarist level × 4 + Constitution modifier**.
- **Hit Die Usage:** You can spend your **Hit Die** on any of your familiars during a short rest.
- **Short Rest HP:** All familiars automatically receive HP equal to your **Spellcasting modifier** on a short rest.

#### **Swarm Subclass:**
![[Familiarist(Swarm).png|250]]
- **Mass Summoning:** You may summon all of your familiars at once with a **bonus action**. This does not override the restriction of re-summoning familiars on the same turn or familiars with 0 HP.
- **Swarm Formation:** In combat, your familiars condense into a **swarm** and act as one unit.
- **Shared Abilities:** The entire swarm can use any abilities that the familiars in the swarm possess.
- **Cap Increase:** Gain 1 more familiar immediately upon gaining the **Swarm** subclass. Your familiar cap increases by 1 and continues to be 1 higher than normal for your career as a Swarm Familiarist.
![[Familiar Swarm.png]]
#### **Symbiote Subclass:**
![[Familiarist(Symbiote).png|250]]
- **Merge Ability:** As a bonus action, merge with your familiar. Keep your speed but gain its movement types (e.g., Fly, Climb).
	- You are able to use the "Special Actions" of your familiar while merged.
 - **Temporary HP:** While merged, you gain **temporary HP** equal to your familiar's HP.
- **Spellcasting:** You may cast your familiar's trained spells. All your spells are touch-range in the same manner as if the familiar was casting them.

### Level 6: **Subclass Feature**
#### **Mega Subclass:**
- **Battle Mount:** Once per long rest, your familiar can use the **Dodge** or **Disengage** action as a **bonus action**.
- You gain the **Mounted Combatant feat** (if you already have it, you may select a different feat).

#### **Swarm Subclass:**
- **Hive Mind:** As an action, you can see, hear, and act through all your familiars simultaneously. You gain **advantage on Perception and Investigation checks** while doing so.
  - You are incapacitated and unaware of your own senses while doing this, but taking damage will stop **Hive Mind**.

#### **Symbiote Subclass:**
- **Dual Mind:** Gain your familiar’s resistances while merged.
- While merged, you can concentrate on **two spells at once**. Concentration checks are required separately when taking damage.

---

### Level 7: **Familiar Gained**
- Familiar cap increased to **3**.

---

### Level 8: **Ability Score Improvement**
- You gain an **Ability Score Improvement feat** or another feat of your choice.

---

### Level 9: **2nd Evolution**
- Familiars evolve again.

---

### Level 10: **Subclass Feature**
At level 10, you gain additional subclass features:

#### **Mega Subclass:**
- **Rapid Return:** Use your bonus action to teleport back to your mount if separated, but within line of sight and not farther than 60ft.

#### **Swarm Subclass:**
- **Swarm Surge:** Once per short rest, you can command your swarm to use an additional ability as part of their attack.
- **Swarm Surround:** When the swarm uses an ability, it can attempt to grapple the target or a target within 5 feet of it. 
	- (*D20 + # of Familiars vs Target’s Athletics*)
- **Swarm Screen:** The swarm may move freely in and out of allied creatures' space; allies within the swarm’s space gain **half cover** against ranged attacks.

#### **Symbiote Subclass:**
- **Refreshing Unison:** While merged, you may use spell slots to replenish your **temporary HP**.
  - Temporary HP gained cannot exceed the familiar's max HP.

---

### Level 11: **Familiar Gained**
- Familiar cap increased to **4**.

---

### Level 12: **Ability Score Improvement**
- You gain an **Ability Score Improvement feat** or another feat of your choice.

---

### Level 13: **Evolution**
- One of your familiars may now use the **EVOLVED** portion of its stat-block.

---

### Level 14: **Subclass Feature**
At level 14, you gain additional subclass features:

#### **Mega Subclass:**
- **Guardian Mount:** If you are above 1 HP and sustain damage that would drop you to 0 hit points while mounted, transfer any excess damage to your familiar so that you are left with 1 HP.
- **Mega Rest:** Your familiar’s regain up to half their HP on a short rest.
  - Familiars cannot raise their HP above half of their max when healing in this way.

#### **Swarm Subclass:**
- **Helpful Swarm:** The swarm can now perform the **Help** action as a bonus action. (The Swarm cannot help itself.)
- **Collective Suffering:** When you or any familiar in your swarm is hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to re-distribute the damage across your swarm as you see fit.

#### **Symbiote Subclass:**
- **Perfect Symbiosis:** Once per long rest, while merged, you may cast **two spells** with a single action or spellstrike.
  - Additionally, you add your **Spellcasting modifier** to **Concentration Saving Throws**.

---

### Level 15: **Familiar Gained**
- Familiar cap increased to **5**.

---

### Level 16: **Ability Score Improvement**
- You gain an **Ability Score Improvement feat** or another feat of your choice.

---

### Level 17: **Evolution**
- A second familiar may begin using the **Evolved** portion of its stat-block.

---

### Level 18: **Spell Mastery**
- You have achieved such mastery over certain spells that you can cast them at will. Choose a level 1 and a level 2 spell in your school that have a casting time of an action.
  - You always have those spells prepared on every familiar, and you can cast them at their lowest level without expending a spell slot.
  - To cast either spell at a higher level, you must expend a spell slot.

---

### Level 19: **Epic Boon**
- You gain an **Epic Boon feat** or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.

---

### Level 20: **Signature Spells** & Familiar Gained
- Choose two level 3 spells from your **Training Manual** that you can cast once per day without expending a spell slot.
  - When you do so, you can't cast them in this way again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
  - To cast either spell at a higher level, you must expend a spell slot.

- **Familiar Gained:** Familiar cap increased to **6**.
