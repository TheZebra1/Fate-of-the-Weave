# Level 5 Bastion Facilities Catalog
## **Facility Key**  
### **Facility Space & Maximum Area**  
- **Cramped:** 4 ***5x5 squares***  
- **Roomy:** 16  ***5x5 squares***    
- **Vast:** 36  ***5x5 squares***    
### **Facility Space Cost & Time**  
| Space   | Cost   | Time Required |
|---------|--------|--------------|
| Cramped | 500 GP | 20 days      |
| Roomy   | 1,000 GP | 45 days    |
| Vast    | 3,000 GP | 125 days   |

### **Basic Facilities**  
- **Bedroom**  
- **Courtyard**  
- **Dining Room**  
- **Kitchen**  
- **Parlor**  
- **Storage**  

## Arcane Study
- **Prerequisite:** Ability to use an Arcane Focus or a tool as a Spellcasting Focus  
- **Space:** Roomy  
- **Hirelings:** 1  
- **Order:** Craft  
- **Benefits:**
  - **Arcane Study Charm:** After a Long Rest, gain a magical Charm that lasts for 7 days or until used. Allows casting *Identify* without expending a spell slot or using material components.
  - **Crafting Options:**
    - **Arcane Focus:** Takes 7 days, no cost.
    - **Book:** Takes 7 days, costs 10 GP.
    - **Magic Item (Arcana):** If level 9+, craft a **Common or Uncommon magic item** (requires Arcana proficiency and crafting time/cost).
## Armory
- **Prerequisite:** None  
- **Space:** Roomy  
- **Hirelings:** 1  
- **Order:** Trade  
- **Benefits:**
  - Stores weapons, armor, and ammunition.
  - **Trade: Stock Order:** Commissions hirelings to stock the Armory with 100 GP worth of weapons, armor, and ammunition.
  - **Bastion Defenders:** If stocked, provides benefits to the Bastion’s defenses.
  - **Enlargement:** Upgrading to a **Vast Armory** for 2,000 GP allows storage of more defensive equipment.
## Barrack
- **Prerequisite:** None  
- **Space:** Roomy  
- **Hirelings:** 1  
- **Order:** Recruit  
- **Benefits:**
  - Provides sleeping quarters for **Bastion Defenders**.
  - **Recruit Order:** Allows hiring Bastion Defenders.
  - Can have multiple Barracks to accommodate more Defenders.
  - **Enlargement:** A **Vast Barrack** (2,000 GP) houses more Defenders.
## Garden
- **Prerequisite:** None  
- **Space:** Roomy  
- **Hirelings:** 1  
- **Order:** Harvest  
- **Benefits:**
  - A Bastion can have multiple Gardens, each chosen from **Garden Types**.
  - **Harvest Order:** Hireling collects resources (7 days, no cost).
  - **Enlargement:** Can be upgraded to a **Vast Garden** for 2,000 GP, doubling output.

### **Garden Types**
| Type      | Description | Harvest Output |
|-----------|------------|----------------|
| **Decorative** | Aesthetic garden with flowers and topiaries | 10 floral bouquets (5 GP each), 10 vials of perfume, or 10 candles |
| **Food** | Garden of vegetables and mushrooms | 100 days' worth of rations |
| **Herb** | Rare medicinal herbs | 10 Healer’s Kits or 1 Potion of Healing |
| **Poison** | Toxic plants and fungi | 2 vials of Antitoxin or 1 vial of Basic Poison |

## Library
- **Prerequisite:** None  
- **Space:** Roomy  
- **Hirelings:** 1  
- **Order:** Research  
- **Benefits:**
  - **Research: Topical Lore:** Hireling spends **7 days researching** a topic of your choice (legend, event, location, person, creature, or famous object).
  - Provides **up to three accurate pieces of information** (DM determines details).
## Sanctuary
- **Prerequisite:** Ability to use a Holy Symbol or Druidic Focus as a Spellcasting Focus  
- **Space:** Roomy  
- **Hirelings:** 1  
- **Order:** Craft  
- **Benefits:**
  - Religious icons and a quiet place for worship.
  - **Sanctuary Charm:** After a Long Rest, gain a **magical Charm** that lasts for 7 days or until used. Allows casting *Healing Word* once without expending a spell slot.
## Smithy
- **Prerequisite:** None  
- **Space:** Roomy  
- **Hirelings:** 2  
- **Order:** Craft  
- **Benefits:**
  - Equipped with a forge, anvil, and tools for weapon and armor crafting.
  - **Craft Options:**
    - **Smith’s Tools:** Hirelings craft anything using Smith’s Tools (per *Player’s Handbook* rules).
    - **Magic Item (Armament):** If level 9+, craft a **Common or Uncommon magic item** from the Armaments table (requires Arcana proficiency).
## Storehouse
- **Prerequisite:** None  
- **Space:** Roomy  
- **Hirelings:** 1  
- **Order:** Trade  
- **Benefits:**
  - A **storage facility** for nonmagical trade goods (up to **500 GP** value).
  - **Trade: Goods Order:** Hirelings spend **7 days** either:
    - Procuring items (you cover costs).
    - Selling stored items.
  - **Trade Limit Increases:**
    - Level 9: **2,000 GP max**
    - Level 13: **5,000 GP max**
  - **Increased Selling Price:** Buyers pay more for your goods:
    - Level 5: **+10%**
    - Level 9: **+20%**
    - Level 13: **+50%**
    - Level 17: **+100%**
## Workshop
- **Prerequisite:** None  
- **Space:** Roomy  
- **Hirelings:** 3  
- **Order:** Craft  
- **Benefits:**
  - Equipped with **six different types of Artisan’s Tools** (chosen from a list).
  - **Craft Options:**
    - **Adventuring Gear:** Hirelings craft items based on the tools selected when the Workshop was added.
    - **Magic Item (Implement):** If level 9+, hirelings can craft a **Common or Uncommon magic item** from the Implements list.
  - **Source of Inspiration:** Spending a Short Rest in the Workshop grants **Heroic Inspiration** (once per Long Rest).
  - **Enlargement:** Upgrading to a **Vast Workshop** for 2,000 GP adds:
    - **Two additional hirelings.**
    - **Three additional Artisan’s Tools.**
