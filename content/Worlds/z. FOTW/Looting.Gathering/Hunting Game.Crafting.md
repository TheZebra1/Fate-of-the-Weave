>***Hunt Game***  
Food can be gathered with your choice of a **Dexterity** or **Wisdom** check. If you have **proficiency** with the **Survival** skill, you can add your survival modifier to the roll.  

>***Intentionally Slow***  
Slow hunting is a **time-gated** system. It is not intended to be the primary source of materials. Rather than being balanced against the loot tables, it’s balanced against the [[a.Downtime Activities]] (and consequently not particularly efficient).
