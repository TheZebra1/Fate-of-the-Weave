*Characters can **learn a language or gain tool proficiency** through dedicated training.*
## Resources
- Requires **at least 10 workweeks**.
- Training time is reduced in weeks by **Intelligence modifier** (minimum **10 weeks**).
- Costs **25 gp per workweek**.
## Complications
Each **10 workweeks of training** has a **10% chance of a complication**. Roll **d6**:

| d6  | Complication |
|----|-------------|
| **1** | **Instructor disappears**, losing **1 week**. * |
| **2** | Teacher uses **archaic methods**, drawing ridicule. |
| **3** | Teacher is a **spy** gathering intel on you. * |
| **4** | Teacher is a **wanted criminal**. |
| **5** | Teacher is a **cruel taskmaster**. |
| **6** | Teacher **asks for help** dealing with a threat. |

* *Might involve a rival.*
