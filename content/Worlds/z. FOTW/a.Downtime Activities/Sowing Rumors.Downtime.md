***Spreading rumors can elevate allies or ruin enemies, shifting public opinion. Rumors must be simple, concrete, and believable to be effective.***
## Process
- Requires **continuous downtime** in a settlement.
- Costs **1 gp per day** for socializing and drinks.
- At the end, roll **DC 15 Charisma (Deception or Persuasion) check**:
  - **Success:** Community shifts **1 step** toward friendly or hostile.
  - **Failure:** Rumor **fails**, and further attempts won’t work.
## Time Required
| Settlement Size | Time Required |
|----------------|--------------|
| **Village**    | 2d6 days     |
| **Town**       | 4d6 days     |
| **City**       | 6d6 days     |

### Additional Notes
- If a character **leaves mid-process**, the rumor **fades away**.
- Not everyone is affected—some individuals **retain personal opinions**.
