***Carousing is a downtime activity involving a workweek of food, drink, and socializing. Characters can carouse with:***

- **Lower-class** (10 gp): Criminals, laborers, mercenaries, town guard.
- **Middle-class** (50 gp): Guild members, spellcasters, officials.
- **Upper-class** (250 gp): Nobles, elite socialites (requires connections or Disguise Kit + Deception).

## 1. Carousing Check

After carousing, roll a **Charisma (Persuasion) check**:

| Check Total | Result |
|------------|--------|
| 1–5        | Hostile contact. |
| 6–10       | No new contacts. |
| 11–15      | 1 allied contact. |
| 16–20      | 2 allied contacts. |
| 21+        | 3 allied contacts. |

- **Hostile contacts** hinder the character but avoid direct harm.
- **Allied contacts** may offer aid but won’t risk their lives.
- Contacts can be **specific NPCs** or created on the spot.
## 2. Carousing Outcomes

After carousing, roll **d100 + Level** to determine a bonus result:

| Roll  | Result                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01–10 | **Jailed for 1d4 days** for disorderly conduct. Pay **10 gp** to avoid jail or resist arrest.                                                                                           |
| 11–20 | Wake up in a **strange place**, no memory, robbed of **3d6 × 5 gp**.                                                                                                                    |
| 21–30 | Roll on the **Carousing Complications** table.                                                                                                                                          |
| 31–40 | Choose **Make an enemy** or **Whirlwind romance** (roll d20):<br>**1–5:** Ends badly (possible new spiteful rival).<br>**6–10:** Ends amicably.<br>**11–20:** Ongoing (potential bond). |
| 41–80 | Increase the [[loyalty]] by 10 with one allied contacts you interacted with while carousing.                                                                                            |
| 81–90 | **Modest gambling winnings**, covering lifestyle costs + **1d20 × 4 gp**.                                                                                                               |
| 91+   | **Small fortune from gambling**, covering lifestyle costs + **4d6 × 10 gp**. Becomes **local legend**.                                                                                  |
