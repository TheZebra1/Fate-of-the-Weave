*Characters can **attend rituals**, **funerals**, **proselytize**, or **serve at a temple** to gain favor.*
## Resources
- Requires **1 workweek** at a **temple aligned with your beliefs**.
- No gold cost.
## Execution
- Make an **Intelligence (Religion) or Charisma (Persuasion) check**.
- The total result determines benefits.
## Complications
Each **workweek of service** has a **10% chance of a complication**. Roll **d6**:

| d6  | Complication |
|----|-------------|
| **1** | **Offended a priest** with words/actions. * |
| **2** | **Committed blasphemy**, even by accident. |
| **3** | A **secret sect** invites you to join. |
| **4** | Another **temple tries to recruit you** as a spy. * |
| **5** | Temple elders send you on a **holy quest**. |
| **6** | Discover a **fiend worshiper** in the temple. |
* *Might involve a rival.*
