## Process
- Requires **downtime in a city** to find a buyer.
	- Legendary items & artifacts require **adventuring to sell**.
- Roll **DC 20 Intelligence (Investigation) check** to find a buyer.
  - **Fail:** No buyer, **10 days wasted**.
  - **Success:** Buyer found in **1d4 to 1d10 days** (see table).
  - Another character assisting grants **advantage** on the check.
### Salable Magic Items
| Rarity     | Base Price  | Days to Find Buyer | d100 Roll Mod. |
|-----------|------------|--------------------|----------------|
| **Common**     | 100 gp    | 1d4  | +10  |
| **Uncommon**   | 500 gp    | 1d6  | +0   |
| **Rare**       | 5,000 gp  | 1d8  | -10  |
| **Very Rare**  | 50,000 gp | 1d10 | -20  |
## Selling a Magic Item
1. Roll **d100 + modifier** (from table above).
2. Roll **Charisma (Persuasion) check** and **add to total**.
3. Compare result to the table below:

| d100 + Mod. | Offer Found |
|------------|-------------|
| **≤ 20**   | Buyer offers **10%** of base price. |
| **21–40**  | Buyer offers **25%**, shady buyer offers **50%**. |
| **41–80**  | Buyer offers **50%**, shady buyer offers **100%**. |
| **81–90**  | Buyer offers **full price**. |
| **91+**    | Shady buyer offers **150%**, no questions asked. |
### Additional Rules
- **Shady buyers** may cause **legal trouble later**.
- The DM **determines the buyer's identity** (noble, merchant, criminal, etc.).
