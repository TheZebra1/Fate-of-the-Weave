A character can **spend downtime** constructing a bastion. Before work begins, they must acquire **land**, which may require a **royal charter**, **land grant**, or **deed**. Land can also be ***inherited, won or purchased*** (small estates **100–1,000 gp**, large estates **5,000+ gp**).

Once land is secured, **materials and labor** are needed. Work continues while the character is away, but money must be dropped off at a bank for **Transport** *(Transport check: Nat 1 = Money Stolen)* or dropped off **personally** at the **Bastion** site.
## Bastion Construction Costs & Time
| Bastion Type              | Cost       | Time   |
|---------------------------|-----------|--------|
| Abbey                     | 50,000 gp | 400 days |
| Guildhall (town/city)     | 5,000 gp  | 60 days  |
| Keep or small castle      | 50,000 gp | 400 days |
| Noble estate with manor   | 25,000 gp | 150 days |
| Outpost or fort           | 15,000 gp | 100 days |
| Palace or large castle    | 500,000 gp | 1,200 days |
| Temple                    | 50,000 gp | 400 days |
| Tower, fortified          | 15,000 gp | 100 days |
| Trading post              | 5,000 gp  | 60 days  |
- Builder will need to check in periodically so a method of communication should be secured.
