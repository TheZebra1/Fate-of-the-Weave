*Relaxation provides a **break between adventures**, useful for **recovery** or **downtime**.*
## Resources
- Requires **1 week**.
- Must maintain at least a **modest lifestyle**.
## Benefits
- Gain **advantage on saving throws** against **diseases & poisons**.
- At the end of the week, you may:
  - **End one effect** preventing hit point recovery.
  - **Restore one ability score** reduced below normal (unless caused by ongoing magic).
- **Gain perks from your Bastion that come from resting(*Gain these benefits even if not at the Bastion*)**
