*Pit fighting includes **boxing, wrestling, and nonlethal combat** in an organized setting*.
## Resources
- Requires **1 night** of participation.
- **DC:** **5 + 2d10** (randomly determined per opponent).
## Execution
- Make **three checks**:
  - **Strength (Athletics)**
  - **Dexterity (Acrobatics)**
  - **Special Constitution check** (add a roll of your **largest Hit Die** to the result).
  *(One check may be replaced with a weapon attack roll.)*
### Outcome
| Successes | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| **0** | Lose all bouts, earn **nothing**. |
| **1** | Win **50 gp**. |
| **2** | Win **100 gp**. |
| **3** | Win **200 gp**. |
## Special Event 
*If players are **desirous** of a showdown to play out **live**, they may request a special event pit fight played out as a **combat encounter**.*
## Complications
Each **night of pit fighting** has a **10% chance of a complication**.
- Roll 1d20
	- 1 or 2 causes a *complication*
		- ***Complication***: Roll 1**d6**

| d6  | Complication |
|----|-------------|
| **1** | **Opponent vows revenge**. * |
| **2** | **Crime boss** offers a bribe to throw matches. * |
| **3** | Defeat a **popular champion**, angering the crowd. |
| **4** | Defeat a **noble’s servant**, angering their house. * |
| **5** | **Accused of cheating**, reputation suffers. * |
| **6** | **Accidentally injure** an opponent severely. |
* *Might involve a rival.*
