*Gambling is a **high-risk, high-reward** way to win (or lose) gold.*
## 1. Resources
- Requires **1 workweek** and a **stake** (10–1,000+ gp).
- **DC:** **5 + 2d10** (randomly set per competition).
## 2. Execution
- Make **three checks**:
  - **Wisdom (Insight)**
  - **Charisma (Deception)**
  - **Charisma (Intimidation)**  
  *(Proficiency in a gaming set allows replacing one check with tool proficiency.)*
### Outcome
| Successes | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| **0** | Lose **all** your bet and incur **debt equal to it**. |
| **1** | Lose **half** your bet. |
| **2** | Win bet **+50% extra**. |
| **3** | Win **double** your bet. |
## 3. Complications
Each **workweek of gambling** has a **10% risk of a complication**. 
- Roll 1d20
	- 1 or 2 causes a *complication*
		- ***Complication***: Roll 1**d6**

| d6  | Complication |
|----|-------------|
| **1** | Accused of **cheating** – were you framed? * |
| **2** | **Guards raid** the hall and throw you in **jail**. * |
| **3** | **Noble loses badly** and swears **revenge**. * |
| **4** | You won money from a **thieves' guild member**, and they want it back. |
| **5** | **Crime boss** demands you gamble **only at their parlor**. |
| **6** | A **high-stakes gambler** challenges you to a match. |
* *Might involve a rival.*
