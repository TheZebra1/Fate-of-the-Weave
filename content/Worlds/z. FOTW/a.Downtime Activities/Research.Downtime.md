*Characters can **gather lore** on **monsters, locations, magic, or history**.*
## Resources
- Requires **1 workweek** and **50 gp** (bribes, materials, etc.).
	- Each **+100 gp spent** grants **+1 bonus** (max **+6**).
- **Advantage** if using a **well-stocked library or sages**.
## Execution
- Make an **Intelligence check**.
- Determine knowledge gained from the table:
### Research Outcomes
| Check Total | Outcome |
|------------|--------|
| **1–5**  | No useful information. |
| **6–10** | Learn **1 piece of lore**. |
| **11–20** | Learn **2 pieces of lore**. |
| **21+**  | Learn **3 pieces of lore**. |
**Lore examples:** *Creature resistances, dungeon passwords, hidden entrances, secret rituals.*
## Complications
Each **workweek of research** has a **10% chance of a complication**. Roll **d6**:

| d6  | Complication |
|----|-------------|
| **1** | **Damaged a rare book** by accident. |
| **2** | **Offended a sage**, now they demand a gift. * |
| **3** | The **book was cursed** – too late now. |
| **4** | A sage tries to recruit you into **weird theories**. * |
| **5** | **Banned from a library** until reparations are made. * |
| **6** | Gained useful lore, but had to promise a **dangerous task**. |

* *Might involve a rival.*
