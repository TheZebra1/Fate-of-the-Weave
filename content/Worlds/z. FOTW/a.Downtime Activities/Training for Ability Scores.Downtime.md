*Characters may **hire a trainer** and spend **3 months of downtime** after leveling up to **increase an ability score by 1**.*
## Training Requirements
- Must **locate a trainer** in a **big city**.
- Pay the **trainer's fee** (scales based on current ability score).
- Base stat cannot exceed **20 in any ability score**.
## Trainer Fees (per ability score point)
| Current Ability Score | Fee (GP) |
|----------------------|---------|
| **6 or lower**      | 50 gp   |
| **7–8**            | 100 gp  |
| **9–10**           | 250 gp  |
| **11–12**          | 500 gp  |
| **13–14**          | 1,000 gp |
| **15–16**          | 5,000 gp |
| **17**             | 10,000 gp |
| **18**             | 25,000 gp |
| **19**             | 50,000 gp |
| **20+**            | Training unavailable |
### Additional Notes
- Trainers may **refuse students** with poor ***reputations***.
	- The DM may require a **Charisma (Persuasion) check** to secure a trainer.
