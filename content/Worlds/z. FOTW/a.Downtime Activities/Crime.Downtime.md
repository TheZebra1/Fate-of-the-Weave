*Crime offers a **high-risk, high-reward** way to earn gold.*
## Resources
- Requires **1 week** of planning and **25 gp** to gather intel.
- The **DC** (10–25) determines potential earnings.
## Execution
- Make **three checks**: *One player may make all 3 checks or it may be split up between those involved, unless otherwise specified.*
  - **Dexterity (Stealth)**
  - **Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)**
  - **Choice of:** Intelligence (Investigation), Wisdom (Perception), or Charisma (Deception)
### Outcome
| Successes | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| **0** | Caught & jailed; pay fine (equal to profit) & serve **1 week per 25 gp** owed. |
| **1** | Heist **fails**, but you escape. |
| **2** | **Partial success** – earn **half** the payout. |
| **3** | **Full success** – earn full payout. |
## Loot Value
| DC  | Value |
|----|-------|
| **10** | 50 gp – struggling merchant. |
| **15** | 100 gp – prosperous merchant. |
| **20** | 200 gp – noble. |
| **25** | 1,000 gp – richest figure in town. |
## Complications
A failed heist or **partial success** (2 successes) triggers a **complication**. Roll **d8**:

| d8  | Complication |
|----|-------------|
| **1** | Bounty placed on your head **equal to earnings**. * |
| **2** | **Blackmail** – someone threatens to expose you. * |
| **3** | Victim is **financially ruined**. |
| **4** | Someone aware of your crime was **arrested**. * |
| **5** | Loot is **too recognizable** to sell locally. |
| **6** | Victim was **under a crime lord’s protection** – now they seek revenge. |
| **7** | Victim calls in a **favor from law enforcement**, doubling efforts to catch you. |
| **8** | Victim hires an **adventurer to track you down**. |
* *Might involve a rival.*
