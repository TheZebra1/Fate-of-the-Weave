*Characters can earn money through **temporary labor**.*
## Resources + Benefits
- Requires **any specified number of days** of effort.
- While working you will not have to pay living expenses for food and drink of a level equal to or below your wage level.
## Execution
- Make an **ability check** based on the job:
  - **Strength (Athletics)**
  - **Dexterity (Acrobatics)**
  - **Intelligence (relevant tool proficiency)**
  - **Charisma (Performance or musical instrument proficiency)**
### Wages

| Check Total    | Earnings                                             |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **9 or lower** | **Poor** lifestyle wage per day.                     |
| **10–14**      | **Modest** lifestyle wage per day.                   |
| **15–20**      | **Comfortable** wage per day.                        |
| **21+**        | **Comfortable** lifestyle wage per day. + **25 gp**. |
 #### Lifestyle

| Lifestyle        | Price/Day     |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Wretched       | ---          |
| Squalid        | 1 sp         |
| Poor          | 2 sp         |
| Modest        | 1 gp         |
| Comfortable   | 2 gp         |
| Wealthy       | 4 gp         |
| Aristocratic  | 10 gp minimum |
## Complications
Each **workweek** has a **10% chance of a complication**. Roll **d6**:

| d6  | Complication |
|----|-------------|
| **1** | **Difficult customer** or **coworker fight** reduces wages. * |
| **2** | **Employer struggles financially**, you don’t get paid. * |
| **3** | **Coworker with family ties** dislikes you. * |
| **4** | Employer is involved in a **dark cult or criminal group**. |
| **5** | A **crime ring targets your workplace** for extortion. * |
| **6** | You gain a **reputation for laziness**, giving **disadvantage** on future work rolls for 6 weeks. * |
* *Might involve a rival.*
