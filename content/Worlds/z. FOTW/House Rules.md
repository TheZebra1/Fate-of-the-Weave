### 1. **Feats**
1. "Feats" are no longer tied to classes. You gain "Feats" at Levels 4/8/12/16/19, regardless of your Multi-class composition.
	- Fighters will still gain their bonus "Feats" at *6th* & *14th* **Fighter** levels.
	- Rogues will still gain their bonus Feat at *10th* level.

### 2. **_Dramatic Player Death_**
- Dropping to 0 hit points causes a creature to roll on the [[Critical Injury Table]] & suffer the [[conditions#dying|Dying]] condition.
- While suffering this condition you roll a death saving throw on your turn and your agency is restricted to the following:
	1. Gain *1 level of [[conditions#Exhaustion|exhaustion]]* to make an:
		1. Movement at 1/4 speed *Death Crawl*
		2. Object Interaction
		3. Talk a short sentence to someone within 15 feet.
- Player characters will often be allowed some ***Final Words*** as they perish. *(Situational exceptions may occur)*

### 3. **Potions**
- Potions can be an action or bonus action.
1. Drinking a potion with an action yields full healing.

### 4. **Critical Criticals**
1. Critical hits double your damage dice like normal, except the second dice is maximized automatically.
2. Gain one [[conditions#exhaustion|exhaustion]] when receiving a critical hit.

### 6. **More Realistic Combat**
1. No strategizing ***during*** combat.
	- Instead, you have 1 minute in between each round to strategize and then the round starts.
	- Once the round has started there will be no strategizing. **You are in combat**.
2. Players can describe their condition, but do not disclose HP.

### 7. **Sleep Deprivation**
- Failure to sleep on a proper surface results in a 10% Max HP penalty (rounded down).
- Debuff removed by a Long Rest with appropriate accommodations.

### 8. **Malnutrition**
#### 🌾 Food Requirements
- A creature needs an amount of food per day based on its size (see *[[Food Needs per Day]]* table).
- If a creature consumes **half or less** of the required food in a day:
  - It must succeed on a **DC 10 Constitution saving throw**.
  - On a failure, it gains **1 level of Exhaustion** at the end of the day.
- If a creature eats **nothing** for **5 consecutive days**:
  - It automatically gains **1 level of Exhaustion** at the end of the **5th day**.
  - It then gains **1 additional level** for **each day** without food thereafter.
- **Exhaustion caused by malnutrition** cannot be removed until the creature consumes the **full daily food requirement**.
>[[rations-xphb|Rations]]
#### 💧 Water Requirements
- A creature needs water per day depending on its size and environment:
  - **Medium or Small creature**: 1 gallon per day
	  - **In hot weather**: 2 gallons per day
  - *(A standard waterskin holds 0.5 gallons/4 pints)*

- If a creature consumes **half or less** of the required water in a day:
  - It must succeed on a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw**.
	  - On a failure, it gains **1 level of Exhaustion** at the end of the day.
	  - If the creature already has Exhaustion, it gains **2 levels** instead.

- If a creature consumes **no water**:
  - It automatically gains **1 level of Exhaustion** at the end of the day.
  - If the creature already has Exhaustion, it gains **2 levels** instead.

- **Exhaustion caused by dehydration** cannot be removed until the creature drinks the **full daily water requirement**.

### 11. **Dark Vision Rarity**
- Dark vision is rare and most races do not have it.
- Your player character can replace dark vision with an extra skill, language, or even tool proficiency.

### 12. **Initiative Down the River**
- Once per combat you may deny your turn priority and insert yourself farther down in the round where desired.
- You may never increase your priority, only decrease it.

### 13. Upon death, players may allow their character to die or come back as a revenant with a singular powerful goal, approved by the DM, and upon completion, the character is impossible to revive.

### 14. **[[conditions|Condition]] change logs**
1. [[conditions#Stunned|Stunned]] take disadvantage on all saves, checks, attacks, etc. until the end of the stun.
	- Incapacitation effect removed.
2. [[conditions#Paralyzed|Paralyzed]] creatures have a speed of 0 and take disadvantage on all saves, checks, attacks, etc. until the end of paralysis.
	- Incapacitation effect removed.
3. [[conditions#Surprised|Surprised]]
	- ***Uses 2024 rules***
		- Surprised creatures roll initiative at disadvantage.
		- Surprised creatures cannot use reactions until their first turn of combat.

### 15. **Flanking**

### 22. Called Shots *(CAUTION: ACTIVATING THIS RULE WILL ENABLE IT FOR ENEMIES)*
- You may target a specific area of a creature by adding disadvantage to your attack roll.
	- If you have advantage already the attack is neutral
	- You may not use called shots if there is a preexisting instance of disadvantage already affecting the attack.
### 23. Dashing Exhaustion/Sprinting IS Tiring
- Taking the Dash action, not granted by a spell, special ability, or item, will trigger a DC 15 Constitution check giving 1 [[conditions#Exhaustion|Exhuastion]] which expires at the end of combat/initiative as in the [[Chasing Rules]].
	- You cannot die from taking exhaustion in this manner, however you can pass out if level 6 exhaustion is reached.
### 24. Monster Pokédex
- Once you ***defeat*** an enemy, their statblock can be accessed by request at any point afterward.

### 25. Long Rests Are Long.. Short Rests are Shortly Long
#### While in a Safe Haven 
- A **Long Rest** can be achieved in one night of rest.
#### While Adventuring
- A **Short Rest**  can be taken anywhere over the course of 8 hours, 6 of which must be spent sleeping, 2 of which may be [[a.Downtime Activities]].
	- Short rests restore
		- **1/3 maximum Hit Dice**. *(For all)*
		- **1/3 maximum number of spell slots** beginning with the **lowest level first.** *(For classes that normally restore spells on a Long Rest*
- A **Long Rest** can be achieved by completing **3 short rests, without "Breaking Rest"** 
	- At the end of a **3rd** successful **short rest** characters gain:
		- *Maximum Spell Slots* 
		- *All Hit Dice Restored*
		- *Abilities requiring "Long Rests" refresh.*
#### Rest Breakers
> "**Breaking Rest**" grants a choice 
> - No benefits gained from rest 
> - Benefits gained + 1 [[conditions#Exhaustion|Exhaustion]]
- Failing to sleep for 6 hours by choice or circumstance breaks rest. 
	- *(Combat under 1 minute is ignored as long as you go back to sleep **immediately** after the fight)*
- Hitting **0 Hit Points** during a rest adds **1 [[conditions#Exhaustion|Exhustion]] and breaks rest.**

### 26. Quick Rests
- If you have a moment to take a breather (*Roughly 10 mins*) you may use any ***Hit Dice*** you have to replenish health.
- This is only restores health, not abilities that refresh on a short rest.
- Useful in a pinch when the area is too dangerous to stop for long.
